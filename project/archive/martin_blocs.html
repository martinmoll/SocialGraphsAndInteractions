Check 2

Do the blocs speak differently?

What this measures

Captures: shared vocabulary in floor speeches. Each member is a TF-IDF vector; the number is the average cosine similarity of two members in the same bloc minus that of two members in different blocs.

Misses: stance. Two members can use the same word on opposite sides. It also misses everything said outside the House floor.

Why the second setting matters: colleagues from one state share place names and local topics. Comparing only members from different states removes that explanation.
