  PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 1689, DESIGNATION OF HAITI FOR 
                       TEMPORARY PROTECTED STATUS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote adoption of the resolution (H. Res. 
965) providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 1689) to require the 
Secretary of Homeland Security to designate Haiti for temporary 
protected status, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
