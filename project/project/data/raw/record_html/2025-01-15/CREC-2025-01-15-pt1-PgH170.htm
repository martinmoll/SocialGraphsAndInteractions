          UNITED STATES-TAIWAN EXPEDITED DOUBLE-TAX RELIEF ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 33) to 
amend the Internal Revenue Code of 1986 to provide special rules for 
the taxation of certain residents of Taiwan with income from sources 
within the United States, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 423, 
nays 1, not voting 9, as follows:

15, H.R. 33.


                          personal explanation
