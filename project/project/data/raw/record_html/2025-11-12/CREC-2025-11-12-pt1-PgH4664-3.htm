                              ADJOURNMENT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 13 of rule I, and 
pursuant to House Resolution 874, the House stands adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, November 13, 2025, at 9 a.m., as a further mark of 
respect to the memory of the late Honorable Richard B. Cheney.
  Thereupon (at 8 o'clock and 26 minutes p.m.), under its previous 
order, the House adjourned until tomorrow, Thursday, November 13, 2025, 
at 9 a.m.
