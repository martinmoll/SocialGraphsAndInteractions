    REAFFIRMING IRAN REMAINS THE LARGEST STATE SPONSOR OF TERRORISM

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
agree to the resolution (H. Res. 1099) reaffirming Iran remains the 
largest state sponsor of terrorism, on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Mast) that the House suspend the rules and 
agree to the resolution.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 372, 
nays 53, answered ``present'' 2, not voting 5, as follows:
