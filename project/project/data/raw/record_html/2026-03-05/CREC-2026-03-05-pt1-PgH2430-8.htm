             RECOGNIZING SOUTH CAROLINA EXPORT ACHIEVEMENTS

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am grateful for the 
leadership of Governor Henry McMaster, South Carolina Commerce 
Secretary Harry Lightsey, South Carolina Ports Authority President 
Micah Mallace, and Board Chairman Bill Stern.
  The Ports Authority announced that, in 2025, our exports for the 
Palmetto State totaled $38.5 billion, the highest value in 7 years. 
Charleston is led by Mayor William Cogswell.
  Motor vehicles and parts, aircraft and parts, and machinery and parts

[[Page H2431]]

were the most exported commodities. South Carolina has exported to 198 
countries and territories, creating jobs.
  In 2025, we were the leading exporter of BMW, Volvo, and Mercedes 
vehicles, as well as the national leader in the export of Michelin, 
Bridgestone, Continental, Giti, and Trelleborg tires.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstating peace through strength through 
revealing war Putin criminal lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump, 
as Putin supports Iran today after the attacks on civilians in 
Azerbaijan and Syria.
  I am confident that President Ilham Aliyev of Azerbaijan and 
President Ahmed al-Sharaa of Syria will respond, protecting Syrians.
  South Carolina also recognizes the passage of our favorite son, Jesse 
Jackson.
