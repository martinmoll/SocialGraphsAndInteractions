                         HONORING HOLLY HAGLER

  (Mr. CORREA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CORREA. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Holly Hagler and her 
dedication to service through nonprofit work.
  Ms. Hagler has spent much of her career serving our community's 
fastest-growing demographic, adults 65 and older. Since becoming the 
Orange County chapter president and CEO of Meals on Wheels in 2010, Ms. 
Hagler has led the delivery of meals to more than 15,000 older adults 
per year.
  Ms. Hagler has also served on the leadership council of the Orange 
County strategic plan for the aging and as board chair of the National 
Association of Nutrition and Aging Services. She has been recognized 
many times by the L.A. Times in both chambers of the California State 
Legislature.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Holly on a career filled with many 
accomplishments. We will miss her.
