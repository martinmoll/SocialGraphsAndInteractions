         HONORING THE PUBLIC SERVICE OF DR. SYLVIA GUERRA RIOS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 23, 2025

  Mr. CUELLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the extraordinary 
career of Dr. Sylvia Guerra Rios, a dedicated educator and a pillar of 
the South Texas community.
  For 48 years, Dr. Rios has been a steadfast advocate for students and 
teachers--as a classroom teacher, principal, curriculum leader, and 
ultimately, Superintendent of the Laredo Independent School District. 
She has spent her life improving the lives of others through education.
  Since 2017, she has led LISD--serving over 21,000 students and nearly 
4,000 employees--with distinction. Under her leadership, LISD has 
earned 11 National Blue Ribbon School honors, with three more schools 
recently nominated. These achievements reflect her unwavering focus on 
academic excellence and student opportunity.
  A Hebbronville native and proud public school graduate, Dr. Rios was 
valedictorian of her high school class. She went on to earn degrees 
from Texas A&I University and a Ph.D. from Texas A&M University. Her 
professional rise mirrors the values she brought into every school--
discipline, compassion, and vision.
  Dr. Rios has also represented our region with distinction on the 
state stage and was inducted into the Laredo Commission for Women Hall 
of Fame in 2018.
  Mr. Speaker, Dr. Rios is not just an accomplished administrator--she 
is a role model and a servant leader. Her impact will be felt for 
generations.
  On behalf of the 28th District of Texas, I thank Dr. Rios and her 
family for their service and dedication. We wish her the very best in a 
well-earned retirement.
