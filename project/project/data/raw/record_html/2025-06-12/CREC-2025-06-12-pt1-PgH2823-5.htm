           By Mr. OLSZEWSKI:
       H.R. 3988.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       To direct the Secretary of Housing and Urban Development 
     and the Director of the Bureau of the Census to conduct a 
     study and submit a report about how Federal agencies identify 
     and record cases of housing loss in the United States, and 
     for other purposes (Article I, Section 8, Clause 18).
