                      RECOGNIZING E.J. BASLER CO.

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE QUIGLEY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, June 12, 2025

  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize E.J. Basler Co. 
in Schiller Park, Illinois.
  E.J. Basler Co., a family-owned manufacturing company, has been based 
in Schiller Park since 1957. The company was built on the core values 
of quality and customer service, founded by Erv and Anne Basler, 
children of immigrants. Now in its third generation of family 
leadership, E.J. Basler Co. is run by Dennis and Brian Basler. Their 
ongoing commitment to responsive service and high-quality manufacturing 
has supported strong, long-term relationships with clients and vendors 
in Illinois' fifth district and across the globe. E.J. Basler Co. has 
achieved lasting success by staying true to its original values while 
evolving with current times.
  E.J. Basler Co. offers precision machinery services that leverage 
different techniques, cutting-edge technology, and expertise to create 
the most cost-effective solutions for customers. They manufacture from 
iconel, brass, steel, plastic, and copper. Manufacturing is crucial to 
the global economy, and E.J. Basler Co. delivers high-quality solutions 
across industries, working with the best of agriculture, plumbing, 
automotive, industrial, defense, and medical companies. The close 
proximity to E.J. Basler Co. and its valuable services offer companies 
in Illinois' fifth district a competitive advantage and drive economic 
development within the district.

[[Page E572]]

  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating the 
longstanding dedication and success of E.J. Basler Co. I am honored to 
have such an exceptional company in my district.
