                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. CALVERT:
       H.R. 5726. A bill to amend chapter 303 of title 10, United 
     States Code, to require the Secretary of each military 
     department to identify promising research programs of the 
     Small Business Innovation Research Program or Small Business 
     Technology Transfer Program for inclusion in the future 
     budgets and plans of the Department of Defense, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Armed Services.
           By Mr. COHEN:
       H.R. 5727. A bill to eliminate racial, religious, and other 
     discriminatory profiling by law enforcement, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on the Judiciary.

             Balint, Mr. Sherman, Ms. Salinas, Ms. Pettersen, Mrs.

     permit the assumption of loans under the Doug Bereuter 
     Section 502 Single Family Housing Loan Guarantee Program; to

             Biggs of Arizona, Mr. LaMalfa, and Mr. Tiffany):

     impacted by the Dragon Bravo Fire; to the Committee on 
     Natural Resources.

     consideration of such provisions as fall within the 
     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
