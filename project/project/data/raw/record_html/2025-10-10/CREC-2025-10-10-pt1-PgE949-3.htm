   CELEBRATING THE 130TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE ARIEL CLUB OF ASBURY PARK

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. FRANK PALLONE, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 10, 2025

  Mr. PALLONE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to join The Ariel Club, the 
oldest women's club in the State of New Jersey, as it celebrates its 
130th anniversary this year. Since its incorporation in 1895, The Ariel 
Club has provided a supportive social environment for women of Asbury 
Park to connect, share experiences, and foster friendships.
  Together, 10 women chartered and founded the first Asbury Park ariel 
cycles club to pair bicycle riding throughout the city with the 
community. Social clubs create a space for people to connect with their 
interests, hobbies, and businesses, and foster purposeful and 
influential partnerships. The Ariel Club's earliest rewarding community 
endeavors have transpired into a meaningful practice for several 
generations of women to follow.
  In 1896, members began a collective effort to raise funds, volunteer 
their time, and donate supplies to the Asbury Park Library. This 
renowned group of women raised $4,000 for the library of Asbury Park. 
Over the years, they have donated a beautiful large mahogany mantle 
clock, sponsored a beloved reading room, and continued to finance the 
clock's repairs today. It is a significant achievement that the Asbury 
Park Library could undergo several refurbishment projects and remain a 
fine resource for residents of Asbury Park after all these years. With 
forty current members, The Ariel Club remains a vibrant tight-knit 
group whose positive impact on the greater community stands as a 
special reminder of the value of neighborly connection.
  It is my privilege to honor The Ariel Club as its members mark this 
historic 130th anniversary, and I ask my colleagues to join me in 
celebrating this milestone. The Ariel Club truly deserves this body's 
recognition.
