           By Mr. BEGICH:
       H.J. Res. 131.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to enact this joint resolution 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 of the United 
     States Constitution, which grants Congress the power to make 
     all laws necessary and proper for carrying into execution its 
     powers, including the authority provided by chapter 8 of 
     title 5, United States Code (the Congressional Review Act), 
     to disapprove agency rules.
