                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Lisa Wink Schultz, Office of the Senate Chaplain, offered 
the following prayer:
  God of grace, You tell us in the letter to the Galatians that the 
whole law is fulfilled in one simple statement: You shall love your 
neighbor as yourself. Empower us by Your spirit to live our lives full 
of love for You and for every living creature that bears Your image. As 
we enter the tenth day of the government shutdown, remind us that if we 
have all faith and give all our possessions to feed the poor, without 
love, it profits us nothing.
  Lord, may we find peace in knowing that neither height, nor depth, 
nor things present nor things to come can separate us from Your love.
  We pray in Your merciful name.
  Amen.
