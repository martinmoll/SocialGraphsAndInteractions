                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Lord, make Yourself known to us. Reveal Yourself even in the simple 
things: a meal shared, a helping hand, a kind word spoken. Grant us 
Your peace as we travel the roads on which we find ourselves, that in 
these small gestures, we may be aware of Your abiding presence in every 
aspect of our lives.
  And when our hearts are troubled, when questions arise from fear--
fear of an unknown future, fear of harm or retribution, fear of 
loneliness--show us the lengths to which You go to guide us, to protect 
us, and to assure us that You will never leave or forsake us.
  Grant that our eyes would be opened to see the fulfillment of Your 
timeless promises, to understand the divine purposes You are working 
out, to trust in the plans You have designed, which stand eternal 
against the fleeting adversities of our lives.
  Then may we bear witness, in the face of all we endure, to Your 
steadfast and redeeming work in our lives and in the world.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.
