  RECOGNIZING BOBBIE FULLMER AS CARO CHAMBER OF COMMERCE'S 2025 MERIT 
                              AWARD WINNER

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 9, 2026

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Bobbie Fullmer, 
recipient of the Caro Chamber of Commerce 2025 Merit Award, whose 
leadership, dedication, and commitment to service have made a lasting 
impact on the Caro community.
  Bobbie's story is one of both commitment and heart. She spent her 
early years in Lansing before returning to Caro, where she attended 
Caro Community Schools and graduated from Caro High School. She went on 
to earn both her bachelor's and master's degrees in business. Even 
while pursuing her education, Bobbie remained closely connected to her 
hometown, regularly returning on weekends to support her family's 
business, Caro Rental.
  Bobbie later returned home to care for her mother alongside her 
sister, Debbie, showing remarkable strength and devotion during a 
difficult time. Following her mother's passing, she continued to 
support her father and assist with the family business until his 
passing as well. Today, Bobbie and Debbie carry on that legacy as they 
continue to operate Caro Rental, grounded in a deep commitment to 
service and their community.
  Bobbie has followed in her father's footsteps by actively 
participating in civic and community organizations, including the Caro 
Chamber of Commerce, Caro Rotary, Caro Lions Club, Michigan State 
University Alumni, and the Caro Moose Lodge. Her unwavering dedication, 
selfless service, and generosity of time have strengthened the fabric 
of our community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Bobbie 
Fullmer for her outstanding contributions to the Caro community. Her 
commitment, compassion, and leadership make her a true role model, and 
her lasting impact will continue to inspire the community for years to 
come.
