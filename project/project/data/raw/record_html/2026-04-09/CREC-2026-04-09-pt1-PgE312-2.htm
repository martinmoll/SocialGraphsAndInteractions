   HONORING THE 2026 NORTH CENTRAL STATE COLLEGE HALL OF EXCELLENCE 
                               INDUCTEES

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIM JORDAN

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 9, 2026

  Mr. JORDAN. Mr. Speaker, the North Central State College Foundation 
will hold a ceremony in Shelby, Ohio, on April 16, 2026, to mark the 
induction of its 2026 Hall of Excellence class. I am honored to commend 
to the House and include in the Record this year's inductees:

       Mrs. Zoi Romanchuk serves as president of both PR Machine 
     Works and Slick Automated Solutions in Mansfield. She also 
     leads the Regional Manufacturing Coalition, a group she co-
     founded nearly a quarter century ago to support manufacturers 
     across a six-county area. Zoi is a member of the board of The 
     Ohio State University-Mansfield.
       Mr. Mark Romanchuk serves the people of Ohio's 22nd 
     District in the Ohio State Senate, where he chairs the 
     Medicaid Committee and is Vice Chair of the Small Business 
     and Economic Opportunity Committee. Mark is the owner of PR 
     Machine Works and co-founder of the Regional Manufacturing 
     Coalition.
       Mr. David Eichinger recently retired from the Eichinger-
     Brafford-McClead Group of Merrill Lynch Wealth Management, 
     where he held the role of Senior Vice President. He was a 
     founding member of the Richland Community Development Group, 
     formed to spur economic development in the region. As 
     chairman of MedCentral's Board of Trustees, he spearheaded a 
     partnership with OhioHealth that has resulted in a major 
     expansion of health care services in the county.

  Selection for the Hall of Excellence is a high honor accorded to only 
a small number of north central Ohioans each year. To be considered, 
individuals must demonstrate a vision for and dedication to excellence 
in the region.
  On behalf of the people of Ohio's 4th Congressional District, I am 
pleased to join in the accolades for these three distinguished 
community leaders as they are inducted into the Hall of Excellence.
