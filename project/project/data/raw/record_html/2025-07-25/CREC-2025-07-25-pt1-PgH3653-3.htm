                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Gracious God, as those who have been called from the multitude of 
communities that make up this great Nation now return to their 
districts, we pray Your blessing on them that they will find time not 
only to meet their numerous responsibilities, but to refresh, regroup, 
reframe, and rest after several long months of intense work here in the 
House of Representatives.
  Lord, bless them and keep them. Keep them safe from all harm and the 
unwarranted attacks on their person and their loved ones.
  Lord, make Your face to shine upon them and be gracious unto them, 
that they will walk in Your way and receive Your mercy as they turn 
from the way they may otherwise have chosen that was not Yours.
  Lord, turn Your face upon them, that they can do nothing but live 
into the love they find there.
  Grant us each Your peace in these days, in these times, and in our 
hearts.
  Sure of the blessings we receive when we call on Your holy name, we 
offer ourselves and our prayers to You.
  Amen.
