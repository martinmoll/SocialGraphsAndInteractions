             HONORING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF HILDA GUTIERREZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 6, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 100th birthday 
of Hilda Gutierrez in Millburn, New Jersey.
  On August 17, 1925, Hilda Gutierrez was born in Bogota, Columbia. Ms. 
Gutierrez immigrated to the United States of America from Columbia in 
1951. Hilda lived in the Bronx in New York City when she first arrived 
at our Great Nation. After that, she moved to East Orange, New Jersey 
and spent a few wonderful years there. In 1958, Hilda moved to her 
final location in Millburn, New Jersey, where she still resides today. 
She worked as a beautician for 40 years and made a lasting impact on 
her community. Hilda is a devoted wonderful mother, exemplifying the 
hardworking and dedicated sprit of an immigrant who came to the United 
States for a better life. Hilda has shown her resilience, courage, and 
unwavering strength. I am deeply proud of the achievements of Ms. Hilda 
Gutierrez.
  Mr. Speaker, as Hilda Gutierrez celebrates her 100th birthday, I am 
pleased to commend her for being an integral part of the Millburn area. 
I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring this determined woman 
truly dedicated to faith, passion, and the pursuit of a brighter 
future.
