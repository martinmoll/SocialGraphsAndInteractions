                  MAKING IN ORDER MORNING-HOUR DEBATE

  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent 
that the order of the House of January 3, 2025, providing for morning-
hour debate be extended for the remainder of the 119th Congress, except 
that House Resolution 976 shall supplant House Resolution 6.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
