 APPOINTMENT AS MEMBERS OF COMMITTEE TO NOTIFY THE PRESIDENT, PURSUANT 
                        TO HOUSE RESOLUTION 974

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 974, the Chair 
appoints the following Members to the committee to notify the President 
of the United States that a quorum of the House has assembled and that 
the House is ready to receive any communication that he may be pleased 
to make:
  the gentleman from Louisiana (Mr. Scalise) and
  the gentleman from New York (Mr. Jeffries).
