  RECOGNIZING NANA KWAME DWOMOH OF SOUTH DAKOTA FOR WINNING THE 2025 
                      CONGRESSIONAL APP CHALLENGE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DUSTY JOHNSON

                            of south dakota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 6, 2026

  Mr. JOHNSON of South Dakota. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and congratulate Nana Kwame Dwomoh of Brookings, South Dakota, for his 
achievement as the 2025 Congressional App Challenge winner for South 
Dakota.
  Nana Kwame developed an innovative app called WorkWellness, designed 
to promote healthier routines in the workplace. The app runs timed 
workout and stretch sessions, provides notifications to encourage 
movement, and suggests targeted stretches. His app features an AI-
powered wellness assistant that offers guidance on posture, healthy 
habits, and other wellness-related questions.
  Nana Kwame's accomplishment reflects his talent, dedication, and 
initiative, as well as the growing strength of STEM education in South 
Dakota. His success highlights the importance of fostering innovation 
and providing young people with opportunities to explore technology and 
coding.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating Nana Kwame Dwomoh on 
this well-deserved honor and in wishing him continued success in his 
future academic and professional pursuits.
