 CELEBRATING THE 50TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE CARNATIC MUSIC ASSOCIATION OF 
                             NORTH AMERICA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. RAJA KRISHNAMOORTHI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 6, 2026

  Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Carnatic Music Association of North America (CMANA) on the occasion of 
its 50th anniversary and to honor its extraordinary contributions to 
the cultural and musical heritage of our Nation through its 
preservation and promotion of Carnatic music.
  Founded in 1976, CMANA has steadfastly pursued its mission to 
promote, preserve, and advance the rich tradition of Carnatic music, a 
classical form of music that originated in South India. Over the past 
five decades, the organization has become a cornerstone of cultural 
enrichment in North America, bringing together artists, educators, and 
music enthusiasts to celebrate this timeless art form.
  Through its annual concerts, festivals, workshops, lectures, and 
educational initiatives, CMANA has fostered a vibrant community of 
Carnatic musicians and connoisseurs while promoting cross-cultural 
understanding and appreciation. Its programs have inspired generations 
of students and audiences, reinforcing the values of discipline, 
creativity, and collaboration that are integral to the pursuit of 
Carnatic music.
  CMANA has also played an invaluable role in engaging youth, providing 
opportunities for young people to perform, volunteer, and develop 
leadership and communication skills that serve them well beyond the 
stage. By connecting communities and celebrating diversity through 
music, CMANA continues to strengthen the bonds between cultures and 
generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend the leadership, members, and volunteers of the 
Carnatic Music Association of North America for their passion and 
dedication. Their efforts have ensured that Carnatic music continues to 
flourish and inspire in North America. I congratulate CMANA on this 
milestone anniversary and wish the organization continued success in 
the years ahead.
