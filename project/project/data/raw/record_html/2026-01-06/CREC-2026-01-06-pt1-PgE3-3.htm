    HONORING THE HEROISM OF PENNDEL BOROUGH RESIDENT JUSTIN RITORTO

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, January 6, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and commend a 
constituent from the 1st District of Pennsylvania, Mr. Justin Ritorto, 
for his selfless actions and willingness to assist during a residential 
fire in Penndel Borough.
  In the early morning hours of December 11, 2025, a dwelling fire 
placed residents of a Penndel Borough home in immediate danger. Amid 
this unfolding emergency, Mr. Ritorto stepped forward to assist Officer 
Sean Peck of the Penndel Borough Police Department as urgent efforts 
were made to alert occupants and facilitate their safe evacuation.
  At moments of crisis, the actions of individuals can make a profound 
difference. Mr. Ritorto's readiness to help, his calm presence, and his 
concern for the safety of others contributed meaningfully to the 
successful response that morning. His actions reflect the values of 
compassion, civic responsibility, and community-mindedness that 
strengthen public safety and bring neighbors together in times of need.
  While much attention is rightly given to the bravery of first 
responders, it is equally important to acknowledge those private 
citizens who choose to act in support of others without expectation of 
recognition or reward. Mr. Ritorto's conduct serves as a reminder that 
heroism is often found in ordinary individuals who rise to 
extraordinary circumstances.
  Mr. Speaker, acts such as these embody the spirit of service and 
solidarity that define strong communities. I ask my colleagues to join 
me in honoring Justin Ritorto for his actions.
