          130TH ANNIVERSARY OF OUR LADY OF THE LAKE UNIVERSITY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOAQUIN CASTRO

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 21, 2025

  Mr. CASTRO of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate Our Lady of the 
Lake University's 130th anniversary. A true San Antonio institution, 
the university's dedication to public service extends further than the 
classroom, uniting generations of students, faculty, staff, and 
community members.
  Since 1895, Our Lady of the Lake has been a true Jewel of San 
Antonio's Westside. Established by the Sisters of Divine Providence, 
the school was created with the intention of serving the community in 
all aspects of life. Throughout the school's 130 years, it has held 
true to its traditions, while constantly innovating to ensure its 
position as a preeminent institution of higher education. Whether it 
was teaching grade school students at the turn of the twentieth century 
as a parochial school, providing individuals from disadvantaged 
backgrounds with access to higher education, or implementing community-
based social service programs, Our Lady of the Lake has continually 
shown San Antonio and beyond that it is built on a commitment to 
service of all.
  Now, in 2025, Our Lady of the Lake is a university that embraces its 
history to continually drive academic excellence and community 
development. As a leader in numerous academic disciplines, the 
university attracts students from throughout the United States and the 
world. Across Our Lady of the Lake University's three campuses, more 
than 75 undergraduate and graduate programs, and successful athletic 
programs, the university has positioned itself to inspire current and 
future generations.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize an educational legacy defined by 
success, innovation, and an unwavering commitment to service.
