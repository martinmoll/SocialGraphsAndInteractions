                        RECOGNIZING BRIAN DILLON

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 21, 2025

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and recognize the 
exceptional dedication and hard work of one of my outstanding 
constituents, Brian Dillon.
  Brian has recently completed an internship in the Office of the Clerk 
for the House of Representatives, where he has been an invaluable 
asset, assisting with a wide range of matters that have come before 
him. This opportunity came after Brian made the remarkable decision to 
graduate from high school early, demonstrating not only his drive but 
his commitment to public service. With a deep sense of purpose, he 
traveled all the way from Florida to Washington, D.C., to contribute to 
the work of our government and lend a helping hand to the people of 
Florida and the United States.
  Throughout his time here, Brian has played a role in supporting the 
office with various tasks, including assisting staff with vacant 
offices, processing administrative paperwork, and assisting with other 
legislative duties. His dedication and tireless work ethic have not 
gone unnoticed, and he has made an impact during his time with the 
House.
  As Brian's internship draws to a close, he plans to return to 
Florida, where he will embark on a new chapter of his career as an 
electrician apprentice. I have no doubt that his future is bright, and 
I wish him the very best of luck as he takes this important step toward 
a successful career.
  It is young individuals like Brian who inspire all of us with their 
initiative, hard work, and commitment to their communities. I am proud 
to have him represent the great state of Florida, and I look forward to 
seeing the great things he will undoubtedly achieve in the years to 
come.
