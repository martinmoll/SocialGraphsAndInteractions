                        HONORING ROGER LUTZ, III

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 21, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Station Leader Roger Lutz, III of the Napa County Fire Department. I 
have named Mr. Lutz as the California 4th District's Public Safety Hero 
of the Year for Napa County because of his extraordinary selflessness, 
dedication and service to others.
  Born in San Luis Obispo and raised in Napa County, Mr. Lutz has 
dedicated his life to protecting and serving his community. After 
graduating from St Helena High School, Mr. Lutz pursued studies in Fire 
Science at Santa Rosa Junior College before earning his Emergency 
Medical Technician (EMT) Certification from Napa County Junior College.
  Mr. Lutz began his fire service career in 1992 as an Explorer at the 
Deer Park Napa County Fire Station before becoming a Volunteer 
Firefighter in 1993. Over the years, he advanced through the ranks to 
become the Station Lead, a role he has held for over a decade. In 
addition, he has served as an Engine Boss for more than 20 years, 
leading critical fire response efforts across the region. Throughout 
his dedicated service to the community, Mr. Lutz has also maintained 
full-time employment at his family's company.
  Mr. Lutz has been on the front lines of numerous fires. These include 
the Topanga Fire, Tubbs Fire, Glass Fire, Valley Fire, and most 
recently, the Eaton Fire. His leadership and expertise have been 
instrumental in mitigating these devastating wildfires and protecting 
lives and property.
  In addition to his exemplary fieldwork, Mr. Lutz has played a key 
role in shaping the next generation of firefighters. Through his 
mentorship, he has guided many of his peers into successful careers in 
the fire service. His influence and dedication to professional 
development are reflected in his work on multiple committees within the 
Napa County Fire Department, including the Equipment Committee, Adhoc 
Committee, Radio Communications Committee, and Training Officer 
Committee.
  Beyond his professional achievements, Mr. Lutz is known for his 
tireless work ethic, humility, and compassion. His colleagues and 
community members regard him as a leader who leads by example, never 
seeking recognition but always putting the needs of others first. His 
selflessness and dedication make him not only a respected firefighter 
but also a cherished friend and role model to all who know him.
  Mr. Speaker, Station Lead Roger Lutz, III embodies the highest ideals 
of public service and leadership. His decades of dedication to the fire 
service and our community have left an indelible mark on Napa County 
and beyond. It is with great respect and appreciation that we recognize 
his contributions and honor him here today.
