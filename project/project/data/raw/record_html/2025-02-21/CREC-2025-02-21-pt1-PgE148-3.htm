 HONORING LEIA PEREZ AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA FOR 
                          BLACK HISTORY MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, February 21, 2025

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Leia Perez, celebrated for her couture cake 
designs, has achieved widespread recognition for her exceptional 
craftsmanship through her award-winning bakery, Candyland Designs.
  Her work has been featured in prominent magazines, and she has made 
several television appearances regarding her work. Leia's dedication to 
excellence has earned her numerous prestigious accolades, including an 
NAACP award and the Military Order of the Purple Heart award, 
recognizing her profound commitment to philanthropy and community 
impact.
  Holding a B.S. in Business Management & Accounting from Case Western 
Reserve University, Leia actively contributes her expertise by serving 
on the executive boards of several nonprofit organizations. Her 
unwavering dedication to giving back is reflected in her partnerships 
with Make-A-Wish bakers, the Osceola NAACP, the Kissimmee Main Street 
Program, Team Kareem Memorial Foundation, Kiss Kornucopia's Community 
Shared Agriculture program, and the Black Empowerment Community 
Council. She also consistently donates a portion of her profits to No 
Kid Hungry, ensuring her impact extends far beyond her business. Leia's 
passion for philanthropy goes beyond financial contributions--she 
strives to create joy and lasting memories. As the founder of the 
Wedding Wish by Candyland Designs Foundation, she grants dream weddings 
and provides mental health support to couples facing life-altering 
challenges.
  Through initiatives like free Wellness Workshops, offering yoga, 
meditation, and art therapy, Leia empowers her community by promoting 
self-care and connection. Her heartfelt mission is to provide hope, 
joy, and support to those in need--work that she holds closest to her 
heart.
