         REMOVAL OF NAMES OF MEMBERS AS COSPONSORS OF H.R. 3614

  Ms. KAMLAGER-DOVE. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that the 
gentleman from Alabama (Mr. Moore), the gentleman from Florida (Mr. 
Rutherford), and the gentleman from Maryland (Mr. Ivey) be removed as 
cosponsors of H.R. 3614, the Fairness, Inclusion, Rehabilitation, and 
Expungement for Firefighters Act.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. McGuire). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentlewoman from California?
  There was no objection.
