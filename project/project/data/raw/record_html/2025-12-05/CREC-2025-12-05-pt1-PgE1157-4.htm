        CELEBRATING THE SOUTH TAMPA CHAMBER'S 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. KATHY CASTOR

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, December 5, 2025

  Ms. CASTOR of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the South 
Tampa Chamber on its 100th anniversary. The South Tampa Chamber is a 
leader and positive force for the entire Tampa Bay area particularly 
small business owners and the military families who serve at MacDill 
Air Force Base. President Kelly Flannery, in particular, has helped 
lift the organization to one of the stellar``go to'' business 
organizations in Florida. The Chamber has devoted a century of 
commitment to community involvement, connection, and growth, and we 
plan to cherish the lasting partnerships created for generations to 
come.
  The history of the South Tampa Chamber began as the Port Tampa 
Assembly of Businessmen in 1926 with a goal to strengthen the rapidly 
expanding relationship between commerce in the Port Tampa area and the 
community. The South Tampa Chamber called on local leaders to support 
efforts to expand transportation, improve infrastructure and economic 
prosperity. These efforts led to the development of the Port Tampa 
Chamber of Commerce and allowed for the expansion of the South Tampa 
Chamber.
  Throughout the years, the Chamber evolved from the South Gandy 
Chamber of Commerce to what it is known as today, the South Tampa 
Chamber of Commerce. In 2017, the South Tampa Chamber became one of 
twenty chambers in the State of Florida designated as a Certified PLUS 
Chamber by the Florida Association of Chamber Professionals. This 
achievement is due to the dedication to work in the membership areas of 
programming, advocacy, and leadership and administration. These 
membership areas serve as a framework to guide a prospering community 
that may continue to contribute to the Tampa Bay area.
  The South Tampa Chamber membership association comprises over 600 
businesses, organizations, elected officials, and community leaders. 
Through collaboration with partnerships such as the MacDill Air Force 
Base for over 75 years, the South Tampa Chamber has been able to 
support our Military with special programs and initiatives. In 2018, 
the South Tampa Chamber was recognized as a Three-Star Chamber of Valor 
by the U.S. Chamber of Commerce. This award is recognized as the 
highest honor for local and state chambers for a demonstration of 
support to Military personnel and their family members.
  Mr. Speaker, I am incredibly proud to support the network of 
individuals who foster a connection to community and success. On behalf 
of my neighbors of Tampa Bay, I am proud to congratulate the South 
Tampa Chamber on 100 years of exhibiting a forum for members to thrive 
through business partnerships and opportunities. It is dedicated 
business members, like those at the South Tampa Chamber, that make our 
Tampa Bay community a driving city for economic and local commerce 
growth. Our community is proud to have the South Tampa Chamber, and I 
applaud them on this incredible milestone of a century of members 
networking, conducting business, and enriching the South Tampa 
Community.
