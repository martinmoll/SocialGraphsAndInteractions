              HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF JIM GILLIS

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. TIM WALBERG

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, December 5, 2025

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, it is my special privilege to recognize Jim 
Gillis of Niles as

[[Page E1158]]

the Veteran of the Month for December and honor him for his 
distinguished service to our great Nation.
  Jim Gillis dedicated over two decades of his life to defending our 
freedoms as a member of the United States Army. He achieved the rank of 
Sergeant First Class and deployed repeatedly with some of America's 
finest Brigade Combat Teams and Divisions, consistently proving himself 
a leader of uncommon grit and character.
  For his 21 years of active service to our Nation, Jim was awarded 
over 40 U.S. Army and Department of Defense medals, including the 
Bronze Star, Meritorious Service, and the U.S. Army Commendation Medal 
with 10 oak leaf clusters. He has been named Division Iron Man, MP War 
Fighter, MP of the Quarter, Sergeant of the Year, and Distinguished 
Soldier of the Month for both Fort Bragg and the XVIII Airborne Corps.
  After his lengthy service to our Nation, Jim spent an additional 18 
years as a U.S. Army civilian, where he eventually became a contracting 
representative/specialist and the Deputy Housing Chief before his 
retirement.
  Jim has also demonstrated great physical strength throughout his 
career, allowing him to secure a place on the U.S. Army track team and 
set a Southern European record for the 3K run at the SETAF 
Championship.
  Beyond the accolades, what sets Jim apart is the spirit with which he 
served. Whether as an MP or as a Troops to Teachers participant, Jim 
uses both physical and emotional resilience to uplift others. He cares 
deeply for his soldiers, his colleagues, and his community, continuing 
to mentor, teach, and support others. He has also been working to 
establish a Junior Reserve Officers' Training Corps program at 
Edwardsburg High School to ensure the next generation is prepared to 
lead.
  Jim's dedication to his country did not end when he took off the 
uniform. Instead, he has carried it forward as a civilian. I am honored 
to share his story, and I urge my colleagues to join me in celebrating 
the significant accomplishments of this remarkable veteran.
