               'TIS THE WEEK BEFORE CHRISTMAS ON THE HILL

  (Ms. McCOLLUM asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. McCOLLUM. Mr. Speaker, 'tis the week before Christmas, and here 
on the Hill, to keep health insurance premiums from skyrocketing, we 
have just the bill.
  A majority of the House is ready to vote ``yes,'' but the Speaker is 
sending us home. Why? It is anyone's guess.
  ACA enhanced premium tax credits expire at the end of the year. 
Without them, millions will not afford healthcare, I fear.
  Small business owners and employees, gig workers, farmers, and the 
self-employed, too, without those tax credits, they don't know what 
they will do.
  The Speaker shrugged, saying our work for the year is done, but there 
is one bill we should pass before heading home--just one.
  Two hundred eighteen House Members signed a petition to bring a 3-
year tax credit extension to the floor. We should vote on it now before 
we head for the door.
  We will vote in January, so don't give up the fight. In the meantime, 
may your holiday season be merry and bright.
