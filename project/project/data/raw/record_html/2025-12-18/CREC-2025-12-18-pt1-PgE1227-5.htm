   RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF MR. STEVE BACHMANN FROM THE BOEING 
                                COMPANY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ADAM SMITH

                             of washington

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 18, 2025

  Mr. SMITH of Washington. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Steve 
Bachmann for his many years of dedication to our country, both through 
his military service in the United States Air Force and through his 
time at The Boeing Company.
  After graduating with a Bachelor of Science in Aerospace Engineering 
from the University of Virginia, Steve became an officer in the United 
States Air Force. During this time, he earned a Master of Science in 
Mechanical Engineering from the U.S. Air Force Institute of Technology. 
In 2006, after twenty years of military service, Steve went on to join 
The Boeing Company as Vice President for Legislative Affairs. 
Throughout his time at Boeing, Steve was known as a dedicated mentor 
who always made the time to share his knowledge and expertise. An 
honest and forthcoming friend, Steve brought humor and lightheartedness 
to tough conversations. After retirement, Steve plans to spend time 
with his grandkids and enjoying traveling in his RV. It has been a 
pleasure to work with him for the past twenty years and I wish him all 
the best.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Steve Bachmann for a lifetime of service to the 
United States of America and to his community. His legacy will not be 
forgotten.
