            By Mr. BEGICH:
        H.R. 3903.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article IV, Section 3, Clause 2 of the United States 
     Constitution, which provides that ``The Congress shall have 
     Power to dispose of and make all needful Rules and 
     Regulations respecting the Territory or other Property 
     belonging to the United States.''
       This bill exercises that authority by directing and 
     authorizing a land exchange involving federal lands and lands 
     held by the Chugach Alaska Corporation.
