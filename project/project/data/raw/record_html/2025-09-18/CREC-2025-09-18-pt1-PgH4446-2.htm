           COMMEMORATING BRENNA KULIKOWSKI AND DAMON THOMPSON

  (Mr. CRANE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. CRANE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to commemorate the lives of 
Brenna Kulikowski and Damon Thompson of the Timber Mesa Fire and 
Medical District.
  On Sunday, these brave firefighters lost their lives in the line of 
duty following a head-on collision while returning to Show Low.
  Although I had not met Damon, I knew Brenna, and my heart goes out to 
all of those who are grieving this loss. Please continue to pray for 
their families and the White Mountains region as they mourn this 
tragedy.
  As the community gathers in remembrance and gratitude, Brenna and 
Damon will be escorted back to Show Low, Arizona, and welcomed home as 
heroes.
  We are all forever grateful for their dedication and service to their 
fellow Arizonans.
