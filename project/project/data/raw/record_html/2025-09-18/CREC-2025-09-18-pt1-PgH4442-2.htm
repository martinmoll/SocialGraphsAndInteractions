               NATIONAL COAL COUNCIL REESTABLISHMENT ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Strong). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 
3015) to reestablish the National Coal Council in the Department of 
Energy to provide advice and recommendations to the Secretary of Energy 
on matters related to coal and the coal industry, and for other 
purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 217, 
nays 209, not voting 6, as follows:
