         EMPOWERING EMPLOYER CHILD AND ELDER CARE SOLUTIONS ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to recommit on the bill 
(H.R. 2270) to amend the Fair Labor Standards Act of 1938 to exclude 
child and dependent care services and payments from the rate used to 
compute overtime compensation, offered by the gentlewoman from 
Pennsylvania (Ms. Lee), on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk will redesignate the motion.
  The Clerk redesignated the motion.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion to recommit.
  This will be a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 209, 
nays 213, not voting 9, as follows:
