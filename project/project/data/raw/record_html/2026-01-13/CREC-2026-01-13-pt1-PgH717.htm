           By Mr. BAIRD (for himself, Mr. Comer, Mr. Evans of

       H.R. 7024. A bill to amend the Agriculture, Rural

     of the Agricultural Marketing Act of 1946; to the Committee 
     on Agriculture.

     retainer pay; to the Committee on Armed Services, and in 
     addition to the Committee on Veterans' Affairs, for a period 
     to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case

     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
