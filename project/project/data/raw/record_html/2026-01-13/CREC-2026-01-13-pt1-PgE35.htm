              HONORING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF RICHARD LYNCH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 13, 2026

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 100th birthday 
of Richard Lynch in White Township, New Jersey.
  Richard J. Lynch, a Warren County native and World War II U.S. Navy 
veteran, was honored this week. He celebrated his 100th birthday on 
January 7, 2026, while surrounded by family, friends, and fellow 
veterans.
  Lynch further received a quilt from the Quilts of Valor Foundation 
and a Distinguished Military Service Medal from the Warren County War 
Memorial Corporation for his service to our Nation.
  A Navy veteran of World War II, Lynch served as a signalman third 
class aboard the USS LST 1109. After his military service, he worked as 
a printer and volunteered as a Sunday school teacher in his local 
community. I invite my colleagues to join me in honoring this hero. 
There is no greater responsibility, nor debt of gratitude, than that 
which our Nation owes to its veterans.
