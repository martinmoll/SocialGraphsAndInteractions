 REQUESTING THE SENATE TO RETURN TO THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES H.R. 
                                  1834

  Mr. LaLOTA. Mr. Speaker, I send to the desk a privileged resolution 
and ask for its immediate consideration in the House.
  The Clerk read the resolution, as follows:

                              H. Res. 991

       Resolved, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
     request the Senate to return

[[Page H698]]

     to the House the bill (H.R. 1834) entitled ``To advance 
     policy priorities that will break the gridlock.''.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the resolution was agreed 
to.
  There was no objection.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
