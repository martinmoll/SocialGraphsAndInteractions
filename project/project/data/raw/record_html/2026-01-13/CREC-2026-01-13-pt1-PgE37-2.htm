   COMMENDING THE TAIWANESE AMERICAN CHAMBER OF COMMERCE OF GREATER 
                                CHICAGO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. RAJA KRISHNAMOORTHI

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 13, 2026

  Mr. KRISHNAMOORTHI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Taiwanese American Chamber of Commerce of Greater Chicago (TACCGC) for 
its longstanding contributions to economic development, 
entrepreneurship, and civic engagement in the Greater Chicago region.
  Founded in 1988, TACCGC has served as a vital forum for professionals 
and business leaders from a wide range of industries, fostering 
collaboration, supporting business growth, and strengthening the local 
economy.
  Beyond its work in business development, TACCGC actively promotes 
Taiwanese American interests, strengthens economic ties between the 
United States and Taiwan, and encourages participation in American 
civic life. Its commitment to community involvement and international 
cooperation reflects the values that drive lasting prosperity.
  I want to thank President Lisa Su and the other leaders for their 
leadership and service, and I am proud to recognize the organization's 
continued positive impact.
