         CONGRATULATING BISHOP MARIO AVILES ON HIS INSTALLATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 13, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Most Reverend Mario Alberto Aviles, C.O., on his 
appointment by Pope Leo XIV to serve as the ninth Bishop of Corpus 
Christi.
  In his previous role as Auxiliary Bishop for the Diocese of 
Brownsville, he served our region and uplifted those in need through 
his faith and compassion.
  His recent appointment is a testament to this leadership and the 
humility with which he serves. Bishop Aviles has consistently upheld 
the values of integrity and compassion that the Catholic church is 
proud to represent.
  This calling comes at a time when our Nation is facing hardship, and 
our people are divided. I can think of no better servant of God to 
provide ministry to our community.
  As South Texas continues to grow and navigate these complex 
challenges, Bishop Aviles' leadership has been essential in fostering 
hope, stability, and moral clarity for our community.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of a grateful South Texas, I wish to 
congratulate Most Reverend Mario Aviles, C.O., for his appointment as 
Bishop of Corpus Christi. I also wish to thank him for his service to 
the Rio Grande Valley.
