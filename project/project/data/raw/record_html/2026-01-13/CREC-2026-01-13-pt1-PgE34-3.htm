                         RECOGNIZING MARK SMITS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 13, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th Congressional District of Texas. 
The 2025 Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to 
Captain Mark Smits, United States Army, of Coppell, Texas, for his 
honorable service in the Armed Forces of the United States of America 
and for his continued service to his community.
  Captain Smits was commissioned into the United States Army in 1979 
and served as an engineer officer until 1983. During his military 
career, he demonstrated strong leadership and technical skill earning 
the Parachutist Badge, Humanitarian Service Medal, and Army Service 
Ribbon. His dedication to excellence and teamwork exemplified the 
values of duty, integrity, and service to others that define the United 
States Army.
  Following his military service, Captain Smits has continued a 
lifelong commitment to empowering others and supporting those who 
serve. As a Board Member and Mentor for Claymore Operations, he helps 
active-duty service members prepare for the transition to civilian life 
by providing personalized mentorship, career guidance, and leadership 
development. He also serves as Commander of the Military Order of the 
World Wars Dallas Chapter, where he promotes patriotism, civic 
responsibility, and leadership among veterans and students alike.
  As Chairman of the Dallas MOWW's Youth Leadership initiatives, 
Captain Smits leads programs that bring together JROTC cadets and high 
school students from across Texas to build leadership skills and foster 
a deeper appreciation for service and citizenship. His tireless work 
ensures that both current and future generations understand the value 
of duty, honor, and commitment to country.
  Captain Smits' service to his Nation and community is in keeping with 
the finest traditions of the United States Army and reflects great 
credit upon himself, the State of Texas, and the United States of 
America.
