     HONORING CAROL ABREU AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 21, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Carol Abreu as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year for her exemplary 
service and leadership in the Benicia-Vallejo community. Carol has 
earned the admiration and respect of her community through her tireless 
efforts to uplift others, promote good citizenship, and foster a spirit 
of compassion and inclusion. As Assistant to the Senior Pastor at 
Northgate Church, a non-denominational congregation serving the region, 
Carol plays a vital role in connecting individuals to support, 
resources, and

[[Page E983]]

fellowship. Her presence at the church's information desk is a point of 
welcome and warmth for all who enter. She goes out of her way to ensure 
that people are not just seen but known, remembered, and valued by 
name. Carol and her husband, Terry Abreu, a respected former Boy Scout 
Troop Leader, have long exemplified servant leadership. Together, they 
have volunteered countless hours feeding the hungry through organized 
meal programs held in the basements of several churches across Vallejo. 
Their deep commitment to helping others, particularly those who are 
underserved, reflects a shared belief in the dignity of every person 
and the power of community. In 2025, Carol has continued to lead and 
inspire women throughout the Benicia-Vallejo area by providing 
enriching activities and fostering civic engagement. She is known for 
her eagerness to serve and her ability to bring people together for the 
common good. Her leadership does not seek recognition but seeks impact. 
Her legacy of service, humility, and community-building sets a powerful 
example for all of us. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Carol 
Abreu for her invaluable contributions to our community.
