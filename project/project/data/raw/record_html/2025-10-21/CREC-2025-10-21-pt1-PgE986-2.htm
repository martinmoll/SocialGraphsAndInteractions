 CELEBRATING THE 40TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE LAW ENFORCEMENT TORCH RUN AND 
                ITS IMPACT ON SPECIAL OLYMPICS VIRGINIA

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 21, 2025

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 40th 
anniversary of the Law Enforcement Torch Run and its impact on Special 
Olympics Virginia.
  Special Olympics Virginia has provided year-round sports training and 
competition to thousands of athletes for decades, empowering them to 
achieve their personal best while fostering inclusion, respect, and 
community engagement. Through these programs, athletes not only develop 
athletic skills but also gain confidence, leadership abilities, and 
lifelong friendships. As we gather today to honor our athletes and 
their families, I am extremely thankful for our Special Olympics' 
extraordinary service to individuals with intellectual and 
developmental disabilities.
  Additionally, the Law Enforcement Torch Run exemplifies the 
incredible partnership between law enforcement officers and the 
communities they serve. Officers from across the Commonwealth have 
volunteered to carry the Flame of Hope, raising awareness and vital 
funds to support Special Olympics programs. This collaboration has 
helped break down barriers, build trust, and promote understanding for 
individuals with disabilities.
  As we celebrate our athletes and their families, we recognize the 
dedication of their coaches, volunteers, and law enforcement officers 
whose commitment has transformed the lives of countless Virginians. The 
legacy of Special Olympics Virginia and the Law Enforcement Torch Run 
is one of inclusion, compassion, and unwavering support for every 
athlete's potential.
  Mr. Speaker, thank you for joining me in honoring the 40th 
anniversary of the Law Enforcement Torch Run and its impact on Special 
Olympics Virginia.
