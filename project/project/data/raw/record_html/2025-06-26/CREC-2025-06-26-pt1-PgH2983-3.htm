              HIGHLIGHTING THE VOICES OF OUR LOCAL FARMERS

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to highlight the 
voices of our local farmers.
  During a tour of Brown Family Farms and Produce, we met with fourth-
generation farmer Patrick Brown, former United States Representative 
Eva Clayton, a group of local farmers, agricultural leaders, and 
advocates to discuss the future of farming in eastern North Carolina.
  Brown Family Farms was established in 1865 and is a family-run 
operation rooted in rich tradition and innovation.
  During the tour, we discussed the challenges farmers are facing and 
the importance of passing the farm bill. Our farmers deserve to be 
heard. Their voices must guide us to meet the needs of the American 
people.
