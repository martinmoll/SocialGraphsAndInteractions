                    ICE COMING TO SPRINGFIELD, OHIO

  (Ms. BROWN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. BROWN. Mr. Speaker, in Springfield, Ohio, families are preparing 
for the worst kind of ICE storm, a storm fueled by hate and racism, 
years in the making.
  In 2024, Donald Trump and J.D. Vance, peddled hateful lies about 
Haitians in Springfield. Vance even admitted they weren't true. He 
said: ``If I have to create stories . . . that is what I am going to 
do.''
  Well, those stories dehumanized Haitians who were here legally 
working and rebuilding a city. Now the Trump administration is turning 
those lies into policy.

[[Page H1940]]

  Tomorrow, they plan to revoke TPS, despite Haiti's collapse. Legal 
status today, illegal status tomorrow. This is the Project 2025 
playbook for a morally corrupt mass deportation machine.
  For those of us with Black skin, the demonization of Haitian people 
is something that we see, we hear, and we feel deeply.
  This country must do better, and this President must extend TPS 
status and call off the ICE surge now.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Members are reminded to refrain from 
engaging in personalities toward the President and Vice President.
