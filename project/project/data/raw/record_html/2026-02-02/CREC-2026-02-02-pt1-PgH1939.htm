             SOUTH CAROLINA SUCCESS WITH GOVERNOR McMASTER

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, last week, Governor Henry 
McMaster gave the South Carolina State of the State Address.
  I am grateful that under his leadership, the Palmetto State has had 
another historic year of over $900 billion of investments, creating 
8,000 jobs, 100,000 jobs during his record 10 years of service.
  Governor McMaster is correct: `` . . . State government is in 
superior fiscal shape, our laws and policies are business friendly . . 
. The best is yet to come.''
  South Carolina succeeds with Governor McMaster, Republican super 
majorities in the senate, led by President Tom Alexander, the house by 
Speaker Murrell Smith, the Republican Congress, and President Trump, 
all to make America great again.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength, revealing war 
Putin criminals lies, insulting Trump, mocking Trump, with Putin 
destroying Ukrainian utilities to freeze Ukrainians to death just as 
Stalin starved Ukrainians to death, while a maternity ward is being 
targeted by war criminal Putin yesterday to kill babies.
  Our sympathy for the family of Karen Metz, wife of my chief of staff 
from South Carolina, Craig Metz; mother of Preston Metz.
