                              {time}  1920
                          PROTESTS IN PORTLAND

  (Ms. BYNUM asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)

[[Page H1939]]

  

  Ms. BYNUM. Mr. Speaker, it is Black History Month, and I came to give 
you hell all month.
  This weekend, ICE agents tear-gassed kids. Let me repeat that. ICE 
agents tear-gassed kids. Protestors from union families in Portland, 
Oregon, came together to exercise their First Amendment rights. Among 
the protestors were children, the elderly, even pets, and ICE saw that 
as enough of a threat to fire rubber bullets, pepper balls, and clouds 
of tear gas.
  I have said it before, and I will say it again: What we are seeing is 
not law enforcement. It is State-sponsored terror, and this has to stop 
now.
  Mr. Speaker, I am calling on this administration to get out of our 
city, follow the law, and hold those responsible for this accountable. 
Enough is enough.
