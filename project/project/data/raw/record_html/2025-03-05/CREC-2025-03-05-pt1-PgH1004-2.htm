                              ADJOURNMENT

  Mr. NEWHOUSE. Mr. Speaker, I move that the House do now adjourn.
  The motion was agreed to; accordingly (at 5 o'clock and 58 minutes 
p.m.), pursuant to House Resolution 191, the House adjourned until 
tomorrow, Thursday, March 6, 2025, at 9 a.m., as a further mark of 
respect to the memory of the late Honorable Sylvester Turner.
