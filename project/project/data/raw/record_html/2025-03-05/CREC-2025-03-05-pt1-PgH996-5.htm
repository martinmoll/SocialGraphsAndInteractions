 PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE 
    ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY RELATING TO ``NATIONAL EMISSION 
  STANDARDS FOR HAZARDOUS AIR POLLUTANTS: RUBBER TIRE MANUFACTURING''

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the joint resolution 
(H.J. Res. 61) providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 
of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the 
Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``National Emission 
Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: Rubber Tire Manufacturing'' on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the joint resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the joint 
resolution.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 216, 
nays 202, not voting 14, as follows:
