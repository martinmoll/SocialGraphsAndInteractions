              HONORING THE LIFE OF CARLA REYNOLDS FOGELMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 14, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, it is with a heavy heart that I rise 
today to pay tribute to Ms. Carla Reynolds Fogelman, affectionately 
known to all as Carla. Carla was born on December 17, 1961, in 
Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and was a dedicated advocate, passionate 
public servant, and beloved family member. With profound sadness, we 
honor Carla following her passing on July 8, 2025.
  Carla made it her mission to be an agent of positive change wherever 
she could, and she had excelled remarkably in that endeavor. Carla was 
an unwavering advocate, activist, event organizer, humanitarian, and 
beacon of hope for all in our community, especially for those facing 
adversity. Through the Boots on the Ground Foundation, which she 
founded in 2021 alongside Danyelle Eckert, Carla worked relentlessly to 
provide vital resources and support to anyone in need. Whether 
collecting food, toiletries, and other necessities, distributing 
holiday meals and gifts, assisting seniors, providing financial and 
peer-based recovery support, or organizing community and family-driven 
events, Carla had created an organization that served as a vital 
lifeline and incredible resource for individuals and families in our 
community in need of assistance. Carla is also responsible for 
spearheading Bristol Borough's annual Unity Day celebration.
  It is through the work of people like Carla Reynolds that our 
communities thrive, and our Nation grow stronger. Her service, given 
without expectation of recognition, stood as a testament to the belief 
that one individual's dedication can positively ripple through the 
lives of many, sparking change, fostering resilience, and inspiring 
hope. We remember Carla not just for her remarkable deeds but for who 
she is, a true hero in every sense of the word.
  In addition to her professional and community involvement, Carla had 
a vibrant array of interests. She had an eye for beauty and a flair for 
the dramatic, which she expressed through her enjoyment of modeling and 
fashion. Her adventurous spirit led her to travel and explore the 
world, yet she found equal joy in the simple pleasure of shopping or 
spending quality time with loved ones.
  Above all, Carla is cherished by her family. She is survived by her 
loving daughter, Tamara Monroe (Shon), and her beloved grandchildren, 
Naya, Matthew, Shawn Thomas, Patricia, and Camille. Her memory will 
also be treasured by her siblings, Ronald Reynolds (Shiela) and Dawn 
Reynolds King (Anthony), and her nephews, Tyler, Brooklyn, and RJ, who 
will all miss her deeply. Carla's legacy is characterized by her loving 
nature, her kindness, and her friendly demeanor. She had the rare 
ability to make everyone feel like family, and her presence was a 
source of joy and comfort to those who had the privilege of knowing 
her.
  Mr. Speaker, as we say farewell to Carla Reynolds Fogelman, we 
celebrate a life beautifully lived and deeply loved. Her spirit will 
continue to inspire all who knew her, and her contributions to the 
world will be long remembered. As a woman who served her community and 
loved her family, it is with deep respect that I honor her memory 
today. I send my heartfelt condolences to all who had the privilege of 
knowing her.
