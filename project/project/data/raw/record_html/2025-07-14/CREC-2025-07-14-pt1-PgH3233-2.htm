                        KEEPING MEDICAID STRONG

  (Mr. LaMALFA asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LaMALFA. Mr. Speaker, there has been a lot of talk, just flat-out 
deception--you can even call it lies--about the big, beautiful bill and 
that we would be cutting aid to mothers, kids, seniors, and people with 
disabilities.
  That is just flat wrong. That is a deception being pulled on the 
American people to get them to panic about us trying to take corrective 
action on our massive Federal deficit and set things on a good course 
for all Americans.
  We are making sure that Medicaid stays strong for the people who 
truly count on it. In my home State of California, for example, 
Governor Newsom and the State legislature have stretched Medicaid way 
beyond intention and even sustainability.
  In California, it is called Medi-Cal. It had run an additional $12 
billion worth of deficit on the State budget because they want to hand 
it out to illegal immigrants in our home State. That is the effect on 
our State budget, $12 billion in the hole. They wanted to raise taxes 
to fix that.
  Also, by waiving work requirements for able-bodied adults who choose 
not to work, it drains resources from people who are actually the 
intended recipients of the program.
  With this new law, we are cutting waste, fraud, and red tape so 
States can zero in on helping people actually qualified, as designed. 
We are putting billions into long-term care, which means more care at 
home for people or keeping them close by.
  Don't buy the lies. This Medicaid package is going to be helpful long 
term.
