           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 4380.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 3 of section 8 of article I of the 
     Constitution, Congress has the power to regulate commerce 
     among the several states, which includes authority over the 
     designation and naming of portions of the federal highway 
     system.
