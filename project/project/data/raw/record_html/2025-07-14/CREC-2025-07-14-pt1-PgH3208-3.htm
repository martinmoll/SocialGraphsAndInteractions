                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty and wonderful God, You are the creator of the Earth, and You 
have given breath to every living thing. You have made us in Your own 
image and given us dominion over the entirety of Your creation.
  Who is a God like You, who in Your fathomless love embrace each one 
of us, desiring nothing more than that we respond to Your grace with 
righteous living?
  Who is a God like You, who despite Your unconditional love and 
generosity, pardon our iniquity, forgive our rejection of Your intent 
for our lives, and overlook our numerous transgressions?
  Who is a God like You, who does not retain his anger forever but 
delights in showing clemency?
  Who is a God like You, who shows us each Your compassion, who 
separates us from our sin, casting it into the depths of the sea?
  Who is a God like You? Only You, O Lord, and You have shown Your 
people such faithfulness and such unswerving loyalty to those who call 
on Your name.
  So we call on You in the power of Your name this day that we may 
today and always, and with our whole being, honor the only God like 
You.
  Amen.
