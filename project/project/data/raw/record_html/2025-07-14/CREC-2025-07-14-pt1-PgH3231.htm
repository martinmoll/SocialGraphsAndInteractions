                     CONSUMER SAFETY TECHNOLOGY ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1770) to direct the Consumer Product Safety 
Commission to establish a pilot program to explore the use of 
artificial intelligence in support of the mission of the Commission and 
to direct the Secretary of Commerce and the Federal Trade Commission to 
study and report on the use of blockchain technology and tokens, 
respectively, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Ohio (Mr. Latta) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 336, 
nays 36, not voting 59, as follows:
