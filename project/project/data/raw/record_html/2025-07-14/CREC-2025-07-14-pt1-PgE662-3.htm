      RECOGNIZING MACON'S NEXT LEVEL COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. SANFORD D. BISHOP, JR.

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, July 14, 2025

  Mr. BISHOP. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
achievements of the Next Level Community Development Center, a 
community nonprofit organization driven by its mission to foster 
academic achievement, college and career readiness, parental 
involvement, and youth development through mentorship and programming 
in Middle Georgia. The organization celebrated its 25th year of service 
at their annual closing ceremony on July 10, 2025, at the Macon City 
Auditorium in Macon, Georgia.
  Executive Director Lakesia Toomer has worked hard with her staff to 
help ensure that Next Level is a safe haven and launchpad for all 
children and families that need it. It is their solemn mission to 
ensure that no child is left behind, and every child is equipped with 
the tools they need to succeed throughout their lives. For a quarter of 
a decade, the staff and volunteers have worked tirelessly to provide 
students with tutoring and educational programs, as well as courses 
concerning leadership development, financial literacy, and community 
service. Through these activities, students are given the opportunity 
to learn about the jobs of the future and to tour nationally ranked 
colleges and universities while gaining essential college and career 
readiness skills.
  Next Level is known for its immersive summer experiences like Camp 
Zion, but the organization is not limited to the summer months; its 
work continues throughout the year, through crucial after-school 
programming that extends the mission of academic success and personal 
growth for students, while providing parents opportunities to finding 
meaningful employment and attending school so that their families can 
get ahead and stay ahead.
  President Franklin D. Roosevelt said, ``We cannot always build the 
future for our youth, but we can build our youth for the future.''
  At its core, the Next Level Community Development Center is proof 
that when a community comes together to uplift its children, it doesn't 
just change individual lives; it transforms generations to make our 
communities safer, wealthier, and more attractive to economic 
development and growth.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues in the United States House of 
Representatives to join me in celebrating the Next Level Community 
Development Center team and 25 successful years of service to the 
Middle Georgia community. Their dedication, leadership, and compassion 
are invaluable, and I am confident that this organization will continue 
to shape future generations of outstanding young leaders for years to 
come.
