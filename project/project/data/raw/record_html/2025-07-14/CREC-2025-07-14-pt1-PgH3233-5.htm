     BRIGADIER GENERAL DONALD WAYNE HANSEN CELEBRATES 92ND BIRTHDAY

  (Mr. SUBRAMANYAM asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SUBRAMANYAM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Brigadier General 
Donald Wayne Hansen, who recently celebrated his 92nd birthday. This 
milestone marks a lifetime of extraordinary military service and 
leadership.
  General Hansen's nearly three-decade career began commissioning as a 
second lieutenant in the U.S. Army Judge Advocate General's Corps. 
During the Vietnam war, he served as an assistant staff judge advocate 
for the 1st Infantry Division, the legendary Big Red One, and later as 
a staff judge advocate for the U.S. Army Support Command in Saigon.
  In 1985, he was appointed assistant judge advocate general of the 
U.S. Army, capping a distinguished career characterized by integrity, 
valor, and commitment to military justice.

[[Page H3234]]

  I celebrate not only General Hansen's 92nd birthday but also his 
enduring legacy of patriotism and exemplary service that continues to 
inspire our Nation.
  I thank him and wish him a happy birthday.
