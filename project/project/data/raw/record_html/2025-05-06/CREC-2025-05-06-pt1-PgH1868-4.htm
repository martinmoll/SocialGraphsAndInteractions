   PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 276, GULF OF AMERICA ACT; AND 
PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF H.R. 881, DHS RESTRICTIONS ON CONFUCIUS 
             INSTITUTES AND CHINESE ENTITIES OF CONCERN ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on ordering the previous question on 
the resolution (H. Res. 377) providing for consideration of the bill 
(H.R. 276) to rename the Gulf of Mexico as the ``Gulf of America''; 
providing for consideration of the bill (H.R. 881) to establish 
Department of Homeland Security funding restrictions on institutions of 
higher education that have a relationship with Confucius Institutes, 
and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on ordering the previous 
question.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 206, 
nays 200, not voting 27, as follows:
