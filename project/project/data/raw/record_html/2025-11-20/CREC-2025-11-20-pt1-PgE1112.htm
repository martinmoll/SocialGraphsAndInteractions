  CONGRATULATING MS. NINA MARTINEZ ON WINNING THE LONE STAR EMMY AWARD

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 20, 2025

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to 
congratulate Ms. Nina Martinez for winning the Lone Star Emmy Award in 
the Historical/Cultural--Short Form or Long Form Content category. This 
outstanding achievement is a testament to her dedication to telling 
meaningful stories that educate, uplift, and empower viewers throughout 
Corpus Christi and the entire Coastal Bend region.
  Ms. Martinez began her journey in journalism at Texas A&M 
University--Corpus Christi and has since devoted more than two decades 
to television broadcasting and news reporting. Since joining KAJA 
Telemundo Corpus Christi in 2015 as the main evening anchor, she has 
become a trusted and respected voice--consistently delivering accurate, 
compelling, and community-centered reporting.
  Throughout her career, Ms. Martinez has demonstrated not only 
excellence in journalism but a deep commitment to public service. She 
has established herself as a strong advocate for the Latino community, 
particularly on issues related to immigration, economic opportunity, 
and social equity. In addition to her work on air, she serves on the 
Board of the West Side Business Association, where she helps support 
local businesses and advance community-driven initiatives that 
strengthen the region.
  Ms. Martinez is equally dedicated to shaping the next generation of 
journalists. Through mentorship, training, and professional guidance, 
she has invested countless hours in helping young media professionals 
develop their skills and pursue successful careers in broadcasting.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to honor Ms. Nina Martinez for her 
exceptional career, commitment, advocacy, and her well-deserved Lone 
Star Emmy Award. She represents the very best of South Texas, and I 
congratulate her on this remarkable achievement.
