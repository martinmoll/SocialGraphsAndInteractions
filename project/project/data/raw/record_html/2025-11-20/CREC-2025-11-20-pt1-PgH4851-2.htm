 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 2180, 
                   H.R. 759, H.R. 1560, AND H.R. 1559

  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 2180, the Keep 
the Watchdogs Running Act; H.R. 759, the Federal Firefighters Families 
First Act; H.R. 1560, Postal Supervisors and Managers Fairness Act of 
2025; and H.R. 1559, Postal Employee Appeal Rights Amendment Act of 
2025, all bills originally introduced by Representative Connelly of 
Virginia, for the purposes of adding cosponsors and requesting 
reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Virginia?
  There was no objection.
