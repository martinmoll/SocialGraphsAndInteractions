                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. PATRONIS:
       H.R. 5035. A bill to provide for the portability of

     public outdoor recreation; to the Committee on Natural 
     Resources.

             and Mr. Moore of Alabama):

     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.

       H.R. 5039. A bill to amend title 17, United States Code, to

     independent repair providers and consumers, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce, and in

     be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in each case for 
     consideration of such provisions as fall within the
