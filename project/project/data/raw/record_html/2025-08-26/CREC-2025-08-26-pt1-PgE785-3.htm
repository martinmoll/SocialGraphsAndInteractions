           HONORING THE FOOD BANK OF CONTRA COSTA AND SOLANO

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARK DeSAULNIER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, August 26, 2025

  Mr. DeSAULNIER. Mr. Speaker, we rise today to recognize the 50th 
Anniversary of the Food Bank of Contra Costa and Solano and their 
unwavering commitment to fighting hunger in our community.
  The Food Bank was created 50 years ago when a group of dedicated 
community members saw a dire need in our community and sought to 
address it. What began as a small endeavor has since expanded into 10 
Food Bank programs, 245 agency partners, and 7,000 volunteers who we 
are told have served over 110,000 hours last year. The Food Bank has 
built powerful partnerships with Feeding America, local businesses, 
local farmers, and government agencies to produce tangible results in 
our community. As we celebrate the Food Bank's 50th anniversary, I am 
proud to acknowledge the work of the volunteers, founders, Board 
Members, staff, and donors who represent the heart of the Food Bank's 
mission.
  As we understand, the Food Bank serves 98,000 households throughout 
Contra Costa and Solano County and provides enough food to generate 2.7 
million meals per month, 68% of which is fresh produce, protein, and 
dairy. As the needs of the community glow, the Food Bank continues to 
work toward innovative, community-oriented solutions to best serve our 
neighbors. This includes a focus on new initiatives, partnerships, and 
strategies to combat the root causes of hunger. The organization truly 
has a legacy of service and has worked tirelessly to strengthen our 
community and fight food insecurity.
  Please join us in honoring the Food Bank of Contra Costa and Solano 
for their invaluable work and in congratulating them for 50 years of 
incredible service.
