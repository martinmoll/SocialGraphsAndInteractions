              SOCIAL SECURITY CHILD PROTECTION ACT OF 2025

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 5348) to amend title II of the Social Security Act 
to provide for the reissuance of social security account numbers to 
young children in cases where confidentiality has been compromised, as 
amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Missouri (Mr. Smith) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 386, 
nays 0, answered ``present'' 1, not voting 44, as follows:
