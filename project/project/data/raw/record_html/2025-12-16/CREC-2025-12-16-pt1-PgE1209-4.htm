        HONORING THE OUTSTANDING PUBLIC SERVICE OF MARK RODGERS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. TIM WALBERG

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. WALBERG. Mr. Speaker, it is my special privilege to recognize 
Mark Rodgers as he retires after a meaningful and fulfilling career in 
public service.
  Mark's remarkable career, which includes senior leadership roles 
across government at the federal and state levels, stands as a 
testament to his principled leadership and unwavering commitment to 
improving the policies that shape America's modern workforce.
  As Deputy Staff Director of the U.S. House Committee on Education and 
Workforce, a committee that I am now proud to chair, he played a 
pivotal role in shaping workforce policy and overseeing critical 
reforms in some of the most complex areas of law. His service at both 
the U.S. Department of Labor and the Office of Personnel Management 
further underscores his impact.
  At the state level, Mark also served with distinction as Deputy 
Secretary for Labor and Management Relations at the Pennsylvania 
Department of Labor & Industry, where he simultaneously served as a 
senior advisor on labor-management and labor-political issues. In this 
role, Mark demonstrated his exceptional ability to bridge the worlds of 
government, labor, and management, while fostering constructive and 
solutions-oriented relationships.
  Mark's experience also extends beyond traditional government service. 
As Vice President of Industry Relations for the Motor Freight Carriers 
Association, he represented management as the lead labor negotiator in 
the LTL trucking industry, earning respect for his professionalism, 
dedication, and command of labor-management relations. Most recently, 
Mark served as the founder and president of MWR Consulting, guiding 
clients through the complexities of federal legislative and regulatory 
policy with clarity and strategic insight. His work reflects not only 
his deep technical knowledge, but also his genuine understanding of how 
policy affects real people.
  Mark's contributions have left an indelible mark on workforce policy. 
May others know of my high regard for Mark and my best wishes for a 
happy and fulfilling retirement.
