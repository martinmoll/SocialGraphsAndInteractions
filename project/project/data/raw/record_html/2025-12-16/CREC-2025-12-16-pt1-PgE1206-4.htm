           CONGRATULATING MARILYN DILLIHAY ON HER RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. STEVE COHEN

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. COHEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise and include in the Record a 
Proclamation congratulating my longtime Chief of Staff, Marilyn 
Dillihay, on her retirement:
       Whereas, on behalf of the citizens of the 9th Congressional 
     District of Tennessee, I

[[Page E1207]]

     recognize and celebrate the extraordinary career and service 
     of Marilyn Dillihay, who has dedicated more than forty years 
     to public service in the Tennessee General Assembly, the 
     United States House of Representatives, and a brief stint at 
     the Tennessee Lottery; and,
       Whereas, forty years ago, on December 3, 1985, Marilyn 
     began working for me in my Tennessee State Senate office, and 
     I had little idea then how blessed I would be; she 
     immediately became a valued staffer, a close friend, and an 
     advisor on a multitude of subjects quickly proving 
     indispensable not only to our work, but to me personally; 
     and,
       Whereas, Marilyn was integral to the passage of the 
     Tennessee Education Lottery, a landmark effort that took 
     years of dedication, negotiation, and leadership; she was 
     with me on that fight from the beginning, a partnership 
     forever captured in our ``victory celebration and lottery 
     headquarters'' hotel photo; and,
       Whereas, the lottery would later be recognized as one of 
     the most meaningful legislative achievements in recent state 
     history; and has provided over eight billion dollars in 
     unprecedented educational opportunities for Tennessee 
     students; and,
       Whereas, when I was elected to Congress, Marilyn came with 
     me to Washington, established my congressional office from 
     day one, and initially served as my Press Secretary and 
     Legislative Director before becoming my Chief of Staff, a 
     role in which she has done a marvelous job; and,
       Whereas, Marilyn has been the heart and soul of our 
     operation mentoring generations of staff and cultivating an 
     office culture defined by loyalty, kindness, and hard-work; 
     her personality has attracted exceptional staff and 
     colleagues over the years, enhancing our productivity, our 
     reach, and the spirit of our team; and,
       Whereas, Marilyn possesses a knowledge of me and my career 
     that exceeds anyone else's, a continuity and institutional 
     memory that has been invaluable to me as a legislator; and 
     her sound judgment, historical insight, and understanding of 
     government have made her a trusted counselor that I have 
     respected and relied upon; and I appreciated her tenacity and 
     perseverance in securing us an office with a Capitol View; 
     and,
       Whereas, Marilyn's sense of humor, her love of the arts, 
     her passion for animal welfare and prohibiting the soring of 
     horses, and her command of history and international affairs, 
     have enriched not only our work but our friendship, reminding 
     us daily that public service is not only serious business but 
     also a labor of joy, humanity, and heart; she is one of the 
     best things to come out of Muhlenberg County, Kentucky; and,
       Whereas, the 9th Congressional District is profoundly 
     grateful for Marilyn's unwavering devotion to public service, 
     to her colleagues, and to the people of Memphis, and we 
     gather today in celebration of her achievements, her 
     leadership, and her well-deserved retirement; and
       Now, therefore be it resolved that, I, Steve Cohen, Member 
     of Congress, along with the citizens of the 9th Congressional 
     District of Tennessee, do hereby honor Marilyn Dillihay, for 
     her more than forty years of distinguished public service, 
     express our deepest appreciation for her immeasurable 
     contributions, and extend our warmest wishes for a joyful and 
     fulfilling retirement.
