                      RECOGNIZING THERESA MAZZULLO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JOSEPH D. MORELLE

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. MORELLE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an exceptional 
leader and champion

[[Page E1210]]

for innovation in Rochester, New York: Theresa Mazzullo, who is 
retiring after an extraordinary tenure as Chief Executive Officer of 
Excell Partners. Her years of service have left an indelible mark on 
the entrepreneurial landscape of Upstate New York.
  Since stepping into the role in 2006, Theresa has guided Excell 
Partners through a period of remarkable growth. What began as a small 
seed fund has, under her leadership, evolved into a venture capital 
organization overseeing more than $80 million in assets. She played a 
pivotal role in establishing Excell as a cornerstone supporter of 
emerging companies across the region, most recently through the 
creation of the Upstate Biotech Venture Fund to bolster life sciences 
startups.
  Beyond stewarding the firm's investments, Theresa has been a driving 
force in strengthening the broader innovation ecosystem. She has led 
efforts in governance, compliance, fundraising, and community 
engagement, drawing upon more than three decades of experience in 
business and finance--including founding her own small business. Her 
insight and dedication have shaped the strategic direction of Excell 
and the communities it serves.
  Her commitment to the region extends far beyond her executive 
responsibilities. Theresa has contributed her expertise to numerous 
boards and advisory groups, helping influence economic development 
initiatives and supporting the continued growth of entrepreneurial 
networks throughout Upstate New York. Her leadership was recently 
honored with the Upstate Capital Association of New York's Ecosystem 
Champion Award, a testament to her collaborative spirit and her long-
standing dedication to the region's success.
  Mr. Speaker, it is with great admiration and deep gratitude that I 
recognize a dear friend, Theresa Mazzullo, for her remarkable career 
and enduring contributions to the Rochester community. Her legacy will 
continue to benefit our region for years to come.
