 CONGRATULATING SENIOR MASTER SERGEANT ADAM M. WALTER ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to congratulate Senior Master 
Sergeant Adam M. Walter upon the occasion of his retirement from the 
Pennsylvania Air National Guard (PaANG) after 24 years of dedicated 
service.
  Sergeant Walter entered the PaANG in January 2002, diligently working 
his way to the rank of Senior Master Sergeant in October 2022. In 2003, 
he joined the 193rd Special Operations Wing as a video maintenance 
technician. During his illustrious career, he deployed to Kuwait in 
support of Operation Enduring Freedom with the 193rd Expeditionary 
Maintenance Squadron (2005), Qatar (2005, and between 2020 to 2023), 
and in Guam (2025), where he served with the 513th Expeditionary RED 
HORSE Squadron.
  Sergeant Walter has earned a multitude of awards and decorations, 
including the: Meritorious Service Medal (w/3 OLC); Air and Space 
Commendation Medal; and Air and Space Achievement Medal (w/1 OLC). 
Between 2015 and 2017, he oversaw all acts related to the accounting of 
public funds and financial services for 1,800 Wing members, including 
travel pay, accounting, and vendor pay, and processed over 500 travel 
vouchers with a zero-error rate. Between 2020 and 2021, while deployed 
to Al Udeid Air Base in Qatar, his support while battling the worldwide 
Coronavirus pandemic secured a 100 percent mission effectiveness rate 
during the execution of more than 1,300 combat missions. In addition, 
while deployed to Guam this year, his actions expedited an Airman's 
redeployment within 48 hours to ensure a final visit with a family 
during a time of need, and during a unit crisis, his leadership 
coordinated the rapid medical evacuation of two personnel.
  While not performing military duty, Sergeant Walter is a higher 
education full-time administrator, adjunct faculty member, and self-
employed business owner. In 2018, he earned his Doctor of Education and 
published his dissertation, ``Challenges faced by US Student Veterans 
Transitioning to a Community College.'' He previously earned a 
bachelor's degree in accounting from Penn State and a master's degree 
in leadership and business ethics from Duquesne.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Senior Master Sergeant Adam M. 
Walter upon his retirement from the Pennsylvania Air National Guard and 
a job extremely well done. His dedicated service to his fellow Airmen, 
our communities, and our United States of America are the standard by 
which we all should be judged.
