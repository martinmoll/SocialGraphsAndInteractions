  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to recommit on the bill 
(H.R. 3632) to amend the Federal Power Act to adjust the requirements 
for orders, rules, and regulations relating to furnishing adequate 
service, to require owners or operators of generating facilities to 
provide notice of planned retirements of certain electric generating 
units, and for other purposes, offered by the gentlewoman from Michigan 
(Ms. Scholten), on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk will redesignate the motion.
