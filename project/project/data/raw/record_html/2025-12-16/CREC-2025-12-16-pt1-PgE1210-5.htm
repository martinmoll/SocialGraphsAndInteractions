                 HONORING ST. MATTHIAS CATHOLIC SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MIKE QUIGLEY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate St. Matthias 
Catholic School on their designation as a 2025 National Blue Ribbon 
School.
  Since 1982, the Blue-Ribbon School Award has recognized the most 
exemplary public and private schools for outstanding academic 
performance and significant progress in closing achievement gaps among 
students.
  From its inception in 1888 to its continued excellence through the 
21st Century, St. Matthias has continued to pave a bright path of 
academic leadership and set an example for schools and communities in 
the Chicagoland area.
  St. Matthias School is the only PK3-8th Grade Catholic, International 
Baccalaureate (IB), National Blue Ribbon School in Illinois. Student 
performance at St. Matthias at or above grade level surpasses average 
student performance at other Archdiocese of Chicago schools by more 
than 15 percent for reading and math.
  Although the Blue-Ribbon School Award has been canceled, it is 
imperative to recognize schools like St. Matthias, which deserve praise 
and congratulations for this outstanding achievement.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me and all our colleagues in the House of 
Representatives in recognizing St. Matthias Academy for their 
exceptional and continuous success in educating and nurturing the 
brilliant future minds of this Nation.
