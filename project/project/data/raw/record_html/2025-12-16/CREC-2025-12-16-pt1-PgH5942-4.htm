           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 6732.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United States 
     Constitution (the Commerce Clause), which grants Congress the 
     power to regulate commerce among the several States, and 
     pursuant to Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 (the Necessary 
     and Proper Clause; which grants Congress the authority to 
     make all laws necessary. and proper carrying into execution 
     its enumerated powers.
