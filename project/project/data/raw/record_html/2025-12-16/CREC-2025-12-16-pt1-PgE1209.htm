     RECOGNIZING PALISADES REGIONAL FIRE RESCUE CAPTAIN BILL SHICK

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Captain Bill Shick of the Palisades Regional Fire Rescue for his 
exceptional dedication to public safety and his unwavering service to 
the Palisades School District and the greater community.
  For more than 11 years, Captain Shick has been an exemplary partner 
to the Palisades School District. Through his leadership at Palisades 
Regional Fire Rescue, he has played a vital role in ensuring that our 
schools and facilities remain safe, secure, and prepared for 
emergencies. His steady presence, professionalism, and deep expertise 
have provided reassurance to students, staff, parents, and community 
members alike.
  Captain Shick's commitment to collaboration has been instrumental in 
strengthening emergency preparedness across the district. His guidance 
during safety drills, his responsiveness during emergencies, and his 
proactive approach to fire prevention and risk reduction have been 
invaluable. Year after year, his leadership has helped foster a culture 
of preparedness and confidence within our schools.
  Beyond his immediate service, Captain Shick has demonstrated a 
commitment to improving the future of public safety. His advocacy for 
the creation of a Fire Program at the Upper Bucks County Technical 
School reflects his dedication to developing the next generation of 
firefighters. Now, in its second year, this program provides students 
with meaningful career pathways while helping to secure the future of 
our regional volunteer fire services.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Captain Bill 
Shick, whose career exemplifies selfless service, leadership, and 
community partnership. It is with deep gratitude that we honor Mr. Bill 
Shick and his 11 years of unwavering dedication. As he enters 
retirement, we thank him for keeping our schools and community safe, 
for inspiring future first responders, and for leaving a lasting legacy 
of service to our district.
