                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 16, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I missed two votes on December 15, 2025. 
Had I been present, I would have voted: YEA on Roll Call No. 336, final 
passage of H.R. 3187, to require the Secretary of Agriculture to convey 
a parcel of property of the Forest Service to Perry County, Arkansas, 
and for other purposes, and YEA on Roll Call No. 335, final passage of 
S. 284, Congressional Award Program Reauthorization Act.
