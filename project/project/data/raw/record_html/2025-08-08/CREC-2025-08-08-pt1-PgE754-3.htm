                        RECOGNIZING JEREMY METZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 8, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jeremy Metz for 
his promotion to Chief of the West Metro Fire Protection District.
  Deputy Chief Jeremy Metz has served the West Metro Fire Protection 
District for over 27 years. In his previous roles, Jeremy oversaw 
emergency medical services, administration, special teams operations, 
and strategic planning for personal safety and benefit. He has over 
three decades of on-the-ground experience in fire science and emergency 
services administration. West Metro and all the Coloradans they serve 
are lucky to have an incoming Chief with such an impressive and lengthy 
record of public service.
  On behalf of the people of Colorado's Seventh Congressional District, 
it is my honor to congratulate Jeremy Metz for his promotion to Chief 
of the West Metro Fire Protection District. We thank him for his 
continued commitment to the safety of our community.
