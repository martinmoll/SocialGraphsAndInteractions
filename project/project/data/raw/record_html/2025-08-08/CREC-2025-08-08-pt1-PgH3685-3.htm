                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Kevin Peterman, 19th Street Baptist Church, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Eternal God, we pause on this day to acknowledge Your presence in 
this sacred place.
  O Lord, while we are thankful for a new day with brand-new mercies, 
we come acknowledging that our Nation needs You now more than ever.
  Our Nation cries out to You this morning for Your guidance, yearns 
for Your mercy, and hungers for Your healing touch.
  As the legislative work of this day unfolds, may this Chamber, built 
by enslaved men and women, be a sacred place where the truth is spoken 
boldly, justice is pursued relentlessly, and the welfare of all people 
is sought with compassion.
  Help us, O Lord, to remember that the measure of a nation is not in 
its wealth or its power, but in how it treats the least, the lost, and 
the left behind.
  Bless the elected Members of this House.
  Convict those who seek to do Your children harm. Let integrity be 
their compass, humility their posture, and the truth their unwavering 
aim.
  Remind them, O Lord, that the world is equally balanced between good 
and evil, and their next act will tip the scale.
  In Your name we do pray.
  Amen.
