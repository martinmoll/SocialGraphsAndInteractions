    COMMUNICATION FROM THE HONORABLE KAT CAMMACK, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Kat Cammack, Member of Congress:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                                   Washington, DC.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: I am writing to inform you that I will be 
     stepping down from my position on the House Franking 
     Commission, effective immediately. It has been an honor to 
     serve on the Commission and to work alongside my colleagues 
     to ensure transparency, accountability, and compliance with 
     franking regulations.
       I appreciate the opportunity to have contributed to the 
     important work of the Commission and I remain committed to 
     upholding the integrity of official communications on behalf 
     of the American people.
       Thank you for your leadership and for the privilege of 
     serving in this capacity.
           For Freedom,
                                                      Kat Cammack,
     Member of Congress.
