 CELEBRATING DIVJOT WARAICH AND AMANJOT SIDHU ON THEIR RECENT MARRIAGE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JANELLE S. BYNUM

                               of oregon

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 8, 2025

  Ms. BYNUM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the recent marriage 
of Divjot Waraich and Amanjot Sidhu. Divjot, the son of Sarabjeet and 
Ravinder, and Amanjot, the

[[Page E759]]

daughter of Inderjit and Rupinder, cemented their bond on August 1, 
2025. I rise to wish them both a lifetime of love, joy, and success 
together. I am so excited to see what the future brings the two of 
them. I congratulate Divjot and Amanjot.
