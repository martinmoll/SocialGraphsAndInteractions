                        HONORING MARGAUX SAUVAGE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 8, 2025

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Margaux Sauvage, 
for her work as an intern serving in my D.C. office. I would like to 
take this opportunity to thank her for her hard work and recognize the 
meaningful contributions she has made to the office this summer.
  Margaux is from San Diego, California and attends Georgetown 
University. Margaux is entering her second year at Georgetown where she 
plans to study Global Business and National Security.
  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th congressional district. Margaux was chosen from a competitive pool 
of applicants and exceeded every expectation with her positive attitude 
and impressive work ethic. It was a pleasure to work with Margaux, and 
I am so proud to have had such an intelligent and ambitious intern 
representing my office.
  Margaux exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the 
best that our Nation has to offer. I congratulate Margaux on the 
successful completion of her internship and thank her on behalf of the 
people of Western North Carolina.
