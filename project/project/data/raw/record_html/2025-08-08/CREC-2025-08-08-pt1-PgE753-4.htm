                       HONORING ANIYAH YATTASSAYE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, August 8, 2025

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Aniyah 
Yattassaye, for her work as an intern serving in my D.C. office. I 
would like to take this opportunity to thank her for her hard work and 
recognize the meaningful contributions she has made to the office this 
summer.
  Aniyah resides in Seattle, Washington and attends Wake Forest 
University. Aniyah is entering her third year at Wake Forest where she 
is studying Political Science and international.
  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th congressional district. Aniyah was chosen from a competitive pool 
of applicants and exceeded every expectation with her positive attitude 
and impressive work ethic. It was a pleasure to work with Aniyah, and I 
am so proud to have had such an intelligent and ambitious intern 
representing my office.
  Aniyah exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the 
best that our Nation has to offer. I congratulate Aniyah on the 
successful completion of her internship and thank her on behalf of the 
people of Western North Carolina.
