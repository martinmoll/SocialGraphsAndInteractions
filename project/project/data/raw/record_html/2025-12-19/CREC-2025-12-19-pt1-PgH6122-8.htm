           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 6916.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to make all laws which shall be 
     necessary and proper for carrying into execution the powers 
     vested by the Constitution in the Government of the United 
     States, including the power to provide for the general 
     welfare and to regulate spending of federal funds, pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clauses 1 and 18 of the Constitution 
     of the United States.
