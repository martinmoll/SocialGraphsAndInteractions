                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Almighty and all-powerful God, we wait to hear Your voice. In this 
season of overwhelming busyness, when the Earth seems to shake with 
traffic jams and crowded stores, we listen for Your word amid the 
hustle and bustle, but too often the distractions prevent us from 
appreciating it.
  When we hear the sounds of sleigh bells and elevator music, even 
children singing and orchestras in concert halls playing carols and 
festive songs, for some these are just discordant sounds, dissonant 
with their deepest desires. Many simply can't distinguish Your voice 
amid the noise.
  The lights around the neighborhoods are amazing, but the flash and 
color seem to meld into mayhem when we know of so many, maybe we 
ourselves, who are enveloped in the darkness of despair and loss and 
cannot distinguish Your light in their lives.
  Kind and loving God, as we approach the longest night, as we endure 
the bleakness of winter in a world at war and face the shadows that 
loom in our lives, we ask that You would speak with Your own still, 
small voice. Grant us the hope of Your abiding presence and eternal 
light in this season
  In Your steadfast and loving name, we pray.
  Amen.
