                        HONORING RONALD L. BOOK

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 19, 2025

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th district of the 
great state of Florida, I rise today to honor Mr. Ron Book for his 
exemplary philanthropy and commitment to at-risk youth.
  It is my privilege to present Mr. Book with a Congressional Award on 
behalf of the 5000 Role Models of Excellence Project, recognizing his 
significant contributions to our community and steadfast support for 
the youth of South Florida. Mr. Book has been selected as a 
distinguished Role Model Mentor, joining the esteemed ranks of our 
dedicated honorees. His leadership and advocacy for the homeless 
community in Miami-Dade County have profoundly impacted many lives. 
Through his efforts, he has raised awareness of homelessness issues and 
championed innovative solutions, ensuring that individuals have access 
to safe housing and essential resources. His dedication to public 
service and commitment to creating opportunities for young people, 
particularly those from underserved backgrounds, have yielded 
substantial benefits.
  His legal expertise and community engagement have provided invaluable 
support and empowered our youth. The mission of the 5000 Role Models of 
Excellence Project aligns closely with Mr. Book's advocacy. His 
mentorship of young men of color exemplifies the values we instill in 
future leaders. His involvement in community programs and willingness 
to share knowledge inspire those around him. It is with pride that we 
recognize Mr. Book's achievements and express our gratitude for his 
dedication to improving the lives of youth. His leadership continues to 
inspire positive change in our community. I congratulate Mr. Book on 
this well-deserved recognition. I look forward to further collaboration 
in fostering the growth of young leaders in South Florida.
  We now recognize Mr. Ron Book for his exemplary leadership and 
advocacy on behalf of the homeless community in Miami-Dade County. Mr. 
Book has made a significant impact on numerous lives through his 
unwavering dedication. His efforts have not only highlighted the 
pressing issues faced by individuals experiencing homelessness but have 
also fostered the development of innovative solutions aimed at 
improving their conditions.
