                  HONORING SHERIFF ROSIE CORDERO-STUTZ

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 19, 2025

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, from the 24th district of the 
great State of Florida, I rise today to recognize and honor Sheriff 
Rosie Cordero-Stutz on making history as Miami-Dade County's first 
female sheriff. On January 7, 2025, Sheriff Rosie assumed this historic 
role as the Miami-Dade Police Department (MDPD) transitioned back into 
a Sheriff's Office for the first time in nearly 60 years.
  Sheriff Rosie is a trailblazer and dedicated public servant with over 
28 years of exemplary service in law enforcement. Her steadfast 
commitment to protecting and serving the people of Miami-Dade County 
has earned her the respect and admiration of her peers and the 
community alike. Rising through the ranks from police officer to 
assistant director, she has gained a profound understanding of the 
unique needs and challenges facing her community.
  Throughout her distinguished career, Sheriff Rosie has served in a 
wide range of assignments, including uniform patrol, special 
investigations, Media Relations Bureau, Robbery Bureau, Homicide 
Bureau, Professional Compliance Bureau, and Economic Crimes Bureau. Her 
extensive experience in police operations, investigations, supervision, 
and administration has prepared her to lead with confidence and 
integrity.
  In 2020, Sheriff Rosie was promoted to Police Division Chief of the 
North Operations Division, where she oversaw the administration and 
management of multiple districts, including the Intracoastal, 
Northside, Northwest, and Midwest Districts, as well as the Town of 
Miami Lakes. In 2022, she was promoted to Assistant Director of Support 
Services, where she managed Communications, Central Records, Personnel 
Management, and Cyber Crimes Bureaus. In 2024, she supervised 
Investigative Services, including the Criminal Investigations, 
Government Affairs, and Forensic Services Divisions. Sheriff Rosie also 
led Police Services, which included North and South Operations 
Divisions, the Airport and Seaport Operations Bureaus, three 
contractual cities, and the Special Patrol Bureau, serving as acting 
Assistant Director.
  Sheriff Rosie's leadership is defined by her unwavering commitment to 
safety, security, and community trust. Her top priorities as sheriff 
are to ensure the Miami-Dade Sheriff's Office operates with the highest 
standards of ethics, professionalism, and respect. By maintaining 
order, protecting residents, and enforcing laws impartially, she is 
dedicated to making Miami-Dade County a safe and prosperous place for 
residents and visitors alike.
  Sheriff Rosie holds a Master's Degree in Public Administration from 
Florida International University and a Bachelor's Degree in Criminal 
Justice from Marist College. She is a graduate of the Executive 
Leadership Program, the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) National 
Academy (session 265), the Florida Police Chiefs STARS program, the 
Police Executive Leadership Institute (PELI), and the FBI National 
Executive Institute for Leadership (Session 46). Additionally, she 
serves as the current President of the Florida Chapter of the FBI 
National Academy Associates, the largest alumni chapter in the world.
  Mr. Speaker, Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz's groundbreaking appointment 
is an inspiration to future generations of law enforcement leaders. Her 
trailblazing leadership, dedication to public safety, and commitment to 
serving with integrity are truly commendable. I ask my colleagues to 
join me in honoring Sheriff Rosie Cordero-Stutz for her remarkable 
achievements and invaluable contributions to Miami-Dade County.
