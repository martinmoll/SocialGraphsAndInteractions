                     HONORING MAYOR MICHAEL JOSEPH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, December 19, 2025

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I, Congresswoman Frederica S. 
Wilson, from the 24th district of the great state of Florida, rise 
today to recognize Mayor Michael Joseph for his philanthropy and 
commitment to helping at-risk youth.
  I am pleased to deliver this message on behalf of the 5000 Role 
Models of Excellence Project, presenting him with the Congressional 
Award in recognition of his exemplary leadership and commitment to the 
North Miami Beach community. We honor him as a distinguished Role Model 
Mentor and a graduate of our program, acknowledging his remarkable 
accomplishments as the first Haitian American mayor and first Black 
mayor of North Miami Beach.
  His statement, ``God has a plan for me,'' reflects his purpose-driven 
leadership, and his actions exemplify his dedication to improving 
educational opportunities and empowering youth. His commendable efforts 
to foster an environment where young people, particularly young men of 
color, can thrive have made a significant impact. His initiatives to 
enhance education, promote mentorship, and advocate for social equity 
resonate deeply with our mission. The 5000 Role Models of Excellence 
Project values his vision and partnership in empowering youth, His 
collaboration with local organizations has been instrumental in 
creating pathways for success and inspiring others to strive for 
excellence.
  We extend our gratitude for his commitment to enhancing the quality 
of life in North Miami Beach. I congratulate him on this well-deserved 
recognition; we look forward to continuing our partnership in making a 
meaningful difference in the lives of youth.

[[Page E1240]]

  We now recognize Role Model Mayor Michael Joseph, who has achieved a 
significant milestone in the political history of North Miami Beach by 
becoming the first Haitian American mayor and the first Black mayor of 
a city renowned for its vibrant and diverse population. His exemplary 
leadership and steadfast dedication to the youth and communities of 
North Miami Beach are evident in the city's ongoing growth and 
prosperity.
