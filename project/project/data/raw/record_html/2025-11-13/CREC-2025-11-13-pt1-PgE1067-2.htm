                    HONORING DR. MARKESSA V. HUGHES

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, November 13, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi, Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. 
Markessa V. Hughes, a distinguished scholar, urban planner, and native 
of Clinton, Mississippi.
  A proud graduate of Clinton High School, Dr. Hughes earned a 
Bachelor's of Social Work from Jackson State University, a Master's of 
Policy and Administration from Belhaven University, and a Doctor of 
Philosophy in Urban Planning with a concentration in Urban Design. She 
credits her family's love and prayers for the foundation of her 
success.
  With over fifteen years of professional experience, Dr. Hughes is a 
recognized expert in strategic planning, policy research, database 
design, team leadership, and coaching. Her work has helped strengthen 
communities, revitalize urban spaces, and improve quality of life 
across Mississippi and beyond.
  As an Urban Design Planner, she emphasizes the importance of spatial 
qualities, public open spaces, and parks in promoting community health 
and well-being. Currently, Dr. Hughes serves as Project Coordinator and 
Post-Doctoral Researcher in the Department of Urban Planning at Jackson 
State University (JSU). Under the leadership of Dr. Robert Luckett and 
in partnership with Princeton University, she is advancing the newly 
funded project to enhance and refine the historic Margaret Walker 
Center.
  A proud JSU alumna, Dr. Hughes continues to contribute to 
scholarship, community empowerment, and the preservation of culturally 
significant spaces. Her ongoing research and forthcoming publications 
reflect her dedication to equity, urban planning, and the betterment of 
communities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me to celebrate Dr. Markessa 
V. Hughes for her exceptional accomplishments and enduring impact on 
our state and Nation.
