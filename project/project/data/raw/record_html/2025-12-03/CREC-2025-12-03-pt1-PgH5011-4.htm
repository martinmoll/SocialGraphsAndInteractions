    DESTROYING UNNECESSARY, MISALIGNED, AND PROHIBITIVE RED TAPE ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 4305) to 
direct the Chief Counsel for Advocacy of the Small Business 
Administration to establish a Red Tape Hotline to receive notifications 
of burdensome agency rules, and for other purposes, on which the yeas 
and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 269, 
nays 146, not voting 17, as follows:
