                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Sovereign God, receive us into Your presence, as each one who enters 
this Chamber seeks Your guidance in ensuring the welfare of this 
Nation. Though disparate voices and conflicting opinions, we want the 
best for our compatriots and our communities.
  We ask for Your assistance in ensuring that we do not just settle for 
tolerating one another but commit to communicating respectfully and 
carefully in our proceedings. In so doing, may we discover what binds 
us together in the mission that You have set before us.
  In our kinship, our friendship, or just our shared citizenship, we 
pray together for the peace that transcends not just our differences 
but our individual understandings. In Your peace, may we find our 
peace. In our engagements and our arguments, may we rid ourselves of 
all malice, deceit, hypocrisy, envy, and slander, and together show 
respect for the people with whom You have called us to serve.
  In awe, even fear of the judgment that is Yours, we humbly offer this 
day and ourselves to You.
  We pray this in Your merciful name.
  Amen.
