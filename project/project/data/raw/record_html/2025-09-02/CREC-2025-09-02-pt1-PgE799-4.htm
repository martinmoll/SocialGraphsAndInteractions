      REPORT (H. REPT. 119-199) TO ACCOMPANY HOUSE RESOLUTION 580

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. VIRGINIA FOXX

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 2, 2025

  Ms. FOXX. Mr. Speaker, the Rules Committee report (H. Rept. 119-199) 
to accompany House Resolution 580 should have included in its waivers 
of all points of order against consideration of H.R. 3633 and S. 1582 a 
disclosure of the following violation:
  Section 303 of the Congressional Budget Act which prohibits 
consideration of legislation providing new budget authority, a change 
in revenues, or a change in the public debt limit, for a fiscal year 
until the budget resolution for that year has been agreed to.
