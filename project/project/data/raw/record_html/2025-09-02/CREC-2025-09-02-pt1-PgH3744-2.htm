                          HONORING MARC FISHER

  (Mr. LANDSMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Marc Fisher, a 
constituent of mine who has spent more than 35 years leading our Jewish 
community in Cincinnati.
  For the past 13 years, Marc has served as CEO of the Mayerson JCC. He 
has worked incredibly hard for the community in which he grew up, and 
because of his leadership, the J has thrived. Membership grew. Early 
childhood and senior programs expanded, and partnerships flourished. 
During the pandemic, the J was a reliable source of strength and 
connection.
  That is what Marc brought to this work: a true effort to make sure 
everyone could find a place in our community.
  He will retire at the end of the year, but the impact he has had will 
last for a very long time.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Marc for all he has done for the Mayerson JCC 
and for all of southwest Ohio.
