                      RECOGNIZING SHAWN CHRISTMAN

                                  _____
                                 

                      HON. NICHOLAS A. LANGWORTHY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 3, 2026

  Mr. LANGWORTHY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
extraordinary dedication and service of Shawn Christman, who has 
devoted 25 years to serving the Town of Pendleton and the Wendelville 
Volunteer Fire Company. Over the course of his career, Shawn has served 
as President and two-time Chief, holding many officer positions while 
providing guidance and training to new and current firefighters.
  Shawn is a certified State Fire Instructor and has also led community 
initiatives, including the Santa Run for Christmas and the Lancaster 
Christmas Parade, helping the Wendelville Volunteer Fire Company 
achieve

[[Page E98]]

three Overall Championships. He has been instrumental in combining his 
leadership with technical expertise to support both the company and the 
community.
  Shawn's leadership and guidance have strengthened the Wendelville 
Volunteer Fire Company and its connection to the community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that the United States House of Representatives 
join me in honoring Shawn Christman for his remarkable 25 years of 
service and in wishing him continued success and fulfillment in his 
ongoing commitment to the community.
