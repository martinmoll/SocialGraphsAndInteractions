                     CELEBRATING RELIGIOUS FREEDOM

  (Mr. SUOZZI asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. SUOZZI. Mr. Speaker, religious leaders from across the world are 
coming to Washington, D.C., this week to celebrate religious freedom.
  It is hard to imagine that something we take for granted, like the 
right to worship, is denied to much of humankind across the globe.
  Muslim Uyghurs are put in forced labor camps by the Chinese Communist 
Party. Hindu and Buddhist religious minorities are being persecuted in 
Bangladesh. Christians are being tormented in Syria, India, Egypt, 
Nigeria, and elsewhere. Jews worldwide are facing anti-Semitism. Sikh 
turbans are banned, and Muslim headscarves are not permitted.
  Have you heard about the Ahmadis, the Rohingyas, the Druze? What 
about the Tibetan Buddhists in China and the Baha'is in Iran?
  There must be a worldwide effort to celebrate our diversity, promote 
religious freedom, and stop the evil forces that try and divide us 
instead of seeking the common ground that we all share.
  As we celebrate the 250th anniversary of our Nation, let us promote 
the religious freedom that we enjoy in our country, and let us serve as 
a beacon to the world once again.
