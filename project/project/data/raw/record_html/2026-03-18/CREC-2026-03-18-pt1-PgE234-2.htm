                   HONORING CW5 RICHARD ``VAN'' COOK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 18, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize CW5 Richard 
``Van'' Cook for his extraordinary service, steadfast leadership, and 
unwavering commitment to the United States Army and our Nation.
  Born on January 18, 1972, in Somerset, Kentucky, CW5 Cook graduated 
from Pulaski County High School in 1990 and enlisted in the Army that 
September. He began his career as a 19E M60A3 Armor Crewman and later 
served as a 76J Medical Supply Specialist, with assignments at Aberdeen 
Proving Ground, in Korea, and with the 82nd Airborne Division at Fort 
Bragg, North Carolina.
  After earning his Green Beret in 1998, he joined the storied 5th 
Special Forces Group (Airborne) at Fort Campbell, Kentucky. Serving in 
3rd Battalion, he operated as both a junior and senior Engineer 
Sergeant on multiple Special Forces Operational Detachments--Alpha and 
later as an Assistant Operations Sergeant on a Special Forces 
Operational Detachment--Bravo. During this period, he deployed 
repeatedly in support of Theater Security Cooperation missions and 
combat operations, including Operation Enduring Freedom and Operation 
Iraqi Freedom.
  After completing Warrant Officer Candidate School and the Special 
Forces Warrant Officer Basic Course, he transitioned to the warrant 
offIcer ranks in 2005 and returned to 3rd Battalion, 5th Special Forces 
Group. There, he served as Assistant Detachment Commander, Detachment 
Commander, and Company Operations Warrant Officer, conducting eight 
deployments in support of combat operations, including Operation New 
Dawn.
  His experience later carried into the institutional Army, where he 
served wIth the 1st Special Warfare Training Group at the United States 
Army John F. Kennedy Special Warfare Center and School, helping train 
the next generation of Special Forces soldiers. He subsequently 
returned to 1st Battalion, 5th Special Forces Group and deployed twice 
in support of Operation Inherent Resolve before serving as the Special 
Forces Branch Career Manager at Human Resources Command. From April 
2022 to May 2025, he served as the Command CW5 Warrant Officer of the 
5th Special Forces Group, the senior warrant officer advisor for one of 
the Army's most storied formations.
  CW5 Cook holds a Bachelor of Science in Social Sciences from Troy 
University and has completed extensive military education, including 
Airborne, Air Assault, Jumpmaster, and advanced special operations 
courses.
  He is proudly supported by his wife, Tabitha; their three sons, 
Richie, Clay, and Colby; their daughters-in-law, Victoria and Hunter; 
and their two grandchildren, Tripp and Ford.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his 
remarkable career and in thanking him and his family for their 
sacrifice and service.
