                   TRUMP PROTECTING AMERICAN FAMILIES

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, Iran has been at war for 
47 years, murdering American families, justifying President Donald 
Trump to respond to the imminent threat from the Iranian regime.
  Since the 1979 embassy seizure in Tehran to sponsoring terrorism 
through puppets like Hezbollah, Hamas, Iraqi militants, and Houthis, to 
Iran boasting to Envoy Steve Witkoff of 11 nuclear bombs, the regime in 
Tehran has chosen oppression of Iranians over peace and prosperity. 
They chant: ``Death to America.''
  President Trump has made the decision, with Israel, defending 
Americans: destroying missile capabilities, annihilating naval assets, 
and ensuring that Iran can never have a nuclear weapon.
  We are forever grateful for the servicemembers defending American 
families.
  Courageous commentator Mark Levin of Buckley, Goldwater, and Reagan 
heritage, stands with Trump, condemning isolationists as either dumb or 
evil.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump as Putin 
provides targeting information to Iran to kill Americans.
  Our prayers for Suzie Wiles for a full recovery.
