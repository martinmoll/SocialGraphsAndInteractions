            By Mr. SWALWELL:
        H.R. 7988.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill is grounded in Congress's authority under Article 
     I, Section 9, Clause 7 of the U.S. Constitution 
     (Appropriations Clause), which allows Congress to control how 
     federal funds are spent. It also relies on the Necessary and 
     Proper Clause (Article I, Section 8, Clause 18) to structure 
     how federal agencies carry out enforcement activities.
