                    RECOGNIZING LIEUTENANT TIM GILL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 18, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Lieutenant Tim Gill for his distinguished 29-year career in law 
enforcement and his dedicated service to our North Texas community.
  Lieutenant Gill began his career with the Grapevine Police Department 
on February 24, 1997, and was certified as a Texas Peace Officer on 
July 7, 2004. Over nearly three decades, he has demonstrated unwavering 
commitment, leadership, and professionalism in every role he has held. 
Lieutenant Gill holds a master's degree from Southern Methodist 
University and a bachelor's degree in criminal justice from Bluefield 
College. He is also a graduate of the FBI National Academy, the Senior 
Management Institute for Police, and the Police Executive Research 
Forum, reflecting his continued commitment to professional development 
and excellence in law enforcement.
  Throughout his career, Lieutenant Gill has served in a wide range of 
impactful roles, including Patrol Officer, Field Training Officer, 
Instructor, and Explorer Advisor. He made significant contributions to 
community engagement and public safety through his work as a Crime 
Prevention Officer, Crime Prevention Inspector, and Homeowner's 
Insurance Inspector. He also served in both the Patrol and Criminal 
Investigation Divisions before advancing to Lieutenant in Patrol and 
later serving as the Office of Professional Standards Lieutenant. In 
addition, Lieutenant Gill played a critical role as the Accreditation 
Manager for the Grapevine Police Department's first accreditation 
through the Law Enforcement Accreditation Program, helping to advance 
the department's professional standards and accountability.
  Lieutenant Gill's career exemplifies integrity, leadership, and 
dedication to service. His contributions have left a lasting impact on 
the Grapevine Police Department and the community he has faithfully 
served. As he moves into this next chapter, I offer my best wishes for 
his well-earned retirement.
