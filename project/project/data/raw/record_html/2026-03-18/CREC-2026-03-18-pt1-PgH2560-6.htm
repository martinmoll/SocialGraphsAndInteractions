              RECOGNIZING ELIZABETH CITY STATE UNIVERSITY

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, founded in 1891, Elizabeth 
City State University celebrated its 135th anniversary.
  As the community gathered to celebrate a convocation, Chancellor 
Hargrove announced MacKenzie Scott's generous $42 million contribution 
to the university, the largest dollar per student enrolled gift of any 
HBCU of her recent gifts.
  Our HBCUs are game changers. They are transforming regions and lives 
in northeastern North Carolina.
  We are reclaiming our future and keeping the American Dream within 
reach.
  Mr. Speaker, I am so grateful that MacKenzie Scott sees the value of 
educational institutions like Elizabeth City State University. Viking 
pride, Viking pride, Viking pride.
