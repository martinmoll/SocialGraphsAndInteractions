                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of peace, grant that Your spirit of peace would descend upon us 
in this day. You have granted us this unique and daunting opportunity 
to work with those who represent every corner of our United States, on 
behalf of every citizen of this Nation, and we pray Your spirit lead 
us.
  Show us how to respect those who labor with us, to acknowledge the 
gifts and graces You have bestowed on them, and to treat our 
differences with dignity and not disdain. Give us both the opportunity 
and the inclination to esteem them highly with the same love

[[Page H2559]]

and esteem You show to us. May Your spirit of peace reside among us.
  When we encounter those who fail to value the work with which You 
have entrusted us, may we reprove them, not as adversaries but as 
peers. Keep our eyes watchful for the fainthearted, that we would 
encourage them. May we share with the weak the strength You have 
provided. Let Your spirit of fellowship guide us.
  With all people, especially in these days where discord is the 
cultural default, may we show patience to one another, proving the 
better part of our beings. May Your spirit of love uphold us.
  In all that we do, may we choose not to repay evil with evil but seek 
to advance the common good and strive to follow the course of kindness 
in all of our endeavors.
  As we pray in the power of Your name, we ask that You send Your 
spirit to lead us this day.
  Amen.
