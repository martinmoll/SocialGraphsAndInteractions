                    HONORING COLONEL BRENT LINDEMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MATT VAN EPPS

                              of tennessee

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, March 18, 2026

  Mr. VAN EPPS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Colonel Brent 
Lindeman for his extraordinary service to our Nation and his steadfast 
leadership in defense of the United States.
  Colonel Lindeman was commissioned as an Infantry officer in 1998 
after graduating from Texas Christian University. He began his career 
with the 2nd Battalion, 325th Airborne Infantry Regiment of the 82nd 
Airborne Division, where he quickly distinguished himself as a capable 
and committed leader of paratroopers.
  Answering a higher call to serve in our Nation's most elite 
formations, Colonel Lindeman completed Special Forces Assessment and 
Selection and was selected for the Special Forces Regiment. After 
completing the rigorous Special Forces Qualification Course, he was 
assigned to the storied 5th Special Forces Group, where he would spend 
the majority of his career. There, he led Operational Detachment--Alpha 
545 and went on to serve in key leadership roles, including 
Headquarters and Headquarters Company Commander, Battalion Executive 
Officer, Company Commander, Group Executive Officer, and ultimately 
Battalion Commander.
  Throughout his career in Special Operations, Colonel Lindeman served 
extensively in the Middle East in support of combat operations and 
theater campaign activities under United States Special Operations 
Command Central. His leadership was critical to advancing our national 
security objectives during some of the most complex and demanding 
operations of our time.
  Following battalion command, he served as J35 Director of Future 
Operations for United States Special Operations Command Central, and 
later as J5 Director of Future Plans for the Special Operations Joint 
Task Force supporting Operation Inherent Resolve. He also served as 
Deputy Commander and Commanding Officer of the 5th Special Forces 
Group, where he continued to shape the next generation of Special 
Forces leaders.
  Colonel Lindeman concluded his distinguished Army career as Chief of 
the Army's Senate Liaison Division, strengthening the vital 
relationship between the United States Army and the United States 
Senate and ensuring that Members of this body were fully informed on 
matters impacting our soldiers and national defense.
  A dedicated scholar as well as a warrior, Colonel Lindeman earned a 
Master of Science in Defense Analysis from the Naval Postgraduate 
School. He also completed an Army War College Fellowship with the 
International Institute for Counter-Terrorism at the Interdisciplinary 
Center in Herzliya, Israel, further deepening his expertise in 
counterterrorism and strategic security studies.
  Behind every great leader stands a strong family. Colonel Lindeman is 
supported by his wife, Manivanh, and their four children, whose love 
and sacrifice have been essential to his decades of service.
  Mr. Speaker, Colonel Brent Lindeman's career reflects the very best 
of the United States Army: courage under fire, strategic vision, and an 
unwavering commitment to mission and country. On behalf of a grateful 
Nation, we thank him for his remarkable service and wish him and his 
family continued success in the years ahead.
