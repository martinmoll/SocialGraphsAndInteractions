                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy and most compassionate, most merciful God, our redeemer and 
friend, we pray Your blessing on our individual journeys of faith and 
ask Your guidance over believers of the Muslim faith as they commence 
their holy season of Ramadan and on Christians as they enter into the 
season of Lent later this week.
  As each community dedicates these next several days and weeks to 
prayer and fasting, open their hearts, cleanse their minds, and purify 
their spirits. May their dedication to the disciplines which serve to 
deny the countless things that attempt to distract us from You lead 
them and us to cultivate compassion to each other and to those around 
us.
  No matter what practices we use to draw closer to You, may they be 
motivated by the purest desire to know You more clearly, to love You 
more dearly, and to follow You more nearly.
  Bless us and guide our steps and light our paths.
  We offer these prayers in the name You have revealed to us, and I in 
the name of Jesus.
  Amen.
