           CELEBRATING 30TH ANNIVERSARY OF TUBBY'S TANK HOUSE

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
30th anniversary of Tubby's Tank House. Since opening in 1995, Tubby's 
has been a beloved coastal dining destination known for its fresh 
seafood, spacious outdoor patio, and warm hospitality.
  The owner and my good friend, Ansley Williams, expressed his 
gratitude for the community's support over the past 30 years.
  Tubby's has become more than a restaurant. It is a gathering place 
for

[[Page H940]]

locals and visitors alike. Its welcoming atmosphere and dedication to 
quality has made it a staple in the Savannah and Thunderbolt area.
  Guests enjoy outdoor games, keepsake photos, and a beautiful 
waterfront view that makes it so special.
  Tubby's has provided not only great seafood, but a place for people 
to connect and create lasting memories. As Tubby's celebrates this 
milestone, we recognize the dedication and hard work that made it 
successful.
  Here is to many more years of great food, great company, and great 
memories.
