   HONORING THE 45TH ANNIVERSARY OF ARROYO VISTA FAMILY HEALTH CENTER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JIMMY GOMEZ

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 20, 2026

  Mr. GOMEZ. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor the 45th Anniversary of 
Arroyo Vista Family Health Center located in Northeast Los Angeles.
  In February of 1981, Arroyo Vista Family Health Center was 
established to ensure access to health services ``for the community, by 
the community.'' Over the last 45 years, the non-profit community 
health center has become a trusted service provider, with a mobile 
clinic and four locations across Northeast Los Angeles. Every year, the 
center serves roughly 18,000 patients with a wide range of services 
such as family medicine, behavioral health, prenatal care, dental 
services, and pharmacy.
  For more than four decades, Arroyo Vista Family Health Center has 
cemented its status as a pillar for accessible, high-quality health 
care services for all. As our Northeast Los Angeles communities 
continue to grow and transform, Arroyo Vista Family Health Center 
remains committed to addressing the current and future health needs of 
residents through access to comprehensive health services. The impact 
of their efforts can be felt in every corner of Northeast Los Angeles, 
where residents no longer have to travel long distances for care and 
instead receive affordable, quality services from trusted providers.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Arroyo Vista 
Family Health Center on their 45th Anniversary.
