                     RECOGNIZING RONALD FRANK MOORE

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 20, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th District of Texas. The 2025 
Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to Corporal 
Ronald Frank Moore, United States Marine Corps, of Coppell, Texas, for 
his honorable service in the Armed Forces of the United States of 
America and for his continued service to his community.
  Corporal Moore enlisted in the United States Marine Corps in 1973 and 
was assigned to the 3rd Marine Division Headquarters Company during the 
Vietnam era. While serving, he demonstrated outstanding leadership, 
character, and dedication to his fellow Marines, earning the Good 
Conduct Medal for exemplary service. During his time in uniform, he was 
frequently invited to organize and lead morale-building events for his 
unit, including speaking engagements and community programs across 
Okinawa and the Pacific.
  Following his military service, Corporal Moore dedicated his life to 
communication, mentorship, and community service. For more than five 
decades, he has worked as a national broadcaster, speaker, and 
community leader hosting programs such as Higher Life and Morning Cup 
of Motivation, and serving as a voice for K-LOVE Radio. Through these 
platforms, he has inspired thousands with messages of resilience, 
leadership, and hope.
  He is the Founder of Fields of Grace Church, an outreach-focused 
organization that provides encouragement and service to others, and 
continues to lead programs across Texas and beyond that uplift and 
strengthen communities.
  Corporal Moore's lifelong commitment to service, both in uniform and 
as a civilian leader, reflects the highest traditions of the United 
States Marine Corps and brings great credit upon himself, the State of 
Texas, and the United States of America.
