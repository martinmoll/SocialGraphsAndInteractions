                         MAIN STREET PARITY ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 5763) to amend the Small Business Investment Act of 
1958 to modify the criteria for loans for plant acquisition, 
construction, conversion or expansion, and for other purposes, on which 
the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Texas (Mr. Williams) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 383, 
nays 8, not voting 40, as follows:
