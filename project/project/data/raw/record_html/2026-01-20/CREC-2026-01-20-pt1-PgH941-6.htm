   RECOGNIZING HELEN HAYES HOSPITAL CELEBRATING 125 YEARS OF SERVICE

  (Mr. LAWLER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LAWLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an extraordinary 
milestone for one of our community's most cherished institutions, Helen 
Hayes Hospital, celebrating over 125 years of service as of last year.
  Founded in 1900 as one of our Nation's earliest hospitals dedicated 
to caring for children with physical disabilities, Helen Hayes Hospital 
has grown from a small cottage in Tarrytown to a nationally recognized 
leader in rehabilitation right in the heart of West Haverstraw.
  For generations, families across the Hudson Valley and far beyond 
have turned to Helen Hayes during some of their most challenging 
moments, including my grandmother who was recovering from a heart 
attack. Time after time, the doctors, nurses, therapists, and staff 
have delivered world-class care rooted in compassion, expertise, and a 
deep commitment to every patient's recovery.
  From treating children affected by tuberculosis and polio to helping 
today's patients rebuild their lives after strokes, spinal cord 
injuries, brain injuries, and complex surgeries, Helen

[[Page H942]]

Hayes Hospital has never wavered in its mission.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank every member of their team, past and present, 
for the life-changing work they have done for our community for the 
last 125 years.
