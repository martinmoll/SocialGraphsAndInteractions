         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. JORDAN: Committee on the Judiciary. H.R. 7640. A bill 
     to amend the Immigration and Nationality Act to improve 
     public safety through the enforcement of Federal immigration 
     law in the interior of the United States, and for other 
     purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-541). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2709. A 
     bill to improve the health and resiliency of giant sequoias, 
     and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-542 Pt. 
     1). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state 
     of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 6380. A 
     bill to establish the Chiricahua National Park in the State 
     of Arizona as a unit of the National Park System, and for 
     other purposes; with amendments (Rept. 119-543). Referred to 
     the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.


                         DISCHARGE OF COMMITTEE

  Pursuant to clause 2 of rule XIII, the Committee on Agriculture 
discharged from further consideration. H.R. 2709 referred to the 
Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
