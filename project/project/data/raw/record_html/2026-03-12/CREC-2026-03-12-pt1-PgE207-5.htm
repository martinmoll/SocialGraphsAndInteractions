      CELEBRATING THE 100TH BIRTHDAY OF GEORGENE MARIE TILLI MORAN

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 12, 2026

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker. I rise today to celebrate an 
extraordinary milestone in the life of a remarkable constituent from my 
district. Mrs. Georgene Marie Tilli Moran, who has recently turned 100 
years old.
  Born on March 6, 1926 in Paterson, New Jersey, Georgene spent her 
early years exploring nature while living on Garrett Mountain. She 
attended Clifton High School where she met the love of her life, Jack 
Moran, whom she married and spent nearly 64 joyful years prior to his 
passing in 2012. After graduating, she spent her early career at 
Schering-Plough before dedicating her time to raising her two children, 
Barbara and Betty. Ever the caretaker, Georgene also spent her later 
years taking care of her mother, Marie Tilli, who lived to 103 years 
old.
  Georgene loved her time as a homemaker while also pursuing her 
passions of cooking and gardening. She was a true matriarch for her 
family, always making sure they were welcomed home with a warm meal and 
an inviting home. Additionally, Georgene spent a lot of her free time 
volunteering, making sure to give back to the communities that her 
family chose to call home. Her volunteer efforts include working with 
the Catholic Church, children's schools, at the local libraries and 
hospitals, as well as the Red Cross.
  An avid reader since childhood, Georgene cultivated a true passion 
for history during her moves to Chester Springs, Williamsburg, 
Virginia, and in our community of Bucks County where she has resided 
for the past 13 years. Her time in these crucial locations to our 
Nation's early history helped to form a true love for our country, 
which she and her husband Jack spent much of their downtime traveling 
together. She and Jack also shared a love of golf, traveling often for 
Jack's work in marshaling PGA events and his participation in golf 
tournaments.
  Now, in her 100th year of life, Georgene remains an incredible source 
of energy and love, enjoying her time with her two children and two 
grandchildren--Dennis and Kasey. Following her move to Bucks County, 
she has continued to pursue her hobbies of reading, enjoying history 
and novels, as well as attending family celebrations.
  As we celebrate Georgene's 100th birthday, we honor her enduring 
dedication to our community as a caretaker, steward, mother and a woman 
of enduring faith and joy.
