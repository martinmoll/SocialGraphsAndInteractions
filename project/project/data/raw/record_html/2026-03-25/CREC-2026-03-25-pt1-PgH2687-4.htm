  APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO THE COMMISSION TO STUDY THE POTENTIAL 
  CREATION OF A NATIONAL MUSEUM OF ASIAN PACIFIC AMERICAN HISTORY AND 
                                CULTURE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Fine). The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to 2(b)(2) of the Commission to Study the 
Potential Creation of a National Museum of Asian Pacific American 
History and Culture Act (Public Law 117-140) and the order of the House 
of January 3, 2025, of the following individual on the part of the 
House to the Commission to Study the Potential Creation of a National 
Museum of Asian Pacific American History and Culture to fill the 
existing vacancy thereon:
  Mr. Anh ``Joseph'' Cao, Metairie, Louisiana
