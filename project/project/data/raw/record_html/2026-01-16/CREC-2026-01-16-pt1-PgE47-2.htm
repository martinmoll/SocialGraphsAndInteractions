                   HONORING MAJOR MATTHEW N. TOPITZER

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JOHN R. CARTER

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 16, 2026

  Mr. CARTER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I proudly salute Major Matthew N. 
Topitzer for his outstanding service to the United States Army and for 
the remarkable professionalism he has demonstrated throughout his 
military career. Major Topitzer has consistently embodied the 
dedication, integrity, and commitment to excellence that define the 
finest officers in our Armed Forces.
  Major Topitzer's military journey reflects broad operational 
experience and steadfast leadership. He began his service as a Signal 
Platoon Leader in the 2nd Combat Aviation Brigade at Camp Humphreys, 
South Korea, and later led paratroopers in the 112th Signal Battalion 
at Fort Bragg. His expertise in communications and network operations 
took him to Special Operations Command--Europe in Stuttgart, Germany, 
and to Fort Belvoir, where he served as a technical project's leader 
for the 1st Capabilities Integration Group. In 2020, he deployed to 
Kabul, Afghanistan, serving as Deputy CJ6 for NATO Special Operations 
Joint Task Force--Afghanistan.
  I had the privilege of working closely with Major Topitzer when he 
served as my Defense Fellow through the Army Congressional Fellowship 
Program. His thoughtful counsel, analytical rigor, and deep 
understanding of defense policy were invaluable to my office. Following 
his fellowship, he continued his exemplary service as a Congressional 
Budget Liaison to the Assistant Secretary of the Army for Financial 
Management and Comptroller, presenting key research, development, and 
intelligence portfolios before Congress.
  Major Topitzer's achievements also include earning a Master of 
Legislative Affairs from George Washington University and completing 
advanced military education, including SERE High-Risk, Airborne School, 
and the Interagency Communications Course. He further demonstrates his 
commitment to service through his role as a White House Military Social 
Aide.
  It is an honor to commend Major Topitzer for his distinguished 
service to the Army and the Nation. I extend my warmest congratulations 
to Matthew and his wife Ana as they continue this journey of service 
together.
