                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God, be patient with us. In Your kindness, do not begrudge us our 
unfaithfulness. Show us instead how You do not boast that You alone 
know all the answers that we can't seem to grasp. In Your humility, 
show us what honor is.
  Do not deal with us in anger, but wipe clean any record of wrongs 
that we ourselves have tallied.
  Remind us that You take no delight in evil, but You rejoice when 
truth prevails.
  Protect us. Trust us. Place Your hope in us. Persevere with us. Love 
us. For love never fails. And You, O God, are love.
  In Your compassionate and eternal name, we pray.
  Amen.
