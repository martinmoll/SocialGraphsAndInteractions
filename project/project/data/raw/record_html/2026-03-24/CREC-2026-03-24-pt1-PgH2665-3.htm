                     AMERICAN WATER STEWARDSHIP ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 6422) to amend the Federal Water Pollution Control 
Act to reauthorize certain EPA geographic programs, and for other 
purposes, as amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Ohio (Mr. Taylor) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 378, 
nays 32, not voting 21, as follows:
