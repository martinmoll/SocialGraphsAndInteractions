              GEORGIAN DREAM PARTY COLLABORATES WITH IRAN

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, friends of freedom are 
saddened by the anti-American Georgian Dream party support for the 
terrorist regime in Iran. A recent report by the Hudson Institute, 
``Georgia's Iranian Turn,'' outlines Georgian Dream support for Tehran, 
attacking President Donald Trump.
  The Georgian Dream puppet prime minister of Tbilisi traveled to Iran 
twice in 2024, standing with leaders of Hamas and Hezbollah. In 2025, 
the Georgian Dream party expressed sympathy for the Iranian terrorists 
killed in U.S.-Israel airstrikes. Most recently, on March 2, the 
Georgian Dream party expressed condolences on the death of the Iranian 
supreme mass murderer Khamenei.
  The people of Georgia deserve fair and free elections, not the rigged 
elections by war criminal Putin, making his Mafia collaborators in 
charge. The legitimate President is Salome Zourabichvili of the 
Republic of Georgia.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump, with massive 
Russian drone attacks today on Ukrainian apartment buildings to kill 
civilians.
