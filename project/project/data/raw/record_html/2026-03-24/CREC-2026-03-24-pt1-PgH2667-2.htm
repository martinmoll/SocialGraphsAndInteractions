                             AIRPORT CHAOS

  (Ms. DEXTER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DEXTER. Mr. Speaker, people just want their time in airports to 
be uneventful. They want to get through security, get to their gate, 
get to their home and their families, or onto their travels without 
chaos, delays, or confusion.
  The White House is making that impossible. Rather than working with 
Democrats to fund TSA, Trump is sending ICE agents into our country's 
airports. ICE agents are not trained in aviation security or customer 
service. They are not able to help TSA agents, who have been working 
without pay for 47 percent of this fiscal year. ICE agents create fear, 
confusion, and certainly more chaos in our airports.
  There is a better way. Republicans should focus on sending paychecks, 
not ICE, to our airports, and let Americans get where they are going 
safely and uneventfully.
  Stay loud.
