                HONORING THE MADISON COUNTY HIGH SCHOOL

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 31, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
accomplishment of Madison County High School in Madison, Virginia, for 
being selected as one of the Top 25 schools nationwide--and the only 
school in Virginia--in T-Mobile's Friday Night 5G Lights competition.
  This remarkable achievement distinguishes Madison County High School 
from thousands of schools across the nation that participated in this 
innovative program. The Friday Night 5G Lights initiative is more than 
just a contest--it is a nationwide movement designed to fuel school 
pride, performance, and possibility. Through this multimillion-dollar 
program, T-Mobile supports small towns and rural communities by 
empowering high schools to showcase their school spirit, strengthen 
community engagement, and compete for a football field upgrade that 
enhances student and fan experiences.
  Madison County High School's selection as a Top 25 finalist reflects 
the extraordinary dedication of its students, faculty, staff, and 
community. Their collective enthusiasm and spirit exemplify the pride 
and resilience of Madison County and the Commonwealth of Virginia.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Madison County High School for its 
recognition and commend the entire community for rallying behind their 
students. I know this community will continue to support their school 
as they inspire our young leaders in demonstrating that teamwork, 
community, and perseverance can light up even the smallest towns in the 
brightest ways.
