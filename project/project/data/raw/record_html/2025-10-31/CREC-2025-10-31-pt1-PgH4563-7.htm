                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                 October 30, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Pursuant to section 451 of the 
     Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (Pub. L. 113-128), I

       Thank you for your consideration of this appointment.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
                                                Democratic Leader.
