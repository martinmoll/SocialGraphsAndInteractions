                          HONORING LINDA NEAL

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 31, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Linda 
Neal, who has been named the 2025 Agriculturist of the Year by the Napa 
County Farm Bureau. Ms. Neal is being recognized for her decades of 
dedicated service and lasting contributions to Napa Valley's 
agricultural community.
  Ms. Neal's path to agriculture began after her family moved from 
Orange County to Oakdale in Stanislaus County during her childhood. 
Inspired by local students involved in farming, she joined the Future 
Farmers of America (FFA) at Oakdale High School, where she earned a 
State Farmer Degree and numerous awards. Ms. Neal has often said that 
everything good in her life can be traced back to her involvement with 
FFA, where she came to see agriculture as the only truly essential 
industry. She went on to earn an Associate of Science degree in 
Agricultural Business and a Bachelor of Arts degree in Business, 
graduating summa cum laude, and became a licensed Pest Control Adviser.
  Ms. Neal arrived in Napa Valley in 1983, where she initially worked 
as a sales representative for the French agricultural manufacturer 
Rhone Poulenc. Soon after, she and her then-husband, Michael Neal, co-
founded M&L Vineyard Management, which began with a single quarter-acre 
vineyard and grew to manage more than 1,000 acres for renowned wineries 
including Cakebread, Mondavi, and Opus One. In 1986, she represented 
Napa County at the California Farm Bureau Federation competition in 
Monterey, earning first

[[Page E1022]]

place in debate. The following year, she became a member of the Napa 
County Farm Bureau and purchased an agricultural property in Oakville. 
By 1989, she had terraced the hillside and planted four acres of 
Cabernet Sauvignon on the property her vineyard crew would later name 
Tierra Roja for its distinctive red volcanic soil.
  After two decades leading M&L Vineyard Management, Ms. Neal turned 
her focus to her own properties. She operated Mellowood Vineyard in El 
Dorado County before selling the winery in 2018 to concentrate fully on 
Tierra Roja. Working closely with vineyard manager Ruben Rubio, Ms. 
Neal manages every aspect of her four-acre vineyard year-round, with 
each vine individually tended. She produces about 250 cases annually of 
highly regarded Cabernet Sauvignon from what has been called ``Magic 
Hill'' for its location among Oakville's most celebrated vineyards.
  In addition to her professional success, Ms. Neal has been a driving 
force for agricultural education and community engagement. Twenty-five 
years ago, she worked with local farmers, including Larry Bettinelli, 
to establish the Agriculture Program at St. Helena High School, where 
she served as treasurer, secretary, and fundraiser. What began as a 
vision for a single classroom has grown into a three-teacher program 
with more than half of St. Helena High School's students participating. 
Ms. Neal was also elected by local farmers to serve on the USDA Farm 
Service Agency Committee, where she advocated for agricultural 
producers across the region. Throughout her career, she has mentored 
many young people entering agriculture, giving individuals who are now 
industry leaders their first start in the field.
  Ms. Neal's commitment to service extended beyond our Napa Valley when 
she joined the Peace Corps in 2018 and served in Morocco until 2020, 
sharing her agricultural expertise with communities abroad before 
returning to continue her work here at home.
  Mr. Speaker, through decades of work in viticulture, dedication to 
agricultural education, and commitment to mentoring the next generation 
of farmers, Linda Neal has made lasting contributions to our Napa 
Valley's agricultural community. It is fitting and proper that we honor 
her here today as the Napa County Farm Bureau's 2025 Agriculturist of 
the Year.
