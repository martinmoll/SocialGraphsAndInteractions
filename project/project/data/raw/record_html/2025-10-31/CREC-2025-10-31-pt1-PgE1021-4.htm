     HONORING MARIA STATS AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 31, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Maria Stats, 
recognized as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of 
the Year. Maria is a proud first-generation American who has 
participated in community service and the arts as a performer, 
director, activist, author, and educator for more than thirty-five 
years. She is the founder and artistic director of On The Fringe Visual 
and Performing Arts, an international award-winning arts company. Maria 
is also the first DEI chair for the National Organization for Women, 
California, working directly under Helen Grieco. One of her major 
achievements was bringing Rock the Vote with MTV News and the State 
Registrar's office to Solano County. She is a founding member of the 
S.H.E. Institute, the Vallejo Charter School, and the Vallejo Literacy 
Alliance. Maria was also recognized by Playbill magazine in 2020 as one 
of the five community theatres giving the most back to their community. 
Maria was also recognized in 2024 as a Freddie G Fellow, the highest 
musical theatre teaching award outside the Tony, a teaching award from 
Music Theatre International and the Broadway League recognizing 
teachers who change the world one musical at a time. Currently, Maria 
is serving her second term as the Vallejo Museum's Board President. Her 
original musical, Wonderlandia, was workshopped in Vallejo and will be 
performed in conjunction with Dia de Muertos bringing a new Vallejo 
tradition to the downtown. Maria is most proud of being a mom to four 
amazing kids. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize Maria Stats for her 
invaluable contributions to our community.
