                STOP FORCED ORGAN HARVESTING ACT OF 2025

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1503) to combat forced organ harvesting and 
trafficking in persons for purposes of the removal of organs, and for 
other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Florida (Mr. Mast) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 406, 
nays 1, not voting 25, as follows:
