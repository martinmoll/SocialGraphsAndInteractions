            RECOGNIZING THE ACCOMPLISHMENTS OF MILTON HUDSON

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BARRY MOORE

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                         Wednesday, May 7, 2025

  Mr. MOORE of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize the 
outstanding contributions, hard work, and legacy of Mr. Milton Hudson. 
His dedicated service as a World War II Navy Veteran has made a lasting 
impact on our country.
  Mr. Hudson's time in the Navy has shown his bravery, dedication, and 
deep love for his country. Throughout Mr. Hudson's military service, he 
has had the honor of receiving the Asiatic-Pacific Ribbon, a 
Commendation by Captain Strauss of the U.S. Navy, an American Campaign 
Ribbon, and a Victory Medal.
  I would like to wish Mr. Milton Hudson a Happy 98th Birthday and 
thank him for his great service to our country.
