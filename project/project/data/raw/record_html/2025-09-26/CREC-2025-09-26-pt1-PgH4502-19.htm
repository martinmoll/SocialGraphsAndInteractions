           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 5580.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 5 of the United States 
     Constitution grants Congress the power ``To coin Money, 
     regulate the Value thereof, and of foreign Coin, and fix the 
     Standard of Weights and Measures.''
