                         HONORING MATT MARTINKA

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to selfless service Linemen 
Matt Martinka of Duke Energy.
  Mr. Martinka is a Journeyman Lineman in the Asheville area working 
for Duke Energy, a leading energy company that focuses on delivering 
electricity and natural gas services to the citizens of North Carolina. 
Mr. Martinka, along with apprentice lineman Nathan Curlee, were single 
handedly responsible for restoring power to the Asheville Veterans 
Affairs (VA) Hospital after the catastrophic events of Hurricane 
Helene.
  Hiking over a mile and a half through flooded roads, fallen trees, 
and a devastated landscape, Mr. Martinka demonstrated remarkable 
dedication and perseverance to restore power to the VA hospital. Mr. 
Martinka is a veteran from a multi-generational military family, 
including his father, grandfather, son, and nieces, and embodies a 
lifelong commitment to service. Upon learning that power could be 
restored to the hospital, Mr. Martinka didn't hesitate to act. The 
people of Asheville have since expressed their deep gratitude, 
commending Mr. Martinka for his unparalleled courage and selflessness.
  On behalf of all of Western North Carolina, I would like to take a 
moment to sincerely thank Mr. Martinka for his dedication and service 
to his community and to those who have served our country in uniform.
