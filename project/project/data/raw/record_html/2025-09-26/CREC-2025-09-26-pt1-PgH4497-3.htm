                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Lord, we appeal to You, You who are in Your holy temple, seated on 
Your heavenly throne. From there, You examine all humanity, our comings 
and our goings, our thoughts and our actions.
  Test us, all of us, the righteous and the wicked. Examine our hearts 
and hold us accountable to Your divine will.
  If we be found as those who resort to violence, rain down on our 
heartlessness coals that will burn the paths we have chosen to take. 
Deny us any reward for our cruelty.
  But if, by Your mercy, we are found to be righteous, if we have 
heeded Your word and acted according to Your grace plan for our 
communities and our country, and for all of Your creation, if You are 
pleased with both our work and our intentions, may we see Your face and 
know the blessing of Your favor this day and the days to come.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.
