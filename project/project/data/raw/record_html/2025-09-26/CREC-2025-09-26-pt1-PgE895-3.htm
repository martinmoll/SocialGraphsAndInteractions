                    RECOGNIZING DR. WILMA J. HUGHEY

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Dr. Wilma 
J. Hughey, a woman whose spiritual influence can be seen throughout 
Western New York through children of all ages and parishioners beyond 
the walls of church on Sunday.
  Dr. Hughey studied at Elmira College, obtaining her bachelor's and 
master's degrees in Education. She later obtained a second master's 
degree in Educational Leadership from SUNY Cortland, as well as earning 
her Ph.D in Biblical Studies from Newburgh Theological Seminary. Dr. 
Hughey has been selected as Supervisor of Women for the New York 
Western Second Ecclesiastical Jurisdiction of the Church in God in 
Christ, Administrative Facilitator for Licensing and Credentials for 
the International Department of Women, and appointed as the First 
Assistant General Supervisor by Presiding Bishop John Drew Sheard.
  For nearly three decades, Dr. Hughey served as Principal, completing 
her distinguished tenure in education and administration. While working 
with educators and families to ensure students' success, Dr. Hughey 
held numerous positions in New York State and positions on numerous 
community boards and committees, providing leadership and guidance, and 
fostering connections between community members and city officials.
  In partnership for more than half a century, Dr. Hughey, alongside 
her husband, the late Mr. Leo Hughey, faithfully shared her ministry. 
Bound by love and strengthened by prayer, they organized and 
established Faith Temple Community Church of God in Christ in Elmira, 
New York. Dr. and Mr. Hughey's unwavering commitment to ``build and 
expand the Lord's kingdom'' is centered on promoting social, cultural, 
and educational programming for community members throughout Upstate 
New York.
  Today, as we recognize the incredible service of Dr. Wilma J. Hughey, 
let us not only acknowledge her dedicated career as an educational 
leader and her steadfast commitment to children in need, but also her 
dedication to ministry at Faith Temple Community Church of God in 
Christ, providing a safe space for all to be heard. Dr. Hughey's 
service is a blessing, and we look forward to many more years of her 
inspired leadership.
