     HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF LIEUTENANT ROBERT JOHN HEANEY

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. APRIL McCLAIN DELANEY

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mrs. McCLAIN DELANEY. Mr. Speaker, Lieutenant Robert John Heaney was 
born on September 24, 1975, in Parlin, New Jersey. He later moved with 
his family to Derwood, Maryland, where he graduated from Colonel Zadok 
Magruder High School in 1993. From a young age, Robert demonstrated a 
strong commitment to service, a trait that would guide the course of 
his life and career.
  On October 10, 2000, he joined the District of Columbia Fire and 
Emergency Medical Services Department. Following the successful 
completion of his recruit training, he was assigned to Engine Company 
14 in the Fort Totten area of Washington, D.C., in February 2001. Over 
the next two decades, he served with distinction in some of the 
department's most challenging and demanding assignments.
  Rob was a proud and dedicated member of the Trinidad firehouse, known 
as the ``House of Pain,'' a firehouse which was repeatedly recognized 
as the busiest in the entire United States. It was there that he earned 
a reputation for courage, professionalism, and unwavering commitment to 
those he served alongside. He later rose to the rank of Lieutenant and 
was assigned to Rescue Squad 2, one of the most elite companies within 
the department.
  In 2023, Lieutenant Heaney was diagnosed with bladder cancer, a 
condition now recognized as an occupational illness associated with the 
fire service. Despite his diagnosis, he continued to show up with 
determination and resolve, remaining steadfast in his dedication to the 
job and his colleagues. On January 22, 2025, at the age of 49, he 
passed away from occupational cancer. His death was formally recognized 
as a line-of-duty death.
  Lieutenant Heaney is survived by his loving wife, Beth Heaney, and 
their two children, Molly and Finn. He also leaves behind his father, 
Robert Heaney Sr., his brother Sean, and his sister, Kellie.
  Lieutenant Robert John Heaney's legacy is one of service, sacrifice, 
and honor. His memory lives on through his family, his colleagues, and 
the countless lives he touched throughout his career. It is an honor to 
enter Lieutenant Heaney's legacy in the Congressional Record, and I 
extend my deepest gratitude for his devotion to others.
