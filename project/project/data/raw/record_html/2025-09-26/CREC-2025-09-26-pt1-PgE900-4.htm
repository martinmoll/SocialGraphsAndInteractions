HONORING ARNALDO OLIVERAS AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                      FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Arnaldo Oliveras is the founder and president 
of 180 Commercial Real Estate Advisors, a technology-forward brokerage 
and consultancy headquartered in Orlando. Licensed in Florida and 
equipped with an MBA in global management, he has devoted more than two 
decades to catalyzing job growth and revitalizing commercial corridors 
across the State--most notably within Florida's Ninth Congressional 
District, which includes Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Lake Nona, and south 
Orlando.
  Through 180 Commercial Real Estate Advisors, Mr. Oliveras has 
directed over $600 million in retail and mixed-use transactions, 
ushering both national brands and emerging concepts into Central 
Florida while pioneering lease structures that maximize landlord value 
and tenant viability. His firm's concierge platform--integrating tenant 
and landlord representation, market analytics, project coordination, 
and post-occupancy advisory--has become a blueprint for sustainable 
development along the Osceola Parkway and U.S. 192 corridors, 
generating hundreds of local jobs and broadening consumer amenities for 
district residents. Mindful that thriving main streets anchor healthy 
economies, he also advises small retailers and family-owned enterprises 
on fair-lease negotiations and expansion strategies, ensuring small 
businesses share in the region's growth.
  A passionate civic leader, Mr. Oliveras serves as the 2026 chair of 
the Hispanic Chamber of Commerce of Metro Orlando, championing 
entrepreneurship programs that directly benefit the Ninth District's 
vibrant business community. Oliveras also sits on the Executive Board 
at the Winter Park Chamber of

[[Page E901]]

Commerce and the board of Opera Orlando and as vice-chair for the 
Rollins Museum of Art board, leveraging arts advocacy to boost cultural 
tourism and business revenue throughout Central Florida.
  Renowned for disciplined optimism and a consultative style, Mr. 
Oliveras continues to align private-sector innovation with public 
priorities, advancing a more prosperous and resilient Central Florida.
