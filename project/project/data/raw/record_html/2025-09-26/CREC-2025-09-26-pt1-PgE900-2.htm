         CELEBRATING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF OSWALD'S PHARMACY

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Oswald's Pharmacy 
as they celebrate their remarkable 150th anniversary.
  Since its founding in 1875, Oswald's Pharmacy has been one of 
Naperville's oldest continually family-run businesses and the longest 
running family pharmacy in business. Oswald's is a beloved 
establishment in Naperville and beyond due to its commitment to 
personalized care and reinvesting in the local community.
  To address the evolving needs of the community it serves, Oswald's 
Pharmacy has expanded its services beyond just filling prescriptions to 
include vaccinations and health consultations. During times of crisis, 
including the COVID-19 pandemic, Oswald's remained a vital resource for 
Naperville residents, providing critical care and information when it 
was needed most. As we mark this incredible milestone, it is important 
to honor the Oswald family for their unwavering commitment to 
excellence and community service.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Oswald's Pharmacy as they 
celebrate their 150th anniversary. Their longstanding history in 
Naperville is a testament to their commitment to service, community, 
and resilience. I wish Oswald's Pharmacy continued success for 
generations to come.
