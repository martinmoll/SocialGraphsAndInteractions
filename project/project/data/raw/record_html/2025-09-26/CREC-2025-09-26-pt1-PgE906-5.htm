 HONORING DILIA CASTILLO AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL FLORIDA 
                      FOR HISPANIC HERITAGE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, September 26, 2025

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Dilia Castillo was born in Manhattan, New 
York, to parents from the Dominican Republic. In 1989, she moved to 
Florida, where she began her career as a freelance photographer, 
focusing on community events, newspapers, and magazines. Due

[[Page E907]]

to her passionate dedication to community engagement, she is 
affectionatly know as ``The People's Photographer,'' or ``La Fotografa 
del Pueblo,'' in Central Florida.
  Dilia's work and warm personality have earned her affection and 
recognition within the Central Florida community. Her creative approach 
to photography has been acknowledged through several awards, including 
the 2013 APEIH Certificate of Appreciation, 2015 Revista Mujer Award, 
2016 Latina Role Models Certificate of Recognition, 2016 Kissimmee 
Family Mission Inc. Certificate of Recognition, 2016 HAPBWA Award, 2018 
Portada Latina Magazine Award, 2019 Premios Boriken Award--Excellence 
in Artistic Creativity Award at the Puerto Rico Parade in Osceola, 2020 
Access Community Center Recognition for Meals on Wheels, 2023 
Leadership Awards at the Cuban Sandwich Festival, 2023 Premios Boriken 
Event Recognition, and El Premio Coqui de Oro Event Recognition.
  As a professional photographer, Dilia has contributed to numerous 
publications, including Caribbean American Passport Magazine, Portada 
Latina, Yes Business Magazine, the Osceola Star, El Nuevo Amanecer, 
Enlace Magazine, Karas Magazine, Politica--Politico Magazine, Revista 
Mujer, El Periodico El Sol de la Florida, and the HAPBWA Foundation.
  Dilia Castillo's contributions and impact on her community continue 
to be celebrated.
