  APPOINTMENT OF TELLERS ON THE PART OF THE HOUSE TO COUNT ELECTORAL 
                                 VOTES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to Senate Concurrent Resolution 2, 
and the order of the House of January 3, 2025, the Chair announces the 
Speaker's appointment of two Members as tellers on the part of the 
House to count the electoral votes.
  The gentleman from Wisconsin (Mr. Steil), and
  The gentleman from New York (Mr. Morelle).
