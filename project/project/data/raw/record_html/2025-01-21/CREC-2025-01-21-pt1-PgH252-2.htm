                  TARBORO HIGH SCHOOL STATE CHAMPIONS

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
Tarboro High School football team for clinching our State's 1A 
Championship and ending their season with a 14-1 record.
  As the reigning champions, they own a whopping nine State titles. The 
Vikings defeated a physical Corvian Community Cardinal team 45-24.
  I am so proud to congratulate Coach Jeff Craddock, also known as the 
GOAT. I congratulate all of the coaches, parents, the community, and 
players, including Kamerin McDowell-Moore, who scored a sensational 
four touchdowns to secure the MVP.
  Tarboro High School, in the heart of eastern North Carolina, has 
produced NFL greats such as Kelvin Bryant, Shaun Draughn, Donald Frank, 
Todd Gurley, Bill Hull, and Tyquan Lewis. Clearly, the Vikings are a 
powerhouse. If America didn't know about Tarboro High School, well, now 
they know.
