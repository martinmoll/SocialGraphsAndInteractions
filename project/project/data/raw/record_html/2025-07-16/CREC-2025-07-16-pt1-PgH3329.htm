                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the House of the 
following title:

       H.R. 1815. An act to amend title 38, United States Code, to 
     authorize the Secretary of Veterans Affairs to take certain 
     actions in the case of a default on a home loan guaranteed by 
     the Secretary, and for other purposes.
