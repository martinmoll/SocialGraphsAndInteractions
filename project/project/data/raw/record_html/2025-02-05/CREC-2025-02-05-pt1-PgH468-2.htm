               CELEBRATING THE LIFE OF VERONICA LYNN HORN

  (Ms. McDONALD RIVET asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. McDONALD RIVET. Mr. Speaker, I rise to celebrate the life of 
Veronica Lynn Horn of Frankenmuth, Michigan.
  Throughout her life, Veronica fought ferociously to make mid-Michigan 
a better place to work, start a business, and raise a family.
  Veronica's leadership quite literally shaped communities like Saginaw 
County. She served in two congressional offices, worked for some of the 
biggest job creators in our district, was the president and CEO of the 
Saginaw County Chamber of Commerce, and worked with countless 
nonprofits across the region.
  As I entered the State senate, I was lucky to count Veronica and her 
husband, Ken, as allies in the fight for Saginaw County. Veronica would 
work with anyone, regardless of political affiliation, to find common 
ground and attack a problem together.
  Mr. Speaker, may Veronica's memory serve as a shining example of 
commitment to faith, family, and community.
