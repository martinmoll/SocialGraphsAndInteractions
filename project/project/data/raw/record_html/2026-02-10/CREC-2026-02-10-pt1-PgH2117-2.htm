            CELEBRATING THE WOMAN'S CLUB OF LARCHMONT, INC.

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to salute the outstanding work 
of The Woman's Club of Larchmont, Inc., for their fine contributions to 
the Larchmont community.
  Celebrating their 100th year last year, they have embarked on their 
second 100 years with programs to promote education, literature, the 
arts, to provide philanthropic financial support to nonprofits and 
charities, and engender a healthy public dialogue on local critical 
issues.
  This year, The Woman's Club is led by Anne McAndrews, a former mayor 
who serves as club president. Other top leadership posts are held by 
Joyce Callahan, Ronnie McGovern, Sophie Kent, Theresa Fanelli, Marsha 
Kirchoff, Jenny Carter, and so many other outstanding women of the 
community.
  From the floor of the House of Representatives, we recognize the 
outstanding achievements of The Woman's Club of Larchmont, Inc.
