       RECOGNIZING OUTSTANDING PUERTO RICO POLICE BUREAU MEMBERS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, February 10, 2026

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with profound appreciation 
to recognize a group of individuals who make up the Puerto Rico Police 
Bureau, and to commemorate Police Week in Puerto Rico.
  During Police Week, observed from February 15, 2026, through February 
21, 2026, we recognize the men and women of the Puerto Rico Police 
Bureau, an institution composed of dedicated public servants committed 
to safeguarding Puerto Rico and improving the quality of life of its 
people.
  The Puerto Rico Police Bureau is made up of individuals with a strong 
sense of duty and a deep commitment to public service. Through 
coordinated administrative and operational teams, and in collaboration 
with state and federal agencies, the Bureau remains firmly committed to 
combating crime, protecting lives and property, and upholding law and 
order. The members of the Puerto Rico Police Bureau stand as a 
cornerstone of public safety and public trust.
  As part of this observance, I include in the Record the following 
list recognizing officers assigned to the Puerto Nuevo Precinct 282, 
who distinguished themselves through exemplary performance, 
professionalism, and commitment to the Bureau's core values:

       Supervisor of the Year 2025: Sgt. Omar Lopez Figueroa.
       Male Agent of the Year 2025: Agt. Robert Figueroa De Jesus.
       Female Agent of the Year 2025: Agt. Janisel Casiano Calo.
       Outstanding Captain & Lieutenant of the Year 2025: Capt. 
     Ruben Gomez Sanabria, Lt. II Jose Alvarez Torres.
       Outstanding Agents of the Year 2025: Agt. Griselle Vega 
     Baez, Agt. Jose L. Chacon Colon, Agt. Isaias Melendez 
     Maldonado, Agt. Francisco Esquilin Correa, Agt. Oscar Delgado 
     Jimenez, Agt. Nicasio Lima Candelario, Agt. Jose L. Ortiz 
     Rodriguez, Agt. Norberto Otero Robles, Agt. Joelys De Jesus 
     Ortega, Agt. Jeannette Gonzalez Vazquez, Agt. Marangelly De 
     Leon Sustache.

  Their service reflects the highest standards of integrity, 
dedication, and public service.
