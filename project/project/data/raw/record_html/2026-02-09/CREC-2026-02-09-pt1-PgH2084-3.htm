                HONORING LIFE AND SERVICE OF GRANT WARD

  (Mr. OBERNOLTE asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. OBERNOLTE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to honor 
the life and service of my dear friend, Sergeant Grant Ward, a 
respected leader within the San Bernardino County Sheriff's Department.
  Grant passed away on February 1 following a long and extremely 
courageous battle with cancer.
  Grant was also the longtime president of the San Bernardino County 
Safety Employees Benefit Association. In that role, he was a tireless 
advocate for strengthening the relationship between our county's 
leaders and the first responders whom he represented.
  I can unequivocally say that Grant was one of the finest public 
servants whom I have ever had the privilege to know. He was a true 
servant-leader, always putting the wants and needs of the people whom 
he represented above his own.
  Grant was also a tireless advocate for the public service officers 
whom he represented. He strengthened collective bargaining within the 
county. He improved legal protections for officers, expanded member 
services, and ensured that the voices of law enforcement were heard at 
every level of our government.
  Godspeed to my friend, Grant, who I will dearly miss.
