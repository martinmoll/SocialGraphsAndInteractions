    COMMEMORATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE SAN MARINO CITY CLUB

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. JUDY CHU

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, February 9, 2026

  Ms. CHU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to include in the Record the following 
proclamation recognizing the distinguished San Marino City Club on 
their 100th Anniversary:
  Whereas, in 1926, the San Marino City Club was founded with the 
mission to secure the residents of the San Marino Unified School 
District the best civic, educational, and cultural services and 
facilities; and
  Whereas, for a century, the San Marino City Club has promoted the 
belief that higher education holds an important role in shaping the 
future of our country and therefore must support the intellectual and 
ethical foundations that our democracy depends on; and
  Whereas, the San Marino City Club encourages the highest standards of 
citizenship by sponsoring public forums and debates before city-wide 
elections, including city council, school board, and local bond-
financing elections. They also offer regular monthly dinner meetings 
for members to connect with distinguished speakers to discuss local, 
regional and national topics; and
  Whereas, each year, the San Marino City Club awards financial support 
to selected community projects. From donating to the San Marino High 
School Girls and Boys Athletics to the San Marino Public Library, their 
funding allows our local schools, civic support groups, and local 
charitable organizations to thrive and prosper; and
  Whereas, as one of the leading civic organizations in San Marino, 
their membership includes distinguished members of the community, 
including present and past city council members, school board members. 
and local business owner; and
  Whereas, the 100th anniversary of the San Marino City Club is a 
moment to honor the legacy and achievements of the organization and its 
members, and its ability to foster a stronger, more vibrant San Marino 
by providing services that enrich the lives of our community and 
embrace civic and cultural traditions; and
  Now, therefore, be it resolved that I, Congresswoman Judy Chu, 
representing California's 28th Congressional District, do hereby 
recognize and commend the San Marino City Club on the occasion of their 
100th Anniversary, and extend sincere appreciation for their enduring 
contributions to the people of San Marino and beyond.
