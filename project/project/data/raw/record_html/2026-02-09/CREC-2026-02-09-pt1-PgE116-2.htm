 HONORING SOUTH OAK CLIFF HIGH SCHOOL FOR HISTORIC ATHLETIC, ACADEMIC, 
                        AND COMMUNITY EXCELLENCE

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. JASMINE CROCKETT

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, February 9, 2026

  Ms. CROCKETT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize South Oak Cliff 
(SOC) High School, a pillar of the South Dallas community and a place 
where pride, perseverance, and belief in young people run deep.
  I am proud to celebrate a historic achievement by the students, 
educators, coaches, families, and supporters of South Oak Cliff High 
School. Under the leadership of Principal Dr. Willie Johnson, the 
school has experienced a powerful transformation marked by higher 
academic expectations, a strong culture, and renewed community pride. 
That pride has been on full display through the Golden Bears football 
program, which has made five consecutive appearances in the Texas 
University Interscholastic League State Championship and captured UIL 
5A Division II state titles in 2021, 2022, and 2025.
  Winning three state championships is extraordinary. Doing so with 
consistency, resilience, and integrity is historic. After facing 
adversity on the biggest stage, these student-athletes stayed focused, 
trusted the process, and returned stronger, showing the entire state, 
what South Dallas football is about.
  At the center of this success are the young men of South Oak Cliff 
High School--students who balanced academics and athletics, supported 
one another, and represented their community with pride. Their 
accomplishments reflect what is possible when students are supported, 
believed in, and held to high standards.
  I also proudly recognize Head Football Coach Jason Todd, who led 
South Oak Cliff to three state championships, becoming the first 
African-American head football coach in Texas history to win three UIL 
state titles. His leadership emphasizes discipline, accountability, 
character, and academic responsibility, ensuring that success extends 
far beyond the football field.
  I congratulate the students, coaches, educators, families, and all of 
SOC nation on this historic accomplishment. South Oak Cliff High School 
did not just win championships--it made history and inspired a 
community.
