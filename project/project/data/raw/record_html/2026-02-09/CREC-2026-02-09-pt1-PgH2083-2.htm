                  EMPOWERING AMERICA'S NEXT GENERATION

  (Ms. BYNUM asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. BYNUM. Mr. Speaker, I first ran for public office to make all of 
our

[[Page H2084]]

kids' futures brighter, and our government hasn't done the same. 
Millions of young Americans have done exactly what has been asked of 
them, but our country isn't returning the favor.
  Fewer children than ever outearn their parents. The average age of a 
first-time home buyer is 40 years old, which is at an all-time high, 
and things feel too costly. Opportunity feels too scarce.
  I think Americans need to start believing in young people, and that 
is exactly why I introduced my K-30 agenda. The legislation takes key 
commonsense steps to make a difference, from investing in rural schools 
and supporting our teachers to cutting red tape for small businesses 
and revitalizing rural economies.
  Mr. Speaker, this isn't about politics. It is about building a 
foundation to empower America's next generation and showing them that 
we have their backs.
