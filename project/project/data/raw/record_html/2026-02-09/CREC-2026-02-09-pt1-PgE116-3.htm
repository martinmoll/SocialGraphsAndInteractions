  CONGRATULATING J.T. MILLER ON HIS SELECTION TO THE TEAM USA HOCKEY 
                  ROSTER FOR THE 2026 WINTER OLYMPICS

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. MICHAEL A. RULLI

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Monday, February 9, 2026

  Mr. RULLI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate J.T. Miller on 
his selection to the Team USA hockey roster for the 2026 Winter 
Olympics. From East Palestine, J.T. Miller showcases the grit and 
toughness of a community that is built on pride.
  J.T. has represented his country at multiple hockey events, including 
the World Junior Ice Hockey Championships. J.T. was drafted in 2011 by 
the New York Rangers with the 15th overall selection. Following his 
tenure with the Rangers, J.T. was traded to the Tampa Bay Lightning. 
After Tampa Bay, he was traded to the Vancouver Canucks, where he 
scored over 150 goals in six seasons with the team, and his stellar 
play earned him an alternate captainship. In 2024, J.T. was selected to 
play in the NHL All-Star Game and went on to net 37 goals and record 
over 100 points. Currently, J.T. is back in New York City, playing for 
the Rangers, where he serves as the team's captain.
  Having played in over 900 games, J.T. has scored more than 250 goals 
and has assisted on more than 450 goals. J.T. has 17 overtime goals, 
which rank eighth all time, showcasing his ability to perform in high-
pressure situations. His coast-to-coast goal against the Arizona 
Coyotes in February 2022 demonstrates his exceptional skating skills, 
his ability to deke defenders, and his knack for burying the puck in 
the back of the net. There is no doubt in my mind that J.T. will do 
everything in his power to ensure that the United States men's hockey 
team brings home gold at the Olympics in Milan.
  I invite my colleagues in the United States House of Representatives 
to join me in congratulating J.T. Miller on his selection to the Team 
USA hockey roster for the 2026 Winter Olympics.
