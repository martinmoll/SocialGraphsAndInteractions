                 HONORING GARY MOSES OF VICTORIA, TEXAS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL CLOUD

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 25, 2025

  Mr. CLOUD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with great pride to honor one of 
the most beloved sons of Texas's 27th Congressional District, Brother 
Gary Moses of Victoria, Texas.
  For more than fifty years, Brother Gary has been the heartbeat of 
Victoria and the entire Crossroads region. As a longtime educator at 
Patti Welder Middle School, a legendary radio voice on Majic 95.9, and 
Victoria's undisputed ambassador of joy, he has touched countless lives 
with his infectious smile and his genuine love for every person he 
meets.
  Whether he's emceeing the ``Gorgeous Grandmas'' pageant, television 
commercials supporting the Marines Toys for Tots, firing up the crowd 
at Riverside Stadium for the Victoria Generals, or leading the 
Christmas parade, Brother Gary shows up with enthusiasm that lifts an 
entire community.
  At 73 years young, he is always reminding us that in Victoria, we are 
all family. His nickname ``Brother Gary'' is not just a radio handle; 
it is the truest description of how he treats everyone he encounters. 
Local businesses surprise him with appreciation breakfasts, and 
residents make sure he's invited to every celebration because they know 
an event simply isn't complete without Brother Gary's presence.
  Mr. Speaker, Brother Gary Moses embodies the very best of the Texas 
spirit: faith, family, and hard work. On behalf of the people of the 
27th District and the countless lives he has brightened, I am proud to 
recognize him today and thank him for reminding us all how one person 
truly can make a community feel like home.
