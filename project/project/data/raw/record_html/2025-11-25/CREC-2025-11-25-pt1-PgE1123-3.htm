                    HONORING MARIANNA DEWITT HAWKINS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 25, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor 
Marianna Dewitt Hawkins for her more than 35 years of dedicated service 
to the Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Helena and Calistoga. Mrs. Hawkins is 
being recognized for her leadership as a founding Board Member and her 
commitment to children and families in the community.
  Marianna was born in Kalamazoo, Michigan, and moved to California 
during her middle school years, later settling in St. Helena with her 
husband, John Hawkins. She earned a bachelor's degree from Lewis & 
Clark College in Portland, Oregon, and pursued graduate studies in 
Marriage, Family, and Child Counseling at the University of Santa 
Clara. Before dedicating herself to community service, Mrs. Hawkins 
worked as National Sales and Marketing Director for Round Hill and 
Rutherford Ranch Wineries.
  In 1989, Mrs. Hawkins joined forces with Father John Brenkle and 
philanthropist Vera Trinchero Torres, as founder of the Boys & Girls 
Club of St. Helena and Calistoga. As one of the organization's original 
Board Members, she helped launch the first summer and after-school 
programs, which served more than 300 children in the opening week. Mrs. 
Hawkins helped develop partnerships with local organizations including 
Rotary, Kiwanis, Soroptimists, and local vintners to sustain and expand 
programing.
  Mrs. Hawkins played an important role in securing the Club's 
permanent facility in St. Helena, working with community leaders 
including the Trinchero and Torres families and Andy Beckstoffer. She 
also supported the Club's expansion to Howell Mountain and Calistoga, 
including the successful capital campaign for the Calistoga Clubhouse, 
which opened in 2017. Her advocacy for teen mental health and 
leadership development programs has helped establish programs focused 
on youth wellness and personal development.
  During the Napa Valley wildfires and the COVID-19 pandemic, Mrs. 
Hawkins helped coordinate relief efforts through partnerships with Feed 
Our Families and World Central Kitchen, providing meals and essential 
supplies to families and first responders. Marianna has also been an 
active member of St. Helena Catholic Church, supporting charitable 
outreach programs for migrant-worker families and affordable childcare 
initiatives.
  Mr. Speaker, through more than 35 years of service as a founding 
Board Member of the Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Helena and Calistoga, 
Marianna Hawkins has made lasting contributions to youth and families 
throughout the Napa Valley. It is fitting and proper that we honor her 
here today.
