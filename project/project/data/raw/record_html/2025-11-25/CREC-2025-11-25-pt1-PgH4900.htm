               ENROLLED BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly 
enrolled a bill and a Joint Resolution of the House of the following 
titles, which were thereupon signed by the Speaker on Friday, November 
21, 2025:

       H.R. 1512. An act to amend the Taiwan Assurance Act of 2020 
     to require periodic reviews and updated reports relating to 
     the Department of State's Taiwan Guidelines.
       H.J. Res. 133. An act requesting the Secretary of the 
     Interior to authorize unique and one-time arrangements for 
     displays on the National Mall and the Washington Monument 
     during the period beginning on December 31, 2025, and ending 
     on January 5, 2026.
