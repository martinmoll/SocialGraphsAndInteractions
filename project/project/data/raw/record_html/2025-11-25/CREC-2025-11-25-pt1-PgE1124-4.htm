                       HONORING MARKENZY LAPOINTE

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. FREDERICA S. WILSON

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 25, 2025

  Ms. WILSON of Florida. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
distinguished career and inspiring journey of Markenzy Lapointe, the 
United States Attorney for the Southern District of Florida. Appointed 
by President Joseph Biden and confirmed by the U.S. Senate in December 
of 2022, Mr. Lapointe led an office of nearly 500 attorneys and support 
professionals dedicated to investigating and prosecuting federal crimes 
in South Florida and litigating civil matters in which the United 
States has an interest. Mr. Lapointe served in this capacity until he 
ended his term with the outgoing Biden Administration. His leadership 
in this pivotal role serves as a testament to his unwavering commitment 
to justice and public service.
  Mr. Lapointe's remarkable legal career began with the Florida Supreme 
Court. where he served as a law clerk to the Honorable Harry Lee 
Anstead from 1999 to 2001. He subsequently joined the U.S. Attorney's 
Office for the Southern District of Florida as an Assistant U.S. 
Attorney, handling federal criminal matters ranging from narcotics and 
firearms offenses to bank and mortgage fraud. His tenure in private 
practice included over a decade at Boies Schiller Flexner LLP, where he 
rose from litigation associate to partner before joining Pillsbury 
Winthrop Shaw Pittman LLP.
  Mr. Lapointe served as a partner at Pillsbury Winthrop Shaw Pittman 
LLP in Miami, where he handled complex commercial disputes, high-
exposure product liability cases, professional malpractice matters, and 
criminal investigations. As co-chair of the firm's Advancement of 
Diverse Attorneys Committee, he demonstrated a steadfast dedication to 
promoting the retention and advancement of attorneys from various 
backgrounds, exemplifying his commitment to equity and inclusion within 
the legal profession.
  Throughout his career, Mr. Lapointe has also demonstrated an 
exceptional commitment to pro bono service. He has received numerous 
accolades for his advocacy, including the Legal Aid/Put Something Back 
Pro Bono Award in Child Advocacy and the Daily Business Review's Most 
Effective Lawyer Award for Pro Bono in Family Law.
  A proud alumnus of Florida State University, Mr. Lapointe earned both 
his law degree from the College of Law and his degree in finance from 
the College of Business. He also attended Miami Dade College, 
reflecting his deep ties to the South Florida community.
  Beyond his professional accomplishments, Mr. Lapointe's personal 
story is a profound source of inspiration. Born in Haiti, he immigrated 
to the United States as a teenager and settled in Miami's Liberty City 
neighborhood. A graduate of Edison High School, Mr. Lapointe went on to 
serve his country as a U.S. Marine during the Gulf War. Today, he is 
not only a

[[Page E1125]]

distinguished legal professional but also a trailblazer as the first 
Haitian-born American to serve as U.S. Attorney.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me as I recognize Markenzy Lapointe. He 
embodies the values of perseverance, service, and excellence. His 
journey from Liberty City to leading one of the most significant U.S. 
Attorney's Offices in the Nation is a testament to the boundless 
opportunities our country provides. I extend my heartfelt 
congratulations and gratitude to Mr. Lapointe for his service to our 
Nation and his dedication to the pursuit of justice.
