         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. GUTHRIE: Committee on Energy and Commerce. Supplemental 
     report on H.R. 4312. A bill to protect the name, image, and 
     likeness rights of student athletes and to promote fair 
     competition with respect to intercollegiate athletics, and 
     for other purposes (Rept. 119-270, Pt. 3). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WALBERG: Committee on Education and Workforce. 
     Supplemental report on H.R. 4312. A bill to protect the name, 
     image, and likeness rights of student athletes and to promote 
     fair competition with respect to intercollegiate athletics, 
     and for other purposes (Rept. 119-270, Pt. 4). Referred to 
     the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GUTHRIE: Committee on Energy and Commerce. Supplemental 
     report on H.R. 3668. A bill to promote interagency 
     coordination for reviewing certain authorizations under 
     section 3 of the Natural Gas Act, and for other purposes 
     (Rept. 119-297, Pt. 2). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. GUTHRIE: Committee on Energy and Commerce. Supplemental 
     report on H.R. 3616. A bill to require the Federal Energy 
     Regulatory Commission to review regulations that may affect 
     the reliable operation of the bulk-power system (Rept. 119-
     302, Pt. 2). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on 
     the state of the Union.
       Mr. GUTHRIE: Committee on Energy and Commerce. Supplemental 
     report on H.R. 3638. A bill to direct the Secretary of Energy 
     to prepare periodic assessments and submit reports on the 
     supply chain for the generation and transmission of 
     electricity, and for other purposes (Rept. 119-304, Pt. 2). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. GUTHRIE: Committee on Energy and Commerce. Supplemental 
     report on H.R. 3632. A bill to amend the Federal Power Act to 
     adjust the requirements for orders, rules, and regulations 
     relating to furnishing adequate service, to require owners or 
     operators of generating facilities to provide notice of 
     planned retirements of certain electric generating units, and 
     for other purposes (Rept. 119-307, Pt. 2). Referred to the 
     Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 302. A 
     bill to prohibit the conditioning of any permit, lease, or 
     other use agreement on the transfer of any water right to the 
     United States by the Secretary of the Interior and the 
     Secretary of Agriculture, and for other purposes; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 119-384, Pt. 1). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 556. A 
     bill to prohibit the
