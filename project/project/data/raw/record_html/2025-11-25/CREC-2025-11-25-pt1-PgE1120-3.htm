                          HONORING NIMBUS ARTS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 25, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Nimbus 
Arts as it celebrates its 20th anniversary. Founded in 2005 by Jamie 
Graff and Dana Johnson, Nimbus Arts has become an intrinsic part of our 
community, providing art education and fostering creative expression 
throughout our Napa Valley.
  The idea for Nimbus Arts was born out of a deeply personal 
experience. When Ms. Johnson's daughter was undergoing treatment at 
UCSF Children's Hospital, she witnessed the healing power of art 
through the hospital's family art programs. Wanting to bring that same 
sense of connection and creativity to her own community, Ms. Johnson 
partnered with fellow Napa Valley resident Jamie Graff and Janet 
Pagano, Executive Director of The Cantus Fund, to create an accessible, 
community-serving art center. Ms. Graff took on the role of Executive 
Director, and Nimbus Arts was launched in 2005, operating at first from 
borrowed spaces in churches and schools.
  Today, Nimbus Arts has grown into a $1.7 million enterprise with 
three well-equipped studios, including a dedicated ceramics and metal 
arts facility. The organization offers more than 800 classes and events 
annually to a diverse group of students, from toddlers to retirees. The 
organization works within public school systems providing arts 
activities on campuses and also works in court and community schools, 
including at Napa's Juvenile Hall, where art serves as a tool for self-
expression and emotional exploration. Through its Hundreds of Hands 
initiative, Nimbus Arts coordinates large-scale community-based free 
public art projects throughout Napa Valley. The organization employs 
more than 40 artist instructors, many of whom have been with Nimbus 
since its founding.
  Nimbus Arts' success is a direct reflection of the vision and 
dedication of its co-founders. Under Ms. Graff's leadership as 
Executive Director, Nimbus Arts has developed classes, camps, and 
workshops across disciplines ranging from painting and glass fusing to 
sewing and culinary arts. She also launched the Nim+BUS, a mobile art 
studio that delivers hands-on art experiences to schools and community 
events across our Napa County. Ms. Graff also worked with the board and 
staff to establish NIMBASH, the organization's annual gala and 
fundraiser. Her leadership has helped make Nimbus Arts a hub of 
creativity and collaboration, earning her recognition as Napa County's 
2015 Arts Hero. Ms Johnson

[[Page E1121]]

has served as Board President since Nimbus Arts' founding, bringing 
business, marketing, and fundraising expertise to the organization. She 
plays an active role in its ongoing success, participating in NIMBASH 
and serving on the Finance, Fundraising, and Vision Committees. Beyond 
Nimbus Arts, Ms. Johnson has also served as a Board Commissioner for 
the St. Helena Public Library and held positions with The Alex Fund, 
The Alpheios Project, and The Cantus Fund.
  Supporting local artists through meaningful employment and exhibition 
opportunities remains central to Nimbus Arts' mission. Ms. Graff is 
currently leading expansion efforts in south Napa, including the 
establishment of a new studio and a partnership with the Napa Valley 
Education Foundation to bring arts programming to the Foundation's new 
Discovery Campus. In recognition of its contributions to community 
life, Nimbus Arts was named St. Helena's Non-Profit of the Year in 
2020.
  Mr. Speaker, for 20 years, Nimbus Arts has served as a vibrant hub 
for community members of all ages and backgrounds to enrich their lives 
through art, The Nimbus Arts team's commitment to making art accessible 
to everyone has transformed countless lives and I am so grateful for 
their dedication to our community. I look forward to watching this 
creative community continue to grow for the next 20 years and beyond. 
Therefore, it is fitting and proper that we honor them today.
