                         HONORING ROBERT TORRES

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE THOMPSON

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 25, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Robert 
Torres for his more than 25 years of service to the Boys & Girls Clubs 
of St. Helena and Calistoga. Mr. Torres is being recognized for his 
leadership and philanthropy that have undoubtedly benefited the growth 
and sustainability of the Club.
  Mr. Torres was born and raised in St. Helena and represents the third 
generation of the Trinchero family, the founders of Trinchero Family 
Estates. He graduated from St. Helena High School in 1978 with honors 
and scholarships in mathematics and art and went on to earn a Bachelor 
of Arts in Architecture from the University of California, Berkeley. 
Mr. Torres serves as Principal. Vice Chair, Director, and third-
generation owner of Trinchero Family Estates. He is married to Maria 
Franchi Torres, and they are the proud parents of two children, Matthew 
and Julia.
  Mr. Torres has been a Board Member and Executive Committee Member of 
the Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Helena and Calistoga since 1999. During 
his tenure, Mr. Torres has been instrumental in expanding the Club from 
modest after-school programs into a multi-site organization now 
operating six facilities across our Napa Valley community, including 
Teen Centers, Clubhouses, and satellite school sites. Mr. Torres helped 
lead multiple capital campaigns, including the successful funding and 
construction of the St. Helena Clubhouse, which opened in 2008, and the 
Calistoga Clubhouse, which opened in 2017. Under his leadership, the 
Club's annual operating budget has grown from $300,000 to $2.8 million, 
ensuring long-term stability and expanded services for thousands of 
children throughout our Upper Napa Valley.
  In 2012, Mr. Torres co-founded and co-chaired The BIG Night, the 
Club's premier annual fundraiser. The event has become the Club's 
largest and most impactful fundraiser, generating $1.2 million in 2025 
alone. Over the years, Mr. Torres has donated more than $2 million to 
the Boys & Girls Clubs of St. Helena and Calistoga. He has been a 
strong advocate for affordability and access for all families, helping 
create a scholarship policy ensuring that every child can participate 
regardless of financial circumstances.
  Beyond his work with the Boys & Girls Clubs, Mr. Torres has been a 
lifelong philanthropist supporting local education, family services, 
and youth development initiatives in our Napa Valley. Through Trinchero 
Family Estates giving initiatives, he has championed community welfare 
and family-centered programs, promoting safe and enriching environments 
for children throughout our region.
  Mr. Speaker, through more than 25 years of dedicated service, 
leadership, and generosity, Robert Torres has made a lasting impact on 
youth in our St. Helena, Calistoga, and the broader Napa Valley 
community. It is fitting and proper that we honor him here today.
