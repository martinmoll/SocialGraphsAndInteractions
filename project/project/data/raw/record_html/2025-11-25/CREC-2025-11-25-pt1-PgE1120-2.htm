                  HONORING MAJOR LILIBETH F. GOLDSHINE

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. ANDRE CARSON

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 25, 2025

  Mr. CARSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Major Lilibeth F. 
Goldshine, a decorated veteran and dedicated educator whose lifelong 
commitment to service and leadership continues to inspire our 
community.
  Major Goldshine served in the U.S. Army from 1976 to 1997, including 
a tour of duty in Berlin where she earned the National Defense Service 
Medal with a Bronze Service Star. Throughout her career, she 
exemplified courage, dedication, and resilience in service to our 
Nation.
  Since retiring from active duty, Major Goldshine has continued her 
mission of service by leading the Junior Reserve Officers' Training 
Corps program in Indianapolis Public Schools for nearly 25 years. 
Through her mentorship and teaching, she has instilled discipline, 
leadership, and a sense of responsibility in countless young people, 
preparing them to face life's challenges with integrity and confidence.
  Major Goldshine is a pillar in the community, a role model for women 
veterans whose stories of service and sacrifice often go unrecognized. 
Her dedication embodies the spirit of the Sheila Corcoran Hoosier Women 
Hero Award, which honors outstanding Hoosier women veterans whose 
contributions to our community and Nation that are unmatched. Major 
Goldshine was a recipient of this award over the summer, and now, I am 
honored to cement her legacy in the Congressional Record.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Major 
Lilibeth F. Goldshine for her exceptional service to our country, her 
unwavering commitment to education, and her lasting impact on the lives 
of Indianapolis youth. Her leadership and resilience continue to 
strengthen our community and inspire future generations.
