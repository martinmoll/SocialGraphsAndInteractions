      RESIGNATION OF CHIEF ADMINISTRATIVE OFFICER OF THE HOUSE OF 
                            REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Chief Administrative Officer of the House of 
Representatives:

         Office of the Chief Administrative Officer, House of 
           Representatives,
                                Washington, DC, November 19, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: I am writing to inform you of my 
     retirement, effective December 31, 2025. Over the last 14 
     years I have had the honor of serving the House working in 
     the Chief Administrative Officer organization and the last 5 
     years as the Chief Administrative Officer. It has been a 
     great pleasure to serve this institution and lead an 
     organization that is dedicated to serving the Members.
       Please let me know if I can be of any help moving forward.
           Thank you,
                                            Catherine L. Szpindor,
                                      Chief Administrator Officer.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the resignation is 
accepted.
  There was no objection.
