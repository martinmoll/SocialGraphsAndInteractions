  NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES 
           FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 164



 =========================== NOTE =========================== 

  
  On page H1472, April 8, 2025, in the first column, the following 
appeared: NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER RESOLUTION RAISING A 
QUESTION OF THE PRIVILEGES OF THE HOUSE
  
  The online version has been corrected to read: NOTICE OF 
INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES FROM 
FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF HOUSE RESOLUTION 164


 ========================= END NOTE ========================= 


  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, I 
rise to provide notice my intent to offer a motion to discharge the 
Committee on Rules from the further consideration of House Resolution 
164.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Did the gentlewoman sign the petition?
  Ms. PETTERSEN. I did, Mr. Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, a motion 
to discharge is privileged only at a time designated by the Speaker 
within 2 legislative days after the intent to offer such motion is 
offered. The Chair will entertain the gentlewoman's motion at that 
time.
