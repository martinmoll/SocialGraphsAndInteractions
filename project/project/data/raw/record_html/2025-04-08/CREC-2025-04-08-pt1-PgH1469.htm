          CONGRATULATING ALEXANDRIA HIGH SCHOOL ROBOTICS TEAM

  (Mrs. Fischbach of Minnesota was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Mrs. FISCHBACH. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate Mechatronics Team 
3133, the Alexandria Area High School's robotics team, on winning the 
Impact Award.
  This prestigious award will send the team to the world championship 
in Houston, Texas. This is their third Impact Award, and it is clear 
why. The team is extremely dedicated, having offered hours of their 
time in the summer to work with elementary and middle school robotics 
teams.
  Teammates Bridger Klavetter and Gunner Sellnow said they appreciate 
the worldwide community that the sport has allowed them to be part of 
and said that their team works to be professional in defeat and 
gracious in victory.
  On behalf of the Seventh District, I wish them the best of luck in 
Houston.
