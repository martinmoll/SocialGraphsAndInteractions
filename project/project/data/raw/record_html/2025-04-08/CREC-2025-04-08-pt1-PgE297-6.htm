    HONORING THE UNIVERSITY PLACE BOOK CLUB ON ITS 100TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, April 8, 2025

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
dedicated members of the University Place Book Club on their 100th 
anniversary. Founded in 1925, this all-women's book club has served as 
a haven for women in Fort Worth who share a passion for literature. 
Beyond being a place for social connection, the club has played a vital 
role in the cultural and civic development of the community.
  At its inception in the 1920s, the wives of several faculty members 
and administrators at Texas Christian University gathered in what is 
now known as Robert Carr Chapel to discuss literature and music. 
Throughout its storied history, its members have explored topics 
spanning music, art, and literature.
  Additionally, the University Place Book Club has made a significant 
impact on future generations by promoting literacy through initiatives 
such as hosting storytime sessions and providing financial support to 
schools in Fort Worth. The women of this club attribute its longevity 
to their enduring love of literature, which continues to be passed down 
through generations. Their dedication has not only enriched the lives 
of members but has also brought the gift of reading to many children in 
the community.

[[Page E298]]

  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
recognize the University Place Book Club for its significant 
contributions to the City of Fort Worth. The members' passion for 
literature and commitment to enhancing educational resources for 
children have left a lasting impact on the community. I extend my best 
wishes to all its members as they continue the legacy of this historic 
book club.
