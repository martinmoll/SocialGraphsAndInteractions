          HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF RONALD W. SURIN, II

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 17, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life 
and legacy of Ronald W. Surin, II. I include in the Record the 
following Proclamation honoring his life and legacy:

       Whereas, Ronald W. Surin, II, born on April 2, 2003, in 
     Pembroke Pines, Florida, was a bright light to all who had 
     the pleasure of knowing him. The beloved son of Ronald and 
     Shebella Surin, Ronald lived a life marked by faith, 
     determination, love, and kindness far beyond his 22 years; 
     and
       Whereas, Ronald's spiritual journey began at a young age, 
     culminating in his baptism on Easter Sunday, April 5, 2015, 
     shortly after being diagnosed with juvenile diabetes. Despite 
     life's challenges, he faced each day with courage, grace, and 
     an unwavering faith in God; and
       Whereas, a proud graduate of Pines Carter High School and a 
     senior at Florida International University, Ronald pursued 
     his passion for Sports Management and Physical Therapy with 
     the dream of one day following in his father's footsteps and 
     attending law school. He also demonstrated his commitment to 
     responsibility and service through his work with Target 
     Corporation; and
       Whereas, known for his calm, cool, and collected demeanor, 
     Ronald touched countless lives with his infectious smile, his 
     warm and loving spirit, and his genuine, friendly nature. An 
     exceptional athlete, Ronald excelled in football, basketball, 
     and track and field, and was especially known for his speed 
     on the track. His dedication, discipline, and sportsmanship 
     inspired all who knew him; and
       Whereas, Ronald was deeply loved by his family, friends, 
     classmates, co-workers, and community. He leaves behind a 
     legacy of kindness, strength, and love that will never be 
     forgotten. He will be dearly missed by his parents, Shebella 
     and Ronald; his siblings, Shenelle, Wellington, and Ronalda; 
     his grandmothers, Ella and Otelia; his great-grandmother 
     Fannie; his girlfriend Sara; his aunts Miriam, Nanotte, 
     Kechnide, and Naucie; and a large extended family of cousins, 
     uncles, nieces, nephews, and friends, as well as the many 
     families he was connected to, including the Sarin, Poitier, 
     Antoine, Cave, Jean-Pierre, Coachman, Sean-Mary, Gilles, 
     Darbouze, Jackson, Lee, and Beavers families; and therefore, 
     be it
       Proclaimed That we, the 20th Congressional District of 
     Florida, do hereby honor and celebrate the life of Ronald W. 
     Surin, II. His legacy of faith, kindness, resilience, and joy 
     will continue to uplift and inspire those who knew him. May 
     his memory forever serve as a beacon of hope, strength, and 
     love within our community and beyond.
