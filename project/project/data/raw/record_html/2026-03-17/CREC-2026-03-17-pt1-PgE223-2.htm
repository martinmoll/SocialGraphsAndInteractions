                        HONORING MORGAN HERITAGE

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 17, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Morgan 
Heritage, and include in the Record the following proclamation in its 
honor:

       Whereas, Morgan Heritage, comprised of the iconic trio 
     Peetah Morgan, Gramps Morgan, and Mr. Mojo has carried 
     forward the musical and cultural legacy of their father, the 
     late Hon. Ras Denroy Morgan, Sr., by becoming one of the most 
     globally recognized ambassadors of reggae music; and
       Whereas, over the course of more than three decades, Morgan 
     Heritage has released twelve studio albums earning critical 
     accolades and commercial success, including three Grammy 
     Awards and multiple international honors, solidifying their 
     legacy; and
       Whereas, deeply rooted in both Jamaican and African 
     ancestry, Morgan Heritage has used music as a powerful 
     vehicle for unity, resistance, humanitarianism, and love; 
     promoting conscious music, while fostering connections 
     between the Caribbean and the African nations through 
     collaborations and cultural engagement; and
       Whereas, Morgan Heritage has proudly represented both 
     Brooklyn, New York, and Jamaica on the world stage, becoming 
     cultural stewards and artistic visionaries whose music 
     transcends borders and unites people across nations; and 
     therefore, be it
       Resolved, That we, Florida's 20th Congressional District, 
     in recognition of Caribbean-American Heritage Month, do 
     hereby honor Morgan Heritage for their extraordinary musical 
     contributions, their dedication to preserving and evolving 
     reggae music, and their unwavering commitment to global 
     cultural exchange and unity, peace, and love.
