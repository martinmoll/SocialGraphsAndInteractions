                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of the ages, we recall the words of Saint Patrick's breastplate, 
when we too bear witness to the strength of Heaven revealed in the 
light of the Sun, in the speed of the lightning, in the swiftness of 
the sea, in the stability of the Earth, and in the firmness of the 
rock.
  This is the strength that You have provided us to face this day. And 
You, O Lord, alone are the source of that strength.
  From You and through You and for You are all things. Deep are the 
riches of Your wisdom and knowledge.
  And it is Your omniscience that directs us, Your might that upholds 
us, and Your spirit that inspires us to serve You. May we live up to 
the responsibility You have entrusted to us as we labor in these Halls.
  To You, God Almighty, be all praise and honor. Amen.
