               HONORING KHADINE ``MISS KITTY'' WILKINSON

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 17, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Khadine 
``Miss Kitty'' Wilkinson and include in the Record the following 
proclamation in celebration of Caribbean-American Heritage month:

       Whereas, Khadine ``Miss Kitty'' Wilkinson is considered one 
     of the most influential voices in the Caribbean, exemplifying 
     excellence as an attorney-at-law, media personality, keynote 
     speaker, and advocate for social change; and
       Whereas, through her dynamic presence across radio, 
     television, and live events, Mrs. Wilkinson has consistently 
     used her platform to inform, educate, and uplift audiences, 
     while promoting Caribbean culture, identity, and values on a 
     global scale; and
       Whereas, as a cultural ambassador and media icon, ``Miss 
     Kitty'' embodies the vibrant spirit, resilience, and 
     innovation of the Caribbean people, and serves as a beacon of 
     inspiration to the Caribbean diasporic communities around the 
     world; and
       Whereas, through her philanthropic work, she has awarded 
     scholarships to fourteen students at The University of the 
     West Indies, promoting educational advancement and 
     opportunity for the next generation of Caribbean leaders 
     while also organizing critical health initiatives, 
     particularly voluntary blood donation, that in turn provide 
     high-impact support to public health in Jamaica and the wider 
     region; and
       Whereas, her contributions have earned her prestigious 
     accolades, including the Governor General's 30th Anniversary 
     Award for Community Development and The UWI 75th Anniversary 
     Outstanding Volunteer Award, as well as the distinction of 
     serving as Patron of the inaugural European Union-Jamaica 5K 
     Road Race in 2023; and
       Therefore, be it resolved that we, Florida's 20th 
     Congressional District, in celebration of Caribbean-American 
     Heritage Month, do hereby recognize and honor Khadine ``Miss 
     Kitty'' Wilkinson for her unique contributions to the 
     preservation, celebration, and elevation of Caribbean 
     heritage, and commend her for her enduring service to 
     culture, education, and community.
