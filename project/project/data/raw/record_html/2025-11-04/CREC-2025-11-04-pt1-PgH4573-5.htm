           By Mr. BIGGS of Arizona:
       H.R. 5904.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 4 of the United States 
     Constitution, which grants Congress the authority to 
     establish a uniform rule of naturalization; and
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the United States 
     Constitution, which grants Congress the authority to regulate 
     commerce with foreign nations and with the Indian tribes.
