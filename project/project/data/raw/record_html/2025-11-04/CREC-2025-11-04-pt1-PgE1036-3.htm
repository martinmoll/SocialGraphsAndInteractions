HONORING TRINA JACKSON-LINCOLN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Trina Jackson-
Lincoln as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of 
the Year in tribute to her more than 30 years of dedicated public 
service. Since 1995, Ms. Jackson-Lincoln has served in the Office of 
the City Council, beginning with a temporary role supporting emergency 
services and growing into her current position as Project Coordinator 
and City Council Liaison. In this role, she has supported 
Councilmembers, coordinated key city events, and helped countless 
residents navigate local government. She has led or contributed to 
numerous community projects, including the Richmond Beautification 
Project, AIDS Awareness Day, the Taste of Richmond, Sisters in 
Solidarity, and the Literacy Fair, while also serving as liaison to the 
Richmond Sister City Program and as former staff to the Richmond Youth 
Council. Trina also helped launch and manage the Richmond Cares Hotline 
during the COVID-19 pandemic, mentored student interns, and played a 
key role in advancing racial equity through her leadership in the 
Government Alliance on Race and Equity. A Richmond native, Trina is a 
graduate of Contra Costa College and California State University, East 
Bay, where she earned her bachelor's degree in human development. Over 
her decades of service, community engagement, and commitment to 
inclusion, she has had a lasting impact. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, I am honored to recognize 
Trina Jackson-Lincoln for her decades of dedicated public service, her 
steadfast commitment to equity, and her enduring contributions to the 
City of Richmond and its residents.
