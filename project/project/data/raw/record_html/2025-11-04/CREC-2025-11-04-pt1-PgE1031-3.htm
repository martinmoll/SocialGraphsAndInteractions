     HONORING SARAH WALLY AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Sarah Wally and to honor her as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year in recognition of her 
outstanding contributions to the Richmond community. With over 30 years 
of experience in real estate development, historic preservation, and 
community-based initiatives, Ms. Wally has dedicated her career to 
fostering economic growth, sustainability, and inclusive opportunity in 
Richmond and the greater Bay Area. Since moving to Richmond 11 years 
ago, Ms. Wally has played a pivotal role in revitalizing the city 
through her leadership at Richmond Main Street and PLANN-A Development. 
She has also served on the Richmond Workforce Development Board for 
more than a decade, helping prepare residents for meaningful employment 
and sustainable careers. Beyond her professional achievements, Ms. 
Wally actively engages with youth through partnerships with the 
Richmond Police Activities League and she contributes to her community 
as a member of the Rotary Club. Her dedication to community 
empowerment, mentorship, and economic development embodies the spirit 
of service that strengthens our neighborhoods. On behalf of the people 
of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor 
and recognize Sarah Wally for her invaluable contributions to our 
community.
