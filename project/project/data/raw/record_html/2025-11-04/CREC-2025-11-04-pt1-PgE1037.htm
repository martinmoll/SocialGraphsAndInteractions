                       HONORING MR. KEITH JOHNSON

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Mr. Keith Johnson, a 32-year-old musician, author, and 
cultural ambassador who continues to carry forward his family's musical 
legacy while proudly representing the Mississippi Delta on an 
international stage. Known on stage as the ``Prince of the Delta 
Blues,'' Mr. Johnson is the great-nephew of McKinley Morganfield, 
better known to the world as Muddy Waters.
  Born and raised in Glen Allan, a small community south of Greenville 
on the edge of Lake Washington in Washington County, Mr. Johnson grew 
up immersed in a rich tradition of gospel music. While many of his 
peers focused on sports, his childhood was filled with music and church 
singing. By the age of 15, he was already a member of the church choir 
and went on to form his first gospel quartet, The Delta Sensations.
  During his senior year at Riverside High School, Mr. Johnson wrote 
his first official song, Outstanding Love, which was adopted as the 
school's anthem. This pivotal moment sparked his deeper commitment to 
music and inspired him to pursue it professionally. His passion led him 
to the Delta Music Institute at Delta State University in Cleveland, 
Mississippi, where he studied audio engineering, songwriting, and 
performance.
  In 2018, Mr. Johnson earned an MBA with a focus in human resource 
management, combining his passion for music with business expertise to 
manage all aspects of his career. That same year, he released his debut 
album, Come to Mississippi, honoring his home State's blues legacy. He 
has since toured internationally, performing across Europe and later in 
Brazil through a cultural exchange sponsored by Delta State University.
  Alongside his solo work, Mr. Johnson performs with Morgan Freeman's 
Blues Symphonic Orchestra, also known as the Morgan Freeman Blues 
Experience, contributing vocals, guitar, and harmonica. He has toured 
nationally with the group over the past two months.
  Mr. Johnson, a published author of two books and currently at work on 
his third, is also channeling his creative energy into music. He is in 
the process of recording a new album, Deep in the Mud, alongside 
Grammy-nominated producer Vasti Jackson at the legendary Royal Studios 
in Memphis--a place steeped in musical history. Mr. Johnson describes 
the collaboration as ``history in the making,'' a testament to both the 
studio's legacy and the project's cultural significance.
  Driven by a deep sense of purpose, Mr. Johnson remains unwavering in 
his commitment to honoring his roots. He is passionate about shining a 
spotlight on Glen Allan and the broader Mississippi Delta, working 
tirelessly to ensure their rich cultural heritage receives the 
recognition it deserves on the global stage.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me in celebrating the 
achievements of Mr. Keith Johnson, and in recognizing his extraordinary 
contributions to music and blues in Mississippi and his unwavering 
commitment to proving that the blues still carries power, soul, and 
purpose.
