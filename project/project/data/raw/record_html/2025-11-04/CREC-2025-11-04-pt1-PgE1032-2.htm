                 HONORING MR. PAUL-RAYMOND BUITRON, III

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Professeur Paul-
Raymond Buitron, III--an internationally acclaimed martial artist, 
educator, and cultural ambassador whose life's work has elevated both 
the sport and the spirit of martial arts.
  Professeur Buitron began his training at the age of six under his 
uncle, Maitre Isidro Chapa, studying the Basque art of Zipora. His 
early discipline and creativity shaped a lifetime dedicated to mastery, 
mentorship, and cultural exchange.
  Through years of study in Aikido, Kung Fu, Kenpo, Boxing, and 
especially Savate, Professeur Buitron broke barriers.
  He became the first American and first non-Frenchman to earn the 
title of Professeur in all disciplines of Savate and was the first 
American to receive France's prestigious Silver Glove and Silver 
Troisieme Degre.
  After an injury cut short his competitive career, he founded the 
first official School of Savate in the United States, training 
generations of champions and introducing Savate to countless 
communities.
  His leadership through the Buitron Academy and his creation of the 
Savate Danse De Rue movement have bridged cultures and inspired 
athletes worldwide.
  Mr. Speaker, Professeur Paul-Raymond Buitron, III's achievements as a 
teacher, innovator, and cultural ambassador embody excellence and 
perseverance.
  On behalf of the 28th District of Texas, I commend him for his 
lifelong contributions to sport, education, and international 
friendship.
