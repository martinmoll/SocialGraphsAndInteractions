HONORING THE SERVICE OF UNITED STATES AIR FORCE COLONEL MARLENA PARKER, 
                                RETIRED

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a true 
American hero from Pennsylvania's 1st Congressional District, Colonel 
Marlena Parker, Retired, United States Air Force, and to commemorate 
the inaugural presentation of the ``Colonel Marlena Parker 
Inspirational Veteran Award''. This award bears her name for a reason. 
It celebrates the rarest kind of courage one could imagine. The courage 
not only to serve, but to continue serving even when the world demands 
more than we ever thought we had to give.
  Colonel Marlena Parker represents the very best of our Nation. A 
distinguished graduate of the United States Air Force Academy, she went 
on to fly the KC-10 Extender, one of the most demanding and mission-
critical refueling aircraft in the U.S. military. She deployed to the 
United Arab Emirates in support of Operation Desert Shield and for 
nearly a decade, served on active duty, enabling and protecting 
missions around the world. She is known not only for her exceptional 
skill as a pilot but for her steadfast leadership, grace under 
pressure, and quiet strength.
  Following her transition from active military service to focus on 
family life, Colonel Parker embarked on a notable civilian aviation 
career as a commercial pilot with United Airlines. Throughout this 
professional shift, she persistently demonstrated the discipline, 
composure, and commitment to public service cultivated during her years 
with the United States Air Force. The events of September 11, 2001, 
marked a significant turning point in her career trajectory. While 
piloting an aircraft that day, Colonel Parker was confronted by the 
magnitude of the national crisis and, in response, reaffirmed her 
dedication to public service by seeking once again to contribute to the 
Nation's defense. This critical moment underscores the continuity of 
her commitment across both military and civilian roles.
  She put on her uniform again with the United States Air Force 
Reserves, continuing to fly and command, shaping the careers and lives 
of the airmen entrusted to her. Later she became the first female 
Commander of the 73rd Airlift Squadron at Scott Air Force Base. After 
her 2017 promotion to Colonel, she was appointed Vice Commander at 
Joint Base McGuire-Dix-Lakehurst, overseeing major operations and 
humanitarian missions until her retirement in April 2020, concluding 26 
years of distinguished service. Her commitment to her country was 
unwavering, and so was her dedication to those she led.
  Today, Colonel Parker faces an adversary that no training, no 
aircraft, and no mission briefing can prepare a person for: Amyotrophic 
Lateral Sclerosis, also known as ALS. This unforgiving disease has 
taken from her the ability to fly, a calling that defined much of her 
life. Yet, even in the face of profound physical challenge, her courage 
remains unbroken.
  Colonel Parker's enduring influence upon those around her is rooted 
in her demonstrated resilience, which serves as a powerful source of 
inspiration. The manner in which she confronts adversity, marked by her 
optimism and profound faith, exemplifies that the essence of service 
transcends the quantitative metrics of duration or achievements accrued 
during military tenure. Instead, her persistent resolve and commitment 
illustrate that the true impact of service lies in the integrity, 
compassion, and purpose with which individuals approach every 
responsibility. Whether fulfilling missions in defense of the Nation or 
educating others on the value of gratitude and purposeful living, 
Colonel Parker's resilience reinforces an inspirational model for 
service and leadership.
  The ``Colonel Marlena Parker Inspirational Veteran Award'' will be 
presented every year to a veteran who reflects the character, strength, 
and unwavering spirit embodied by its namesake. It will be presented to 
someone whose life reminds us that service does not end when the 
uniform comes off.
  Colonel Parker's life teaches us that courage is not just found in 
the sky but is found in the everyday choices we make to lead with 
compassion, to persevere through hardship, and to serve others with 
humility and grace.
  On behalf of a grateful community and a grateful Nation, we thank her 
for her service, thank her for her sacrifice, and thank her for her 
inspiration.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that this recognition of Colonel Marlena Parker 
and the establishment of the ``Colonel Marlena Parker Inspirational 
Veteran Award'' be entered into the Record so that her legacy of 
service and courage may endure for generations to come.
