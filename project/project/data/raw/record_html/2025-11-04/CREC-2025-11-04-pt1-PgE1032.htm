                 HONORING MAYOR KENNETH ``KEN'' SUNSERI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. ADERHOLT. Mr. Speaker, today I would like to recognize and honor 
Mayor Kenneth ``Ken'' Sunseri of my hometown of Haleyville, Alabama, as 
he retires after many years of dedicated service to his community. 
Mayor Sunseri has faithfully served as Mayor of Haleyville since 2008, 
providing steady and visionary leadership for the people of our 
community. Throughout his tenure, he has exemplified a spirit of 
service and commitment to the wellbeing of every citizen. His 
leadership has been marked by progress, integrity, and a deep love for 
his hometown.
  Before his time in public office, Mayor Sunseri served his country 
with distinction in the United States Army during the Vietnam War as a 
platoon leader with the First Infantry Division. He went on to complete 
28 years of military service, retiring with the rank of Colonel. His 
leadership and dedication to his soldiers and his country exemplify the 
best of American service.
  Beyond his military career, Mayor Sunseri has been deeply involved in 
every facet of civic life in Haleyville. He has served as President and 
Deputy District Governor of the Haleyville Lions Club, President of the 
Haleyville Chamber of Commerce, President of the Haleyville Merchants 
Association, and as a member of the Haleyville Library Board, Housing 
Authority, and Industrial Recruiting Committee. He has also served on 
the Individual and Family Support Board and as Chairman of the 310 
Board for Winston and Marion Counties.
  In addition to his mayoral duties, he has served on the Haleyville 
Water and Sewer Board since 1986, chairing it since the early 1990's. 
Under his guidance, Haleyville has advanced key infrastructure 
improvements and strengthened its community services.
  Over the years, Mayor Sunseri has received numerous awards 
recognizing his contributions, including: Civitan Citizen of the Year 
(2004); Alabama Communities of Excellence Volunteer of the Year (2005); 
State of Alabama RC&D Council Member of the Year (2017); and 
Outstanding Mayor Award from Alabama Communities of Excellence in both 
2015 and 2024.
  One of his proudest achievements as Mayor was the construction of the 
new Haleyville City Hall, completed in January 2016, which now stands 
as a lasting testament to his leadership and vision for future 
generations.
  Beyond his public service, Mayor Sunseri's greatest source of pride 
has been his family. He and his wife Kay Sunseri have three children--
David Sunseri, Beth Moore, and Jennifer Sunseri--and are the proud 
grandparents of five grandchildren.
  Mr. Speaker, Mayor Kenneth ``Ken'' Sunseri has lived a life devoted 
to faith, family, community, and country. His legacy in Haleyville will 
continue to inspire those who follow him in public service. On behalf 
of the people of Alabama's 4th Congressional District, I extend my 
deepest gratitude and congratulations to Mayor Sunseri for his decades 
of exemplary service and wish him and Kay all the best in the years to 
come.
