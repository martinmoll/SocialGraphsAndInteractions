                      RECOGNIZING JESSE LOVE, JR.

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ADDISON P. McDOWELL

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. McDOWELL. Mr. Speaker, over the past weekend, on November 1, 
2025, Jesse Love, Jr. brought home the 2025 NASCAR Xfinity title from 
Phoenix, Arizona. Love held the lead for the final 24 laps, displaying 
his dominance as he secured his third career victory on his first-ever 
non-superspeedway track.
  At just 20 years old, Love is no stranger to winning and bringing 
back hardware to the stock car racing capital of the world. At age 15, 
he set a record as the youngest winner of the ARCA Menards Series West 
and the youngest winner in any NASCAR-sanctioned race.
  North Carolina is proud of its long heritage in racing. Love drives 
for Richard Childress Racing out of Welcome, North Carolina, a team 
known for its historic caliber of elite racers, from Dale Earnhardt 
claiming six NASCAR Cup Series championships and two Daytona 500s, to 
Austin Dillon bringing home a third Daytona 500 victory in 2018. RCR 
racing continues to represent the pride of North Carolina in the stock 
car racing community around the world.
  I congratulate Love and the entire RCR racing team. The future of 
NASCAR is with the drivers who represent the sport with honor and 
spirit.
