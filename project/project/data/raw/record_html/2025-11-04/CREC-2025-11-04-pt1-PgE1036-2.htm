CELEBRATING THE 100TH ANNIVERSARY OF NEW HOPE MISSIONARY BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                     HON. DEBBIE WASSERMAN SCHULTZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Ms. WASSERMAN SCHULTZ. Mr. Speaker, it is with great enthusiasm that 
I rise to recognize the exceptional work of the New Hope Missionary 
Baptist Church on their 100th Anniversary.
  Founded on a warm Sunday in August 1925, New Hope Baptist Church has 
stood as a steadfast beacon of hope, compassion, and perseverance for 
the people of South Florida. From its earliest days, the church has 
provided not only a place of worship, but a source of strength and 
solidarity for generations of families in Fort Lauderdale.
  Over the past century, New Hope has evolved from a modest wooden 
structure into a vital cornerstone of spiritual and community life in 
Broward County. The church has been shepherded by 13 pastors, each 
adding to its legacy through the establishment of new ministries, 
outreach programs, and community partnerships.
  Under the leadership of current pastor Reverend Ricky Scott, the 
church continues to move forward with a renewed vision for spiritual 
growth and community engagement. New Hope has continually lived out its 
mission to serve the Lord by serving others. Through its ministries and 
outreach the church has fed the hungry, uplifted youth, and comforted 
families in times of need.
  Notably, the church's participation in ``Souls to the Polls'' has 
helped strengthen democracy and amplify the voices of those too often 
unheard. Through efforts like these, New Hope Baptist Church 
demonstrates that faith and civic duty can work hand in hand to build a 
more just and compassionate society.
  As New Hope Baptist Church marks its centennial year, we celebrate 
its unwavering commitment to faith, service, and community empowerment. 
Its century-long legacy stands as a testament to what can be achieved 
when a congregation dedicates itself to love, justice, and hope.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the United States Congress, I extend 
heartfelt congratulations to Pastor Ricky Scott and the entire New Hope 
Baptist Church family for 100 years of ministry and community 
leadership. May the next century be filled with continued blessings, 
growth, and service to all.
