  HONORING SONIA BUSTAMANTE AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Sonia Bustamante, 
recognized as one of California's 8th Congressional District's Women of 
the Year. As Chief of Staff to the Contra Costa County Board of 
Supervisors since 2015, Ms. Bustamante has demonstrated exceptional 
leadership, vision, and commitment to advancing equity and inclusion 
across local government. A dedicated public servant and coalition 
builder, Sonia has led transformative initiatives that strengthen civic 
engagement and expand opportunity for underrepresented communities. Her 
coordination of the 2025 Community Impact Fund Townhalls exemplifies, 
her commitment to accessibility and inclusion, ensuring multilingual 
participation, hybrid access, and youth involvement in the policymaking 
process. Sonia's leadership has been instrumental in advancing key 
county initiatives, including the Guaranteed Income Pilot, immigrant 
legal services, and the African American Wellness Hub, all of which are 
designed to address systemic inequities in health, housing, and 
economic stability. A proud UC Berkeley alumna with a bachelor's degree 
in Sociology and a Master of Public Administration with a concentration 
in Leadership from California State University, San Bernardino, Sonia 
brings both academic rigor and deep community insight to her work. She 
is also a mentor to the next generation of public servants, overseeing 
the county's youth internship program and empowering young leaders to 
pursue careers in civic engagement and public policy. Known for her 
ability to manage complex projects and unite diverse stakeholders, 
Sonia embodies the very spirit of public service, guided by empathy, 
integrity, and action. On behalf of the people of California's 8th 
Congressional District, I am proud to recognize Sonia Bustamante for 
her outstanding leadership, her lifelong dedication to equity and 
justice, and her enduring contributions to the people of Contra Costa 
County.
