                        TRIBUTE TO LORETTA YOUNG

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize the 
extraordinary service and enduring legacy of Ms. Loretta Young, 
Commander of American Legion Post 365. Her steadfast leadership and 
tireless advocacy have shaped the lives of countless veterans and 
community members across South Florida. Ms. Young will be honored in 
Sunrise, Florida, for more than 25 years of distinguished service as 
Commander, the second-longest tenure of any Post Commander in the State 
of Florida.
  Born in 1941 in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, and raised between the 
city and the heartlands of South Jersey, Ms. Young is a proud member of 
the Lenape Nation of New Jersey. Her early years were marked by 
academic excellence and a deep sense of civic duty, inspired by her 
father, a retired Philadelphia firefighter. That sense of duty led her 
to enlist in the United States Air Force, where she served with honor 
at Travis Air Force Base in California, achieving the rank of A3C in 
Administration and Base Personnel.
  Since assuming command of Post 365 in the year 2000, Ms. Young has 
exemplified the values of patriotism, leadership, and service. Her 
tenure includes roles as Post Historian, Service Officer, Executive 
Officer, and 9th District Commander. She has served on national 
committees for Legislative Affairs and National Security, and chaired 
the Department's JROTC and Junior Shooting Sports Program for over two 
decades. Her leadership has extended far beyond the American Legion, 
touching civic institutions, youth organizations, and advisory boards 
throughout Broward County. Ms. Young's community engagement is both 
vast and impactful. She co-founded the JROTC Support Association in 
2006 and currently serves as its President. She has chaired the 23rd 
Congressional Academy Board for the past ten years and was appointed to 
the Veterans Advisory Board in 2015. Her commitment to youth, veterans, 
and civic education has earned her recognition as a Pioneer Day 
Recipient by the City of Sunrise and induction into the Broward Senior 
Hall of Fame in 2015.
  Her journey of service began in earnest after retiring from American 
Airlines in 1995,

[[Page E1038]]

when she began volunteering at assisted living facilities and later co-
founded the Professional Women Veterans Association, the first 
nonprofit in Broward County dedicated to female veterans. Through 
workshops, retreats, and outreach, she helped elevate the voices and 
needs of women in uniform and played a formative role in the early 
development of Mission United, a program supporting homeless veterans. 
Ms. Young's story is one of resilience, vision, and unwavering 
commitment. She is a global traveler, a devoted mother and grandmother, 
and a beacon of optimism who sees the glass half full. Her life 
reflects a profound dedication to service, safety, and the belief that 
every challenge is an opportunity to uplift others.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Loretta Young for her 
decades of leadership, advocacy, and community-building. Her legacy is 
etched into the fabric of South Florida and serves as a powerful 
reminder of the impact one individual can have when guided by purpose, 
compassion, and a deep love for country and community.
