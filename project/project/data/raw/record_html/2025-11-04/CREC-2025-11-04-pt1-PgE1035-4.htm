HONORING SENIOR MASTER SERGEANT SALUD ASAELI AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH 
               CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of SMSgt. Salud Asaeli, as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year for her 
distinguished leadership, mentorship, and advocacy that exemplify the 
highest standards of dedication, compassion, and resilience. Previously 
as First Sergeant for both the 60th Healthcare Operations Squadron and 
the 60th Medical Group Staff at Travis Air Force Base, SMSgt. Asaeli 
supported the well-being of over 600 Airmen and civilians. Her 
commitment extends beyond duty hours, as she provides 24/7 leadership 
to address quality of life, morale, health, and readiness issues. From 
September 2024 to April 2025, she served with distinction as First 
Sergeant for the 379th Expeditionary Security Forces Squadron at Al 
Udeid Air Base, Qatar, offering steadfast support to deployed personnel 
during a critical mission period. At Travis AFB, SMSgt. Asaeli also 
serves as Senior Mentor to the First Sergeant Council, guiding 27 First

[[Page E1036]]

Sergeants responsible for over 8,000 Airmen and their families, 
demonstrating her far-reaching influence and leadership. Her dedication 
to service is equally strong in the community. For over four years, she 
has led Operation Warm Heart, a nonprofit that provides emergency 
financial support to military families. As both President and Secretary 
of the executive council, she has helped raise more than $148,000, 
organized school supply drives, coordinated the Gold Star Family Ruck 
March, and supported local shelters and memorial associations. Her 
commitment is both personal and professional. Having experienced the 
challenges of deployment and reintegration in her own family, SMSgt. 
Asaeli has used those lessons to mentor and uplift others navigating 
similar hardships. Her strength, empathy, and leadership continue to 
shape lives and strengthen bonds across the military community. Her 
more than two decades of military service, selfless leadership, and 
profound impact on others make her a true inspiration and a deserving 
honoree. On behalf of the people of California's 8th Congressional 
District, it is my pleasure to honor and recognize SMSgt. Salud Asaeli 
for her invaluable contributions to our country and to her community.
