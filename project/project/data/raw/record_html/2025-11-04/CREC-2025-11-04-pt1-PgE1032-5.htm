                 HONORING ST. BENEDICT CATHOLIC CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GREG STANTON

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. STANTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize St. Benedict Catholic 
Church, a mainstay

[[Page E1033]]

of the Ahwatukee community, as it celebrates its 40-year anniversary. 
St. Benedict has helped families live out the Gospel through worship 
and service for four decades.
  Ahwatukee began to grow rapidly in the mid-1980's. The community was 
still discovering its identity as new families moved in and schools and 
neighborhoods took shape. In an era of great change, St. Benedict 
became a spiritual home for the people who built Ahwatukee. It offered 
a place for neighbors to gather, raise their children in faith, and 
support one another through service.
  St. Benedict Catholic Church began in 1985, holding its first Mass 
with 79 families. As the parish grew, it moved to Ahwatukee in 2003, 
next to the newly established St. John Bosco Catholic School, which 
became one of its key ministries.
  Today, under Father Manasseh Iorchir, St. Benedict serves more than 
2,000 families today and continues to grow, including liturgical and 
outreach programs, youth formation, and volunteer groups such as the 
St. Vincent de Paul Society and Knights of Columbus. Its new church 
building, dedicated in 2024, stands as a testament to the faith, 
perseverance, and generosity of its parishioners.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating St. 
Benedict Catholic Church on this remarkable milestone. Its 40 years of 
faith and service have strengthened Ahwatukee and enriched the lives of 
families in Arizona.
