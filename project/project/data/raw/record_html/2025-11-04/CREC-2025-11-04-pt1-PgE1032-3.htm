   HONORING SHELMAE TURNER AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, November 4, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Shelmae Turner, who is being honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year for her 
outstanding service and unwavering dedication to the Solano County 
community for over 50 years. Ms. Turner has long been a steadfast 
advocate for young people, a cultural ambassador, and a committed 
community servant. Her work has ensured that essential resources are 
accessible to those in need, and her impact is deeply felt across 
generations. For more than 25 years, Ms. Turner has been an active 
member of the Chinese American Association of Solano County, an 
organization with deep personal meaning to her since her father was one 
of its founding members. In the past decade, she has served as 
Secretary of CAASC, continuing her family's legacy of service and civic 
involvement. Under her leadership, CAASC has awarded scholarships to 
409 students. Ms. Turner has also played a key role in promoting 
cultural awareness and diversity in Solano County. She has organized 
Chinese New Year and Lunar New Year celebrations and ensured CAASC's 
active participation in local events such as the Vacaville Fiesta Days 
Parade and the Fairfield 4th of July Parade, uplifting Asian American 
voices and traditions in the community. In addition to her cultural 
work, Ms. Turner has dedicated over 13 years to Fairfield High School, 
her alma mater, as the Associated Student Body Treasurer. Her efforts 
have allowed countless students to experience safe and memorable 
events, from proms to club activities, ensuring that every student 
feels valued and supported. Recognizing the growing needs of students 
and families, Ms. Turner has also volunteered for the past 10 years to 
manage Fairfield High School's clothing closet and food pantry. Thanks 
to her dedication, up to 20 individuals a day receive clothing, 
supplies, and food. Ms. Turner represents the very best of our 
community. Her legacy of compassion, inclusion, and service will 
continue to inspire generations to come. On behalf of the people of 
California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor and 
recognize Shelmae Turner for her invaluable contributions to our 
community.
