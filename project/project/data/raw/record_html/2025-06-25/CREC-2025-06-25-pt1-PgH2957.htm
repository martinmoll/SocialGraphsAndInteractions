XVIII, the Chair declares the House in the Committee of the Whole House

                     In the Committee of the Whole

Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 3944) making appropriations for military construction, 
the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related agencies for the fiscal 
year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes, with Mr. Taylor

amendment No. 21, printed in House Report 119-167, offered by the 
gentleman from Ohio (Mr. Joyce), had been disposed of.
