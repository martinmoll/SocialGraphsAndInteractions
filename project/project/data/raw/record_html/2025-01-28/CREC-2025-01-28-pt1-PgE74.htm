              HONORING THE LEGACY AND SERVICE OF BILL HOSE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 28, 2025

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor retired Sheriff 
William ``Bill'' Hose for his legacy of service to the York community.
  Mr. Hose spent 29 years as a York City Police Officer, advancing to 
the Chief of Police in his last 11 years of service. He then served as 
York County Sheriff between 1996 and 2007, finishing a 37-year-long 
career in law enforcement, protecting the York community.
  Mr. Hose's impact extends beyond his professional career. In his 
spare time, he has volunteered with crime prevention and youth services 
nonprofits in York County. He is also a U.S. Air Force Veteran, having 
been stationed in Berlin during the Berlin Crisis.
  For his impactful record of service, the community thanks and honors 
Sheriff Bill Hose. York County residents should be proud to have such 
an outstanding model of selfless service in their community.
