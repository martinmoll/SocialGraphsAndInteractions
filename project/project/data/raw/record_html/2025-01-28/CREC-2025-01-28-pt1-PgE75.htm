       HONORING GARRISON McMURTREY'S SERVICE AS DISTRICT DIRECTOR

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANGIE CRAIG

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 28, 2025

  Ms. CRAIG. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to thank and congratulate 
Garrison McMurtrey for serving for over two years as my District 
Director.
  Garrison is a dedicated public servant with a passion for workforce 
development and economic justice, with a focus on equity and community 
engagement.
  Garrison started his career working on campaign field teams, went on 
to work as an Outreach Director for Senator Klobuchar and spent time in 
corporate public affairs. Hopefully by the time he sees this, he's now 
Ramsey County's newest Commissioner.
  Garrison has been invaluable to my team. All with a person-centered 
approach, he assisted in elevating the work carried out in the district 
office. He instituted new casework initiatives, ensuring accessibility 
and reliability. He operationalized outreach strategies to foster 
meaningful relationships with community partners.
  I've appreciated Garrison's leadership and support during his time 
with my office. I will miss his dedication and infectious laughter. I'm 
certain that Garrison will find success in this next chapter and I know 
he'll remain committed to serving the public for the better.
