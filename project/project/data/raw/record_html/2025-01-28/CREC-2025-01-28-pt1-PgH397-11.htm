                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                 January 24, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Pursuant to Section 4 of the United 
     States Semiquincentennial Commission Act of 2016 (Pub. L. 
     114-196), I am pleased to appoint the following Members to 
     serve as Commissioners to the United States 
     Semiquincentennial Commission:
       The Honorable Bonnie Watson Coleman of New Jersey.
       The Honorable Dwight Evans of Pennsylvania.
       Thank you for your attention to these recommendations.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
                                                Democratic Leader.
