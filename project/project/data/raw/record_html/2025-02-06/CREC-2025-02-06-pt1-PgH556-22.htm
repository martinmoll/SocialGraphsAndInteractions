            By Mr. COLLINS:
        H.R. 1057.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article 1, Section 8, Clause 3 of the U.S. Constitution 
     empowers Congress to regulate commerce between the states, 
     which includes criminal law relating to safe passage along 
     interstate highways.
