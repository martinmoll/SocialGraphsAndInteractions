                  CONGRATULATING THE GORDON LONGHORNS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair recognizes the gentleman from 
Texas (Mr. Williams) for 5 minutes.
  Mr. WILLIAMS of Texas. Madam Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
the Gordon Longhorns for their win over the Whiteface Antelopes with a 
score of 70-24 to become the UIL Class 1A Division 1 State Football 
Champions.
  The Gordon Longhorns finished the season with a perfect record of 15 
wins and 0 losses. After a season of early mornings, long hours on the 
field, and staying focused in school, these student athletes and 
trainers and coaches showed dedication, strength, and an unwavering 
commitment to victory. I am proud that all their hard work and 
dedication throughout the year has paid off.
  As some of these bright and talented individuals prepare for their 
next endeavors, I wish them the best of luck and blessings for an even 
brighter and more victorious future. Go Longhorns. In God we trust.
