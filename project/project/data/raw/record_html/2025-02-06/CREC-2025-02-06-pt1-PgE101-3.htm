         RECOGNIZING MARK PEMBLETON FOR HIS COURAGEOUS ACTIONS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. GERALD E. CONNOLLY

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, February 6, 2025

  Mr. CONNOLLY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an extraordinary 
individual, Mark Pembleton, a Navy veteran whose courage, selflessness, 
and dedication to service exemplify the very best of our Nation's 
values.
  Mark proudly served our country as a member of the U.S. Navy Seabees, 
embodying their motto, ``We Build, We Fight.'' Upon retiring from the 
Navy, Mark and his close friend Joe founded Veteran Built Homes, LLC on 
October 31, 2017.
  What began as a small operation with just one truck and three 
employees has grown into a thriving business, specializing in 
residential remodeling with a fleet of seven trucks and a team of 35 
hardworking individuals. Through determination and leadership, Mark and 
Joe built a company that not only provides high-quality craftsmanship 
but also creates opportunities for veterans and skilled workers in the 
community.
  However, it is not only Mark's service in uniform or his 
contributions as a business owner that define his character, but also 
his profound heroism.
  One evening, while driving home from his children's ice-skating 
practice, Mark witnessed a horrific accident in which a car struck a 
semi-truck, causing it to overturn and burst into flames. Without 
hesitation, Mark pulled over, instructed his children to stay in the 
car, and ran toward the burning wreckage. Mark risked his own life, 
running into the flames again and again, until he was able to pull the 
man from the fire to safety.
  Mark suffered severe injuries from his heroic rescue, requiring 
multiple surgeries and a long recovery. Yet, true to his resilient 
nature, he has continued to lead and inspire those around him.
  Mark Pembleton represents the very best of what it means to be an 
American, a warrior, a builder, a leader, and a selfless protector of 
others. His unwavering commitment to service, whether on the 
battlefield, in the workforce, or in an unexpected moment of crisis, is 
a testament to the values we hold dear.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring and 
commending Mark Pembleton for his extraordinary heroism and service. He 
is a true inspiration, and we are forever grateful for his 
contributions to our Nation and our community.
