                     HONORING VETERAN GLENN HOFFNER

  (Mr. McDOWELL asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. McDOWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor an extraordinary 
North Carolinian, Mr. Glenn Hoffner of Salisbury, who recently 
celebrated his 100th birthday.
  Mr. Hoffner proudly served our Nation in World War II with 
distinction.
  After the war, he came home, raised a family, and spent his life 
working hard and giving back to his community.
  The local VFW Post 3006 held a celebration recognizing not only the 
rare achievement of a century well lived but also a legacy of heroism 
in defense of our liberties.
  It was an outpouring of gratitude for a man who truly embodies 
service and sacrifice. The Greatest Generation continues to inspire 
those who follow.
  With red, white, and blue flowing through Mr. Hoffner's veins, his 
life reminds us of the values that America stands for.

[[Page H3834]]

  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of North Carolina's Sixth Congressional 
District, I thank Glenn Hoffner for his service. It is an honor to 
represent a true hero.
