                   CELEBRATING GRANNY'S 80TH BIRTHDAY

  (Mrs. McCLAIN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today for a very important reason.
  Today is my mom's birthday, and I want to wish my mom a happy 80th 
birthday. Her name is Carolyn, but if you are in the Ninth District, 
you probably know her as Granny.
  Let me tell you a little bit about Granny. She is tougher than any 
whip count, and she is more disciplined than running the House floor. I 
will tell you that is saying something up here.
  Let me tell you what you really need to know about my mom. She is the 
life of every party at 80 years young, and she lives every day to its 
fullest. She taught me how to work hard, to listen more than I should 
speak, to never ever back down, and to believe in myself. She is the 
very reason that, today, I am standing where I am standing.
  She is the heart of our family, a role model for our grandkids, and 
the woman who reminds me every single day of why I came up here: to 
fight for the people of my district, the Ninth District.
  Mr. Speaker, I wish my mom a happy birthday. I love her more than 
words can say, and I can't wait to get home and celebrate with her.
