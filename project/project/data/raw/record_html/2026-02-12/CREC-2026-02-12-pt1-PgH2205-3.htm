               HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF DENIS TROY

  (Mr. LAWLER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LAWLER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of my good 
friend and a dedicated public servant, Denis Troy, who passed away at 
the age of 78 from a courageous battle with cancer.
  A proud Bronx native and Manhattan College alumnus, Denis and his 
wife, Bea, lived in Pearl River for over 34 years, raising their four 
children and constantly giving back to their community.
  Denis was one of those people who did not just serve his community. 
He lived for it. From his years on the Orangetown Board and in the 
county legislature, to the countless nights and weekends he gave to 
youth sports; veterans; and local organizations, including his beloved 
AOH Division III, Denis showed us what real public service looked like 
by always putting his community first.
  Beyond the titles and accomplishments, Denis was simply a good man. 
He was generous with his time, steady in his values, and always 
supportive and encouraging, especially to those of us who were lucky 
enough to call him a friend. Orangetown and Pearl River are better 
places because of Denis, and his legacy will never be forgotten.
  My heartfelt condolences go out to his family and all who had the 
pleasure of knowing him. I thank Denis for his many years of mentorship 
and friendship.
  Denis, I will miss you. You are loved.
