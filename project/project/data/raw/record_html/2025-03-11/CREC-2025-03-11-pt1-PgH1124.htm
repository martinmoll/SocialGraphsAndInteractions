              PANDEMIC UNEMPLOYMENT FRAUD ENFORCEMENT ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 1156) to 
amend the CARES Act to extend the statute of limitations for fraud 
under certain unemployment programs, and for other purposes, on which 
the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 295, 
nays 127, not voting 10, as follows:
