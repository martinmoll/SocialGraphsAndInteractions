                  WHAT IS IN THE CONTINUING RESOLUTION

  (Mr. LATIMER asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LATIMER. Mr. Speaker, the House just voted to pass a radical, 
partisan government funding bill. I voted ``no'' because it lets Elon 
Musk decide how this money is going to be spent by Federal agencies.
  There is a lot wrong with this bill.
  This bill fails to lower the cost of housing and instead cuts rent 
subsidies for low-income Americans, including seniors and domestic 
violence survivors, by $700 million.
  This enables President Trump and Elon Musk to cancel transportation 
infrastructure projects already underway. These projects are reducing 
traffic congestion and improving subway systems.
  This reduces the emergency food program by $20 million at a time when 
food prices are on the rise.
  This allows Musk and Trump to fire thousands of employees at the 
Social Security Administration.
  It cuts funding for healthcare for veterans who are exposed to 
toxins, and so much more that is worse.
  We needed a bill that protects the middle class and makes life less 
expensive for residents in Westchester and the Bronx. This continuing 
resolution is the opposite of that. Remember, I told you so.
