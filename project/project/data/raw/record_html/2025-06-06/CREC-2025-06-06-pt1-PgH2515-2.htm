 COMPOSITION OF MEMBERS OF PERMANENT SELECT COMMITTEE ON INTELLIGENCE 
                       DURING THE 119TH CONGRESS

  Ms. FOXX. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that notwithstanding 
the requirement of clause 11(a)(1) of rule X, during the 119th 
Congress, the Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence be composed of 
not more than 27 Members, of whom not more than 15 be from the same 
party.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Schmidt). Is there objection to the 
request of the gentlewoman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
