                  HONORING CARLSBAD MAYOR DALE JANWAY

  (Mr. VASQUEZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. VASQUEZ. Madam Speaker, I rise to honor my friend and an 
honorable New Mexican, Carlsbad Mayor Dale Janway, who passed away on 
December 30, 2024.
  Mayor Janway served Carlsbad with integrity and distinction as mayor 
for 13 years. He was deeply involved in his community and made 
remarkable progress during his tenure. He worked with leaders of all 
political stripes to improve the city's infrastructure, including the 
Double Eagle water project, which we worked on together.
  Although we came from different parties, Mayor Janway was a good 
friend and was always willing to work together for the good of his 
community. He had a passion for educating youth so that they would 
become thoughtful, engaged citizens. He helped to create jobs, improved 
the lives of Carlsbad families, and protected critical industries.
  The mayor also proudly served his country in the Army National Guard. 
Mayor Janway exemplified leadership, leaving behind a legacy that 
reminds us all to serve with kindness and with purpose.
  I thank Mayor Janway for his service.
