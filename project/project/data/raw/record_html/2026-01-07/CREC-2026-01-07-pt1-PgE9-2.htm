                      HONORING TRACEY ANN TIPPETT

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 7, 2026

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate Ms. Tracey 
Ann Tippett on being named Michigan Marine of the Year and on her 
induction into the Oscar G. Johnson VA Medical Center Hall of Heroes. 
This well-deserved honor reflects her steadfast dedication to Veterans, 
our Nation, and the communities throughout Northern Michigan and the 
Upper Peninsula.
  Ms. Tippett served honorably as a heavy equipment mechanic in the 
United States Marine Corps, attaining the rank of Sergeant. Throughout 
her distinguished military career, she was awarded the Good Conduct 
Medal, National Defense Service Medal, Rifle Marksmanship Badge, as 
well as a Letter of Appreciation and Certificate of Appreciation. Her 
unwavering commitment to her fellow Marines and Veterans has not 
wavered since returning from her service.
  Since being honorably discharged in October of 1992, Ms. Tippett has 
devoted her time and energy to serving Veterans and strengthening her 
community. While it would be difficult to fully capture the scope of 
her service, her leadership and dedication are evident across Michigan. 
Recently, she was elected as Commandant of the Michigan Marine Corps 
League, an organization that supports and unites Marines from both the 
past and present. Notably, Ms. Tippett is only the second woman to hold 
this position, serving as a powerful inspiration to young women who 
aspire to serve our Nation.
  In addition to her work with Veterans, Ms. Tippett has been a 
constant presence in her community, supporting programs like Toys for 
Tots, the Marquette and Ishpeming Christmas parades, Negaunee Pioneer 
Days, and Trinity Lutheran Church. Her actions within her community 
have been recognized on multiple occasions, including being named 2022 
Upper Peninsula Veteran of the Year and 2021 State of Michigan Veteran 
of the Year.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in congratulating Ms. Tracey Ann Tippett 
on earning the distinguished honor of being named Michigan Marine of 
the Year and on her induction into the Oscar G. Johnson VA Medical 
Center Hall of Heroes. I thank her for her enduring commitment to 
Veterans, her community, and our great country.
