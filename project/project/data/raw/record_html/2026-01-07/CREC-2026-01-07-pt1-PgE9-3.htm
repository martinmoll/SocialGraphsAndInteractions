 HONORING THE LIFE AND SERVICE OF MRS. JEAN BREWER OF MARSHALL COUNTY, 
                                ALABAMA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. ROBERT B. ADERHOLT

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 7, 2026

  Mr. ADERHOLT. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and pay tribute to a 
remarkable leader, devoted patriot, and tireless advocate for 
conservative values in the great State of Alabama, Mrs. Jean Brewer of 
Marshall County.
  Jean Brewer's life has been one defined by service to our Nation, her 
community, and her beloved state. Upon graduating from DAR High School 
in Grant, Alabama, Jean embarked on her first professional journey with 
the Federal Bureau of Investigation in Maryland, where she served with 
distinction early in her career.
  Jean was married to the late Don Brewer, whose service in the United 
States Air Force took their family around the world. Jean worked 
faithfully alongside her husband after he enlisted and earned his 
commission as an officer; she served as a liaison to other military 
spouses and providing support, encouragement, and leadership to 
families navigating the demands of military life. Together, Jean and 
Don raised three children, Jennifer, Renea, and Roger, instilling in 
them the same values of faith, service, and love of country that have 
guided their own lives.
  Following Don's retirement from the Air Force, the Brewer family 
returned home to Grant, Alabama. Never content to rest after years of 
service, Jean became deeply involved in local civic and political life. 
Her leadership and dedication were recognized by her community when she 
was elected and served for several years as President of the Marshall 
County Republican Party, where she worked tirelessly to strengthen 
grassroots involvement and uphold conservative principles.
  Jean Brewer loves this state, she loves this country, and she has 
consistently worked to preserve and restore the values that so many 
Alabamians hold dear. Through her leadership, mentorship, and steadfast 
commitment to service, she has left a lasting impact on Marshall County 
and beyond.
  As the Member of Congress from the 4th Congressional District of 
Alabama, it is my privilege to recognize Jean's lifetime of service and 
to commend her for her devotion to family, community, and country.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Mrs. Jean Brewer and 
extending our deepest appreciation for her many contributions.
  God bless Jean Brewer, and God bless the United States of America.
