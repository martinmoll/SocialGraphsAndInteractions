        RECOGNIZING THE DEDICATED PUBLIC SERVICE OF BETTY BUCHER

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOHN JOYCE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Wednesday, January 7, 2026

  Mr. JOYCE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the 
dedicated public service of Betty Bucher, who recently concluded a 16-
year tenure as a member of Littlestown Borough Council in Adams County.
  During the December meeting of the borough council, Betty was honored 
for the steady and reliable leadership that she provided to the 
community.
  Serving in the role of Council Vice President, Betty was presented a 
proclamation that highlighted her service on the borough's union and 
police contract committee and a design committee that facilitated the 
creation of a mural for the new borough building.
  Betty's servant leadership in the borough stretches from the mid-
1960s, when she was instrumental in the creation and early operation of 
the former Littlestown Swimming Pool.
  With professionalism and integrity over the last 16 years, Betty has 
most certainly personified the ideals of representative governance.
  On behalf of constituents throughout Pennsylvania's 13th 
Congressional District, I thank Betty for her faithful public service.
