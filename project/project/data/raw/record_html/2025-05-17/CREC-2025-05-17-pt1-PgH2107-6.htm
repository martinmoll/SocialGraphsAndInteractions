              JOINT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on May 16, 2025, 
the following joint resolutions were presented to the President of the 
United States for approval.

       H.J. Res. 61. Providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Environmental Protection Agency relating to 
     ``National Emission Standards for Hazardous Air Pollutants: 
     Rubber Tire Manufacturing''
       H.J. Res. 60. Providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the National Park Service relating to ``Glen 
     Canyon National Recreation Area: Motor Vehicles''
