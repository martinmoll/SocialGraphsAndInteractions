                                 PRAYER

  The Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, 
D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, our great country is in Your hands. We thank You for 
all that You have blessed us with and pray that we might be good 
stewards of these blessings. Look with favor upon the public servants 
that represent us in Congress, as well as their staffs and families. 
Grant them clarity, foresight, and mutual support as they work together 
to lead this land. It is with humble and grateful hearts that we 
beseech You.
  Amen.
