     HONORING THE MEMORY OF FOUNTAIN VALLEY FIRE CHIEF BILL MCQUAID

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DEREK TRAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 15, 2025

  Mr. TRAN. Mr. Speaker, it is with solemn remembrance that I rise 
today to pay tribute to a beloved husband, devoted father, local 
leader, and selfless public servant, Fountain Valley Fire Department 
Chief Bill McQuaid. Chief McQuaid, a fixture in the Fountain Valley 
community, passed away much too soon on July 25, 2025, at the age of 
57.
  A native of Fountain Valley, California, Chief McQuaid was a proud 
graduate of Fountain Valley High School and entered the workforce with 
a dream of joining the fire service. In 1996, his dream was realized 
when he joined the Fountain Valley Fire Department, where he stayed for 
nearly 30 years. Chief McQuaid held numerous positions over the course 
of his career, including firefighter/paramedic, fire engineer, fire 
captain, battalion chief, and division chief of operations, before 
ultimately being named chief in January 2023.
  Throughout his life, Chief McQuaid served the city of Fountain Valley 
with unwavering integrity and commitment. He often spoke about how his 
role as chief was not just his job, but his calling to serve the people 
of his hometown. In addition to the incalculable support Chief McQuaid 
lent to his native city, he also faithfully served the state of 
California. Over the years before becoming chief, McQuaid led multiple 
teams of firefighters as a strike team leader to battle some of the 
largest wildfires in the state's history, including the Thomas fire in 
Ventura and Santa Barbara counties, and the Dixie fire in Northern 
California.
  I am grateful to have had the opportunity to work with Chief McQuaid 
several times during my tenure in Congress, and I got to see firsthand 
the level of respect and compassion his city had for him. Chief 
McQuaid's loss has been felt deeply across our Fountain Valley 
community, and the impact he has made on both his department and his 
hometown will never be forgotten.
  Chief McQuaid is survived by his wife, Tracy, and their daughters, 
Ally and Julie. I have requested that the United States flag be flown 
over our Nation's Capital in recognition of Chief McQuaid's life and 
contributions to his community. Chief McQuaid will be dearly missed by 
his friends and family, and his legacy will continue in the minds of 
everyone who has met him.
