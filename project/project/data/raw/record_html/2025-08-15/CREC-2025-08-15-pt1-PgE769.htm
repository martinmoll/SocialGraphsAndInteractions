 RECOGNIZING BUCKINGHAM TOWNSHIP POLICE DEPARTMENT LIEUTENANT WILLIAM 
                     MOFFETT'S 35 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 15, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize an exceptional 
individual from my district, Lieutenant William Moffett of the 
Buckingham Township Police Department. Today, I proudly commend him on 
his well-earned retirement, following decades of committed service to 
the people of Bucks County.
  Lieutenant Moffett's distinguished career in public service began in 
1981, when he served as an EMT with the Bucks County Rescue Squad and 
Bensalem Rescue Squad. Two years later, he joined the Bucks County 
Department of Communications as a Police, Fire, and EMS dispatcher, 
demonstrating his deep commitment to public safety.
  In 1987, Lieutenant Moffett transitioned to a career in law 
enforcement as a Deputy Sheriff with the Bucks County's Sheriff's 
Department. Then, in 1990, he joined the Buckingham Township Police 
Department, where he would serve for the next 35 years.
  He began his service as an Officer and quickly distinguished himself 
through exceptional dedication and leadership. Throughout his tenure, 
he earned multiple promotions, rising through the ranks of Officer, 
Corporal, Sergeant, and ultimately Lieutenant. In each role, Lieutenant 
Moffett brought integrity and professionalism.
  Notably, Lieutenant Moffett played a major role in implementing a new 
record management system, obtaining body-worn and in-car dash cameras, 
and acquiring taser devices for the department. His efforts helped to 
modernize the force and enhance public safety.
  Beyond these contributions, he has shared his vast experiences and 
leadership abilities with generations of the department's officers. 
Above all, Lieutenant Moffett's pride in serving the residents of Bucks 
County was always evident--so much so that this legacy continues, with 
all three of his sons now serving as police officers in communities 
across the county.
  After a long and honorable career in law enforcement, Lieutenant 
Moffett looks forward to a well-deserved retirement.
  Mr. Speaker, I extend my heartfelt congratulations to Lieutenant 
William Moffett on his retirement and thank him for 44 years of 
distinguished service to the citizens of Bucks County. His leadership 
and dedication will leave a lasting mark on the community he so 
faithfully served.
