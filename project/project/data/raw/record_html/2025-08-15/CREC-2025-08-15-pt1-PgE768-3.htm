                   HONORING OFFICER LARRY BLACKSMITH

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. RANDY K. WEBER, SR.

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 15, 2025

  Mr. WEBER of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heavy heart to 
honor the life and legacy of Officer Larry Blacksmith of the Lakeview 
Police Department. On July 15, 2025, while on his way to protect and 
serve the people of Lakeview, Officer Blacksmith was called home to be 
with our Lord.
  Officer Blacksmith lived a life defined by duty, service, and 
sacrifice. A proud veteran of our United States military, he later 
retired as a deputy with the Harris County Sheriffs Office. But even in 
retirement, his calling to serve never faded. He answered that call 
once more by joining the Lakeview Police Department, continuing his 
mission to protect his fellow citizens.
  But he was more than his uniform and badge. He served with heart. He 
mentored others with patience and purpose, encouraging young men and 
women to walk the noble path of public service. He had a calm, steady 
hand and a clear voice, always leading with grace, even in the toughest 
of circumstances.
  Over the course of his remarkable 45-year career in law enforcement, 
Officer Blacksmith treated everyone he encountered with dignity, 
compassion, and respect. He stood firm in his convictions and lived out 
the values represented by the badge, integrity, courage, and honor.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that this Chamber join me in lifting up Officer 
Blacksmith's family and the entire Lakeview law enforcement community 
in prayer. We mourn this tremendous loss, and we thank God for the life 
of this good and faithful servant. May his legacy inspire others to 
serve with the same dedication and heart.
