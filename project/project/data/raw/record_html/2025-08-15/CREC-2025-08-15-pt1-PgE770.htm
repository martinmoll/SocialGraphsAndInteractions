                  HONORING THE LIFE OF STEPHEN WALLIN

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 15, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, it is with a heavy heart and solemn 
remembrance that I rise today to pay tribute to a passionate community 
educator, devoted public servant, and deeply committed family man--Mr. 
Stephen Wallin.
  With sorrow, we honor Stephen following his passing on June 14, 2025.
  Stephen was born on January 8, 1953, in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania to 
his loving parents Alexander Wallin and Dorothy Juraky Wallin. He was a 
lifelong resident of Upper Southampton Township and a proud graduate of 
William Tennent High School. He furthered his education at Cambridge 
International University, earning a Bachelor of Science with 
distinction in Education Administration.
  As the President and CEO of the Bucks County School of Beauty Culture 
in Feasterville, Pennsylvania, Stephen provided elite beauty education 
to his community for nearly five decades. By preparing students for 
careers as hairdressers, estheticians, nail technicians, and 
electrologists, he championed a holistic approach to beauty culture--
one that emphasized its artistic and scientific qualities. Under his 
leadership, the school recruited high-quality faculty who, in turn, 
produced high-achieving graduates.
  Beyond his role as an educator, Stephen dedicated himself to public 
service through a variety of political and civic roles. In Upper 
Southampton Township, he contributed as an Auditor, a member of the 
Municipal Authority, and a member of the Board of Supervisors. His 
commitment extended further, with roles on the Pennsylvania State 
Cosmetology Board, the Board of the Pennsylvania Private School 
Administrators, the Upper Southampton Republican Club, and as President 
of the Lower Bucks County Chamber of Commerce. In each of these 
positions, Stephen exemplified a deep and unwavering commitment to his 
community--always striving to support the people around him.
  Above all, Stephen was a devoted family man. He will be lovingly 
remembered by his wife, Gayle R. Kennedy Wallin; his son, Scott Wallin; 
his daughter and son-in-law, Melissa and Matthew Neamand; and his son 
and daughter-in-law, Ryan and Megan Wallin. He was also a beloved 
brother, grandfather, and uncle.
  Stephen found joy in various endeavors--especially fishing and 
sharing his knowledge of firearms at the gun range. These were not just 
hobbies, but opportunities for connection and teaching.
  Mr. Stephen Wallin was a remarkable member of my district, and it is 
with deep respect that I honor his memory today. I send my heartfelt 
condolences to his family, friends, and all who had the privilege of 
knowing him.
