                WELCOMING GUEST CHAPLAIN DR. KEVIN MOORE

  The SPEAKER. Without objection, the gentleman from Alabama (Mr. 
Strong) is recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. STRONG. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to introduce the senior 
pastor, Dr. Kevin Moore, this morning to offer the opening prayer of 
the United States House of Representatives 119th Congress.
  Dr. Moore is my home pastor at Mount Zion Baptist Church. Mount Zion 
was founded in 1855 in the small community affectionately known as 
Monrovia. It is located a couple of miles north of Redstone Arsenal and 
Research Park.
  Mount Zion has grown to over 2,500 members in the thriving 
Huntsville, Alabama, Rocket City, USA. Dr. Moore is a humble and kind 
leader. Our community is very blessed to have him lead our growing 
church.
  Dr. Moore is married to Stephanie, and they have four children: Kevin 
James, David Moses, Liza Grace, and May Louise. I welcome Dr. Moore to 
the United States House of Representatives.
