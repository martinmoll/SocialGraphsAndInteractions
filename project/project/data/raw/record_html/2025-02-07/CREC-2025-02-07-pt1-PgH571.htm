              GOLDEN AGE OF AMERICAN FISCAL RESPONSIBILITY

  (Mr. STUTZMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. STUTZMAN. Mr. Speaker, we, the people, are tired of watching 
billions of our hard-earned tax dollars being sent overseas carelessly, 
and we are tired of the lack of transparency that surrounds those 
decisions. The American people voted for accountability, and they voted 
to keep the government in check.
  The legacy media has painted Elon Musk as a tyrant and President 
Trump as a deceitful puppet master. Their attacks lean on the premise 
that Elon Musk was never elected by the American people.
  My response to them is: How many people elected Kamala Harris to be 
the Democratic nominee for President? How many people elected those 
woke bureaucrats who fraudulently doled out billions to foreign lands, 
even terrorist organizations possibly? Every dollar they wasted was 
earned off the sweat of the brow of the American taxpayer.
  Elon Musk, President Trump, and the Department of Government 
Efficiency have done more to save tax dollars for the American people 
in the past month than I have seen government do in decades. Instead of 
running them through the mud, they should be celebrated in the streets. 
The golden age of American fiscal responsibility is upon us, and I, for 
one, am excited to join the ride.
