  APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO THE COMMISSION TO STUDY THE POTENTIAL 
  CREATION OF A NATIONAL MUSEUM OF ASIAN PACIFIC AMERICAN HISTORY AND 
                              CULTURE ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces the Speaker's 
appointment, pursuant to section 2(b)(2) of the Commission to Study the 
Potential Creation of a National Museum of Asian Pacific American 
History and Culture Act (Public Law 117-140), and the order of the 
House of January 3, 2025, of the following individual on the part of 
the House to the Commission to Study the Potential Creation of a 
National Museum of Asian Pacific American History and Culture to fill 
the existing vacancy thereon:
  Ms. Michelle Park Steel, Surfside, California
