CELEBRATING 80 YEARS OF THE UNITED NATIONS ASSOCIATION OF THE NATIONAL 
                              CAPITAL AREA

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. DONALD S. BEYER, JR.

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 2025

  Mr. BEYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
outstanding contributions of the United Nations Association of the 
National Capital Area (UNA-NCA) on the occasion of its United Nations 
80th Anniversary Gala.
  The United Nations was founded in 1945, in the aftermath of World War 
II, to promote peace, security, development, and human rights around 
the world. As we mark 80 years of this global institution, it is 
fitting that we also honor one of its most steadfast and influential 
community champions: the UNA-NCA.
  Founded in 1952, the United Nations Association of the National 
Capital Area is one of the oldest and largest divisions of the United 
Nations Association of the USA (UNA-USA) and a member of the World 
Federation of United Nations Associations (WFUNA).
  With the support of more than 1,000 members, volunteers, and 
supporters in the greater Washington area, UNA-NCA has led efforts to 
educate, inspire, and mobilize citizens across our region to support 
the principles and vital work of the United Nations and its specialized 
agencies.
  For over seven decades, UNA-NCA has worked to build public knowledge, 
strengthen US-UN relations, and aid the UN in achieving its goals. It 
continues to serve as a critical bridge between the United Nations' 
international ideals and the everyday lives of citizens in our Nation's 
capital and surrounding regions. Its work reminds us that local 
engagement can make a global impact.
  UNA-NCA's mission is brought to life through a variety of programs 
that promote education, civic engagement, and leadership development, 
including the flagship Global Classroom DC Model UN Program, Graduate 
Fellows Program, Global Goals at Home, Young Professionals Program, the 
Annual Human Rights Awards, and more.
  Over the years, my staff and I have had the opportunity to 
participate in and support several UNA-NCA events, including on Capitol 
Hill in 2017 to highlight the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), 
the Annual Human Rights Awards, and regular meetings with the chapter 
as part of its congressional advocacy efforts, I was also honored to 
receive the UNA-NCA Arthur W. Johnson Leadership Award in 2022. 
Recognition from the UN holds personal significance as my grandmother, 
Clara Mortenson Beyer, was the first ever U.S. Representative to the 
International Labor Organization in Geneva.
  Finally, I want to offer special recognition to President Paula 
Boland, Board Chair Anne Simmons-Benton, and the board, staff, members, 
volunteers, and students whose steadfast work ensure UNA-NCA's success 
in its mission.
  As we celebrate UN80, I ask my colleagues to join me in saluting the 
UNA-NCA for its visionary leadership, its tireless advocacy, and its 
unwavering commitment to a more peaceful and just world.
