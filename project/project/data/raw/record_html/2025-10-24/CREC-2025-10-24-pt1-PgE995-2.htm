HONORING ANNA DURAN AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S 
                           WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Anna Duran as she is honored as one of California's 
8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Anna is a Crime 
Prevention Specialist for the Richmond Police Department and has served 
in that role since 2024. But her work and community service precede her 
time with Richmond PD. Anna brings her experience working for the 
Office of Neighborhood Safety as a case manager assisting families, 
many whom were undocumented. She has spent years volunteering with the 
Latina Center, where she has helped promote advocacy, outreach, and 
empowerment among her fellow Latina community members. Anna is known 
for her generosity and kindness, which extends beyond her professional 
obligations. She makes hospital visits to victims of crime, provides 
assistance to families who navigate immigration challenges, and has 
assisted in building support networks for undocumented residents. Even 
before officially joining the Richmond Police Department, Anna 
volunteered her time to and help the department by supporting families 
with needs assessments and referrals, in her personal time. Anna uses 
her personal experiences, genuine compassion, and resourcefulness to 
make a difference in the community, especially by assisting those who 
may be most in-need. It is these traits, and many more, that make Anna 
such a crucial asset to the overall community. On behalf of the people 
of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to honor 
and recognize Anna Duran for her positive contributions to our 
community.
