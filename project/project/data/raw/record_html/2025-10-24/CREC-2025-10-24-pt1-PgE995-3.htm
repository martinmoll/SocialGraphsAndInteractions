  HONORING VALLEY HEALTH SERVICES AND VALLEY RESIDENTIAL SERVICES ON 
                         THEIR 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ELISE M. STEFANIK

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 2025

  Ms. STEFANIK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 50th 
anniversary of Valley Health Services (VHS) and Valley Residential 
Services (VRS), two cornerstone institutions that have provided 
exceptional long-term care to seniors and their families in the Mohawk 
Valley for half a century.
  What began as a 32-bed facility in the former Herkimer Memorial 
Hospital building has grown into a comprehensive center for elder care 
and rehabilitation. From the start, VHS was guided by a simple but 
powerful mission: to care for the mind, body, and spirit of every 
individual who walks through its doors. That mission has endured--and 
expanded--for fifty years.
  In the 1980's, renovation projects increased the facility's capacity 
to 160 beds, laying the foundation for new services that continue to 
define VHS today. These include adult day healthcare, outpatient 
rehabilitation, subacute care, and skilled nursing. Each program 
reflects a commitment to person-centered care and a deep respect for 
the dignity and independence of every resident.
  The staff, families, and volunteers who fill its halls have created a 
home-like environment where residents experience comfort, privacy, and 
connection. Families are not just visitors but partners in care, 
joining in activities and celebrations that make life at VHS meaningful 
and full.
  In 2015, Valley Residential Services opened its doors as a natural 
extension of that mission. The modern senior living facility began with 
46 apartments across more than 220 acres of scenic land. Designed with 
comfort and independence in mind, each apartment features a living 
room, kitchenette, private bedroom, and bath. Common spaces include

[[Page E996]]

an activity room with a cafe, social gathering areas, and an on-site 
beauty salon.
  VRS expanded again in 2018, adding a new wing with 14 double-
occupancy apartments and a fitness center for active residents. This 
growth reflects not only success, but the community's trust in the 
care, compassion, and excellence that define both VHS and VRS.
  On behalf of New York's 21st Congressional District, I am honored to 
recognize Valley Health Services and Valley Residential Services for 
fifty years of service, care, and commitment to our seniors. Their 
legacy of compassion will continue to shape the community for 
generations to come.
