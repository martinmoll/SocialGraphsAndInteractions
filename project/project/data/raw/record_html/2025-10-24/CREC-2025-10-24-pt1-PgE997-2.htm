   HONORING BRITTNEY GREER AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH CONGRESSIONAL 
                      DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
contributions of Brittney Greer as she is honored as one of 
California's 8th Congressional District's Women of the Year. Brittney 
serves her community as the driver of Bus 9 for the Travis Unified 
School District. Through her work, she not only ensures the safety and 
secure transportation of the children of our community, but she does so 
with consistent positivity and optimism. As a loving mother of three 
children, it comes as no surprise that Brittney's kindness, care, and 
diligence make a daily impact on the students and families of the 
Travis-USD community. Brittney goes above and beyond to ensure the 
safety of her students, checking the bus for any potential mechanical 
issues every morning, and fixing them herself if possible. She is a 
source of consistency for parents providing a reliable, safe and caring 
environment for the students. This is a responsibility she does not 
take lightly, and she does so with grace. In addition to her role as a 
school bus driver, Brittney also displays leadership by serving her 
church congregation in Antioch, helping address the needs of her 
community. After realizing her community lacked support groups for 
women, she began studying to attain her certificate in Christian Life 
Coaching, with the goal of starting a women's support group. Brittney 
is a committed, pivotal part of the community, and her work improves 
the lives of the students and families within it. On behalf of the 
people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my pleasure to 
honor and recognize Brittney Greer for her positive contributions to 
our community.
