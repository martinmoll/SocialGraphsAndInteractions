                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Divine Provider, You have given us everything we need for life and 
godliness. With these things and Your great promises, You equip us to 
escape the corruption of this world and to participate in Your own 
divine nature.
  For this very reason, You instruct us to make every effort to support 
the faith we profess with goodness, that we would not bear false 
witness to Your love for all people. You command us to support goodness 
with knowledge, that we would not be ignorant of the complexity of the 
issues we face but wise in our stewardship of Your generosity in this 
time of asperity.
  It is Your expectation that our knowledge be reined in with self-
control, that we would not use our intellect or position to abuse or 
deride those with whom we disagree or oppose.
  In these days of contest and crisis, in the throes of this divisive 
shutdown, You call us to endure with pacific composure, and to reflect, 
with godliness and mutual concern, a faithful image of Your steadfast 
loving-kindness to those whom and with whom we serve.
  May we so obey and please You as we seek to follow You in this place 
and in this time.
  In Your merciful name we pray.
  Amen.
