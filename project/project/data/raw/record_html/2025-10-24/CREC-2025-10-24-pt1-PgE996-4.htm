    PROCLAMATION HONORING REVEREND ANTHONY ``FATHER TONY'' GALLAGHER

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MARCY KAPTUR

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 24, 2025

  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
proclamation honoring Reverend Anthony ``Father Tony'' Gallagher:

       Whereas, the Reverend Anthony ``Father Tony'' Gallagher, a 
     beloved son of Toledo and a priest of the Diocese of Toledo 
     since his ordination in Rome in 1961, has devoted more than 
     six decades of his life to faith, service, and the pursuit of 
     justice for all people; and
       Whereas, as the eldest of six children, Father Gallagher 
     has exemplified a lifetime of humility and leadership, 
     serving parishes from Port Clinton to Grand Rapids, and 
     providing spiritual guidance rooted in compassion, 
     inclusivity, and a steadfast belief in the dignity of every 
     human being; and
       Whereas, Father Gallagher has embodied the principles of 
     social justice throughout

[[Page E997]]

     his ministry, walking in the proud tradition of priests such 
     as Monsignor Geno Baroni and Father Martin Hernady, 
     tirelessly advocating for the poor, the marginalized, and 
     those without a voice in our society; and
       Whereas, in retirement from assigned parish ministry since 
     2009, Father Gallagher has continued his vocation of 
     service--working with the Multifaith Council, Social Justice 
     Advocates, Care of Creation, the CCMT School, and the 
     Association of U.S. Catholic Priests--to advance 
     understanding, promote equality, and strengthen bonds among 
     people of all faiths and backgrounds; and
       Whereas, his ministry of presence, his philosophy to ``show 
     up, shut up, and cheer,'' has inspired generations of 
     Toledoans, demonstrating what it means to encounter and 
     accompany others with grace and humility; and
       Whereas, Father Gallagher's deep empathy and compassion are 
     reflected not only in his advocacy, but in the quiet acts of 
     kindness that have defined his life from feeding the hungry 
     at Claver House and St. Paul's Community Center to offering 
     comfort and companionship to those in prison and on the 
     streets; and
       Whereas, as Father Gallagher celebrates his 90th birthday 
     on November 21, 2025, the people of Northwest Ohio and all 
     who his ministry has touched give thanks for his enduring 
     witness to faith, justice, and love of neighbor;
       Now, therefore, be it proclaimed, that I, Marcy Kaptur, 
     Member of Congress for the Ninth Congressional District of 
     Ohio, do hereby recognize and commend Reverend Anthony 
     ``Father Tony'' Gallagher for his lifetime of selfless 
     service, his moral courage, and his unwavering commitment to 
     the common good. Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the City of Toledo hereby recognizes and 
     commends the Reverend Anthony Gallagher for his outstanding 
     service and significant contributions to our community and to 
     our common good; and be it further
       Resolved, That the City Citation of Merit be presented to 
     Father Gallagher as a token of this city's deep appreciation 
     and gratitude for his unwavering dedication to making Toledo 
     a better place for us all.
