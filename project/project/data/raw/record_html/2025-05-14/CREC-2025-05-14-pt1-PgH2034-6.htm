                   HONORING THE LIFE OF CARL BALLTON

  (Ms. KAMLAGER-DOVE asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAMLAGER-DOVE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of my 
friend and a beloved champion for south Los Angeles, the late Carl 
Ballton.
  I met Carl through my work at Crystal Stairs where he served on the 
board for over 20 years, including a decade as chair. Under his 
leadership, Crystal Stairs grew into a respected institution serving 
thousands of children and families.
  Carl cared deeply about social justice and the Los Angeles community. 
That passion guided his service beyond Crystal Stairs. He led the Union 
Bank Foundation and served on the boards of the LA Urban League, United 
Negro College Fund, Habitat for Humanity, and many other organizations.
  Carl and his beloved wife, Lula, used their power for good, 
especially within the Black community.
  His passing is a deep loss for us all, but his legacy lives on in the 
countless lives he has touched and the institutions he helped build.

[[Page H2035]]

  He will be deeply missed.
