                        RECOGNIZING LORI VENTURA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 14, 2025

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
contributions that Lori Ventura has made through her work with

[[Page E427]]

the CARES Senior Clinic. Lori serves as Healthcare Ambassador for the 
CARES Senior Health Clinic. Lori helps coordinate the care for 1,200 
patients, including frail, disabled, low income & homeless clients. She 
helps coordinate treatments and community resources to aide in the 
health and wellbeing of all in the community. She has helped save the 
lives of countless individuals.
  It is clear Lori Ventura has made a profoundly positive impact on our 
community, and I am grateful for her service. May we all strive to 
serve our communities with the same dedication and humility.
