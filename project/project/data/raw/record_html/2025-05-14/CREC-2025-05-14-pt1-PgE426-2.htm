                       RECOGNIZING LISA DeGENNARO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 14, 2025

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
contributions that Lisa DeGennaro has made through her work with the 
CARES Senior Clinic. Lisa is the clinic's competent receptionist. She 
is the first to greet all who call and walk into the clinic, always 
with a smile. She is eager to assist and direct patients to the right 
place. The clinic would not run smoothly without her diligent efforts.
  It is clear Lisa DeGennaro has made a profoundly positive impact on 
our community, and I am grateful for her service. May we all strive to 
serve our communities with the same dedication and humility.
