                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-3. The SPEAKER presented a petition of Mr. Gregory D. 
     Watson, relative to requesting enactment of Federal 
     legislation to require that the formal documents to be signed 
     by the President must be witnessed by, and be attested to by, 
     a notary public and include an official notarial seal; which 
     was referred to the Committee on the Judiciary.
