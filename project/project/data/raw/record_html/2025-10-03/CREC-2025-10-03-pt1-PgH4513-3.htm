                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Eternal God, each day is Yours. You have made every moment for our 
use and enjoyment. You have given us freedom to do with today as we 
will. Your only desire is that we allow our will to be Yours.
  Remind us that while all things may be permitted, not all things are 
beneficial. Though we assert our rights, however we may define them, 
they may infringe not just on other's rights but on their welfare.
  Grant us wisdom to discern when and how we are to surrender our 
advantage to work for the benefit of our communities and our country.
  May whatever we do or say this day, how we act toward one another or 
perform the work You have set before us in these challenging times, may 
all of this ever bring glory to You, for therein we will find wisdom 
and peace.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.
