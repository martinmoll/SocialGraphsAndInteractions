             HONORING THE LIFE OF MICHAEL ``MIKE'' FRIEDMAN

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. RASHIDA TLAIB

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 3, 2025

  Ms. TLAIB. Mr. Speaker, today, the 12th Congressional District honors 
the extraordinary life and legacy of Michael ``Mike'' Friedman, a 
tireless champion of economic justice and workplace democracy. From his 
early days as a truck driver and founding member of Teamsters for a 
Democratic Union to his distinguished legal career. Mike's life was 
rooted in advancing the rights and dignity of working people.
  As General Counsel at the Center for Community-Based Enterprise, Mike 
dedicated himself to helping businesses transition to worker ownership, 
empowering employees, and strengthening communities. His leadership 
extended beyond C2BE to the Michigan Center for Employee Ownership, 
Jewish Voice for Peace, and the Detroit Community Wealth Fund, where he 
shared his expertise and vision for a fairer, more just world.
  Mike believed deeply in the power of unions to transform lives, which 
was reflected in his lifelong advocacy and teaching. His legacy will 
continue to inspire all who fight for justice, equity, and workers 
rights. Our thoughts are with his wife Barbara Harvey.
  We extend our love to his wife, siblings, children, grandchildren, 
and friends as we celebrate his life, and we are committed to carrying 
forward his spirit of service, joy, and justice.
