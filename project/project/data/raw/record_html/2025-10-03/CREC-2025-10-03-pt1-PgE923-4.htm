                 CELEBRATING SERGIO'S 50TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. CARLOS A. GIMENEZ

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 3, 2025

  Mr. GIMENEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 50th 
anniversary of one of South Florida's most iconic institutions, 
Sergio's Cuban American Kitchen.
  For half a century, Sergio's has been more than a restaurant, it has 
become a cultural landmark and a gathering place for generations of 
families across Miami-Dade. Founded in 1975, this family-owned business 
has proudly embodied the values of faith, family, hard work, and 
community service that define the Cuban-American community.
  From its humble beginnings in Westchester, Sergio's has grown into a 
household name with multiple locations across South Florida, serving 
traditional Cuban comfort food that brings our community together.
  Sergio's is also a proud local employer, providing opportunities to 
hundreds of South Floridians.
  Throughout hurricanes and economic challenges, Sergio's family has 
remained steadfast in serving the community, supporting first 
responders, and helping neighbors in need.
  I am honored to celebrate this milestone and to thank the entire 
Sergio's family for 50 years of tradition, resilience, and hospitality.
  I congratulate Sergio's Cuban Restaurant on its 50th anniversary and 
their extraordinary contributions to the cultural fabric and economic 
vitality of South Florida and the Cuban-American community.
