                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                      May 5, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Speaker Johnson: Pursuant to section 
     18(c)(2)(A)(i)(VIII) and (IX) of the Reforming Intelligence 
     and Securing America

[[Page H1815]]

     Act (Public Law 118-49), I am pleased to appoint the 
     following Member to serve as a Commissioner to the FISA 
     Reform Commission:
       The Honorable James A. Himes of Connecticut
       I am also pleased to appoint the following individuals to 
     serve as Commissioners to the FISA Reform Commission:
       Ms. Sharon Bradford Franklin of Bethesda, Maryland
       Mr. Glenn Steven Gerstell of Washington, District of 
     Columbia
       Thank you for your attention to this matter.
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.
