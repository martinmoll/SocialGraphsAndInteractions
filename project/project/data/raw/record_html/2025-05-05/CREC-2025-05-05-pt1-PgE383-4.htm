                       REMEMBERING DAVID GERLACH

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC SWALWELL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 5, 2025

  Mr. SWALWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise with my esteemed colleague from 
Illinois, Representative Darin LaHood, to honor the life and legacy of 
David ``Dave'' Gerlach, a dedicated educator, loving husband, father, 
and grandfather, on the occasion of his passing on March 26, 2025, at 
the age of 62.
  Born in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, in 1962, Dave grew up in Bethel 
Park alongside his brothers Jeff and Steve before moving to Fairport, 
New York. After graduating from Fairport High School, Dave attended 
SUNY Canton, where he earned his Associate of Applied Science degree in 
Business Administration and met his wife of 39 years, Lisa McIntosh.
  Dave dedicated his life to education and service, beginning as a 
student worker at Chaney Dining Center and rising through the ranks to 
become Vice President for Advancement at SUNY Canton, where he spent 
three decades helping to shape the college's future. His work 
overseeing the Canton College Foundation, Alumni and Development, 
Admissions, and Athletics left a lasting impact, including leading the 
Centennial Campaign, the most successful fundraising effort in the 
college's history.
  After completing his Ph.D. in Higher Education from Syracuse 
University, Dave fulfilled his lifelong dream of becoming President of 
Lincoln College in Illinois, where he led the school through a period 
of growth and achievement. Even after Lincoln's closure during the 
pandemic, Dave's dedication to students and learning never wavered.
  In retirement, Dave moved to Houston to be near his beloved 
grandchildren, Harrison and Ruby. He was affectionately known as 
``Papa,'' attending every game, concert, and school play, and building 
a new community of friends in Texas.
  Dave's legacy is one of leadership, mentorship, kindness, and 
unwavering commitment to family and community. His favorite Christmas 
movie, It's a Wonderful Life, embodied the spirit with which he lived--
showing that ``no man is a failure who has friends.''
  Dave is survived by his wife Lisa, his daughter Kayla, his son Josh, 
and his grandchildren Harrison and Ruby. May his memory continue to 
inspire all who knew and loved him.
