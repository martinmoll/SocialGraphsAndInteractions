                                ACES ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 530) to provide for a study by the National 
Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine on the prevalence and 
mortality of cancer among individuals who served as active duty aircrew 
in the Armed Forces, and for other purposes, as amended, on which the 
yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Michigan (Mr. Bergman) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 376, 
nays 5, not voting 52, as follows:
