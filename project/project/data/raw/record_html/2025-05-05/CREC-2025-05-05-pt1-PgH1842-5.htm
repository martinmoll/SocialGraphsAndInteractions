      RECOGNIZING INTERNATIONAL FIREFIGHTERS DAY AND TREVOR BROWN

  (Mr. SUBRAMANYAM asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SUBRAMANYAM. Mr. Speaker, I rise in recognition of International 
Firefighters Day to honor the brave firefighters in Virginia and around 
the world.
  Virginia is home to thousands of dedicated firefighters who put their 
lives on the line every day to keep our communities safe.
  Whether it is battling wildfires, responding to plane crashes, or 
kitchen fires, firefighters are always there to answer the call.
  International Firefighters Day is also a solemn reminder of those we 
have lost in the line of duty. Just last year, our community lost one 
of our own, Firefighter Trevor Brown, a husband and father of three 
from Sterling. He passed away responding to an emergency gas leak call. 
His sacrifice, like that of many others, is a testament to the courage 
that firefighters display daily, running into danger to keep us safe.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank the fighters in Virginia and around the world 
for their service. Because of them we are safer and stronger and our 
communities are more prosperous.
