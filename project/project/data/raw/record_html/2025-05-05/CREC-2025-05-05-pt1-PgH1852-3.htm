              JOINT RESOLUTIONS PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on May 5, 2025, 
the following joint resolutions were presented to the President of the 
United States for approval:

       H.J. Res. 24. Providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Department of Energy relating to ``Energy 
     Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for Walk-
     In Coolers and Walk-In Freezers''.
       H.J. Res. 20. Providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Department of Energy relating to ``Energy 
     Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards for 
     Consumer Gas-fired Instantaneous Water Heaters''.
