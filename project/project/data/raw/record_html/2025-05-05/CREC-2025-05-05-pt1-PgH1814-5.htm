                       TRUMP DELIVERS ON PROMISES

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, Americans continue to 
benefit from the wins accomplished by the first 100 days as President 
Donald Trump took back the White House by election mandate. He and the 
Republican-led House, with Speaker   Mike Johnson, and the Senate will 
continue to fight on behalf of American families.
  In just 100 days, the Trump administration has secured the borders, 
stopping terrorists and child predators; restored energy independence; 
begun peace through strength; reduced duplicative regulations; and 
brought massive investments, creating jobs and making America 
competitive again.
  President Trump and the Republican-led House are making the country 
strong, safe, and secure with promises made, promises kept with the 
Trump mandate.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Open borders for dictators put all Americans at risk of more 
9/11 attacks imminent, as warned by the FBI. Trump is reinstituting 
existing laws to protect American families with peace through strength, 
revealing that war criminal Putin lies as, this weekend, the Moscow 
dictatorship reiterated Ukraine does not exist, justifying mass murder 
of Ukrainians by war criminal Putin.
