            CONGRESSIONAL AWARD PROGRAM REAUTHORIZATION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (S. 284) to reauthorize the Congressional Award Act, on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Michigan (Mr. Walberg) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 370, 
nays 22, not voting 41, as follows:
