              CELEBRATING 25 YEARS OF PSYKO STEVE PRESENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. GREG STANTON

                               of arizona

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, December 15, 2025

  Mr. STANTON. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Psyko Steve Presents, a 
homegrown Arizona company that is celebrating their 25-year anniversary 
next week. Psyko Steve Presents shapes our state's modern music 
culture. Psyko Steve Presents brings artists, audiences, and 
communities together and strengthens the creative fabric that makes 
Phoenix such a vibrant place to live.
  Founded in 2000 by Steve Chilton, known throughout the Valley as 
``Psyko Steve,'' the company began with small DIY shows booked while he 
was still in high school and at Arizona State University. What started 
as a hobby quickly became a driving force in reviving live music in 
Phoenix at a time when the local scene needed new energy. Steve helps 
emerging musicians find their footing, many of whom later grew into 
nationally recognized acts. Today, Psyko Steve Presents produces more 
than 700 concerts annually across venues of every size, from intimate 
performances at The Rebel Lounge to major events at The Van Buren, the 
Marquee Theatre, the Arizona Financial Theatre, and festivals including 
M3F, ZONA Music Festival, and 8123 Fest.
  Steve is more than a promoter. He is also advocates for artists and 
independent venues across the U.S. As a co-founder and early leader of 
the National Independent Venue Association, he helped organize the 
grassroots #SaveOurStages campaign during the Covid-19 pandemic. The 
campaign led Congress to pass the Save Our Stages Act and create the 
Shuttered Venue Operators Grant program, which kept local venues afloat 
in their darkest hour. His work demonstrated how central live music is 
to the economic and cultural vitality of communities across America.
  Psyko Steve Presents has grown a great deal over the past 25 years, 
but its mission has remained constant: support local artists and give 
Arizonans access to unforgettable live music experiences.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating the 25th 
anniversary of Psyko Steve Presents and congratulating Steve Chilton 
and his team. Their commitment to creativity, community, and 
independent music has enriched our state, and we look forward to many 
more years of music ahead.
