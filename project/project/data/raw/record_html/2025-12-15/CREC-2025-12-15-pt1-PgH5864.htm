  HONORING THE SERVICE AND SACRIFICE OF UNITED STATES ARMY SPECIALIST 
   SARAH BECKSTROM AND UNITED STATES AIR FORCE STAFF SERGEANT ANDREW 
  WOLFE, WHO WERE TRAGICALLY SHOT IN WASHINGTON, D.C., IN A TARGETED 
   ASSAULT AGAINST UNITED STATES SERVICE MEMBERS ON NOVEMBER 26, 2025

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I move to suspend the rules and agree to 
the resolution (H. Res. 923) a resolution honoring the service and 
sacrifice of United States Army Specialist Sarah Beckstrom and United 
States Air Force Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe, who were tragically shot 
in Washington, D.C., in a targeted assault against United States 
servicemembers on November 26, 2025, as amended.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 923

       Whereas, on the afternoon of November 26, 2025, members of 
     the National Guard on patrol in Washington, D.C. as part of 
     the D.C. Safe and Beautiful Task Force were violently 
     assaulted with gunfire in a vicious, targeted attack;
       Whereas United States Army Specialist Sarah Beckstrom and 
     United States Air Force Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe, both 
     members of the West Virginia National Guard, sustained severe 
     gunshot wounds during the attack;
       Whereas Specialist Beckstrom succumbed to her injuries and 
     passed away surrounded by her loved ones on November 27, 
     2025, making the ultimate sacrifice in service to her 
     country;
       Whereas Staff Sergeant Wolfe was hospitalized before a 
     transfer to an in-patient rehabilitation facility;
       Whereas United States Army Specialist Sarah Beckstrom, who 
     was 20 years old at the time of her passing, was from 
     Summersville, West Virginia, enlisted on June 6, 2023, weeks 
     after graduating with honors from Webster County High School, 
     and was assigned to the 863rd Military Police Company, 111th 
     Engineer Brigade of the West Virginia Army National Guard;

[[Page H5865]]

       Whereas Specialist Beckstrom worked a civilian job as a 
     community engagement specialist at a Certified Community 
     Behavioral Health Center in Summersville, West Virginia that 
     provides mental health and substance use disorder treatment;
       Whereas United States Air Force Staff Sergeant Andrew 
     Wolfe, age 24, of Martinsburg, West Virginia, a graduate of 
     Musselman High School in Inwood, West Virginia, enlisted on 
     February 5, 2019, while still in high school, and was 
     assigned to the Force Support Squadron, 167th Airlift Wing of 
     the West Virginia Air National Guard;
       Whereas, in his civilian job, Staff Sergeant Wolfe has 
     worked as a lineman with Frontier Communications since 2023, 
     which has benefitted his community by bringing connectivity 
     to more homes and businesses in West Virginia;
       Whereas Specialist Beckstrom and Staff Sergeant Wolfe have 
     both received an outpouring of support and praise from their 
     communities and from around the country as individuals who 
     served with distinction, earned the admiration and respect of 
     their communities, and acted selflessly and courageously on 
     behalf of others; and
       Whereas Specialist Beckstrom and Staff Sergeant Wolfe had 
     both recently volunteered to extend their deployments in 
     Washington, D.C. and continue their service to help keep the 
     Capital of the United States safe: Now, therefore, be it
       Resolved, That the House of Representatives--
       (1) honors the memory, service and sacrifice of United 
     States Army Specialist Sarah Beckstrom for her dedicated 
     service to her State and to her country as a member of the 
     West Virginia National Guard;
       (2) honors the service and sacrifice of United States Air 
     Force Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe for his dedicated service 
     to his State and to his country as a member of the West 
     Virginia National Guard;
       (3) extends its sympathies, gratitude, and support for the 
     Beckstrom and Wolfe families for all they have endured and 
     will face because of this attack, as their love and support 
     have been the foundation of the service of Specialist 
     Beckstrom and Staff Sergeant Wolfe to their State and the 
     United States;
       (4) honors the bravery of National Guard members on the 
     scene who displayed courage and strength while under attack;
       (5) expresses gratitude for the swift action of members of 
     the National Guard and first responders on the scene who 
     responded to the attack and apprehended the attacker;
       (6) condemns in the strongest terms the horrific, violent 
     assault on United States servicemembers in the Capital of the 
     United States; and
       (7) recognizes that the men and women who volunteer to 
     serve their communities and their country as members of the 
     National Guard represent the very best of the United States 
     of America.

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I yield myself such time as I may consume.
  Mr. Speaker, United States Army Specialist Sarah Beckstrom and United 
States Air Force Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe were tragically shot just 
before the Thanksgiving holiday here in Washington, D.C.
  As members of the West Virginia National Guard on duty in the 
Nation's Capital, Specialist Beckstrom and Staff Sergeant Wolfe were 
targeted and violently assaulted with a barrage of gunfire for doing 
nothing less than performing their duty as a soldier and an airman. 
Both of them sustained severe gunshot wounds during the attack.
  Sadly, Specialist Beckstrom succumbed to her injuries and passed 
away, surrounded by loved ones, on Thanksgiving Day, while Staff 
Sergeant Wolfe remains in recovery. We wish him speedy success in that 
effort to be back on his feet.
  These two young guardsmen represent the best of what our Nation has 
to offer, making this horrific attack a truly devastating loss not just 
for those who knew them, served with them, and loved them, but it is, 
indeed, a tragic event for our entire Nation.
  Specialist Beckstrom was 20 years old at the time of her passing. An 
excellent student and dedicated guardsman, she graduated with honors 
from Webster County High School in West Virginia. Weeks later, on June 
6, she joined the West Virginia National Guard. Sarah embodied selfless 
service.
  In Sarah's civilian job, she worked as a community engagement 
specialist at a certified community behavioral health clinic that 
provides mental health and substance abuse disorder treatment to those 
in need. She recently volunteered to extend her deployment here in 
Washington, D.C.
  Sarah was admired by all who knew her. She served admirably and with 
distinction, and I would argue she is admired even by those who didn't 
know her for her selfless sacrifice on behalf of her colleagues in West 
Virginia, but also on behalf of grateful citizens across our Nation.
  We honor her dedication, honor her service, and express our deepest 
condolences to her loved ones.
  Sarah will indeed be missed, but her legacy will live on. Her 
selfless sacrifice will live on in the hearts and minds of her fellow 
citizens in West Virginia, but also those of us across the Nation.
  Staff Sergeant Wolfe enlisted in the West Virginia National Guard 
before he even finished high school. At 24 years old, he voluntarily 
requested to continue his mission here in Washington, D.C., through the 
holidays.
  A dedicated airman, Andrew is also an asset to his community, his 
State, and our Nation. As a lineman with Frontier Communications, 
Andrew brought connectivity and communications to many communities 
across West Virginia, in many areas that needed it the most.
  Andrew was dedicated to making sure that people had that connectivity 
and had that connection to the internet that they needed just for the 
basic elements of life, but also for educational opportunities.
  Like Sarah, Andrew proved himself to be a selfless warrior, serving 
with distinction and earning the admiration of all who knew him.
  We join with his friends and family in praying for his full recovery, 
and we hope his journey to healing is swift and painless.
  I would be remiss if I did not thank the first responders, law 
enforcement, and National Guardsmen and -women who acted quickly to 
subdue the threat and provide first aid. They acted with courage and 
bravery while under fire to protect the people of Washington, D.C.
  Mr. Speaker, this is what makes our Nation great, folks who are 
willing to serve our Nation, like Sarah and Andrew, who are willing to 
don the cloth of this Nation, willing to provide that selfless 
sacrifice, willing to put their lives on the line each and every day 
for what we all believe in, that great Constitution of our United 
States of America.
  Today, we honor and recognize their selfless service and understand 
what it takes in those dangerous situations to truly guard this beacon 
of freedom and liberty here in our great United States of America.
  Mr. Speaker, I reserve the balance of my time.

  Mr. Speaker, Specialist Beckstrom was only 20 years old when she died 
on November 27, 2025, from her injuries. She was a daughter, a student, 
an athlete, a caregiver, and a soldier.
  In her civilian life, as we heard, she worked at a behavioral health 
clinic in Summersville, West Virginia, helping others heal. She 
initially joined the Army to help pay for college. She soon found a 
deeper calling in service, proudly serving as a military police 
officer.
  At Webster County High School, she excelled both in the classroom and 
on the softball field, studied health science, and participated in the 
JROTC, learning early the values of citizenship, duty, and service in a 
democracy.
  She dreamed of a future in law enforcement, possibly with the FBI, a 
dream rooted in her desire to protect others and to be a part of 
something greater than herself. Her life was far too short, but her 
example will endure.

[[Page H5866]]

  Staff Sergeant Andrew Wolfe, just 24 years old, also exemplifies that 
same quiet dedication to country. A lineman in his civilian life and a 
force management airman in the West Virginia Air National Guard, he 
serves in a role that rarely makes headlines but is essential to the 
readiness and effectiveness of our Armed Forces. His work ensures that 
our people, resources, and missions are aligned so the military can 
function when it matters most.
  A graduate of high school in Inwood, West Virginia, Staff Sergeant 
Wolfe joined the Guard in 2019, stepping forward to serve, while 
building a life at home. I am encouraged by reports of his progress and 
that he is now breathing on his own and standing without assistance. I 
join all who are rooting for his full and speedy recovery.
  Specialist Beckstrom and Staff Sergeant Wolfe represent the very best 
of our Nation. They are young Americans, driven by service, compassion, 
and a sense of duty. Their willingness to step forward and to serve 
their communities and their country reflects the highest ideals of 
military and public service.
  Mr. Speaker, there is no place in a civil and democratic society for 
this kind of violence. I am deeply grateful to all who wear the uniform 
and to the families and the loved ones who stand behind them, often 
bearing the quiet burden and heavy sacrifices on their own.

923, and I am extremely proud to support its passage.
