                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-59. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Arizona, relative to House Memorial 2001, 
     urging the President and the Congress of the United States to 
     withdraw from the United Nations; to the Committee on Foreign 
     Affairs.
       ML-60. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the 
     State of Ohio, relative to House Concurrent Resolution No. 
     13, urging Congress to designate the Buckeye Trail as a 
     National Scenic Trail; to the Committee on Natural Resources.
