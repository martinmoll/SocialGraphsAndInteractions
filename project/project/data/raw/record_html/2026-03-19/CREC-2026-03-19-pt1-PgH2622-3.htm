            By Ms. MOORE of Wisconsin:
        H.R. 7995.
        Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I of the U.S. Constitution states that all 
     legislative powers herein granted shall be vested in a 
     Congress of the United States, which includes the authority 
     to write laws. This article establishes Congress as the sole 
     entity responsible for creating and amending laws.
