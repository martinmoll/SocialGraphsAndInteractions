                                 PRAYER

  Dr. Brent Carr, Carolina Bible College, Anderson, South Carolina, 
offered the following prayer:
  Heavenly Father, we come before You today with humility and 
gratitude, acknowledging that You are the sovereign God who raises up 
nations and sustains them by Your providence.
  In this place of leadership, we recognize that every good gift our 
Nation has received has come from Your hand of grace.
  For nearly 250 years, Lord, You have shown remarkable faithfulness to 
the United States through wars, trials, testing, and prosperity.
  Like General George Washington knelt in the snow of Valley Forge and 
prayed that we confess that we are in need of Your guidance and Your 
protection, we pray that today. For our strength does not come from 
affluence or from armies, but our strength comes from Almighty God.
  Bless today the leaders that are gathered in this Capitol. Lord, 
grant them, wisdom, courage, and humility as they lead us in truth and 
justice for all.
  Forgive us where we have strayed from Your ways. Lord, we ask today, 
as we feel Your presence in this place, that You would draw the heart 
of America back to You.
  For Thine is the kingdom and Thine is the power and Thine is the 
glory forever and ever. In Jesus' name.
  Amen.
