                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Ms. Lasky, one of its clerks, announced 
that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in which the 
concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 1884. An act to clarify the Holocaust Expropriated Art 
     Recovery Act of 2016, to appropriately limit the application 
     of defenses based on the passage of time and other non-merits 
     defenses to claims under that Act.
       S. 2584. An act to amend title 18, United States Code, 
     regarding additional assessments on convicted persons, and 
     for other purposes.
       S. 3424. An act to amend titles 11 and 28, United States 
     Code, to modify the compensation payable to trustees serving 
     in cases under chapter 7 of title 11, United States Code, to 
     extend the term of certain temporary offices of bankruptcy 
     judges, and for other purposes.
