                       ELECTRIC SUPPLY CHAIN ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 3638) to 
amend the Public Utility Regulatory Policies Act of 1978 to add a 
standard related to State consideration of reliable generation, and for 
other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 267, 
nays 159, not voting 7, as follows:
