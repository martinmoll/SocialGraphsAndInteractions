                       HONORING MR. IAN MATTHEWS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and commend an outstanding educator from Mississippi's Delta region, 
Mr. Ian Matthews, a dedicated science teacher at West Bolivar High 
School in Rosedale. Mr. Matthews has recently been selected to serve on 
the Mississippi Department of Education's Science Standard Review 
Committee, a prestigious and highly selective body tasked with 
reviewing and shaping the future of science education across our state.
  Mr. Matthews represents Mississippi's 2nd Congressional District on 
this committee and was chosen from nearly 100 applicants statewide, 
with only about two dozen ultimately selected. His appointment reflects 
his exceptional knowledge of science curriculum standards, his 
effectiveness in the classroom, and his deep commitment to student 
achievement--particularly in high-stakes, state-tested subject areas 
such as Biology.
  A proud native of Benoit, Mississippi, Mr. Matthews is a 2016 
graduate of Ray Brooks High School, which once served the same

[[Page E1182]]

school district he now represents with such distinction. His 
educational journey is a testament to perseverance and dedication. 
After graduating from high school, he attended Coahoma Community 
College, majoring in Sports Medicine, and later earned his bachelor's 
degree in Exercise Science and Allied Health from Delta State 
University in 2020. He then went on to complete a master's degree in 
teaching in 2022 and an Educational Specialist Degree in Leadership 
from Arkansas State University in 2024. He is currently pursuing his 
doctorate in Educational Leadership at Trident University.
  In the classroom, Mr. Matthews has shown remarkable results. Over the 
past several years, he has consistently achieved a 70 percent passing 
rate in his science classes, with 48 percent proficiency and a 75 
percent pass rate in the last academic year alone. These gains are 
attributed to his use of hands-on learning strategies, the integration 
of lab-based activities, and meaningful communication with parents to 
support student learning beyond the classroom.
  In addition to teaching, he coaches student athletics, extending his 
philosophy of resilience, teamwork, and discipline to all areas of 
student development. He often tells his students that ``education is 
the passport to the future'', encouraging them to pursue both academic 
and career pathways, and to believe that their background should never 
limit their aspirations. His personal motto--``If there's no seat at 
the table, make your own''--reflects the bold leadership and vision he 
instills in the next generation.
  Mr. Matthews is not just an educator; he is a hometown hero, a 
mentor, a leader, and a living example of what it means to return home 
and serve. As he takes on this important role with the Mississippi 
Department of Education, he brings with him not only experience and 
expertise but also the heart and soul of the Mississippi Delta.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that my colleagues join me in celebrating the 
achievements of Mr. Ian Matthews, and in recognizing his extraordinary 
contributions to education in Mississippi and his unwavering commitment 
to student success.
