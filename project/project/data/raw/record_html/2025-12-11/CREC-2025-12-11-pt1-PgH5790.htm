                 INCREASING INVESTOR OPPORTUNITIES ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to House Resolution 936 and rule 
XVIII, the Chair declares the House in the Committee of the Whole House 
on the state of the Union for the further consideration of the bill, 
H.R. 3383.
  Will the gentleman from Texas (Mr. Moran) kindly take the chair.

                              {time}  1422


                     In the Committee of the Whole

  Accordingly, the House resolved itself into the Committee of the 
Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 3383) to amend the Investment Company Act of 1940 with 
respect to the authority of closed-end companies to invest in private 
funds, with Mr. Moran (Acting Chair) in the chair.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The Acting CHAIR. When the Committee of the Whole rose on Wednesday, 
December 10, 2025, amendment No. 5, printed in part B of House Report 
119-399, offered by the gentlewoman from California (Ms. Waters) had 
been postponed.
