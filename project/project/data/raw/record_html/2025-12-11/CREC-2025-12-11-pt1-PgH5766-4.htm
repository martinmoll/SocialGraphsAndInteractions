                RECOGNIZING REPRESENTATIVE HARRY WARREN

  (Mr. McDOWELL asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McDOWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to send my greatest gratitude 
to Representative Harry Warren as he retires after eight terms serving 
the people of Rowan County in North Carolina's 76th District.
  Representative Warren has been a steady, respected voice in Raleigh, 
and folks back home knew that they could always count on him. He 
listened, he worked hard, and he treated everyone with fairness and 
respect.
  As someone who represents Rowan County in Congress, I have seen the 
impact of his work up close. He has been a strong advocate for the 
community, a trusted partner, and a genuinely good man. We are grateful 
for his service, and I wish him all the best in his next chapter.
