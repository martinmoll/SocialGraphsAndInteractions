                    HONORING ARTHUR ``SQUEAKY'' KIRK

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. KWEISI MFUME

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. MFUME. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a beloved figure in 
Baltimore City, Mr. Arthur ``Squeaky'' Kirk, a man I have had the honor 
of working alongside many times in our shared community. Arthur's 
extraordinary contributions to our city and his unparalleled dedication 
to its people epitomize the very essence of a Baltimorean.
  Arthur is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of the Ruth M. Kirk 
Recreation and Learning Center, which is named in honor of his late 
mother. Ruth was a dear friend and an exceptional public servant in the 
Maryland House of Delegates, representing West Baltimore with pride and 
dedication for 28 years. It is evident that Ruth passed her passion for 
public service on to her son, who continues to live by the principles 
that she raised him with.
  As a promise to his mother before her passing, Arthur agreed to take 
over the recreation center, in August 2011, he embarked on a journey, 
refurbishing and revitalizing the building, ensuring it remains as a 
beacon of hope and a symbol of communal resilience in our city. Since 
then, he has forged a sanctuary in the heart of the Franklin Square 
Neighborhood, where students are protected from neighborhood violence 
and families can get the resources they desperately need.
  Each week, the center offers after-school tutoring and meals, GED 
adult programs, youth mentorship, and more, all the while supplying 450 
Baltimore families with groceries. Arthur works diligently to beautify 
the community by enlisting the help of neighborhood children, 
encouraging them to learn about gardening and agriculture in his 
community garden. His work has been recognized by many in our 
Congressional District, from the Baltimore Ravens to Governors of 
Maryland on both sides of the aisle, past and present. ``Squeaky'' has 
made remarkable contributions to countless families across West 
Baltimore while honoring his mother's legacy and continuing her work.
  Arthur's motivation to be a mentor and change-maker in the lives of 
our young people only grew in 2022, when he was struck by the greatest 
of tragedies, the loss of his young son Elijah. I know that the loss is 
a wound that he will always carry, but Elijah's memory is also one of 
Arthur's greatest sources of strength.
  In addition to his work with the center, Arthur has helped keep our 
city safe as a dispatcher for the Baltimore City Fire Department for 
over two decades. He is the stoic voice on the other side of the line 
that helps maintain a sense of calm and composure during an emergency 
while simultaneously guiding first responders to your location. He is a 
proud member of the International Association of Firefighters (IAFF) 
Local 734, where he advocates for the interests of paramedics and 
firefighters across Baltimore. In both his personal and professional 
life, ``Squeaky'' consistently shows his selflessness and his deep love 
for Baltimore and its people.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend Mr. Arthur ``Squeaky'' Kirk for his unwavering 
generosity and for continuing the estimable legacy of his mother. His 
tireless dedication to his students, his profound compassion for their 
families, and his steadfast community leadership have enriched the 
lives of so many Baltimoreans. Today, we honor not just a man, but a 
living testament to the transformative power of service, and the 
enduring impact of one individual's dedication to uplifting the needy.
