      HONORING ROBERT ``BOB'' HARVEY WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Specialist Robert 
``Bob'' Harvey of Celina, Texas, and to honor him with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. Specialist Harvey honorably served in the United 
States Naval Reserve from 1955 to 1956 and in the United States Army 
from 1957 to 1963, dedicating his career to the defense of our nation 
with distinction and commitment.
  During his military service, Specialist Harvey served as an Engineer 
and was a proud member of both the 101st and 82nd Airborne units. He 
began as Assistant Platoon Leader and was later promoted to Assistant 
to the First Sergeant for B Company, demonstrating leadership, 
responsibility, and dedication to his fellow soldiers. His service took 
him to the Naval Base in San Diego, California, Fort Bragg, and Fort 
Campbell, and to participate in operations in Lebanon. For his service, 
he earned the Parachutist Badge and the Good Conduct Medal, reflecting 
his skill, dedication, and professionalism.
  Following his military career, Specialist Harvey continued his 
commitment to service in the Celina community. For over seven years, he 
provided support to his wife, Carolyn, at

[[Page E1179]]

every downtown event held in the city, demonstrating his dedication to 
wife and community engagement. With his engineering background, Bob 
served eight years on the Celina Planning and Zoning Commission, 
including two years as Chair, and contributed to the Comprehensive Plan 
Committee, helping set the vision for Celina's future. In 2011, he was 
asked to lead the Celina Square Project, overseeing the construction of 
a pavilion and handicap-accessible ramps, which he completed in time 
for the city's 100-year celebration.
  I am deeply honored to recognize Specialist Bob Harvey for his 
courage, patriotism, and lifelong dedication to serving both his 
country and his community. He exemplifies the very best of Texas' 4th 
Congressional District and of the United States of America.
