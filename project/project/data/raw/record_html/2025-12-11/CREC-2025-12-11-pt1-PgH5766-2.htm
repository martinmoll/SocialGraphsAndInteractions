                            I SHALL MARCH ON

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, and still I rise to announce that 
last night I called for the impeachment of Donald John Trump, President 
of the United States of America, for threatening judges and for calling 
for the execution of Members of Congress.
  If ever there was a person who ought to be impeached, it is Donald 
John Trump. Now, there are many people who don't want to see this 
happen. They don't want to vote for impeachment.
  Well, there were people who didn't want to cross the Edmund Pettus 
Bridge, but they marched on. I shall march on.
  I have been told that this vote will take place sometime after 1:30 
today. I assure you that I will be here for that vote. I will also 
speak on Friday for about 1 hour after about 11 o'clock. I will explain 
more about it at that time.
  This President is a reckless, ruthless, lawless person who doesn't 
deserve the title of President of the United States of America.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Owens). Members are reminded to refrain 
from engaging in personalities toward the President.
