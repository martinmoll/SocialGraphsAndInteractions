       CELEBRATING BATTALION CHIEF JAMES JONES ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RUDY YAKYM III

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Battalion Chief 
James Jones of the South Bend Fire Department, as he retires after 35 
years of outstanding service to the City of South Bend and to the state 
of Indiana.
  Chief Jones began his career in April of 1991 and has spent more than 
three decades protecting the safety and well-being of our community. He 
has served with distinction in every role he has held, most recently as 
the Battalion Chief for C-shift, where his leadership, professionalism, 
and steady presence have earned the respect of both his colleagues and 
the citizens he served.
  In 2016, Chief Jones faced a personal battle with prostate cancer. 
Throughout his treatment, he continued to report for duty and remained 
committed to the people who depended on him. This strength of character 
reflects the very best of public service and stands as an inspiration 
to all who know his story.
  Today, we offer our deepest gratitude.
  I thank Chief Jones for his devotion to duty, his courage in the face 
of adversity, and his unwavering commitment to our community. His 
service has made a lasting impact on our community, and he leaves a 
legacy of integrity, compassion, and dedication.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Battalion Chief James 
Jones and congratulating him on his well-deserved retirement.
