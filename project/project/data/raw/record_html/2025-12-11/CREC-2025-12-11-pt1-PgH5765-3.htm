                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Eternal God, how vast and wide is Your bounty. Your generosity to us 
knows no end. Just as You set the limits of the vast oceans and 
separated the waters from the loftiness of the heavens and divided the 
light from the darkness, so, too, by the presence of Your Holy Spirit 
have You set before us boundaries that we may live as You desire and 
enjoy Your abundant grace to its fullest.
  Set that hedge around us this day, O Lord. Sanctify our lives and set 
them apart for Your purpose, that we would share in Your vision for a 
life that is good, right, pleasing, and perfect in Your eyes.
  If that means we must draw a line that does not allow us to pursue 
selfish desires nor to lie, hate, or fear, then make that line clear to 
us that we would have no desire to even approach it.
  When we come up against the limits that You have set on our attitudes 
and speech, may we prove accountable to the righteousness You desire 
from us.
  When we choose to live, think, act, and love as You have loved us, in 
our faithfulness may we discover the freedom found only in Your 
boundless mercy. May we find countless ways to express our profound 
love for You and for the life You have so generously given us.
  May we find infinite joy in You, O God, when You delight in us.
  In the strength of Your name we pray.
  Amen.
