 RECOGNIZING MOODY HIGH SCHOOL FOR WINNING THE AHSAA CLASS 1-5A STATE 
                  CHAMPIONSHIP IN GIRLS' FLAG FOOTBALL

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Moody High School girls' flag football team for winning the Alabama 
High School Athletic Association (AHSAA) 1-5A State Championship.
  At Protective Stadium in Birmingham, Alabama, on December 3, 2025, 
the Moody flag football team, with a record of 22-3, secured the 
school's first state championship in flag football over Montgomery 
Catholic School with a score of 28-17.
  The championship team from Moody, Alabama, is led by Coach Wren Moore 
and is the school's first flag football state title.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Moody girls' flag 
football team, Coach Moore and the coaching staff, the faculty and fans 
on this exciting win for the school. I congratulate them and Go Blue 
Devils.
