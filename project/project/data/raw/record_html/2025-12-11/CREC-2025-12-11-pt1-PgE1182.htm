                     HONORING DR. MARCUS STADELMANN

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Dr. Marcus Stadelmann 
of UT Tyler.
  Since 1990, Dr. Stadelmann has been leading the Tyler community. 
During his 35 years of service, he has prepared countless students for 
their careers and worked tirelessly to support UT Tyler.
  His dedication and passion have been at the cornerstone of the 
Political Science Department. Dr. Stadelmann invests in each of his 
students, ensuring they are prepared for success no matter what the 
next chapter brings for them. He has instilled a lifelong love of 
learning and discovery.
  His influence extends far beyond the classroom. His leadership, 
innovative research, and work across the university have left a lasting 
mark on the institution. As a professor, he has fostered an incredible 
classroom environment, bringing new ideas to light for his students. 
Although he challenges his students, the approach allows them to 
flourish.
  The Tyler community will miss his daily presence and the spirit he 
brought to the faculty, staff, and to the classroom.
  I congratulate Dr. Stadelmann on his well-earned retirement. I 
congratulate him on building a strong legacy. I am grateful for the 
robust programs he has built and the strong communities he created. May 
he please accept my best wishes on a retirement filled with joy and new 
adventures. The Tyler community looks forward to Dr. Stadelmann 
remaining a valued part of the community.
