                        HONORING SUSAN BAXMEYER

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RUDY YAKYM III

                               of indiana

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. YAKYM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and express my 
sincere gratitude for the extraordinary service and cherished 
friendship of Susan Baxmeyer, whose decades of dedication have 
strengthened our community and enriched public life across Indiana.
  Susan began her political involvement in 1980, volunteering for John 
Hiler's congressional campaign. Her skill and dedication were 
immediately evident, and after his election, she joined his staff as 
District Secretary and Scheduler. She quickly became a trusted contact 
for constituents and a reliable support to the office. Whether at 
county fairs, fish fries, Lincoln Day dinners, or state conventions, 
she immersed herself fully in the work and built lasting relationships 
throughout the former 3rd Congressional District.
  After leaving the congressional office in 1987 to join her husband 
Carl in their planning and consulting business, Susan remained deeply 
engaged in public service. She has served continuously as a Precinct 
Committeeman and held key leadership roles at both the district and 
county levels, including positions within the Indiana Republican State 
Committee and the St. Joseph County Republican Women. Her service as a 
delegate to state and national conventions and her recognition as a St. 
Joseph County Republican Woman of the Year nominee reflect the respect 
she has earned across our community and our state. She lifts others up 
simply by being who she is, and she has strengthened every organization 
and every effort she has touched.
  On a personal note, I want to express my deep appreciation for her 
friendship. In public life, the support and kindness of people like 
Susan mean more than can be measured. Her counsel, encouragement, and 
loyalty have been invaluable, and I am honored to call her a friend.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Susan 
Baxmeyer for her outstanding contributions, her unwavering dedication 
to our community, and her lifelong commitment to service. Indiana is 
better because of her, and we are grateful for all she has given.
