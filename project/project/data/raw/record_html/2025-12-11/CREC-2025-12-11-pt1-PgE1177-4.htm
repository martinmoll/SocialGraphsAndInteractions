  RECOGNIZING MOODY HIGH SCHOOL FOR WINNING THE AHSAA CLASS 5A STATE 
                        CHAMPIONSHIP IN FOOTBALL

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. MIKE ROGERS

                               of alabama

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. ROGERS of Alabama. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the 
Moody High School football team for winning the Alabama High School 
Athletic Association (AHSAA) 5A State Championship.
  At Protective Stadium in Birmingham, Alabama, on December 4, 2025, 
the Moody football team, with a record of 13-2, secured the school's 
second state championship over Vigor High School with a shutout of 25-
0.
  The championship team from Moody, Alabama, is led by Coach Jake Ganus 
and is the school's second football state title.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing the Moody football team, 
Coach Ganus and his coaching staff, the faculty and fans on this 
exciting win for the school. I congratulate them and Go Blue Devils.
