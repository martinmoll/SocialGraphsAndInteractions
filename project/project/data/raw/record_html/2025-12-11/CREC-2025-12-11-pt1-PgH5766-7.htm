          HONORING THE LIFE AND COURAGE OF RALPH LAWRENCE CARR

  (Mr. TAKANO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. TAKANO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and courage 
of Ralph Lawrence Carr, the 29th Governor of Colorado, whose birthday 
we celebrate today.
  He was in office when Executive Order 9066 was issued, the order that 
permitted the mass incarceration of 120,000 Japanese Americans, 
including my grandparents and parents during World War II.
  A staunch Republican, he refused to comply with the order and instead 
opened the State of Colorado to Japanese Americans fleeing the Federal 
Government. To a group of farmers concerned with his support of 
Japanese Americans, Governor Carr said, ``An American citizen of 
Japanese descent has the same rights as any other citizen. If you harm 
them, you must first harm me.''
  Mr. Speaker, the Governor lost re-election, largely due to his 
defense of Japanese Americans. Let his courage be a lesson to us today 
as we, once again, see a dark tide spread across our country.
