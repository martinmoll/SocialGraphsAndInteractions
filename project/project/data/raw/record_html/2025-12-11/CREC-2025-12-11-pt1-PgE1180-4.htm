      HONORING ROBERT ``ROB'' ALTMAN WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN 
                              COMMENDATION

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Major Robert 
``Rob'' Altman of Frisco, Texas, and to honor him with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. Major Altman honorably served for over 25 years 
in the United States Army, demonstrating exceptional leadership and 
dedication to our Nation.
  At just nineteen years old, Rob began his military career in 1993, 
serving both as an enlisted soldier and a commissioned officer. 
Throughout his distinguished service, he held numerous leadership 
roles, including team leader, platoon leader, company commander, 
operations cell leader, diplomatic and intelligence advisor, battalion 
executive officer, and chief of staff. His assignments took him to 
Germany and included multiple deployments, during which he spent more 
than 30 months in combat zones. For his exemplary service, he earned 
the Bronze Star Medal, Meritorious Service Medal, Army Commendation 
Medal, Navy Achievement Medal, Iraq Campaign Medal with Campaign Star, 
and the Global War on Terrorism Expeditionary and Service Medals.
  Rob has also excelled academically. He completed the Leaders Program 
in Advanced Security Studies at the George C. Marshall Center in 
Germany in 2011, earned a Master of Arts in International Affairs from 
the George H. W. Bush School of Government and Public Service at Texas 
A&M University in 2013, and was selected for the Art of War Scholars 
Program at the U.S. Army Command and General Staff College in 2014. He 
is now completing Ph.D. coursework in history at the University of 
North Texas, focusing on U.S. Army mobilization and interwar planning.
  In his community, Rob serves as Chair of the Frisco Veterans Advisory 
Committee and on the National Security Advisory Council for America's 
Future Series. He is a member of the Sons of the American Revolution 
and a life member of both the VFW and American Legion. Dedicated to 
developing young leaders, he served six years as Assistant Scoutmaster 
and Scoutmaster for Scouting America Troop 45. In December 2024, he 
continued his public service when he was elected to the Board of 
Directors of the Denton Central Appraisal District.
  I am deeply honored to recognize Major Robert Altman for his 
extraordinary courage, patriotism, and lifelong commitment to service. 
He represents the very best of Texas' 4th Congressional District and of 
our great Nation.
