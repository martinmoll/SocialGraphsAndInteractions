RECOGNIZING THE ST. PAUL HIGH SCHOOL CHEER TEAM ON WINNING THE DIVISION 
                    V OHSCCA BEST IN THE STATE TITLE

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I rise 
to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school cheer team 
in Ohio's 5th Congressional District. The young student athletes of the 
St. Paul High School cheer team have represented their school ably on 
their way to achieving the Ohio High School Cheerleading and Dance 
Coaches Association Division V Best in the State title.
  In their effort to surpass all other teams in the division, St. Paul 
overcame the challenges posed by intense competition. The members of 
this very special team have shown that their sport requires an 
individual effort for a team result. Their hard work and dedication, 
both on and off the floor, in achieving this accomplishment, is truly 
outstanding.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special tribute 
to the St. Paul cheer team. On behalf of the people of the 5th District 
of Ohio, I am proud to recognize this great achievement.
