  HONORING THOMAS UNERFUSSER WITH A CONGRESSIONAL VETERAN COMMENDATION

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. PAT FALLON

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Thursday, December 11, 2025

  Mr. FALLON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Captain Thomas 
Unerfusser of Denison, Texas, and to honor him with a Congressional 
Veteran Commendation. Captain Unerfusser honorably served in the United 
States Air Force from 1985 to 2006.
  Captain Unerfusser began his distinguished military career as an 
enlisted airman, achieving the rank of Technical Sergeant (E-6) before 
earning his commission as an officer, and rising to the rank of Captain 
(O-3E). His service took him to Altus Air Force Base, Loring Air Force 
Base, Vandenberg Air Force Base, and numerous other locations around 
the world. Throughout his career, he served in the Persian Gulf War, 
Operation Desert Shield, Operation Desert Storm, Operation Provide 
Comfort, Operation Desert Calm, Operation Northern Watch, and other 
classified missions across Europe and Africa.
  For his exemplary service, Captain Unerfusser received numerous 
honors, including the Meritorious Service Medal, the Air Medal, the Air 
Force Commendation Medal, and the Air Force Achievement Medal. His 
dedication and excellence also earned him recognition as Boom Operator 
of the Year and Honor Guard of the Year, reflecting his 
professionalism, precision, and leadership both in the air and on the 
ground.
  Following his distinguished military career, Captain Unerfusser 
continued his service to others by joining the Denison Police 
Department, where he achieved Master Police Officer certification. 
After retiring from the department, he continued his commitment to 
justice and public service as a Grayson County District Attorney 
Investigator. Captain Unerfusser remains active in the veteran 
community as a lifetime member of the Sherman Veterans of Foreign Wars, 
the Perrin Air Force Base Museum, the Air Force Sergeants Association, 
and the Air Force Association.
  I am deeply honored to recognize Captain Thomas Unerfusser for his 
courage, patriotism, and lifelong devotion to serving others. He 
exemplifies the very best of Texas' 4th Congressional District and of 
the United States of America.
