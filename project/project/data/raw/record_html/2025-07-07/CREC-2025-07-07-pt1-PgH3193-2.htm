                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of all creation, the day does not dawn, nor the sun set if You 
yourself have not spoken Your word. Neither does the rain fall nor the 
waters rise without Your knowledge.
  Speak then into the tragedy that has unfolded in Kerr County, and the 
surrounding areas in Texas. Direct the missing toward safety and the 
first responders to their rescue. Save the stranded from the waters, 
from the mire where there is no foothold. Relieve those who cry to You 
for help, whose eyes fail waiting for Your salvation.
  From the debris of tents, trailers, and cabins, bring forth the hope 
of survivors, and relief to families and friends who are enduring the 
excruciatingly long wait for news of their loved ones.
  And as others grieve the loss of more than 80 men, women, and 
children who have died, answer their grief with the goodness of Your 
love and wrap them in the comfort of Your loving arms. Then, in the 
aftermath of this flood's terrible destruction, and in the fullness of 
time, grant them Your peace.
  In Your eternal name, we pray.
  Amen.
  The SPEAKER. Pursuant to clause 13, rule I, the Journal of the last 
day's proceedings is approved.
