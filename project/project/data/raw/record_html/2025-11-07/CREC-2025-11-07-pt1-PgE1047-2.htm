          HONORING THE 25TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE HOUSE OF HEROES

                                  _____
                                 

                      HON. SANFORD D. BISHOP, JR.

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 7, 2025

  Mr. BISHOP. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor the 25th 
Anniversary of the House of Heroes, a remarkable organization founded 
by Rev. Wayne Anthony in 1999, in Columbus, Georgia. The organization 
celebrated this occasion with an event that was held on November 6, 
2025, at 6 p.m. at the Columbus Country Club in Columbus, Georgia.
  For the past quarter-century, House of Heroes has been dedicated to 
honoring the service and sacrifice of our military and public safety 
veterans by providing essential home repairs and support services to 
them and their families. Through its tireless efforts, the organization 
has restored dignity and safety in the homes of over a thousand 
deserving veterans across our Nation.
  Rev. Wayne Anthony had a vision--a vision to ensure that those who 
have bravely served our country are not forgotten, and that they 
receive the assistance they so rightly deserve. This organization grew 
from a single humble initiative into a national movement, fueled by the 
commitment of volunteers, local chapters, and countless community 
partners who have rallied in support of these heroes.
  As we celebrate this significant milestone, let us remember the 
countless lives that House of Heroes has changed for the better. From 
repairing leaky roofs to building wheelchair ramps, their work goes 
beyond mere physical improvements. They restore hope, instill a sense 
of pride, and affirm the sacrifices made by our veterans and their 
families. Currently, the organization has a presence in 18 states and 
has repaired over 2,400 homes in the last 25 years. These repairs were 
valued at more than 24 million dollars, but there can be no value 
placed on what our Veterans have meant to our country.
  My Office has had a longstanding relationship with House of Heroes 
over the years. I have been honored to fly a flag over the United 
States Capitol in honor of those who have had repairs completed by this 
great organization.
  It is my sincere hope that the spirit of service and compassion that 
embodies House of Heroes continues to inspire communities across our 
country for many years to come. I extend my deepest gratitude to Rev. 
Wayne Anthony, the dedicated staff, and volunteers of House of Heroes, 
for their unwavering commitment to supporting our heroes. Albert Pike 
once said that ``What we do for ourselves dies with us, what we do for 
others, and the world remains and is immortal.'' What our Veterans 
sacrificed for us is immortal and what the House of Heroes has done 
across the country for them builds on that immortality.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join my wife, Vivian, and me, 
along with the 765,000 people of Georgia's 2nd Congressional District 
in honoring the House of Heroes on its silver anniversary and 
congratulating them on upholding the values of our great country. 
Furthermore, we know that they will continue to inspire future 
generations to serve those who have served us.
