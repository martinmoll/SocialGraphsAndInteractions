           By Mr. SELF:
       H.R. 5979.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to establish a uniform rule of 
     naturalization and to make all laws necessary and proper for 
     carrying into execution the powers vested by the Constitution 
     in the Government of the United States, pursuant to Article 
     I, Section 8, Clauses 4 and 18 of the Constitution of the 
     United States.
