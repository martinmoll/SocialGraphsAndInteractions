         HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF COMMISSIONER GEORGE COPADIS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. CHRIS PAPPAS

                            of new hampshire

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, November 7, 2025

  Mr. PAPPAS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of the 
retirement of George Copadis, the Commissioner of New Hampshire 
Employment Security (NHES). George's longstanding career in public 
service encourages us all to serve as strong advocates for our local 
communities and state. George will be retiring after decades as a 
public servant.
  As the NHES Commissioner and Chair of the Board of Commissioners on 
the Manchester Housing and Redevelopment Authority, George consistently 
demonstrated his commitment to his colleagues and his community. 
George's steady leadership guided the agency through the COVID-19 
pandemic, ensuring Granite Staters received efficient and reliable 
services. Also serving as the President of the Board of Directors at 
St. George Greek Orthodox Cathedral, George's generous and cooperative 
spirit knows no bounds.
  A lifelong resident of Manchester, George represents the very best of 
our community. George's compassion and kindness provided immense 
support to individuals experiencing challenging situations. His 
dedication to his work had a significantly positive impact on countless 
Granite Staters who benefitted from George's knowledge and support. But 
perhaps most of all, George will be remembered for the delicious 
baklava that he brought to special meetings.
  On behalf of the constituents of New Hampshire's First Congressional 
District, I commend George for his dedication to Granite Staters. My 
well wishes are with George and his family as he enjoys retirement.
