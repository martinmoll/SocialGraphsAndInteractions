   HONORING STAFF SERGEANT DAVID JACOBY'S 24 YEARS OF SERVICE IN THE 
    UNITED STATES NAVY RESERVES AND PENNSYLVANIA AIR NATIONAL GUARD

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 10, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Staff Sergeant David Jacoby. a distinguished servicemember, educator, 
and community leader from Churchville, Pennsylvania. His career 
represents an exceptional commitment to service to our Nation, to our 
students, and to our veterans.
  A native of Churchville, Staff Sergeant Jacoby is a graduate of East 
Stroudsburg University, where he earned his Bachelor of Science degree 
in Education. He continued his pursuit of academic excellence, earning 
a Master of Education in Curriculum and Instruction from National Louis 
University and attaining National Board Certification in 2001, which is 
one of the highest achievements in the teaching profession.
  Staff Sergeant Jacoby began his military career in 2002, enlisting in 
the United States Navy Reserve as an Intelligence Specialist. From 2002 
to 2005, he served as an enlisted sailor, supporting Naval Air Station 
Willow Grove and deploying repeatedly to the Sixth Fleet area of 
responsibility, including Naples, Italy, and Sigonella, Sicily, in 
support of the Global War on Terrorism. In recognition of his early 
contributions, he earned the Navy Achievement Medal, the Overseas 
Service Ribbon, and the Meritorious Service Medal.
  In 2006, he was commissioned as an officer in the United States Navy 
Reserve, where he served until 2017. During this period, he was 
assigned to the EUCOM Joint Analysis Center (JAC 1066), drilling at the 
Pentagon and later serving in Naples, Italy, with the Standing Joint 
Force Headquarters. For his support to NATO and Sixth Fleet operations, 
his unit earned the first of three Joint Meritorious Unit Awards. His 
outstanding analytical work resulted in the Joint Service Achievement 
Medal.
  In 2009, Staff Sergeant Jacoby was mobilized to Iraq, serving with 
Army Special Forces Command (CJSOTF-AP) at Balad Air Force Base. His 
contributions to joint special operations earned him the Joint 
Commendation Medal, the Global War on Terrorism Expeditionary Medal, 
the Iraq Campaign Medal, the Overseas Service Medal, and qualification 
as a Rifle Expert. He later served with the Office of Naval 
Intelligence (ONI 1201) and the Chief of Naval Operations N2/N6 
Directorate, where he also served as the Sexual Assault Prevention and 
Response (SAPR) and Command Managed Equal Opportunity (CMEO) advocate.
  After completing more than fifteen years of combined enlisted and 
commissioned service in the Navy Reserve, Staff Sergeant Jacoby 
continued his military commitment by enlisting in the Pennsylvania Air 
National Guard in 2018, where he served seven additional years with the 
111th Attack Wing, Logistics Readiness Squadron, Ground Transportation 
Flight.
  Over the course of his military career, Staff Sergeant Jacoby has 
earned the Joint Commendation Medal, Joint Service Achievement Medal, 
Navy and Marine Corps Achievement Medal, and multiple unit and campaign 
awards.
  Beyond his uniformed service, Staff Sergeant Jacoby has dedicated 
over 30 years to education and currently serves as the Social Studies 
Department Coordinator at Council Rock High School South. He is active 
in his community through the Veterans of Foreign Wars, American Legion, 
Free Masons, Shriners and Scottish Rite. He resides in Doylestown, 
Pennsylvania.
  Mr. Speaker, Staff Sergeant David Jacoby will be honored at his 
retirement ceremony on Sunday, November 9, 2025. His career reflects 
the highest ideals of service, leadership, and civic responsibility. On 
behalf of the people of Pennsylvania's 1st Congressional District, I 
thank him for his steadfast dedication to our Nation and our community. 
It is my privilege to recognize him in the United States House of 
Representatives.
