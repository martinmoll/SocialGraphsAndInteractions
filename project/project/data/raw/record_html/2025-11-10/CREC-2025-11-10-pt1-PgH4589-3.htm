                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Embolden us, O God, with Your Holy Spirit, that we would not shrink 
from our faith or prove timid in our testimony.
  In the face of all that seeks to overwhelm us--the uncertainty of 
these times, the disappointment in our situation, the cynicism we feel 
for our future--may our faith in You hold fast.
  For You have given us the power to speak a different truth, a truth 
that reveals Your healing strength and the renewed hope that You alone 
offer.
  You have shown us what it means to love: to notice when people are 
hurting, to feel compassion for those in need, to act on behalf of 
those whose welfare depends on our decisions.
  Grant us the self-discipline You have promised, that we would not 
advance our own priorities, but acknowledge Your will. For Your kingdom 
is not produced by our efforts, but is revealed when we yield ourselves 
to Your beneficence and justice.
  God of grace and mercy, we need not be afraid nor shall we falter 
when we walk confidently the direction in which You lead us and love 
unconditionally those You have called us to serve.
  In the strength of Your name, we pray for Your blessings on this day.
  Amen.
