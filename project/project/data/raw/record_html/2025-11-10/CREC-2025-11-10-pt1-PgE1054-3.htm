   HONORING THE 150TH ANNIVERSARY OF THE BROWNSVILLE FIRE DEPARTMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Monday, November 10, 2025

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
150th anniversary of the Brownsville Fire Department. Their unwavering 
service to our community stands as a testament to the courage, 
dedication, and spirit of public service that defines South Texas.
  Throughout their 150 years of service, the Brownsville Fire 
Department has repeatedly proven its devotion to the city of 
Brownsville and to South Texas. They play a critical role in protecting 
our community, from extinguishing fires to saving lives in the face of 
a natural disaster.
  Not only do they provide an essential service to our community, they 
also fill a role few others are brave enough to perform. I am proud to 
represent such courageous individuals, and I look forward to seeing 
their legacy of dedication and heroism continue for generations to 
come.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of my grateful constituents in South Texas, I 
congratulate the Brownsville Fire Department and all the brave men and 
women who have contributed to its mission over the past century and a 
half.
