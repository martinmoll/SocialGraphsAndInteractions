                 HONORING THE SERVICE OF LIONEL MENDOZA

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 27, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Firefighter Lionel Mendoza on the occasion of his retirement 
after 36 years and 7 months of dedicated service with the Corpus 
Christi Fire Department. Throughout his distinguished career, 
Firefighter Mendoza responded to thousands of emergency incidents and 
served our community with professionalism, courage, and an unwavering 
commitment to public safety.
  Mendoza served at Firehouses 1, 3, 8, 10, and 18, where he earned the 
respect of his colleagues and set a strong example for generations of 
firefighters to follow. His leadership, integrity, and devotion to duty 
have left a lasting impact on the Corpus Christi Fire Department and 
the community he so faithfully protected.
  As he concludes his final shift, we extend our deepest gratitude to 
Firefighter Mendoza and his family for their sacrifices and service. I 
congratulate him on a remarkable career and wish him continued success, 
good health, and fulfillment in his well-earned retirement.
