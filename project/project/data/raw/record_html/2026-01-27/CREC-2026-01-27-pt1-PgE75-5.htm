   HONORING THE 2025 VARSITY FOOTBALL TEAM OF ST. MARY'S HIGH SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOSH HARDER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 27, 2026

  Mr. HARDER of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 2025 
varsity football team of St. Mary's High School of Stockton, 
California, for winning the CIF Division Two AA State Championship and 
delivering a historic season for their school and community.
  The Rams completed the 2025 season with a thirteen-win, two-loss 
overall record and an undefeated league record, finishing first in 
conference play. Throughout the regular season, the team demonstrated 
disciplined execution, explosive scoring, and the ability to compete at 
a high level against strong opponents. Early setbacks tested the team's 
resolve, but players remained focused and committed to improvement. 
That resilience proved essential as the team entered postseason play 
prepared and confident.
  The Rams' playoff run was defined by perseverance and teamwork. Game 
after game, the team rose to the challenge, earning hard-fought 
victories through late-game execution and collective effort. Players 
stepped up in critical moments on both sides of the ball, while coaches 
and staff reinforced preparation and accountability. This steady 
approach allowed the team to advance through each round and earn a 
place in the state championship game.
  In the championship contest, the Rams secured a dramatic twenty-four 
to twenty-one victory to claim the state title. The win capped a 
postseason filled with determination, composure under pressure, and 
trust among teammates. Beyond the scoreboard, the team reflected a deep 
sense of brotherhood, built through years of shared experience and 
commitment. Their success brought pride to Stockton and served as a 
unifying moment for the community.
  The 2025 St. Mary's High School football team leaves behind a legacy 
of excellence, character, and resilience. I ask my colleagues to join 
me in congratulating these student-athletes, coaches, and supporters on 
a truly historic achievement.
