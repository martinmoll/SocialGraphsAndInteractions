                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                      HON. HERBERT C. CONAWAY, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 27, 2026

  Mr. CONAWAY. Mr. Speaker, on May 1, 2025, I was unable to cast my 
vote. Had I been present, I would have voted ``NAY'' on Roll Call No. 
113.
  I strongly oppose H.J. Res. 78, Providing for congressional 
disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
submitted by the United States Fish and Wildlife Service relating to 
``Endangered and Threatened Wildlife and Plants; Endangered Species 
Status for the San Francisco Bay-Delta Distinct Population Segment of 
the Longfin Smelt''. If enacted this resolution would threaten the 
recovery of the Longfin Smelt which was listed as endangered in 2024 by 
the U.S. Fish and Wildlife Service. It is imperative that we allow 
scientific experts to manage and determine which species are able to be 
delisted or enlisted.
