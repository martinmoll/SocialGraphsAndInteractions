                        REMEMBERING LORAN SCHMIT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE FLOOD

                              of nebraska

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, January 27, 2026

  Mr. FLOOD. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to honor a great Nebraskan who 
passed way last year, Loran Schmit.
  Loran's life of remarkable public service began with humble roots on 
his family's farm, shaping his deep understanding of rural life and 
inspiring his bold vision for Nebraska's future. Loran's leadership and 
vision were instrumental in developing our state's great ethanol 
industry--helping strengthen our ag economy for generations.
  First elected to the Unicameral in 1969, Loran devoted nearly 25 
years to serving our state. Among his many accomplishments, Loran 
helped create what would become the Nebraska Ethanol Board, an 
institution that continues to drive innovation in our ag economy.
  Known as an advocate for agriculture and biofuels, Loran remained 
devoted to these causes with an unwavering sense of purpose. His 
presence will be deeply missed.
  Our hearts and our prayers are with his family as we celebrate a life 
of service and lasting impact.
