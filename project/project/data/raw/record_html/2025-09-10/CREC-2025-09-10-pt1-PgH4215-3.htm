Chair declares the House in the Committee of the Whole House on the 
state of the Union for the further consideration of the bill, H.R. 
3838.
  Will the gentleman from Texas (Mr. Ellzey) kindly resume the chair.

                     In the Committee of the Whole

  Accordingly, the House resolved itself into the Committee of the 
Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 3838) to authorize appropriations for fiscal year 2026 
for military activities of the Department of Defense, for military 
construction, and for defense activities of the Department of Energy, 
to prescribe military personnel strengths for such fiscal year, and for 
other purposes, with Mr. Ellzey (Acting Chair) in the chair.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The Acting CHAIR. When the Committee of the Whole House rose earlier 
today, amendment No. 34, printed in part A of House Report 119-255, 
offered by the gentleman from New York (Mr. Meeks) had been disposed 
of.
