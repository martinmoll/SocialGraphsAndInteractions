           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 5253.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution of the 
     United States, which grants Congress the authority to provide 
     for the general welfare by appropriating Federal funds, and 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 3, which grants Congress the 
     power to regulate commerce with foreign nations.
