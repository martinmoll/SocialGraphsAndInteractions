                         RECOGNIZING LEE IRWIN

  (Mr. McCORMICK asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, it is my great honor to recognize Lee 
Irwin on the week of her 90th birthday.
  She has lived an extraordinary life. Mr. Speaker, she has spent 90 
years of serving the community, making tens of thousands of dresses and 
britches for those young people over in Africa that don't have very 
much, serving her community and the Methodist Church that she served as 
both a secretary and now a volunteer teaching accordion, and raising 
three lovely children, also known as my cousins.
  Welcome to just a great woman here in the House to recognize a life 
well lived. Happy birthday. I love you, and God bless you.
