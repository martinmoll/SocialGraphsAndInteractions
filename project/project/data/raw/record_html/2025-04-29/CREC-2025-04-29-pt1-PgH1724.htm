                    ENROLLED JOINT RESOLUTION SIGNED

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported and found truly an 
enrolled joint resolution of the House of the following title, which 
was thereupon signed by the Speaker:

       H.J. Res. 20. Joint Resolution providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, 
     of the rule submitted by the Department of Energy relating to 
     ``Energy Conservation Program: Energy Conservation Standards 
     for Consumer Gas-Fired Instantaneous Water Heaters''.
