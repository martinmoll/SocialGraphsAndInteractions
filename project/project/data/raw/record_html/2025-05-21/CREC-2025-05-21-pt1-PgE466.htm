  HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY, VIRGINIA FIRE DEPARTMENT VALOR 
                            AWARD RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 21, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Prince William County Fire Department Valor Award recipients, 
recognizing the extraordinary bravery and selfless service demonstrated 
by its firefighters, paramedics, and rescue personnel. These awards 
reflect the courageous actions of the department's dedicated men and 
women who put themselves in harm's way to protect and serve the 
community in its most critical moments.
  Each recipient exemplifies what it means to act with valor--facing 
danger without hesitation, responding swiftly in times of crisis, and 
placing the safety of others above their own. Whether battling intense 
fires, delivering life-saving medical aid, or navigating complex 
emergency situations, these professionals demonstrate unwavering 
resolve and deep compassion for those they serve.
  Their heroism builds trust and deepens the connections that hold our 
community together, fostering a shared sense of safety, resilience, and 
unity across neighborhoods, schools, and families. Today, we recognize 
and celebrate the collective courage and commitment of these 
individuals, whose actions inspire us all and uphold the highest ideals 
of public service.
  We are deeply grateful for their bravery, and proud to honor the 
Prince William County Fire Department for their outstanding dedication 
to the safety and well-being of our community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the Prince 
William County Fire Department for their remarkable contributions to 
our community.
