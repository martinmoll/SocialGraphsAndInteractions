 HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY, VIRGINIA POLICE DEPARTMENT VALOR 
                            AWARD RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 21, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Prince William County Police Department Valor Award recipients, 
recognizing the exceptional bravery and selfless service demonstrated 
by its officers. These awards reflect the courageous efforts of many 
dedicated men and women across the department including officers, 
detectives, sergeants, and specialists alike, who put their lives on 
the line to protect our community.
  Each recipient represents a powerful example of valor and commitment, 
embodying the highest ideals of public service. Whether responding to 
immediate threats, conducting investigations, or supporting victims in 
moments of crisis, these individuals demonstrate unwavering dedication 
to the safety and well-being of the people they serve.
  Their heroic actions strengthen the bonds between law enforcement and 
the community, building trust, security, and resilience. Today, we 
recognize and celebrate the collective courage and sacrifice of these 
officers, whose service uplifts us all and sets a powerful standard of 
integrity and compassion.
  We are grateful for their bravery and proud to honor the Prince 
William County Police Department for their outstanding commitment to 
justice and public safety.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the Prince 
William County Police Department for their remarkable contributions to 
our community.
