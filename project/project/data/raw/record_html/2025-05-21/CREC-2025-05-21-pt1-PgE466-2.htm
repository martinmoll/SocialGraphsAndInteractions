                    RECOGNIZING PRICILLA CRUZ-SEADO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 21, 2025

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the outstanding 
contributions that Pricilla Cruz-Seado has made to our community 
through her work with New Beginnings Youth Shelter, which serves 
homeless kids aged 10-17 who are between foster homes, abandoned, or 
homeless. The whole team jumps in to take care of the kids that have no 
one else looking out for them. They teach kids structure, respect, 
understanding and so much more in a loving environment. The team really 
cares, and each member is deeply invested in the kids' long-term 
success.
  It is clear Pricilla Cruz-Seado has made a profoundly positive impact 
on our community, and I am grateful for her service. May we all strive 
to serve our communities with the same dedication and humility.
