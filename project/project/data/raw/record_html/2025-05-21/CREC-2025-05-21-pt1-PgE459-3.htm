  HONORING THE PRINCE WILLIAM COUNTY, VIRGINIA DEPARTMENT OF FIRE AND 
                     RESCUE VALOR AWARD RECIPIENTS

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 21, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and celebrate the 
Prince William County Fire and Rescue Department Valor Award 
recipients, recognizing the extraordinary bravery and selfless service 
demonstrated by its firefighters, paramedics, and rescue personnel. 
These awards reflect the courageous actions of the department's 
dedicated men and women who put themselves in harm's way to protect and 
serve the community in its most critical moments.
  Each recipient exemplifies true valor, facing danger without 
hesitation, providing life-saving aid, and prioritizing the safety of 
others in the most challenging emergencies. Their courage and 
compassion strengthen the bonds within our community, fostering trust, 
resilience, and a shared sense of security. Today, we honor their 
collective bravery and unwavering commitment to public service.
  Whether responding to fires, medical emergencies, or natural 
disasters, these individuals demonstrate an unyielding commitment to 
preserving life and property. Their professionalism and readiness in 
the face of uncertainty set an example for us all and serve as a 
reminder of the impact that first responders have on the lives of those 
they protect.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the Prince 
William County Fire and Rescue team for their remarkable contributions 
to our community.
