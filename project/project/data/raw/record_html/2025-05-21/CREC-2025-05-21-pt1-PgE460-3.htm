                          RECOGNIZING JAY POST

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 21, 2025

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Jay Post--a 
dedicated patriot, family man, and valued member of his community. Jay 
moved to Westfield, New Jersey, in 1951. He graduated from Westfield 
High School in 1957 and earned his degree from Siena College in 1961. 
Soon after, he completed Officer Basic Training at the U.S. Army Field 
Artillery School in Fort Sill, Oklahoma, and was deployed to Korea with 
the 7th Infantry Division, where he helped defend the Demilitarized 
Zone. After being honorably discharged as a First Lieutenant in 1963, 
Jay worked for Ford Motor Company and later earned an MBA in Finance 
from Columbia University. In 1965, he married his wife, Arleen. They 
returned to Westfield to raise their four children, and in 1995, moved 
to Mountainside, where they still live today.
  I am pleased to join the Borough of Mountainside in recognizing Jay 
Post as a Co-Grand Marshal of this year's Memorial Day Parade. His life 
of service, leadership, and love of country is truly deserving of our 
gratitude and shall be remembered for years to come.
