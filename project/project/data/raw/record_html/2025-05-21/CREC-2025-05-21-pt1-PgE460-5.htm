   SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF 150 YEARS OF FIRST BAPTIST CHURCH OF BILOXI

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. MIKE EZELL

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, May 21, 2025

  Mr. EZELL. Mr. Speaker, this year, the First Baptist Church of Biloxi 
celebrates 150 incredible years of ministry and service.
  Since 1875, this church has stood as a beacon of faith, hope, and 
love--guiding generations through times of challenge and celebration. 
Their commitment to spreading the Gospel, serving the Gulf Coast 
community, and nurturing spiritual growth has made a lasting impact.
  This milestone is not just about looking back--it's a powerful 
reminder of the faithfulness of God and the strength of a community 
rooted in Him.
  I thank Pastor Gibson, the leadership team, and every member of this 
congregation for their dedication, their prayers, and their service. 
May God continue to bless them as they move forward into the next 
chapter of this remarkable journey.
  Congratulations, and happy 150th anniversary.
