                  HONORING FIREFIGHTER HOWARD BENNETT

  (Mr. NORCROSS asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. NORCROSS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Howard Bennett, 
a Camden City, New Jersey, firefighter who tragically lost his life on 
the job last week.
  He served his community for 26 years, and he will forever be 
remembered as husband to Fran, father to David and Jenna, a brother, a 
friend, and a protector.
  When it came to the fire boat, Howard's knowledge was second to none. 
He treated it like an extension of himself, ensuring that it was always 
ready for action.
  His fellow firefighters will remember him for his resourcefulness. 
When others thought something couldn't be fixed, Howard found a way to 
make it work.
  He was a trusted mentor to young firefighters, always willing to 
help, simply because that is who he was.
  Firefighter Howard Bennett served with integrity, humility, and 
heart. Today, I honor his service and extend my deepest condolences to 
his loved ones. May he rest in peace.
