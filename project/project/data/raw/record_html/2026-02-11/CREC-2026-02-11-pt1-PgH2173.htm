               MAKING IT HARDER FOR U.S. CITIZENS TO VOTE

  (Mr. LANDSMAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. LANDSMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today because the House just voted 
on the SAVE America Act, which will make it harder for tens of millions 
of Americans to vote, not noncitizens. Noncitizens can't vote. They 
can't register to vote. If they do, they will be deported.
  American citizens are being denied their right to vote by requiring, 
in this bill, all kinds of paperwork to register, all kinds of 
paperwork to vote.
  Three examples: For married women, there are 70 million women with an 
ID that does not match their birth certificate. It will be harder for 
them to register to vote. Seniors, especially those who have been 
voting by mail, it will be harder for them to vote. College students 
and those who serve our country abroad would have to come home in order 
to register to vote.
  This is a terrible, un-American, undemocratic, honestly insane bill 
that the Senate should not take up.
