 PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 3370 
                             AND H.R. 1742

  Ms. HOULAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that I may 
hereafter be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 3370, PROTECT 
Firefighters Act, and H.R. 1742, Access to Reproductive Care for 
Servicemembers Act, bills originally introduced by Representative 
Sherrill of New Jersey, for the purpose of adding cosponsors and 
requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentlewoman from Pennsylvania?
  There was no objection.
