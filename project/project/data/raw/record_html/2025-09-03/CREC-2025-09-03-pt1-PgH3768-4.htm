                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Sovereign God, establish Your purpose for this day. Though we come to 
the business of this place with many plans and purposes, our minds 
filled with cares and concerns, and our spirits guided by the best of 
intentions, none of this will prosper unless we have Your blessing.
  So we humble ourselves under Your mighty hand, laying before You all 
that we could hope for or desire, that You will exalt only that which 
reveals Your grace plan. We cast our worries before You, trusting that 
You will care for us.
  Into Your keeping we commend this day and pray that all that we say 
and do will bring glory to You.
  Certain of Your abiding presence, and sure of Your salvation, we pray 
in the power of Your name.
  Amen.
