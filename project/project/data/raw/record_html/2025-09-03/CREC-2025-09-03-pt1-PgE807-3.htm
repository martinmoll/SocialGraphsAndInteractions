 HONORING THE EAST JORDAN AREA CHAMBER OF COMMERCE AND THE HISTORY OF 
                         THE PORTSIDE ART FAIR

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 3, 2025

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
East Jordan Area Chamber of Commerce for its continued leadership in 
hosting the 2025 Portside Art Fair, one of Northern Michigan's most 
prestigious and long-standing artistic traditions.
  Founded in August 1962, the Portside Art Fair was the vision of a 
dedicated group of East Jordan residents who believed their community 
was the ideal setting for local artists to share their work with the 
public. What began with just 30 participants has now grown to its full 
capacity of 75, reflecting not only the fair's increasing popularity 
but also the vibrant creativity it continues to inspire.
  Held along the beautiful shores of Lake Charlevoix, the Portside Art 
Fair honors the deep connection between our region's natural beauty and 
the inspiration it provides to artists. It showcases the work of 
established and emerging artists from East Jordan and throughout 
Northern Michigan. Each year, a qualified judge in the visual arts 
carefully selects standout pieces to be honored with inclusion in the 
prestigious Cygred Riley Gallery at Elm Point.
  The standards for recognition at the Portside Art Fair are high, and 
the quality of submissions reflects the extraordinary talent of our 
local artists. The fair serves as a powerful reminder of the important 
role that the arts play in strengthening our communities, inspiring 
future generations, and celebrating the unique character of Northern 
Michigan.
  Mr. Speaker, it is my distinct honor to commend the East Jordan Area 
Chamber of Commerce for organizing the 2025 Portside Art Fair and for 
its unwavering support of the arts. I offer my best wishes to all 
participating artists and look forward to the continued success of this 
treasured cultural event.
