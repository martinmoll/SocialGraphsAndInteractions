  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Gimenez). Pursuant to House Resolution 
672 and rule XVIII, the Chair declares the House in the Committee of 
the Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration

                     In the Committee of the Whole

  Accordingly, the House resolved itself into the Committee of the 
Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 4553) making appropriations for energy and water 
development and related agencies for the fiscal year ending September 
30, 2026, and for other purposes, with Mr. Moran (Acting Chair) in the 
chair.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.

a request for recorded vote on amendment No. 30, printed in House 
Report 119-232, offered by the gentleman from Pennsylvania (Mr. Perry) 
had been postponed.

those amendments printed in House Report 119-232, on which further 
proceedings were postponed, in the following order: Amendment Nos. 23, 
24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 29, and 30 by Mr. Perry of Pennsylvania.
  The Chair will reduce to 2 minutes the minimum time for any 
electronic vote in this series.
