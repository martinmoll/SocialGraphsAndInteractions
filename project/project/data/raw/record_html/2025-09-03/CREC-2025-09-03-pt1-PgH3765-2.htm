                      CONGRATULATING PIERZ LAKERS

  (Mrs. Fischbach of Minnesota was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Mrs. FISCHBACH. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate the Pierz Lakers 
in winning the Minnesota Baseball Association's amateur State 
championship.
  The Lakers fought valiantly through the Class C tournament, defeating 
the Le Sueur Braves 11-1 to win their first-ever State championship.
  It is great to see the tradition of town ball alive and well in 
Minnesota.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate the Pierz Lakers on their victory.
