       HONORING ROBERT AND LINDA McNEILL ON 25 YEARS OF MARRIAGE

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 3, 2025

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate Robert and 
Linda McNeill on the occasion of their 25th wedding anniversary. It is 
with great pleasure and excitement that I share this wonderful news.
  Robert, a man of integrity, loyalty, and strength, has devoted his 
life to family and loved ones. Linda, a kind and graceful soul, 
treasures her family values and cherishes every moment with those 
dearest to her.
  Fate brought Robert and his beautiful wife, Linda, together at the 
Curwood festival in Owosso, Michigan. In a serendipitous moment, as he 
picked her up, their eyes locked, and their lifelong love began. Robert 
and Linda's story reached a cherished milestone on September 23, 2000, 
when they were united in marriage. Since then, they have raised three 
sons together and have continued to grow not just as individuals, but 
also as partners in life.
  Love and marriage represent the joining of two hearts and lives, 
built on a foundation of trust, respect, and shared dreams. Robert and 
Linda embody the promise to stand together through life's joys and 
challenges, growing stronger as individuals and as partners. Their love 
story is a testament to the beauty of finding someone who not only 
inspires you but also walks beside you every step of the way.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in congratulating Robert and Linda on 
this momentous milestone and celebration of their long-lasting love. I 
wish them boundless happiness, love, and success as they continue on 
their journey together.
