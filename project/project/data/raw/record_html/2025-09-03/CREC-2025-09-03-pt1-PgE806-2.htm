                          HONORING CARL LUCAS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, September 3, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. 
Carl Lucas--a dedicated educational leader, proud native of Hollandale, 
Mississippi, and tireless advocate for student achievement and equity 
in education.
  Mr. Lucas began his career as a secondary mathematics teacher, where 
he created a classroom culture of rigor and relevance. His students 
consistently demonstrated significant growth on state assessments, 
earning him recognition for narrowing achievement gaps and instilling 
academic discipline.
  As Principal of Simmons High School, Mr. Lucas set a bold goal of 
earning a B rating. Through systems that raised expectations, improved 
instruction, and increased student accountability, he led the school to 
measurable gains in academic performance, graduation rates, and 
climate.
  In addition to school leadership, Mr. Lucas developed and expanded 
transformative academic programs, including:
  Dual Enrollment & Career Pathways with Mississippi Delta Community 
College and Tougaloo College.
  ACT Prep Initiatives, including summer boot camps and classroom 
integration.
  Credit Recovery & Summer Learning, led by certified teachers.
  Middle College Readiness Programs for college and career 
acceleration.
  Student Support Systems involving mentoring and behavior contracts.
  Mr. Lucas holds a Bachelor's Degree in Mathematics and a Master's 
Degree from Mississippi Valley State University, as well as an 
Education Specialist Degree from Belhaven University.
  He is married to his high school sweetheart, Monet Dye-Lucas, and 
together they are the proud parents of Carl'Kaisha, Carl Jr., Carlnell, 
and Carlandria.
  Mr. Lucas leads with heart, vision, and integrity. He believes every 
child deserves access to a quality education and the opportunity to 
thrive. His life's work reflects that belief, right in the community 
that raised him.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Mr. Carl 
Lucas for his unwavering service to education and his lasting impact on 
the Mississippi Delta.
