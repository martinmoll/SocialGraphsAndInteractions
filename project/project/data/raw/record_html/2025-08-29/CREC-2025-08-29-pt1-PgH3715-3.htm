                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, The Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, today, in the world around us, there is much 
anger and much violence. This is unsettling and even frightening for 
us. May Your presence in our hearts gift us with a supernatural peace. 
May our faces radiate this peace. May we be instruments of this peace 
in this hallowed place and wherever we go.
  We pray this in Your most holy name.
  Amen.
