       FAIR INVESTMENT OPPORTUNITIES FOR PROFESSIONAL EXPERTS ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 3394) to amend the Securities Act of 1933 to codify 
certain qualifications of individuals as accredited investors for 
purposes of the securities laws, as amended, on which the yeas and nays 
were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentlewoman from Missouri (Mrs. Wagner) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 397, 
nays 12, not voting 23, as follows:
