  CONGRATULATING MR. TRAVIS TREJO ON 8 YEARS OF SERVICE TO MARYLAND'S 
                      FIRST CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 23, 2025

  Mr. HARRIS of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my 
Legislative Director, Travis Trejo, on celebrating his eighth 
anniversary of employment with my office. Travis is a dedicated staff 
member who has repeatedly demonstrated his commitment to my legislative 
goals. With loyalty as rare as Travis' on Capitol Hill, I am truly 
grateful to have him as the leader of my legislative team.
  Mr. Trejo has risen through the ranks of my office, beginning as 
Staff Assistant to currently serving as my Legislative Director. He has 
always taken on a supporting role, working tirelessly to ensure my 
legislative agenda is met. He is a mentor to not only the legislative 
team, but also to other members of my staff.
  It is important that Representatives recognize outstanding staff such 
as Mr. Trejo. Oftentimes, the work of these staffers goes unnoticed but 
is nonetheless critical in maintaining the functions of our government. 
Travis is a true patriot, and Maryland's First Congressional District 
undoubtedly owes him a great debt of gratitude.
  I thank Travis.
