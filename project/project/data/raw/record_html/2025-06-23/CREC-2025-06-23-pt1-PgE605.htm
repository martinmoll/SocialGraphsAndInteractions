                      CONGRATULATING KAI AGYEMANG

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. RICK W. ALLEN

                               of georgia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 23, 2025

  Mr. ALLEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my constituent, 
Kai Agyemang, upon his acceptance to the United States Air Force 
Academy.
  As a future cadet, he will be joining the ranks of the world's most 
fearsome fighting force, the United States Air Force.
  Guided by the motto ``Aim High . . . Fly-Fight-Win'' the U.S. Air 
Force is the world's preeminent force in air, space, and cyberspace.
  Throughout our history, American Airmen have demonstrated tremendous 
courage and strength, beating back tyranny to the ends of the earth.
  I was honored to sponsor Kai's nomination, and I am confident that 
his talents will distinguish him among his peers both during his time 
at Air Force Academy and thereafter.
  I congratulate Kai. I know that he will make this Nation proud.
