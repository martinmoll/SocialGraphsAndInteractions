                    SANCTION SEA PIRATES ACT OF 2025

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1998) to require the imposition of sanctions with 
respect to foreign persons engaged in piracy, and for other purposes, 
as amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Tennessee (Mr. Burchett) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 392, 
nays 14, not voting 25, as follows:
