               NEWSLETTER FROM CONGRESSMAN CHUCK EDWARDS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, June 23, 2025

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I include in the Record the following 
newsletter to give an update to my constituents on debris removal after 
Hurricane Helene.

       Dear Friend: There has been an important development 
     regarding debris clean-up efforts from Hurricane Helene. 
     After hearing from residents and stakeholders about improper 
     debris removal, FEMA and the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers 
     (USACE) have agreed to improve oversight and precision in 
     debris removal operations.
       The action plan moving forward is:
       Property owners are encouraged to mark debris areas of 
     concern with a red flag.
       Flagged areas will not be disturbed until the property 
     owner, the county, and USACE engage in a consultation.
       Counties will then make final decisions based on the need 
     to protect downstream infrastructure while respecting private 
     property.
       This agreement will help address the top concerns my team 
     has heard from Western North Carolinians affected by debris 
     removal by:
       Preventing the removal of more debris than necessary.
       Adequately protecting wildlife habitat.
       Ensuring landowners receive due consideration through the 
     process.
       I believe this plan will increase the quality of clean-up 
     efforts, and my office will continue to assess the debris 
     removal process and listen to your concerns as we work 
     together to return to normalcy as quickly as possible.
       For additional information on debris removal projects, 
     visit my website. You will find maps that note areas where 
     debris removal has been completed (marked in green) and plans 
     for upcoming debris removal projects.
           With my warmest regards,
                                                    Chuck Edwards,
     Member of Congress.
