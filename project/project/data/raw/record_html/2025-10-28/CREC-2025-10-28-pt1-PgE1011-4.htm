      HONORING LAKISHA MITCHELL-KEITH AS ONE OF CALIFORNIA'S 8TH 
               CONGRESSIONAL DISTRICT'S WOMEN OF THE YEAR

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. JOHN GARAMENDI

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 28, 2025

  Mr. GARAMENDI. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Lakisha Mitchell-
Keith, recognized as one of California's 8th Congressional District's 
Women of the Year. A proud Richmond native and third-generation member 
of the Mitchell family, renowned for civic and faith-based service, 
Lakisha has long exemplified leadership rooted in purpose and 
compassion. Her 15-year career as a federal law enforcement officer and 
supervisor with the Department of Justice and Department of Homeland 
Security demonstrated integrity, professionalism, and an enduring 
commitment to justice and safety. After her federal service, Lakisha 
earned her Juris Doctor from Golden Gate University School of Law with 
a specialization in Public Interest Law, preparing her to advance 
equity and uplift underserved communities. She brings that same passion 
to her volunteer and advocacy work across Richmond and the Bay Area. A 
lifelong member of Bethel AME Church in San Francisco, she serves in 
multiple leadership roles, including President of the Lay Organization, 
Trustee Board Member, and is a part of the Stewardship & Finance 
Commission and Strategic Planning Team. She also holds regional 
leadership positions in the California Annual Conference Lay 
Organization and the Conference Board of Trustees. In 2025, she 
embraced a new chapter in her spiritual journey, becoming a licensed 
Licentiate in the AME Church. Lakisha's recent advocacy at Stege 
Elementary School further demonstrates her drive for educational 
justice. As a School Site Council member, she helped bring visibility 
to longstanding facility concerns and was a key force behind renewed 
public commitment to the school's future. Whether in law enforcement, 
legal advocacy, faith leadership, or local activism, Lakisha Mitchell-
Keith brings courage, vision, and integrity to everything she does. Her 
life's work reflects the highest ideals of public service. On behalf of 
the people of California's 8th Congressional District, it is my honor 
to recognize Lakisha Mitchell-Keith for her devotion to justice, 
community empowerment, and spiritual service.
