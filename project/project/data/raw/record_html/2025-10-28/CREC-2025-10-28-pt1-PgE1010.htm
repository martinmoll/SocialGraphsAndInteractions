                     HONORING CLAY COUNTY VETERANS

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. AARON BEAN

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 28, 2025

  Mr. BEAN of Florida. Mr. Speaker, Veterans Day is a day when our 
Nation pauses to give thanks to the brave men and women who have worn 
the uniform of the United States of America.
  In Clay County, Florida, honoring our veterans isn't just something 
we do once a year, it is part of who we are. This county's history is 
woven with stories of service: from generations who trained at Camp 
Blanding, to those who served overseas in defense of freedom, and to 
the many veterans who continue to serve our community today as 
teachers, business owners, first responders, and volunteers.
  Clay County's patriotism runs deep. You see it every day, in the 
flags flying over our neighborhoods, the quiet moments of respect at 
community events, and the countless ways our citizens care for those 
who've returned home from service. This is a county that not only 
thanks its veterans but walks beside them.
  Today, as we reflect on their courage and sacrifice, we remember that 
our freedom was, and is, secured by ordinary Americans who answered an 
extraordinary call. We honor those who served, those who continue to 
serve, and those who made the ultimate sacrifice in defense of our 
country.
  So, on behalf of a grateful Nation, We thank the veterans and 
military families of Clay County. They are the living heart of our 
freedom, and we are thankful every single day for all they have given. 
God bless our veterans, God bless Clay County, and God bless the United 
States of America.
