                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Creator God, repair and renew this world, the work of Your wisdom, 
designed to reveal Your own divine will, for we are broken and divided, 
in need and at odds, angry and hungry, unnerved and unmoored from the 
foundation of unity You have intended for us.
  Whatever our pretenses for separation, whether dissatisfaction with 
our situation or disagreement with the other side, we deserve Your 
rebuke, for You compel us as believers to respect the body You have put 
together, admonishing us to have mutual and equal concern for one 
another.
  In these days, when parts of the body are suffering, reveal to us how 
all of us suffer with them. When we cease to put love over legislation, 
our integrity weakens.
  When we honor one another, working to ensure each may enjoy sure and 
certain security of one's basic needs and demonstrating our shared 
commitment to the common good, we then reveal Your grace plan and 
glorify You with our mutual love.
  Grant us then the humility to serve this body as we seek to serve 
this country and You in this time.
  In Your sovereign name we pray.
  Amen.
