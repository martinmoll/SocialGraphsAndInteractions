 INTRODUCTION OF THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA GOVERNMENT TITLE EQUALITY ACT

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. ELEANOR HOLMES NORTON

                      of the district of columbia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, October 28, 2025

  Ms. NORTON. Mr. Speaker, today, I introduce the District of Columbia 
Government Title Equality Act. This bill would amend the District of 
Columbia Home Rule Act to redesignate the Mayor as the Governor, the 
Council as the Legislative Assembly, the Councilmembers as 
Representatives and the Chair of the Council as the Speaker. The new 
titles are the ones used in the State of Washington, Douglass 
Commonwealth constitution, which D.C. adopted in 2016 in anticipation 
of enactment of the Washington, D.C. Admission Act, which would make 
D.C. the 51st state.
  I introduce this bill to highlight to the American people and Members 
of Congress that the transition to statehood would be seamless, because 
D.C. already operates as the functional equivalent of a state, 
providing state-, county- and city-level services, despite being denied 
voting representation in Congress and full local self-government. This 
bill would not change any legal authorities.
  I urge my colleagues to support this bill.
