                        WELCOMING DR. JOHN CROSS

  The SPEAKER. Without objection, Mr. Buchanan of Florida was 
recognized for 1 minute.
  There was no objection.
  Mr. BUCHANAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Dr. John Cross, who 
just delivered this morning's opening prayer.
  John is the senior pastor of First Baptist Church of Sarasota and has 
been the pastor for many, many years. John committed his life to Christ 
in his childhood and ultimately decided to pursue a career in the 
ministry.
  He is a proud graduate of Mercy University and Southwestern Baptist 
Theological Seminary, where he earned his master of divinity degree and 
his doctor of ministry.
  His leadership has extended beyond the local church, having served as 
the president of the Florida Baptist Convention Pastors' Conference and 
president of the Florida Baptist State Convention.
  John is married to Dawn, and together they have been blessed with 5 
children, 3 sons-in-law, a daughter-in-law, and 10 grandkids.
  I deeply value his faith leadership in my life, my family's life, and 
our community. I am grateful for his presence here today.
