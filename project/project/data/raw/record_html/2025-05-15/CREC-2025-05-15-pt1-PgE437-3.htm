                         HONORING JEREMY ZAVITZ

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ILHAN OMAR

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 15, 2025

  Ms. OMAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Jeremy 
Zavitz, a dedicated educator, loving husband, and devoted father who 
touched the lives of so many in our community. Mr. Zavitz passed away 
on February 22, 2025, after a 24-year fight with brain cancer, but his 
legacy will live on in the hearts of all who knew him.
  Mr. Zavitz was born in Minneapolis and was a proud graduate of Orono 
High School. He later attended the University of St. Thomas, where he 
earned a degree in history and teaching. Initially, Mr. Zavitz embarked 
on a career in real estate and business development but ultimately 
found his calling in education. In 2012, Mr. Zavitz began his tenure at 
DeLaSalle High School as a social studies teacher, where his vibrant 
spirit, unwavering dedication, and passion for teaching radiated.
  Known for his energetic and engaging classes, Mr. Zavitz had the 
unique ability to transform lessons into powerful stories that 
captivated his students. His approach to teaching was rooted in 
authenticity and compassion, making every student feel welcome, valued, 
and heard. Colleagues and students alike remember him not just for his 
knowledge and dedication to teaching but also for his commitment to 
living a life of purpose.
  Jeremy is survived by his loving wife, Maud Zavitz, and their 7-year-
old son, Edmund John Zavitz. He leaves behind a legacy not only through 
his family but through the generations of students who carry forward 
his lessons of kindness, authenticity, and perseverance.
  Mr. Speaker, Jeremy Zavitz was a true example of what it means to 
lead a life of service to others, and his impact on DeLaSalle High 
School, his community in South Minneapolis, and his family will never 
be forgotten. Today, we honor his memory and express our deepest 
condolences to his family and all who were fortunate enough to know 
him.
