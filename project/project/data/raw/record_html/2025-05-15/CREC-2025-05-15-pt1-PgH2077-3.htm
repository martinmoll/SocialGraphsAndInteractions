  FEDERAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER SERVICE WEAPON PURCHASE ACT OF 2025

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 2255) to 
allow Federal law enforcement officers to purchase retired service 
weapons, and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 234, 
nays 182, not voting 17, as follows:
