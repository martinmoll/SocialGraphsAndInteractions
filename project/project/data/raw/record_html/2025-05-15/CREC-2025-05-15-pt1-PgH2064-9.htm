                  DALLAS WELCOMES EXCEPTIONAL LEADERS

  (Ms. JOHNSON of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. JOHNSON of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today during National 
Police Week to recognize a new chapter in public safety leadership for 
the city of Dallas.
  On May 5, Dallas officially welcomed two exceptional leaders, Daniel 
Comeaux as chief of police and Justin Ball as chief of fire-rescue.
  Chief Comeaux has over 30 years of law enforcement experience, 
including his recent role as Special Agent in Charge of the DEA's 
Houston Field Division. His leadership has been marked by intelligence-
driven crime reduction and a collaborative approach to community 
policing.
  Chief Ball, a nearly 30-year veteran of Dallas Fire-Rescue, has risen 
through every rank since joining the department in 1997. His dedication 
and innovative leadership have been instrumental in advancing the 
department's mission to protect and serve our community.
  These appointments reflect Dallas' commitment to public safety and 
community trust. I commend City Manager Kimberly Tolbert for her 
thoughtful selection of these leaders.
  I thank Chiefs Comeaux and Ball for their service. I look forward to 
supporting their efforts here in Congress to help ensure the safety and 
well-being of all Dallas residents.
