            HONORING BRAVE MEN AND WOMEN OF LAW ENFORCEMENT

  (Mr. EDWARDS asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1

[[Page H2065]]

minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today during National Police Week to 
honor the brave men and women in law enforcement who have made the 
ultimate sacrifice in the line of duty. These officers stood on the 
front lines to protect our communities, uphold the rule of law, and 
defend the safety of our citizens, often at a great personal risk.
  We remember their courage, their selflessness, and their unwavering 
commitment to service. Behind each badge was a story, a family, and a 
legacy that will not be forgotten.
  We offer our deepest gratitude and our heartfelt condolences to the 
families who carry the burden of their loss. Their loved ones are 
heroes, and their memory will live on in our prayers and in the 
freedoms they helped safeguard.
  Let's continue to support all those who wear the badge and to honor 
those who gave everything to protect us.
