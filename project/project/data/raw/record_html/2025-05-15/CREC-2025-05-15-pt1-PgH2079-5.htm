                      IN MEMORY OF NIHAAL GRANDHI

  (Mr. SUBRAMANYAM asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SUBRAMANYAM. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
memory of Nihaal Grandhi, a brother, son, and student from Loudon 
County who tragically passed away in an accident over the weekend.
  At just 19 years old, Nihaal was a proud graduate of Rock Ridge High 
School and a freshman at Indiana University's Kelley School of 
Business. He was known for his joyful smile and ability to inspire a 
sense of community wherever he went.
  As a volleyball team captain and a STEM competition coach, Nihaal was 
always attuned to the needs of those he led. Whether mentoring a 
teammate

[[Page H2080]]

through a difficult challenge or calmly navigating high-stakes 
competitions, he knew when to guide or be a reassuring presence.
  Above all, he was a man of deep principles and kindness.
  Mr. Speaker, I and so many in my community are sending prayers to his 
family and friends and all who had the privilege to know him.
  May he rest in peace.
