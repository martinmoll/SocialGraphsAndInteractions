         HONORING THE MOREAU CATHOLIC HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC SWALWELL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 15, 2025

  Mr. SWALWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Moreau Catholic 
High School Football Team as they attend their ring ceremony today, May 
15, 2025. The Mariners are being celebrated in the rotunda in Hayward's 
City Hall after making high school and local history for winning the 
California Interscholastic Federation 7-AA Football Championship--the 
first in school history and a first for the city of Hayward, as well.
  The Moreau Catholic Mariners secured this win on December 13, 2024 
with a resounding victory over the Cardinals of Lindsay High School by 
a score of 42-8 in the title game. Prior to this victory, no Hayward 
high school football team had competed for a CIF championship.
  I want to extend a heartfelt congratulations to each of the Moreau 
Catholic High School football players, parents, support staff, and to 
Head Coach Keith Minor, who joined the coaching staff just three years 
prior to this momentous victory. This achievement is a testament to all 
the hard work and dedication put in by the students and adults 
throughout the season. Moments like this make all the long hours, early 
morning, and late-night practices worth it.
  I am proud to represent California's 14th Congressional District 
because of hardworking individuals such as these young athletes and 
coaching staff. Congratulations on their victory and best wishes for a 
wonderful celebration.
