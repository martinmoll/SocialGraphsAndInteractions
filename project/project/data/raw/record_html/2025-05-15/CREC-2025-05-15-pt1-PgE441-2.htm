                 MOUNT OLIVE BAPTIST CHURCH DEDICATION

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. THOMAS H. KEAN, JR.

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 15, 2025

  Mr. KEAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the dedication of 
the historic Mount Olive Baptist Church in Mount Olive Township, New 
Jersey. On June 7th, the community will gather to celebrate the 
completion of a renovation project, honoring the legacy of this 
cherished place of worship. The Mount Olive Baptist Church has been a 
cornerstone of faith, fellowship, and community for generations. Its 
restoration represents not only the preservation of an important 
historic landmark but also the enduring strength of the congregation 
and the broader Mount Olive community. I commend the Mount Olive 
Township and the Mount Olive Township Historical Society for their 
dedication to this project. Their efforts ensure that future 
generations will continue to find connection and purpose within the 
church's walls. As we mark this special occasion, I extend my 
congratulations to the church leadership, parishioners, and community 
members whose vision and hard work brought this renovation to fruition. 
May the Mount Olive Baptist Church continue to serve as a beacon of 
faith and hope for many years to come.
