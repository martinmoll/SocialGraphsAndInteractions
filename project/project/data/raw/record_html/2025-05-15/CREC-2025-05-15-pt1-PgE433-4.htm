              CONGRATULATING WADE WITMER ON HIS RETIREMENT

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. GERALD E. CONNOLLY

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 15, 2025

  Mr. CONNOLLY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Mr. Wade Witmer on his retirement after decades of dedicated and 
exemplary service to the American public. Mr. Witmer's commitment to 
public safety is unparalleled, and our country is significantly safer 
today because of his tireless work throughout his career.
  Mr. Witmer began his federal service at the White House 
Communications Agency, where he served with distinction under four U.S. 
presidents--George W. Bush, Barack Obama, Donald J. Trump, and Joseph 
R. Biden. Among his many contributions, Mr. Witmer played a critical 
role in developing the secure mobile communication system, known as the 
``Traveling Secure Cell Phone System'', housed in the presidential 
motorcade vehicle. This remains vital to the security operations of the 
United States Secret Service.
  Mr. Witmer was later called upon in the wake of Hurricane Katrina to 
address a gap in emergency communication capabilities in the U.S. Mr. 
Witmer was hired to deliver what would become the Integrated Public 
Alert and Warning System (IPAWS). He led a rapid modernization of the 
Federal Emergency Alert System, successfully implementing the 
capability to seize all television broadcasts for presidential alerts 
and ensuring that broadcast control could be reliably returned to 
civilian networks following a national alert. Mr. Witmer currently 
serves as Deputy Director of IPAWS.
  After the Presidential Alert System was shown to work as planned. 
Wade Witmer turned his focus to other public alert systems to further 
protect civilian lives during emergency and disaster scenarios. He 
developed and deployed nationwide systems for Tornado Alerts in 
coordination with the National Weather Service in Oklahoma, Amber 
Alerts to aid in the recovery of abducted children, and Silver Alerts 
to help locate missing seniors.
  Wade's commitment to public safety extends beyond our borders, as he 
traveled extensively throughout his career to support

[[Page E434]]

emergency agencies around the globe in establishing their own cell 
phone-based emergency alerting capabilities.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in thanking Mr. Wade 
Witmer for his years of service. Mr. Witmer's retirement marks the loss 
of one of our foremost experts in emergency management communications. 
May his retirement be filled with health and happiness.
