                    MAKE REFUGEE POLICY SAME FOR ALL

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, this week, a group of 
Afrikaners arrived in the United States. They were greeted with open 
arms and American flags. Every single one of them skipped to the front 
of the line thanks to the expedited pathway that the administration 
handed them.
  Although they are not facing any imminent threat, nor any 
persecution, according to this administration, these farmers were 
victims who were being brutally killed as a part of a genocide. These 
claims are absolutely false.

[[Page H2064]]

  We must ask ourselves: Why wasn't any grace extended to those fleeing 
the Democratic Republic of the Congo, home to the deadliest conflict in 
the world? There is no temporary protected status for them.
  Why wasn't any grace extended to Afghans, who helped our soldiers at 
war and risked their lives for the American people? This administration 
ended their deportation protections on the same day they welcomed 
Afrikaners.
  For those Haitians who fled instability and gang violence, why were 
TPS and humanitarian parole ripped away from them?
  Around the world, we have victims of war, famine, and bloodshed, but 
they have been left out to dry, where these Afrikaners were welcomed 
with open arms and, once again, without any persecution or risk of 
harm.
  We can't simply open our doors to the vulnerable when they serve 
political purposes. Refugee policies should never align with an agenda. 
It should serve those who are in need, those families overseas who will 
be murdered if America abandons them, not to people who serve a 
political purpose and for political gain.
  They should be awarded the same pathway that Afrikaners were given.
