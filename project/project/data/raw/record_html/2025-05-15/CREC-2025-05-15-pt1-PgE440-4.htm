                HONORING REVEREND DR. ARLENE K. NEHRING

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC SWALWELL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                         Thursday, May 15, 2025

  Mr. SWALWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Reverend Dr. Arlene K. 
Nehring on the occasion of her retirement as the pastor of the Eden 
United Church of Christ, in Hayward, California.
  Like me, Reverend Arlene was born in Iowa. She has dedicated her life 
to ministry and started on the path that led her to pastoral service 
when she was just 14 years old. Reverend Arlene found her way to 
California when she joined the Eden United Church of Christ in November 
2002, serving as lead pastor and teacher, staff supervisor, pastoral 
care provider, and community organizer, and retiring as the church's 
Senior Minister and Executive Director.
  Prior to joining the Eden United Church of Christ, she served at the 
Council for Health and Human Service Ministries, the Park Church, and 
the Old South Church in Boston. She earned a Master of Divinity Degree 
from the Andover Newton Theological School and a Doctor of Ministry 
from the Chicago Theological Seminary.
  Throughout her career, Reverend Arlene has worked tirelessly to help 
immigrant families throughout the Eden area. Under her leadership, the 
Eden United Church of Christ has provided sanctuary to immigrants and 
has coordinated interfaith accompaniment ministry with asylees, 
including unaccompanied immigrant youth and children from Central 
America, Mexico, and Colombia. During the COVID-19 pandemic, Reverend 
Arlene worked to coordinate Eden's meal program to continue serving its 
community, many of those who relied on public schools that were closed 
for their breakfasts and lunches.
  As Reverend Arlene enters retirement, I reflect on her career with 
immense gratitude for all that she did to serve our community over the 
last 23 years. I wish Reverend Arlene and her spouse, Stephanie, rest, 
relaxation, and the best of luck in this new chapter of their lives.
