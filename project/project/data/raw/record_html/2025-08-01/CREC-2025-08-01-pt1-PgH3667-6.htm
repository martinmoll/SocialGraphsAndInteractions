                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed bills of the following titles in 
which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 911. An act to amend the Omnibus Crime Control and Safe 
     Streets Act of 1968 to include certain retired law 
     enforcement officers in the public safety officers' death 
     benefits program.
       S. 1020. An act to require the Federal Energy Regulatory 
     Commission to extend the time period during which licensees 
     are required to commence construction of certain hydropower 
     projects.
       S. 1595. An act to establish standards for trauma kits 
     purchased using funds provided under the Edward Byrne 
     Memorial Justice Assistance Grant Program.
