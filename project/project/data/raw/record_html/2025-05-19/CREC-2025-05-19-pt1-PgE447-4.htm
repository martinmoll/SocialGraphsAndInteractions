CONGRATULATING JIMMY EMMONS ON HIS SWEARING IN TO THE NATURAL RESOURCES 
                          CONSERVATION SERVICE

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. FRANK D. LUCAS

                              of oklahoma

                    in the house of representatives

                          Monday, May 19, 2025

  Mr. LUCAS. Mr. Speaker, as a lifelong Oklahoman, Jimmy Emmons has 
spent years working around the state and across the country promoting 
innovative and voluntary conservation practices.
  Having someone like Jimmy in a position to lead farm policy isn't 
just beneficial for Oklahoma, but the entire country and the world.
  I am proud of my longtime friend and fellow Oklahoman for his 
willingness to serve the Nation at the Natural Resources Conservation 
Service and his commitment to the American farmer.
