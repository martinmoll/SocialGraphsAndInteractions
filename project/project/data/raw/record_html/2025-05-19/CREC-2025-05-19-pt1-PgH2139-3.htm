            HONORING SARA SCHALLER, CARNEGIE MEDAL RECIPIENT

  (Mr. MRVAN asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. MRVAN. Mr. Speaker, it is with great respect and sincere 
admiration that I rise to honor Ms. Sara Schaller from Hammond, 
Indiana, who was a recent recipient of the Carnegie Medal.
  Northwest Indiana is home to over 40 miles of incredible shoreline on 
Lake Michigan, which is a tremendous attraction for tourism and our 
economy, but also can at times pose great dangers with its currents and 
riptides.
  In 2023, Ms. Schaller observed a 14-year-old girl who had been caught 
in a dangerous current, and without hesitation, she courageously swam 
over 300 feet to rescue her. Where many hesitated, Sara stepped up and 
took lifesaving action.
  The Carnegie Medal is awarded to civilians who enter extreme danger, 
risk death or serious injury while saving or attempting to save the 
lives of others, and it was recently my honor to present Sara with this 
prestigious award.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you and all my colleagues join me in honoring 
Ms. Sara Schaller for her quick and courageous action and congratulate 
her on her deserved recognition.
