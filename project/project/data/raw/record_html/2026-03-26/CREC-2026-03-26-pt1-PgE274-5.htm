            RECOGNIZING SENIOR MASTER SERGEANT WILLIAM CORL

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. SCOTT PERRY

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 26, 2026

  Mr. PERRY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Senior Master 
Sergeant William Corl upon the auspicious occasion of his retirement 
after more than 37 years of service to our Nation in the Pennsylvania 
Air National Guard.
  Senior Master Sergeant Corl enlisted in the Air National Guard on 
February 15, 1990, with the 193rd Special Operations Wing. For the 
first 26 years of his exemplary career, he served as a Section Chief 
with the 193rd Communications Squadron, wherein he monitored technical 
and administrative operations to guarantee communications readiness for 
the EC-130J mission. While Section Chief, he assisted in mentoring his 
fellow Airmen, managing cyber and communications equipment, ensuring 
cyber network security, and supporting secure communications for 
special operations.
  After his service with the Communications Squadron, Senior Master 
Sergeant Corl became a Flight Chief with the 211th Engineering 
Installation Squadron, wherein he led personnel in engineering, 
installing, and relocating critical communication-electronics systems 
to support U.S. Central Command deployments and missions, and 
strengthened technical compliance, quality control, and operational 
readiness of communications systems--both duty positions in support of 
Operation Enduring Freedom.
  Mr. Speaker, I am honored to recognize Senior Master Sergeant William 
Corl upon his

[[Page E275]]

retirement from the Pennsylvania Air National Guard and on a job 
extremely well done. His dedicated service to his fellow Airmen, our 
communities, the Pennsylvania Air National Guard, the United States Air 
Force, and our United States of America set the standard by which all 
should be judged.
