                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Our help is in the name of the Lord who made Heaven and Earth. Lord, 
we call on Your creative and redemptive aid this day.
  Inundated as we are by the issues before us, we pray that You, O 
Lord, would come before us to make a way through. Torrential as the 
waters of decision may be, we pray that You would speak Your word of 
calm and clear guidance. Enraged as are those who demand attention and 
answers, Lord, still the roaring waves of anxiety with Your word of 
peace.
  Remind us that You, the one who spoke into the darkness and brought 
forth the light and called it good; You, who gathered the waters and 
brought forth the land and saw that it too was good; You, who made us 
in Your image that we would have authority over the creatures of Your 
design; You are the one who gives life to all things and has called 
them all good. It is You who are our strength and our redeemer.
  Our help this day is in the name of the Lord who made Heaven and 
Earth. We pray in Your name that You would grant us Your blessing anew 
on this day.
  In Your eternal name we pray.
  Amen.
