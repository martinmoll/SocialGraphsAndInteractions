    EXPRESSING THE SUPPORT OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES FOR THE 
                    DEPARTMENT OF HOMELAND SECURITY

unfinished business is the vote on adoption of the resolution (H. Res. 
1128) expressing the support of the House of Representatives for the 
Department of Homeland Security, on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the resolution.

nays 187, answered ``present'' 13, not voting 7, as follows:
