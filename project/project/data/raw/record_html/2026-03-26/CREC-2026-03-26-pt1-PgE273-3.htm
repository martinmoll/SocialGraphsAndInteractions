                RECOGNIZING THE AKRON BLACK ARTIST GUILD

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 26, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the Akron Black 
Artist Guild as the Champion of the Week for Ohio's 13th Congressional 
District.
  The Guild is a dynamic community of talented and passionate Black 
artists who have come together with a singular mission: to strengthen 
the creative community in Northeast Ohio.
  The organization works to expand fair access to funding, professional 
growth, and public opportunities for artists. Through its programs, the 
Guild brings people together, connects generations, and uses all to 
promote learning, economic opportunity, civic engagement, and community 
well-being.
  Now in its fifth year, the Akron Black Artist Guild continues to 
build leadership and encourage civic participation throughout Northeast 
Ohio's arts and cultural landscape. The Guild also provides a safe and 
welcoming space for artists while working to overcome barriers created 
by racial and economic inequality.
  Ohio's 13th Congressional District is known as the ``Birthplace of 
Champions.'' The Akron Black Artist Guild has upheld this legacy 
through the work they have put in to strengthen our region. This 
organization has certainly made our community proud and I thank them 
again for their tireless efforts.
