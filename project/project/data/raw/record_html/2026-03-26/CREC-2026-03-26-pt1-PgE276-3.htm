               RECOGNIZING MR. WILLIAM ``BILL'' HUMPHREY

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 26, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize one of my 
outstanding constituents, Mr. William ``Bill'' Humphrey, a dedicated 
volunteer whose leadership and service have made a profound impact 
through Seniors in Service AmeriCorps Seniors RSVP Pasco.
  Bill exemplifies the critical role volunteers play in supporting 
communities that often have nowhere else to turn. As a site coordinator 
for the VITA tax preparation program, he ensures that seniors and low-
income residents in Pasco County have access to free, reliable, and 
trustworthy tax preparation services. His dedication is evident the 
moment you walk into his site--Bill manages a fast-paced, nonstop 
workflow, coordinating appointments, assisting clients, and supporting 
volunteers with unwavering focus and commitment to the mission.
  Bill's service begins long before tax season. He takes on essential 
behind-the-scenes responsibilities, including training volunteers and 
setting up appointment scheduling systems, phone lines, and outreach 
materials so that individuals in need can easily access these services. 
The clients he serves are often seniors on restricted budgets or 
working individuals who rely on VITA as their only safe and accessible 
option. Bill ensures that every client is treated with dignity and 
receives the guidance and support they need. Beyond tax preparation, 
Bill's commitment to community extends to advocating for vulnerable 
neighbors. In past years, he helped protect a blind veteran from being 
taken advantage of, demonstrating the same protective and community-
minded spirit he brings to all his work today. Bill's leadership, 
consistency, and dedication strengthen both clients and volunteers, 
enhancing the reach and impact of the VITA program across Pasco County.
  For his tireless service, steadfast leadership, and meaningful impact 
on the lives of countless neighbors, I am proud to recognize Mr. 
William ``Bill'' Humphrey as a 2026 Hero Among Us. I ask my colleagues 
to join me in honoring his extraordinary commitment and the lasting 
difference he has made in our community.
