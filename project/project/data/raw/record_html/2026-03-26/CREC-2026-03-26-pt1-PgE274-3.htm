          RECOGNIZING NADINE McILWAIN AND GERALDINE RADCLIFFE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. EMILIA STRONG SYKES

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 26, 2026

  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Nadine McIlwain 
and Geraldine Radcliffe as the Champions of the Week for Ohio's 13th 
Congressional District. Nadine and Geraldine worked for 15 years to 
collect the history and stories of Canton residents to write the book, 
African Americans of Canton, Ohio. Their book highlights not only well-
known stories of Canton, but also many that otherwise would have been 
lost to history.
  From Canton's earliest days, the Black population has contributed to 
the city's, and even the Nation's prominence and prosperity. Collecting 
never-before-seen photographs and firsthand accounts from local 
citizens, McIlwain and Radcliffe reveal the celebrated legends, unsung 
heroes, and historic firsts of African Americans residing in the Canton 
community.
  The authors said they hope the book sparks people to write about 
Black history in their own communities, and that it is the first of 
many books on African American history in Stark County.
  Through this recognition, and the many years of dedication that made 
it possible, Nadine and Geraldine are shining examples of why Ohio's 
13th Congressional District is known as the ``Birthplace of 
Champions.'' They have certainly made our community proud.
