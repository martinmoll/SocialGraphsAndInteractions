                       HONORING MR. JOE B. ALLEN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. SYLVIA R. GARCIA

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 17, 2025

  Ms. GARCIA of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mr. Joe B. 
Allen, an accomplished attorney and civic leader who has devoted his 
career to strengthening and uplifting the City of Houston. Scenic 
Houston has recognized Mr. Allen with its Scenic Visionary Award for 
his leadership and his lasting contributions to our community.
  For decades, Mr. Allen has helped guide Houston's growth with a 
steady hand and a clear vision. Through his work in public 
infrastructure and policy, he has advanced the idea that a great city 
must be both functional and beautiful. It must be a place that serves 
its people and reflects their pride. He has been a leading voice for 
thoughtful urban design and the protection of green and scenic spaces 
across our region.
  His commitment to preserving the character of our city has inspired 
generations of Houstonians to see beauty as an essential part of civic 
life. Mr. Allen's influence endures in the parks, neighborhoods, and 
gathering places that define our city.
  Mr. Speaker, I commend Mr. Joe B. Allen on this well-deserved honor 
and thank him for a lifetime of service that has made Houston a 
stronger, more welcoming place for all.
