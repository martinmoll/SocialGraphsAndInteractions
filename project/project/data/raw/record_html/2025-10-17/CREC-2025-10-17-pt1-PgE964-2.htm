            HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF MISS CANDIS KING

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, October 17, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Miss 
Candis King, an adored sophomore at Colgan High School in Prince 
William County, Virginia.
  Candis, a lifelong resident of Prince William County and the 
cherished daughter of Virginia House Delegate Candi King and Fairfax 
Deputy Sheriff Joshua King, was a proud member of her school's Color 
Guard, a dancer, and someone who lit up every room with her kindness 
and creativity.
  Her love for Transformers was more than just a hobby; it was a 
reflection of her cheerful spirit, Candis had a gift for bringing 
happiness to all those around her, and her presence made a lasting 
impact on her peers, teachers, and entire community.
  Sadly, Candis passed away due to complications from sickle cell 
disease, a devastating and often overlooked condition that affects 
marginalized communities. This serves as a critical reminder of how 
much work still needs to be done in sickle cell research, treatment, 
and awareness. We need to invest more in medical research, expand 
access to care, and raise greater national attention to the seriousness 
of this disease.
  I want to offer my deepest condolences to Delegate Candi King and 
Deputy Sheriff Joshua King. Your strength during this time of an 
unimaginable loss is inspiring. I hope you find comfort in knowing that 
Candis's light touched many lives and that her memory will live on in 
the hearts of all who knew and loved her.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring the life of 
Candis King. Though her time with us was far too short, her legacy of 
joy, creativity, and resilience will live on.
