 RECOGNIZING JAMES E. BAKER, II AS CASS CITY'S 2025 CITIZEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 7, 2025

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a man whose lifetime 
of service and dedication to Cass City has left an indelible mark on 
our community. It is with great admiration that I recognize James E. 
Baker, II, as the 2025 Cass City Citizen of the Year.
  Born and raised in Cass City, Jim Baker has lived a life defined by 
generosity, leadership, and an unwavering commitment to others. A proud 
graduate of Cass City High School and Michigan Technological 
University, he has built a successful career as the owner and president 
of Jebco Consulting, where his expertise in electro-mechanical 
engineering has been widely respected. Yet, despite his professional 
success, his true legacy is his dedication to serving his community.
  Jim's contributions to Cass City are immeasurable. As a mentor in the 
Boy Scouts, he has guided generations of young men toward the rank of 
Eagle Scout, instilling in them values of discipline, responsibility, 
and service. His impact extends beyond Scouting, as he has dedicated 
countless hours to coaching youth athletics, shaping the lives of young 
athletes in Little League Baseball, high school football, and the Cass 
City Youth Football initiative. His belief in the power of mentorship 
has left a lasting impression on the young people he has supported and 
inspired.
  Beyond coaching and mentorship, Jim has worked tirelessly to 
strengthen his community. He has been a dedicated member of the Rotary 
Club, an advocate for educational initiatives, and a key organizer in 
numerous fundraising efforts that have uplifted Cass City. Even in the 
face of personal health challenges, undergoing both a liver and kidney 
transplant, Jim has remained steadfast in his commitment to serving 
others. The overwhelming support he received during his health battles 
was a testament to the lives he has touched though his generosity and 
kindness.
  Mr. Speaker, James Baker is more than a community leader--he is a 
pillar of Cass City, embodying the very best of selfless service, 
mentorship, and resilience. It is my great privilege to recognize him 
as the Cass City Citizen of the Year. His legacy of service will 
continue to inspire and strengthen our community for generations to 
come.
