 RECOGNIZING KACEE GRAY AS CASS CITY'S 2025 JUNIOR CITIZEN OF THE YEAR

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. LISA C. McCLAIN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 7, 2025

  Mrs. McCLAIN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize an outstanding 
young leader in our community, Kacee Gray, who has been named the Cass 
City Junior Citizen of the Year for 2025. Kacee exemplifies the values 
of hard work, dedication, and service, making a profound and lasting 
impact on Cass City and beyond.
  A soon-to-be graduate, Kacee plans to attend Alma College to pursue a 
Bachelor's Degree in Business, with the goal of becoming an insurance 
agent. Her academic excellence is evident through numerous honors, 
including being a Red Hawk Rockstar, a consistent presence on the honor 
roll, and earning academic recognition in subjects like English, 
Spanish, and Physics.
  Beyond her academic achievements, Kacee has demonstrated an 
extraordinary commitment to service. She has dedicated her time to 
uplifting those around her through extensive volunteer work, including 
serving at Revive Common Grounds, assisting with local blood drives, 
supporting VFW spaghetti dinners, and bringing holiday joy to children 
through Christmas for Kids. She has played an integral role

[[Page E288]]

in school events, from setting up classrooms to managing concession 
stands and coordinating prom preparations. Her tireless efforts reflect 
a deep-rooted dedication to bettering her community and serving others.
  Kacee is also an accomplished athlete, excelling in volleyball, 
basketball, softball, and track. She has earned multiple accolades, 
including All-Conference First Team, All-Region Outside Hitter, and 
All-State Honorable Mention. Her leadership extends beyond the court--
serving as President of Student Council, Treasurer of the National 
Honor Society, and a dedicated member of various extracurricular 
organizations, always striving to lead with compassion and integrity.
  What sets Kacee apart is her unwavering commitment to lifting others 
up. Whether stepping outside her comfort zone to sing the National 
Anthem at sporting events or leading by example in her community, she 
embodies the spirit of service and resilience.
  Mr. Speaker, it is an honor to recognize Kacee Gray for her 
remarkable achievements and dedication to service. She represents the 
very best of Cass City's next generation of leaders, and I have no 
doubt that she will continue to make a meaningful impact wherever she 
goes.
