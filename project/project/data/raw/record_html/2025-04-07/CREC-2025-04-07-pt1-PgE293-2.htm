                    RECOGNIZING DANOVIN BARSCOCCHINI

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 7, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Danovin 
Barscocchini for earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for 
Youth Award.
  Danovin has overcome many challenges along his journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Danovin, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Danovin's hard work, determination, and perseverance 
at Arvada West High School and is clearly just the beginning of a 
bright and promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Danovin Barscocchini on achieving the 
Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
