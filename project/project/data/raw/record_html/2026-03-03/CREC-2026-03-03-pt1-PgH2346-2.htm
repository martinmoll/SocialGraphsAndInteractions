              INVESTMENT TRANSFORMS EASTERN NORTH CAROLINA

  (Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. DAVIS of North Carolina. Mr. Speaker, eastern North Carolina is 
on the rise. Exciting investments are transforming our communities and 
creating new opportunities for young people and hardworking families.
  U.S. Forged Rings is investing $875 million in Hertford County, 
creating 625 new jobs and revitalizing our economy. In Edgecombe 
County, Amazon is bringing good-paying jobs. LS Cable & System USA is 
fortifying our manufacturing sector with an investment of $50 million 
and 85 new jobs.
  Mr. Speaker, clearly, the momentum is undeniable. These are more than 
just investments. These represent real opportunities. Together, we are 
reshaping our future.
