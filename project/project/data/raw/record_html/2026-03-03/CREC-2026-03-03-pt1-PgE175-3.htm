          HONORING VYBZ KARTEL IN CELEBRATION OF REGGAE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 3, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Vybz 
Kartel in Celebration of Reggae Month. I include in the Record the 
following, proclamation:

       Whereas, reggae and dancehall music remain powerful global 
     expressions of Jamaican culture, creativity, and social 
     commentary, celebrated annually during Reggae Month in 
     recognition of the artists who have shaped and transformed 
     the genre; and
       Whereas, Vybz Kartel, born Adidja Azim Palmer in Kingston, 
     Jamaica, has been one of the most influential and provocative 
     figures in contemporary dancehall, earning titles such as 
     ``Worl' Boss,'' ``Teacha,'' and ``King of Dancehall''; and
       Whereas, emerging in the early 1990s and rising to 
     prominence in the early 2000s, Vybz Kartel became known for 
     his prolific songwriting, sharp lyrical delivery, and 
     cultural impact, helping to redefine the sound and direction 
     of modern dancehall music; and
       Whereas, his hit singles including ``Romping Shop,'' 
     ``Dancehall Hero,'' and ``Summer Time'' have become staples 
     of the genre, while his collaborations with international 
     artists have further expanded dancehall's global reach; and
       Whereas, recognized for his immense productivity and 
     enduring connection to fans worldwide, Vybz Kartel maintained 
     a significant musical presence throughout his career, 
     continuing to influence new generations of artists in Jamaica 
     and abroad; and
       Whereas, his cultural impact has extended beyond Jamaica's 
     shores, inspiring numerous international performers and 
     contributing to the worldwide evolution of dancehall-infused 
     popular music; and therefore, be it
       Resolved, That we, Florida's 20th Congressional District, 
     proudly acknowledge Vybz Kartel in celebration of Reggae 
     Month, recognizing his significant contributions to dancehall 
     music, his cultural influence, and his role in shaping the 
     global sound and legacy of Jamaican music.
