          HONORING WAYNE WONDER IN CELEBRATION OF REGGAE MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                    HON. SHEILA CHERFILUS-McCORMICK

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, March 3, 2026

  Mrs. CHERFILUS-McCORMICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Wayne 
Wonder in Celebration of Reggae Month. I include in the Record the 
following proclamation:

       Whereas, reggae and dancehall music serve as enduring 
     global symbols of Jamaican culture, resilience, and artistic 
     excellence, celebrated annually during Reggae Month in 
     recognition of the artists who have elevated the genre to 
     international acclaim; and
  Whereas, Wayne Wonder, born Von Wayne Charles in Buff Bay, Portland, 
Jamaica, has made a lasting contribution to reggae and dancehall music 
through his smooth vocal style, songwriting talent, and crossover 
appeal; and
  Whereas, beginning his musical journey in church and developing his 
craft from a young age, Wayne Wonder earned early recognition with 
performances at Metro Media in Kingston, laying the foundation for a 
distinguished and enduring career; and
  Whereas, his international hit single ``No Letting Go'' became a 
global anthem in 2003, introducing dancehall-infused reggae to 
mainstream audiences worldwide and solidifying his status as a leading 
voice in the genre; and
  Whereas, through decades of consistent artistry, collaborations, and 
chart-topping releases, Wayne Wonder has helped shape the sound of 
contemporary reggae and dancehall while representing Jamaica with 
distinction on the world stage; and
  Whereas, his musical legacy reflects dedication, versatility, and a 
commitment to spreading positive energy through song; and therefore, be 
it
       Resolved, That we, Florida's 20th Congressional District, 
     proudly honor Wayne Wonder in celebration of Reggae Month, 
     recognizing his significant contributions to reggae and 
     dancehall music, his global cultural influence, and his 
     enduring impact on Jamaican artistry.
