RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF DANNY MILLER AS CO-EXECUTIVE PRODUCER OF 
                               FRESH AIR

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. BRENDAN F. BOYLE

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. BOYLE of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate 
Danny Miller on his retirement as Co-Executive Producer for WHYY's 
Fresh Air with Terry Gross & Tonya Mosely, one of the most successful 
national public radio shows in the Nation.
  Miller, who joined the Fresh Air team in 1978 as an intern, has been 
an integral part of the show's tremendous growth, now reaching over 6 
million weekly listeners across 138 different countries. Miller's 
leadership and judgement is apparent in the more than 20,000 segments 
he helped produce. This expertise was especially critical as he led the 
program through unprecedented periods of technological transition.
  Thanks in large part to Miller, Fresh Air has become a tremendous 
success, with two Peabody Awards under its belt and recognition as one 
of TIME Magazine's top 100 podcasts ever. Miller's indispensable 
relationship with Terry Gross, the show's host, has laid the foundation 
for Fresh Air 's characteristic sound and production style, enabling 
the program to reach its current levels of recognition.
  Under Miller's creative watch, Fresh Air has accumulated an extremely 
impressive list of guests across all industries, including Johnny Cash, 
Maya Angelou, Lin-Manuel Miranda, and Robin Williams. Without Miller's 
intellectual curiosity, detailed nature, and vigorous collaboration, 
none of this would be possible.
  Mr. Speaker, it is altogether fitting that the House recognizes Danny 
Miller for his exceptional career and congratulates him on his well-
deserved retirement.
