HONORING PENNSYLVANIA AIR NATIONAL GUARD CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT JOHN R. 
          HEIDRICK, JR.'S RETIREMENT AFTER 38 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. BRIAN K. FITZPATRICK

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. FITZPATRICK. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor 
Chief Master Sergeant (CMSgt) John R. Heidrick, Jr., for his 
extraordinary service, dedication, and distinguished career throughout 
his nearly four decades in the Pennsylvania Air National Guard. CMSgt 
Heidrick embodies the highest traditions of military professionalism, 
leadership, and devotion to duty.
  CMSgt Heidrick career began with his enlistment in the Pennsylvania 
Air National Guard in June of 1988 and his graduation from Cable and 
Antenna Installation Specialist School in February of 1989. Upon 
graduating, he joined the 270th Engineering and Installation Squadron 
(EIS) at the Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base Willow Grove as a 
Traditional Guardsman.
  Always seeking ways to better serve the mission, he transitioned into 
the Vehicle Maintenance career field in April 1991 and secured a full-
time technician role as a Heavy Equipment Mechanic with the 270th 
Engineering Installation Squadron (EIS). Through exceptional expertise 
and steadfast dedication, he was promoted to becoming a Noncommissioned 
Officer in Charge of the Vehicle Maintenance Section on January 19, 
2004. His superior performance earned him recognition as the 270th 
Senior Noncommissioned Officer of the Year in 2009.
  In June 2014, CMSgt Heidrick was selected as Vehicle Fleet Manager 
for the 111th Attack Wing, where he continued to guide the Vehicle 
Maintenance Section of the 270th EIS until April 2015. His outstanding 
leadership and technical acumen led to his 2016 selection as Vehicle 
Fleet Manager for the 201st RED HORSE Squadron, Detachment 1, where he 
additionally assumed management responsibilities for the unit's 
Logistics Section. Following his promotion to Chief Master Sergeant in 
January 2017, CMSgt Heidrick was appointed as the Senior Enlisted 
Advisor to the RED HORSE Commander. His selection for this position 
reflects the exceptional respect and confidence he earned across the 
organization.
  Beyond his unit level influence, CMSgt Heidrick has served the 
broader Air National Guard community with distinction. From 2012 to 
2018, he served as a valued advisor to the National Guard Bureau 
Vehicle Management Advisory Group, shaping national level policy and 
readiness standards, providing strategic guidance on maintenance and 
logistics programs nationwide. Furthermore, his extensive involvement 
in the Chiefs Council from 2017 to 2025 demonstrates his steadfast 
commitment to the professional development, mentorship, and 
professional growth across the Wing.
  CMSgt Heidrick's commitment to service extends beyond maintenance 
bays and command offices. For close to four decades, he has been a 
proud and devoted member of the Base Honor Guard, first with the 270th 
EIS Honor Guard from 1989 to 1996, and later with the 111th Attack Wing 
Honor Guard from 2004 to 2025. He ensured that proper tribute to our 
fallen heroes and their families were provided with the upmost dignity. 
His solemn service was recognized twice as he was named Honor Guard 
Manager of the Year in 2011 and again in 2015. His professionalism, 
reverence, and dedication in honoring fallen service members earned him 
the title of Honor Guard Manager of the Year in both 2011 and 2015.
  Mr. Speaker, Chief Master Sergeant John R. Heidrick, Jr.'s 
distinguished career represents the very best of our United States Air 
Force Airman that is defined by duty, mission focus, mentorship, and 
honor. His leadership, unwavering commitment, and contributions have 
strengthened his unit, the 111th Attack Wing, and the Pennsylvania Air 
National Guard while elevating unit readiness across the Commonwealth 
of Pennsylvania. This exemplifies the highest ideals of the United 
States Air Force.
  I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing CMSgt Heidrick's 
extraordinary service and in expressing our Nation's deep gratitude for 
his distinguished career in the Pennsylvania Air National Guard.
  On behalf of a grateful Nation, I thank Chief Master Sergeant John R. 
Heidrick, Jr. for his exemplary service and wish him continued success 
in all future endeavors. His legacy offeadership and dedication will 
endure for generations of Airmen to follow.
