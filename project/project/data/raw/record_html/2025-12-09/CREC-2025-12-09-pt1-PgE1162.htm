                HONORING LIEUTENANT GENERAL JOSEPH RYAN

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate Lieutenant 
General Joseph Ryan on his appointment as Commanding General of the 
U.S. Army Forces Command (FORSCOM), one of the most critical and 
consequential leadership positions in the armed forces.
  Lieutenant General Ryan brings to this role an exceptional record of 
service, distinguished by years of operational experience, thoughtful 
leadership, and an unwavering commitment to the Soldiers and families 
of the U.S. Army. He has led at every level, from company through 
division, including service as the Commanding General of the 25th 
Infantry Division and as Chief of Staff of the XVIII Airborne Corps. 
Most recently, he served as the Army's Deputy Chief of Staff, 
responsible for overseeing operations, plans, and training across the 
entire force.
  As he assumes command of FORSCOM, Lieutenant General Ryan takes 
responsibility for the Army's largest component and is charged with 
preparing our Soldiers to rapidly deploy and win our Nation's wars. 
This role demands clarity of purpose, humility, strategic foresight, 
and a steadfast dedication to the mission. Lieutenant General Ryan has 
demonstrated each of these qualities throughout his career, 
particularly during multiple combat deployments and in senior 
leadership posts that shaped Army policy, modernization, and global 
operational posture.
  As Fort Bragg's Congressman, I am proud to welcome Lieutenant General 
Ryan back to Fayetteville. Now more than ever, our Nation faces 
evolving security challenges and increasing demands on our force. I am 
confident that under the leadership of Lieutenant General Ryan, we will 
rise to meet those challenges.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in congratulating Lieutenant 
General Ryan and wishing him success as he leads the men and women of 
the U.S. Army Forces Command.
