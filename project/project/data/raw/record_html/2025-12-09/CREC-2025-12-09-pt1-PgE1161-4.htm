                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I missed all the votes in the vote series 
on December 3, 2025. Had I been present, I would have voted NAY on Roll 
Call No. 312, final passage of H.R. 1005, Combating the Lies of 
Authoritarians in School Systems (CLASS) Act; YES on Roll Call No. 311, 
final passage of H.R. 4305, DUMP Red Tape Act; and NAY on Roll Call No. 
310, final passage of H.R. 2965, Small Business Regulatory Reduction 
Act of 2025.
