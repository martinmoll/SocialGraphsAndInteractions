             HONORING THE SERVICE OF DETECTIVE PETE DEATES

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. BRYAN STEIL

                              of wisconsin

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. STEIL. Mr. Speaker, I rise today in recognition of Detective Pete 
Deates of Kenosha, Wisconsin. Detective Deates has served the Kenosha 
community admirably for over 23 years through his work as a detective 
and as President of the Kenosha Professional Police Association (KPPA).
  Detective Deates' service to the greater Kenosha community includes 
15 years as a detective, with the majority of his time in the Sensitive 
Crimes Unit. Most recently, he helped start the Kenosha Police 
Department's first Internet Crimes Against Children Unit, combatting 
criminals who prey on children. Detective Deates also serves as the 
canine handler for the Kenosha Police Department's Electronic Storage 
Detection Canine, Iris. Detective Deates and Canine Iris spent the last 
several years traveling the State of Wisconsin, holding the worst 
criminals accountable and protecting those in need. His detail-oriented 
approach to policing and his commitment to mentoring the next 
generation of law enforcement deserves recognition. Detective Deates 
consistently modeled the professionalism, dedication, and bravery 
necessary to keep our community safe. In October of this year, 
Detective Deates was recognized as the 2025 First Responder of the Year 
by the Wisconsin State Legislature.
  In his role as President of the KPPA, Detective Deates advocates for 
law enforcement officers and diligently worked to enhance officer 
safety, training, and wellness. Under his 19 years of leadership, 
Detective Deates successfully fostered a strong, collaborative 
relationship between the KPPA and the City of Kenosha, strengthening 
the Kenosha police force and ensuring that safety is the top priority.
  Families across Kenosha are grateful for Detective Deates' work. For 
over two decades, he has served as a steady example of what law 
enforcement should be. Whether helping a traumatized victim, briefing a 
prosecutorial team on a complex case, or standing shoulder-to-shoulder 
with officers after a difficult call, his lead-by-example approach 
makes his department and community stronger.
  Detective Deates' dedication and courage should serve as an 
inspiration to us all. I want to congratulate Detective Deates on his 
retirement and his impressive career and thank him for all he has done 
to make Kenosha and Wisconsin a better, safer place.
