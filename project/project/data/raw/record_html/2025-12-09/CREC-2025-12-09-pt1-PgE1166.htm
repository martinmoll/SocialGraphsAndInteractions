  HONORING THE RETIREMENT OF PATRICK GLEASON AFTER 26 YEARS AT JESSE 
                        BROWN VA MEDICAL CENTER

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. MIKE QUIGLEY

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. QUIGLEY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and congratulate 
Patrick Gleason on his retirement after a remarkable 26-year career 
with the Jesse Brown VA Medical Center.
  Patrick started as a Canteen chief before moving over to voluntary 
services. He collaborated with the Chicago Mattress Company to bring 
veterans in need of a mattress, bed frame, pillows, and sheets. He led 
the first VA food pantry program in the country, which ran from 2013 to 
2020. Patrick coordinated a concert tour for The Dogz Jazz Band, 
performing at 9 different VA hospitals.
  Patrick revived a program to provide ambulance and stretcher services 
to discharging veterans who have no other option to get home after a 
hospital stay. He singlehandedly repaired the relationship with the 
transportation provider who wanted to end their contract after previous 
nonpayment. Now, this service is available for those patients most in-
need. Patrick's leadership and service have made a lasting impact on 
the Jesse Brown VA Medical Center and on the veterans they serve.
  As he begins this new chapter, I offer my gratitude and 
congratulations. Patrick Gleason's 26-year legacy at JBVA will continue 
to inspire leaders and communities for years to come.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me and all our colleagues in the House of 
Representatives in recognizing Patrick Gleason for his immense 
contribution.
