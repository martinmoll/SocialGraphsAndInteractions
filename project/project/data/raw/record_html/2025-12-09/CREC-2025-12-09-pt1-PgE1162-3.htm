       HONORING SHERIFF SCOTT FOSTER FOR OVER 30 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sheriff Scott 
Foster for his 31 years of outstanding service in law enforcement and 
his commitment to the safety of the Fredericksburg community.
  Sheriff Foster began his career in 1994, advancing from the rank of 
Deputy to Captain. On September 15, 2022, he was appointed Sheriff of 
the City of Fredericksburg by the Honorable Gordon F. Willis, Circuit 
Court Judge, and was sworn in shortly afterwards.
  Since taking office, Sheriff Foster has worked to improve the 
Fredericksburg Sheriff's Office by updating operational plans, building 
policies and procedures, and improving training and record-keeping. 
Under his leadership, he also established a Career Development Program 
to support employee growth and recognize professional excellence within 
the department.
  Beyond his leadership duties, Sheriff Foster has remained active in 
the community helping with school safety, participating in the D.A.R.E. 
program, and supporting mental health transports. He has also worked 
closely with Fredericksburg City Public Schools by attending safety 
board meetings and school events. I want to personally congratulate and 
thank Sheriff Foster for his endless dedication to ensuring our 
Fredericksburg communities stay safe and have access to the resources 
they need.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the City of Fredericksburg and its 
residents, I extend our deepest gratitude to Sheriff Foster for his 
unwavering service and dedication. His impact on our community will be 
felt for years to come.
