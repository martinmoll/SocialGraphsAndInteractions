                     HONORING GENERAL ANDREW POPPAS

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. RICHARD HUDSON

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. HUDSON. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the distinguished 
service of General Andrew Poppas as Commanding General of the U.S. Army 
Forces Command (FORSCOM) and to thank him for his lifetime of 
dedication to the armed forces.
  General Poppas assumed command of FORSCOM on July 8, 2022, becoming 
the 24th Commanding General of the Army's largest component. In this 
role, he led more than 750,000 Soldiers and thousands of Army civilians 
across the Active Army, Army Reserve, and Army National Guard. His 
leadership has ensured their readiness to rapidly deploy and win our 
Nation's wars.
  Over the course of his career, General Poppas has led Soldiers at 
every level, from platoon through division and higher-level joint 
commands. His service includes multiple combat deployments to both Iraq 
and Afghanistan, command of the 101st Airborne Division (Air Assault), 
and service as Director of Operations to the Joint Staff prior to 
assuming command of FORSCOM.
  A graduate of the United States Military Academy and a seasoned 
operational and strategic leader, General Poppas has consistently 
exemplified professionalism, integrity, and an unwavering commitment to 
the welfare of Soldiers and their families. Under his leadership, 
FORSCOM strengthened its readiness posture, modernized training 
priorities, and ensured that America's Army remained capable, agile, 
and prepared for the challenges of a rapidly evolving global security 
environment.
  As Fort Bragg's Congressman, I am forever grateful for General 
Poppas' decades of service. His leadership has left an enduring mark on 
the Army and on the generations of Soldiers who have served under him.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me today in honoring the service of General 
Poppas as Commanding General of the U.S. Army Forces Command.
