  NOTICE OF INTENTION TO OFFER MOTION TO DISCHARGE COMMITTEE ON RULES 
               FROM FURTHER CONSIDERATION OF H. RES. 432

  Mr. GOLDEN of Maine. Mr. Speaker, pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, 
I rise to provide notice of my intent to offer a motion to discharge 
the Committee on Rules from further consideration of H. Res. 432.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Did the gentleman sign the petition?
  Mr. GOLDEN of Maine. I did, Mr. Speaker.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 2(c) of rule XV, a motion 
to discharge is privileged only at a time designated by the Speaker 
within 2 legislative days after the intent to offer such motion is 
offered.
  The Chair will entertain the gentleman's motion at that time.
