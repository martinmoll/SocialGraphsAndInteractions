        HONORING CHIEF BRIAN LAYTON FOR OVER 30 YEARS OF SERVICE

                                  _____
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Chief Brian 
Layton on his retirement from the Fredericksburg Police Department 
after more than three decades of dedicated service.
  Over 30 years ago, Chief Layton joined the Department and has served 
with honor since becoming Chief in January 2020. During his tenure, he 
has strengthened recruitment and retention, modernized departmental 
operations, and set the City's Emergency 911 Center on the path to 
achieving national accreditation.
  Under Chief Layton's leadership, the Fredericksburg Police Department 
experienced significant growth and success. He guided the agency 
through the unprecedented challenges of the COVID-19 pandemic; launched 
the Crisis Intervention Response Team (CIRT), which places law 
enforcement officers with mental health providers to support residents 
in crisis; and secured and managed more than $6 million in grant 
funding to enhance officer wellness, expand community programs, and 
strengthen training initiatives. These accomplishments capture only a 
portion of his distinguished record over the course of his service.
  I want to personally congratulate Chief Layton on his endless 
dedication to ensuring our communities stay safe and have access to the 
resources they need.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of the City of Fredericksburg and its 
residents, I extend our deepest gratitude to Chief Layton for his 
unwavering service and dedication. His impact on our community will be 
felt for years to come.
