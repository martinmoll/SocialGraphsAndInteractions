                CELEBRATING THE CAREER OF MR. DAVID WARM

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
career of Mr. David Warm, the executive director for the Mid-America 
Regional Council (MARC).
  Economist John Kenneth Galbraith once wrote, ``All of the great 
leaders have had one characteristic in common: It was the willingness 
to confront unequivocally the major anxiety of their people in their 
time.''
  As Executive Director of MARC for the past 35 years, Mr. Warm has 
confronted the major challenges of the Kansas City region time and time 
again. From emergency management to environmental protection, early 
education to aging services, and transportation to local government 
cooperative services, Mr. Warm has worked tirelessly with a servant's 
heart and a singular focus on the mission ahead.
  Mr. Warm is retiring after 35 distinguished years as MARC's Executive 
Director. Under his leadership, MARC has earned a reputation for 
innovative work and has become a vital organization for the development 
of 119 cities and nine counties in the Kansas City area.
  Prior to Mr. Warm's tenure at MARC, he worked for the city of Kansas 
City, Missouri, and later, the city administrator's office for the City 
of Liberty, Missouri, eventually leading the office. Mr. Warm also 
serves on the boards of several area nonprofits focused on health care, 
community development, and education, including the Hall Family 
Foundation.
  He has been honored with many awards. After becoming a fellow in the 
National Academy of Public Administration, Mr. Warm also received the 
Walter Scheiber Leadership Award from the National Association of 
Regional Councils. In 2012, he received the Professional of the Year 
award from Nonprofit Connect, and in 2020, he was honored with the 
Kansas Citian of the Year award by the Greater Kansas City Chamber of 
Commerce.
  Those who have worked with Mr. Warm describe him as a practical, 
humble, and exemplary leader who has had a positive and critical impact 
on the region. He is known for his dedicated service and devotion to 
the Kansas City region and has been an invaluable asset to my office. 
Mr. Warm has proactively provided my office with valuable knowledge and 
has been an esteemed contributor to my many roundtables addressing 
various issues or concerns within the community. His support, not only 
for my office but also for other public servants, has always been in 
the interest of doing what is best for the region, and we will greatly 
miss his unique insight.
  Please join me in wholeheartedly congratulating Mr. Warm on his 
illustrious career and profound impact on the Kansas City region. I 
wish Mr. Warm a well-deserved and prosperous retirement alongside his 
family.
