             HONORING THE SERVICE AND CAREER OF NELDA OLIVO

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the career and 
contributions of Nelda Olivo, a dedicated public servant and the 
Director of Government Affairs for the Port of Corpus Christi, who has 
spent more than three decades advancing the economic and strategic 
interests of South Texas.
  Ms. Olivo joined the Port in 1993, and since that time she has played 
a vital role in strengthening one of our Nation's most important energy 
and commerce gateways. She is responsible for developing, implementing, 
and managing the Port's government affairs program, serving as a 
trusted liaison with local, state, and federal legislators, 
governmental agencies, and community organizations. Her work has helped 
build broad support for key port initiatives that enhance regional 
mobility, security, and economic development.
  Throughout her career, Ms. Olivo has demonstrated deep expertise in 
pursuing government funding across appropriations, transportation, 
homeland security, and critical grant programs. She is known for 
identifying emerging issues affecting ports nationwide and for leading 
new initiatives that strengthen the Port of Corpus Christi's role as a 
global leader in energy exports and maritime commerce.
  Her leadership extends well beyond the Port. Ms. Olivo serves on 
multiple national, state, and regional committees, including the 
American Association of Port Authorities Government Relations 
Committee, Gulf Ports Association of the Americas Government Affairs 
Committee, Texas Ports Association, the United Chamber of Commerce 
Government Affairs Committee, the Coastal Bend Council of Governments, 
and the Port Industries of Corpus Christi. Her efforts have helped 
shape policy discussions and strengthen collaboration across the 
maritime industry.
  Before joining the Port, Ms. Olivo served the City of Corpus Christi 
in its Housing and Community Development Department, contributing to 
programs that improve quality of life and community resilience. A proud 
native of Corpus Christi, she is a graduate of the University of the 
Incarnate Word in San Antonio, where she earned a Bachelor of Applied 
Arts and Sciences.
  Mr. Speaker, Nelda Olivo's decades of commitment, expertise, and 
outstanding public service have made a lasting impact on the Port of 
Corpus Christi and the entire Coastal Bend region.
  I commend her for her distinguished career and thank her for her 
unwavering dedication to advancing South Texas's economic vitality and 
national security interests.
