            HONORING THE SERVICE OF CHAIRMAN DAVID P. ENGEL

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. HENRY CUELLAR

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 9, 2025

  Mr. CUELLAR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize the distinguished 
career and leadership of David P. Engel, Chairman of the Port of Corpus 
Christi and respected business executive, community leader, and 
dedicated public servant.
  A proud graduate of Southern Methodist University, where he earned 
his bachelor's in

[[Page E1169]]

business administration, Mr. Engel further honed his expertise through 
the Owner President Management Program at Harvard University. For over 
50 years, he has applied his knowledge and vision to every aspect of 
business management, including operations, acquisitions, divestitures, 
and financial performance improvement.
  Before founding Engel and Associates, LLC, a management consulting 
firm, Mr. Engel served as President of Airgas Southwest, Inc., 
overseeing operations across Texas, New Mexico, and Northern Mexico. 
Under his leadership, the company thrived as a subsidiary of Airgas, 
Inc., the largest distributor of welding supplies and packaged gases in 
the United States.
  Mr. Engel's influence extends well beyond the private sector. He 
currently serves on the board of Susser Bank and the advisory board of 
Heldenfel's. He has also served as Chairman of the Corpus Christi 
Regional Economic Development Commission, the CHRISTUS Spohn Health, 
and the Lexington Museum, leaving a lasting impact on economic 
development, healthcare and cultural preservation in his community.
  During Mr. Engel's chairmanship at the Port of Corpus Christi, the 
Port of Corpus Christi experienced record-breaking tonnage in 2024 and 
the first half of 2025. Under his leadership, the Port of Corpus 
Christi achieved transformative milestones including the successful 
completion of the new Port of Corpus Christi Executive Administration 
Building, the opening of the Port of Corpus Christi Center for Wildlife 
Rescue at the Texas State Aquarium, and the completion of the new 
Harbor Bridge. Mr. Engel worked closely with the Port Authority's 
commissioners, executive leadership, staff, industry partners and the 
federal government to complete the milestone of the Corpus Christi Ship 
Channel Improvement Project, which increased the depth of the Corpus 
Christi Ship Channel from 47 to 54 feet and widened it from 400 to 530 
feet, ensuring that the Port of Corpus Christi will continue to advance 
its position as America's energy export gateway to the world.
  Through his career, David Engel has demonstrated unwavering 
commitment to excellence, strategic leadership, and service to others. 
His efforts have strengthened businesses, enhanced regional 
development, and enriched communities across South Texas.
  Mr. Speaker, Chairman David Engel embodies the highest values of 
leadership, dedication, and civic responsibility.
  I commend him for his outstanding career and thank him for his 
continued commitment to our Nation.
