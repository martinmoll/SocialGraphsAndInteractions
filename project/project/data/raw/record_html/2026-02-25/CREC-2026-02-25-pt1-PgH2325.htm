 JOINT APPOINTMENT OF INDIVIDUAL TO SERVE AS INSPECTOR GENERAL FOR THE 
                     U.S. HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The Chair announces, on behalf of the 
Speaker, majority leader and minority leader, their joint appointment, 
pursuant to clause 6 of rule II, and the order of the House of January 
3, 2025, of the following individual to serve as Inspector General for 
the U.S. House of Representatives:
  Ms. Christen Stevenson, Brandywine, Maryland
