              DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA POLICING PROTECTION ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is on the passage of the bill (H.R. 5143) to 
establish standards for law enforcement officers in the District of 
Columbia to engage in vehicular pursuits of suspects, and for other 
purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 245, 
nays 182, not voting 5, as follows:
