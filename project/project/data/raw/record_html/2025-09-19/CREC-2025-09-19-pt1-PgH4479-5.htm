                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has passed without amendment a bill of the 
House of the following title:

       H.R. 2483. An act to reauthorize certain programs that 
     provide for opioid use disorder prevention, treatment, and 
     recovery, and for other purposes.

  The message also announced that pursuant to Public Law 101-509, the 
Chair, on behalf of the Democratic Leader, announces the re-appointment 
of the following individual to serve as a member of the Advisory 
Committee on the Records of Congress:
  Denise A. Hibay of New York.
