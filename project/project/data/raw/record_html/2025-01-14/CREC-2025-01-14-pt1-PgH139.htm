  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Murphy). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the unfinished business is the vote on the motion to recommit on 
the bill (H.R. 28) to amend the Education Amendments of 1972 to provide 
that for purposes of determining compliance with title IX of such Act 
in athletics, sex shall be recognized based solely on a person's 
reproductive biology and genetics at birth, offered by the gentlewoman 
from North Carolina (Ms. Adams), on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
