    ADJOURNMENT TO THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2026; AND ADJOURNMENT FROM 
        THURSDAY, FEBRUARY 5, 2026, TO MONDAY, FEBRUARY 9, 2026.

  Mr. MURPHY. Mr. Speaker, I ask unanimous consent that when the House 
adjourns today, it adjourn to meet at 10 a.m. on Thursday, February 5, 
2026; and further, when the House adjourns on that day, it adjourn to 
meet on Monday, February 9, 2026, when it shall convene at noon for 
morning-hour debate and 2 p.m. for legislative business.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from North Carolina?
  There was no objection.
