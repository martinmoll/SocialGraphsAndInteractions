                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-50. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of Texas, relative to Senate Concurrent 
     Resolution 37, urging the Trump Administration to prioritize 
     diplomatic engagement with Panama to maintain the security, 
     efficiency, and accessibility of the canal for U.S. commerce; 
     to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
       ML-51. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Texas, relative to House Concurrent Resolution 98, urging the 
     United States Congress to enhance coordination among federal, 
     state, and local authorities in the deployment of C-UAS 
     technologies for border security; to the Committee on 
     Homeland Security.
       ML-52. Also, a memorial of the Legislature of the State of 
     Texas, relative to House Concurrent Resolution 13, urging the 
     federal government to take immediate action to curb the 
     spread of the New World screwworm into Texas, including using 
     existing resources to work with Mexico to further develop a 
     plan to combat screwworms; jointly to the Committees on 
     Agriculture, Energy and Commerce, and Armed Services.
