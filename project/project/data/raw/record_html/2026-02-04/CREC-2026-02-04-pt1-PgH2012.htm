                  RECOGNIZING OLYMPIANS FROM COLORADO

  (Mr. CRANE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CRANE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to highlight Colorado as a top 
producer of Olympians representing Team USA at the 2026 Olympic Winter 
Games in Milano Cortina.
  Colorado's Fifth Congressional District is known as Olympic City USA, 
with the U.S. Olympic and Paralympic Committee headquarters in Colorado 
Springs.
  Today, I am honored to highlight three incredible athletes on Team 
USA who reside in my hometown of Colorado Springs: Amber Glenn, Danny 
O'Shea, and Ellie Kam. They are among the 16 U.S. figure skaters 
nominated to the 2026 U.S. Olympic team.
  Mr. Speaker, Colorado's Fifth Congressional District will be rooting 
for Amber, Danny, and Ellie as they compete in the next few weeks.
  Mr. Speaker, with the opening ceremonies taking place this Friday, I 
ask you to join me in wishing them success in their careers and to 
bring the gold home for Team USA.
