                      TRUMP TAX CUTS FOR FAMILIES

  (Mr. Wilson of South Carolina was recognized to address the House for 
5 minutes.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, as the April tax deadline 
approaches, I am grateful that millions of Americans will keep larger 
tax cuts thanks to President Donald Trump signing the Working Families 
Tax Cut Act into law.
  Based on the great law, the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, average tax 
refunds are projected to rise $1,000 this year in tax savings, with 
families keeping their own money.
  These savings come from no tax on tips, no tax on overtime, no tax on 
Social Security for most recipients, and a deduction for made-in-
America auto loan interest.
  With the leadership of President Donald Trump and with the leadership 
of Speaker   Mike Johnson and Senate Majority Leader John Thune, 
Republicans allow families, workers, and seniors to keep their own 
earnings.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting peace through strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies insulting Trump and mocking Trump as this is 
Ukraine Week across America and around the world, supporting 
Ukrainian patriots successfully succeeding as they keep and defend a 
sovereign Ukraine, defeating war criminal Putin from resurrecting the 
failed Soviet Union, as we also support the adoption and promotion of 
fair and free elections in the Republican of Georgia.

  We rejoice for the people of Syria that the Assad dictatorship has 
been removed, and we thank President Donald Trump for his leadership to 
remove the dictatorship in Venezuela of Maduro. The people of Venezuela 
now have an opportunity for freedom and liberty. The people of Syria, 
because of Donald Trump, have been given a chance.
  It is an exciting time in world history, as we have a President who 
stands up for freedom and democracy, for peace through strength.
