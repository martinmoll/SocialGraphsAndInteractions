                   DISABILITY DETERMINATION RENEWALS

  (Mr. SUBRAMANYAM asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. SUBRAMANYAM. Mr. Speaker, families with disabled adult children

[[Page H2015]]

deserve our full support in Congress, but these days they are being 
forced through unnecessary security and disability determination 
renewals to prove that their disabled adult children deserve assistance 
at all.
  That is right, recipients are asked to prove over and over and over 
again whether a person still has a genetic condition, a chromosomal 
disorder, or a known lifelong disability. On top of that, these reviews 
are too often done incorrectly, inconsistently, and without regard for 
medical reality.
  This process is exhausting for families, distressing for individuals 
with disabilities, and a waste of money because--that is right--we are 
spending a lot of taxpayer dollars to prove over and over again that 
Americans with permanent conditions need assistance.
  The burden on these families doesn't stop there because Medicaid 
waiver hours are being reduced nationwide, which means families are 
losing vital community-based care, forcing parents who are able to fill 
the gaps.
  This is not efficient at all. This is neglect. This administration 
must step in, make changes now, and end the unnecessary reviews. 
Support the families instead.
