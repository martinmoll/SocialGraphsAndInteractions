    CONGRATULATING WISCONSIN'S SEVENTH DISTRICT PARALYMPIC ATHLETES

  (Mr. TIFFANY asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute.)
  Mr. TIFFANY. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Paralympic 
athletes from Wisconsin's Seventh District on earning the prestigious 
honor of competing as part of Team USA in the upcoming Olympics and 
Paralympic Winter Games starting this Friday, February 6.
  Liam Cunningham of River Falls is competing in sled hockey. Dan Rose 
of Tomah and Matt Thums of Ogema are both competing in wheelchair 
curling. They overcame tremendous obstacles and hardships to earn their 
position with Team USA. They are role models to young athletes 
everywhere, proving that with resilience and determination, Mr. 
Speaker, you can achieve any goal you set your mind to.
  Keep up the hard work and good luck. Make Wisconsin proud.
