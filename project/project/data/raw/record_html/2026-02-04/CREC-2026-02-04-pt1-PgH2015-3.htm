              CONGRATULATING PAUL BROWN ON HIS RETIREMENT

  (Mr. CARTER of Georgia asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CARTER of Georgia. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Mr. 
Paul W. Brown on his retirement from the FBI, where he served as 
Special Agent in Charge of the FBI Atlanta Field Office.
  Mr. Brown has served the FBI diligently since 2006, with experience 
spanning counterterrorism and weapons of mass destruction.
  Holding degrees from both Western Illinois and Tarleton State 
University, Mr. Brown built a well-rounded foundation that supported 
his work in law enforcement and investigations.
  Mr. Brown's leadership and public service have contributed greatly to 
safety in our communities and will leave a lasting impact on the United 
States of America.
  I congratulate Mr. Brown on his well-deserved retirement. I know the 
FBI and the American people are forever indebted to him for his work to 
keep us safe.
