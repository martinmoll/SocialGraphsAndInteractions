                        CELEBRATING PARADISE GYM

  (Mr. Cisneros of California was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, as every local businessowner knows, every

[[Page H1983]]

penny counts, especially during the holiday season. Elsa Duenez, owner 
of Paradise Gym in San Dimas, took this busy and valuable time to use 
their business to give back to the community by organizing a month-long 
fundraiser and toy drive to support the children and families of the 
City of Hope, one of the largest cancer research and treatment 
hospitals in the United States.
  Paradise Gym encouraged patrons and community members to donate new 
toys to children and their families, sharing the message that donating 
toys not only gives joy but offers hope to those navigating cancer 
treatments and care for their loved ones.
  The support and community of southern California is unlike any other, 
and I am so proud to see our neighbors come together and give back. 
This is a true testament to the character of Paradise Gym and the power 
of community and service.
  I thank Elsa and the staff at Paradise Gym for their commitment to 
service. We are so grateful for them.


                Congratulating Rorimer Elementary School

  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to acknowledge an outstanding 
accomplishment by Rorimer Elementary School in La Puente, California, a 
part of the Rowland Unified School District in California's 31st 
District.
  Rorimer Elementary has been designated as a 2026 Seal of Excellence 
School by the California Association for Bilingual Education, one of 
only four schools in California awarded this designation after 
undergoing an extensive review process.
  This award recognizes schools for their strong vision for biliteracy, 
multicultural competency, and educational equity.
  As a Spanish dual language immersion school, Rorimer Elementary has 
demonstrated notable gains in academic achievements and bilingual 
proficiency among English learners using effective research-based 
instructional strategies.
  Rorimer Elementary's success has been built through creation of a 
school-wide culture that embraces and celebrates developing students' 
bilingual language proficiency and cultural awareness in grades TK-6.
  The strong relationship built between Rorimer's teachers, staff, and 
families has been central to the school's success.
  Congratulations to the team at Rorimer Elementary for setting an 
amazing example for bilingual education.


             Recognizing Christopher Nigg on His Retirement

  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize former La Verne 
Fire Chief Christopher Nigg for his distinguished time of service with 
the La Verne Fire Department.
  While in service at the department, he led significant operational 
and organizational advancements, strengthening emergency response, 
workforce readiness, and regional integration.
  Chief Nigg worked collaboratively with regional partners to enhance 
service-and-response delivery, fostering a culture of professionalism, 
trust, and shared accountability focused on serving the community.
  Notably, he led the department to streamline emergency response with 
an efficient ambulance operator program and established a comprehensive 
succession plan for long-term organizational stability.
  I am so grateful for his leadership and service, promoting safety for 
the community in California's 31st District. He will be remembered as a 
team player, fierce advocate for fire prevention, and an innovative 
leader. We wish him the best in the next chapter of his life.


                   Celebrating 100 Years of Route 66

  Mr. CISNEROS. Mr. Speaker, 2026 marks the 100th anniversary of the 
most famous road in the country, Route 66, and it travels right through 
the 31st Congressional District in California.
  Last month, the city of Azusa unveiled its Route 66 centennial 
monument and mural at the newly designated Route 66 Tribute Corner in 
honor of this historic highway. This new mural and monument are part of 
a series of monuments that will be unveiled from Chicago to Santa 
Monica as part of the official project organized by the U.S. Route 66 
Centennial Commission.
  Mr. Speaker, we are excited that many will continue to get their 
kicks on Route 66, as we celebrate this historic highway that connected 
our community to the rest of the country and opened up a pathway for 
migration, tourism, and development in the San Gabriel Valley and the 
great State of California.
