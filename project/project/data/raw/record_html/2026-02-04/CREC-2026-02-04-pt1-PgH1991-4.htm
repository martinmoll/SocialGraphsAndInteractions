                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  In all things, O Lord, we rejoice, for our joy is not found in 
anything we do or any good fortune that comes our way. Our joy is found 
in Your abiding presence, which remains with us through both good times 
and bad, the successes and the challenges we face. We rejoice always, 
for the joy we find in You, O Lord, is our strength.
  May our prayers prove as constant as Your promises. Not just in these 
sacred moments of devotion or during the all-too-occasional seasons of 
distress, but may we pray continually, acknowledging that You are the 
source of even the smallest blessings, the hope to be found in every 
moment.
  In this certainty, may we have reason to give thanks in all 
circumstances, in every situation we encounter this day, in every 
opportunity we enjoy, and in every disappointment we endure, 
acknowledging that it is Your will, Your divine desire that we trust in 
Your provision for us and in Your presence with us in our everyday 
lives.
  Believing in Your power and claiming the assurance that is ours when 
we call on Your name, we pray.
  Amen.
