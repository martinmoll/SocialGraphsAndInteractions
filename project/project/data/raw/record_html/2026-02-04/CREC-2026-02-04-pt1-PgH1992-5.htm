                         REINING IN ICE AGENTS

  (Mr. GOLDMAN of New York asked and was given permission to address 
the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GOLDMAN of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to condemn the 
out-of-control, untrained, secret ICE paramilitary that is beating and 
killing Americans all around the country.
  Under this administration, rogue, lawless immigration agents are 
sowing chaos, causing terror and fear, and inciting violence on our 
streets. They are doing it with impunity because there is no 
accountability from the FBI.
  This is not the America we know and love. This is not the democracy 
that freed itself from a king.
  This is fascism, pure and simple. If the administration will not hold 
these rogue agents accountable, then we must do it.
  That is why we must pass my ICE OUT Act to end the current qualified 
immunity standard for ICE and CBP agents and ensure victims of this 
excessive force can seek justice and accountability.
  I will keep pushing Congress to do everything in its power to rein in 
this authoritarian immigration dragnet and get ICE out of our 
communities.
