                   HONORING THE LIFE OF JOHN GOURLIE

  (Mr. FINE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy of 
former Lady Lake Commissioner John Gourlie.
  John Gourlie, a beloved community member known for his musical talent 
and infectious personality, unfortunately passed away a few days ago at 
the age of 74.
  Commissioner Gourlie was first elected in 2022 and reelected in 2024. 
He proudly represented what it meant to be a Floridian. I mourn the 
passing of a man whose life reflected a dedication to servant 
leadership.
  During his time in office, Commissioner Gourlie represented Lady Lake 
with the utmost precision and attention to what made the city great. As 
a former businessowner, welder, and auctioneer, he understood the 
necessity and vitality of connecting local commerce and fiscal 
responsibility.
  Beloved within the community, those who knew him knew him not just as 
a commissioner, but as a friend who could put a smile on anyone's face 
with his karaoke and guitar. As a man of many roles and regions, there 
was no better representative of Florida.
  I know there is no replacement for a man this rooted and invested 
within the community, and there are no words I can speak which may ease 
the pain, loss, and grief experienced by both his family and the town 
he so lovingly served.
  Mr. Speaker, to the town of Lady Lake, I offer my full support as a 
Member of Congress and wish his family the best in this difficult time.
