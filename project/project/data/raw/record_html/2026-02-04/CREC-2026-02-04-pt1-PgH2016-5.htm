                FAMILY, SACRIFICE, AND THE ROCKET SPIRIT

  (Ms. KAPTUR asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. KAPTUR. Mr. Speaker, I rise today with a heart full of joy to 
celebrate a story that reminds us why sports, family, and community 
matter.
  At the University of Toledo, sophomore Rocket Miriam Diala was 
greeted after a hard-fought win by her mother, Francisca. Her mom had 
traveled 4,300 miles from Germany to see her daughter play women's 
college basketball at UT in person for the very first time.
  Francisca often stays up past midnight to watch Miriam's games on TV 
because of the 6-hour time difference. We can just imagine the embrace 
in the stands, the tears, the joy, and the gratitude.
  Miriam's performance showed talent, discipline, and resilience, but 
the real victory was love made visible. This community wrapped an 
international student in a sense of belonging, friendship, and a second 
family. That is the spirit of Toledo. That is the promise of America.
  Mr. Speaker, I thank Francisca for her sacrifice to Miriam and for 
inspiring us. To the Rocket family, keep lifting one another higher.
