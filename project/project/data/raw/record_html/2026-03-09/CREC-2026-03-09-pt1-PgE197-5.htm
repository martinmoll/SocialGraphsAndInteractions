                       RECOGNIZING DENISE O'BRIEN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. GUS M. BILIRAKIS

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 9, 2026

  Mr. BILIRAKIS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Denise O'Brien, 
a devoted community servant whose compassion, generosity, and steady 
leadership have made a lasting difference throughout our community. 
Since joining the Krewe of Chasco in 2017, Denise has become far more 
than a member--she has become its heart. Lovingly known as the ``Krewe 
Mom,'' she has earned this title through her unwavering care, 
encouragement, and willingness to support others in every season. 
Whether offering guidance, organizing efforts behind the scenes, or 
simply providing a listening ear, Denise consistently puts the needs of 
others first.
  Her dedication to service extends well beyond the Krewe. Denise is 
actively involved in numerous community organizations, including the 
Community Service Council, A.F.I.R.E., Amvets, and the Junior Service 
League. Through each of these organizations, she invests her time and 
energy into advocacy, fundraising, and hands-on volunteer work that 
strengthens families and uplifts those in need. Denise's impact is not 
measured solely by the hours she gives, but by the heart she brings to 
every cause. Her steady, compassionate presence fosters unity and 
encouragement among fellow volunteers and community members alike. She 
leads not through fanfare, but through consistency, kindness, and a 
genuine desire to serve. In every organization she touches, Denise 
O'Brien leaves it stronger, more connected, and more compassionate. Her 
commitment reminds us that true leadership is rooted in service and 
that the most meaningful contributions often come from those who 
quietly and faithfully show up for others.

[[Page E198]]

  For her selfless dedication, generous spirit, and profound impact on 
our community, I am proud to recognize Denise O'Brien today as a 2026 
Hero Among Us for Florida's 12th Congressional District.
