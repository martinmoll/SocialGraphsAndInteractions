                  MEDICAID CUTS MEAN HOSPITAL CLOSURES

  (Mr. KHANNA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KHANNA. Mr. Speaker, the Republican budget calls for a trillion 
dollars in Medicaid cuts to pay for tax breaks for the very wealthy.
  Mr. Speaker, 83 percent of the tax breaks go to 1 percent of 
Americans. What do these Medicaid cuts mean? It means the closure of 
hospitals and seniors denied coverage in long-term care.
  Elon Musk talks about reducing deficits, but this bill would add $2.8 
trillion to the deficit, taking it to $24.9 trillion.
  Here is what is going on in the Republican family: While Musk and 
DOGE scrounge around for change under the couch, Speaker Johnson is 
running the credit card, bankrupting our Nation.
  They are taking away your Medicaid to line the pockets of the 
wealthiest Americans who are controlling our government.
