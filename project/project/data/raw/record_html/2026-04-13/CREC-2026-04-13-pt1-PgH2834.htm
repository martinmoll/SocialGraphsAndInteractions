                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER (for himself and Mr. Lawler):
       H.R. 8247. A bill to establish an interagency group on 
     categorical exclusions, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Natural Resources.
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER (for himself and Mr. Lawler):
       H.R. 8248. A bill to amend the Federal Power Act to allow a 
     person to construct or modify an electric transmission 
     facility in a national interest electric transmission 
     corridor if the person self-certifies certain information to 
     the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER:
       H.R. 8249. A bill to amend the National Environmental 
     Policy Act of 1969 to clarify the scope of review, establish 
     limits for judicial review of environmental documents 
     relating to energy infrastructure, and for other purposes; to 
     the Committee on Natural Resources.
           By Mr. GOTTHEIMER (for himself and Ms. Stefanik):
       H.R. 8250. A bill to require operating system providers to 
     verify the age of any user of an operating system, and for 
     other purposes; to the Committee on Energy and Commerce.
           By Mr. HUIZENGA (for himself and Mr. Panetta):
       H.R. 8251. A bill to amend the Agricultural Act of 2014 
     with respect to the tree assistance program, and for other 
     purposes; to the Committee on Agriculture.
           By Mr. KILEY of California:
       H.R. 8252. A bill to amend title 23, United States Code, to 
     provide for withholding of funds for noncompliance with State 
     gas tax restriction, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Transportation and Infrastructure.
           By Mr. McCAUL (for himself and Mr. Cuellar):
       H.R. 8253. A bill to amend the Homeland Security Act of 
     2002 to establish in the Department of Homeland Security the 
     Biometric Identification Transnational Migration Alert 
     Program, and for other purposes; to the Committee on Homeland 
     Security.
           By Ms. TLAIB (for herself, Mrs. Dingell, Mr. Cleaver, 
             Mr. Deluzio, Mr. Gomez, Mr. Huffman, Ms. Jacobs, Ms. 
             Jayapal, Ms. Lee of Pennsylvania, Mr. Mullin, Ms. 
             Norton, Mrs. Ramirez, Mr. Thanedar, and Ms. 
             Velazquez):
       H.R. 8254. A bill to establish a low-income water 
     assistance program, and for other purposes; to the Committee 
     on Transportation and Infrastructure, and in addition to the 
     Committees on Energy and Commerce, Oversight and Government 
     Reform, and Ways and Means, for a period to be subsequently 
     determined by the Speaker, in each case for consideration of 
     such provisions as fall within the jurisdiction of the 
     committee concerned.
           By Ms. TLAIB:
       H. Con. Res. 84. Concurrent resolution directing the 
     President pursuant to section 5(c) of the War Powers 
     Resolution to remove United States Armed Forces from Lebanon; 
     to the Committee on Foreign Affairs.
           By Mrs. BICE (for herself, Mr. Brecheen, Mr. Moore of 
             Alabama, and Mr. Carter of Georgia):
       H. Res. 1164. A resolution directing Members required to 
     reimburse the Treasury for payments related to certain claims 
     to appear before the Clerk for public disclosure of the 
     reasons for the reimbursement; to the Committee on House 
     Administration, and in addition to the Committee on Rules, 
     for a period to be subsequently determined by the Speaker, in 
     each case for consideration of such provisions as fall within 
     the jurisdiction of the committee concerned.
           By Mrs. DINGELL (for herself, Mrs. McClain, Ms. 
             McDonald Rivet, Ms. Tlaib, Ms. Underwood, Mr. 
             Thanedar, Ms. Stevens, Mr. Moolenaar, Mr. Stanton, 
             Mr. Bergman, Ms. Meng, Ms. Scholten, Mr. Walberg, Mr. 
             Huizenga, Mr. Barrett, and Ms. Bynum):
       H. Res. 1165. A resolution congratulating the University of 
     Michigan Wolverines men's basketball team on winning the 2026 
     National Collegiate Athletic Association Division I Men's 
     Basketball Championship; to the Committee on Education and 
     Workforce.
           By Mrs. LUNA:
       H. Res. 1166. A resolution providing for the expulsion of 
     Representative Eric Swalwell from the United States House of 
     Representatives; to the Committee on Ethics.
