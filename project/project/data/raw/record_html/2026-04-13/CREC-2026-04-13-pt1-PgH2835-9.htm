                            PETITIONS, ETC.

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       PT-9. The SPEAKER presented a petition of the Mayor and 
     City Commission of the City of North Miami Beach, relative to 
     Resolution No. R2026-28, Denouncing the termination of 
     Temporary Protected Status (TPS) for Haiti and for other 
     countries, and urging President Donald J. Trump and the 
     United States Congress to reinstate TPS protections for 
     Haitians and for others protected by TPS; which was referred 
     to the Committee on the Judiciary.
