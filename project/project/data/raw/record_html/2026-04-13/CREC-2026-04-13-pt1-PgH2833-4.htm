                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Stephanie Vader, Capitol Hill United Methodist Church, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Holy mystery, help us keep finding steady ground in this time of 
instability.
  Holy mystery, remind us in these days of spectacle to stay human by 
telling the truth, caring for each other, and practicing mercy instead 
of revenge.
  Holy mystery, help us to cultivate community by listening, trusting, 
and staying present to each other.
  Holy mystery, remind us that courage and wisdom come when we stand 
shoulder to shoulder with all of our beloved siblings working for the 
flourishing of all.
  Holy mystery, in this season of resurrection, we do not wait for new 
life to begin some day; we practice it now by tending to life and 
resisting fear.
  Holy mystery, we do not know what comes next, but we trust that we 
already carry what we need in shared values, shared songs, and shared 
responsibilities.
  Amen.
