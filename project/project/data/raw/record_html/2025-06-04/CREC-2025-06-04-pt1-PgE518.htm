                         RECOGNIZING KLOEY KEHL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BRITTANY PETTERSEN

                              of colorado

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, June 4, 2025

  Ms. PETTERSEN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Kloey Kehl for 
earning the Arvada Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
  Kloey has overcome many challenges along her journey to success, 
demonstrating perseverance at every step. Students who strive to make 
the most of their education, like Kloey, develop crucial skills and a 
work ethic that will guide them for the rest of their lives. This award 
is a testament to Kloey's hard work, determination, and perseverance at 
Three Creeks K-8 and is clearly just the beginning of a bright and 
promising future.
  It is my honor to congratulate Kloey Kehl on achieving the Arvada 
Wheat Ridge Service Ambassadors for Youth Award.
