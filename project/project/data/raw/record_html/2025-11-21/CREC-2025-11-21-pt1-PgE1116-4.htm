                        HONORING ANNIE CLARKSON

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. CHUCK EDWARDS

                           of north carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 21, 2025

  Mr. EDWARDS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. Annie Clarkson 
for her work as an intern, serving in my D.C. office. I would like to 
take this opportunity to thank her for her hard work and recognize the 
meaningful contributions she has made to the office this fall.
  Interns work with staff to serve constituents in North Carolina's 
11th congressional district. Annie was chosen from a competitive pool 
of applicants and exceeded every expectation with her positive attitude 
and impressive work ethic. It was a pleasure to work with Annie, and I 
am so proud to have had such an intelligent and ambitious intern 
representing my office.
  Annie resides in Washington, D.C. and is a recent graduate of the 
University of Richmond. Annie is entering a position with a market 
research firm, where she will be working as a Business Development 
Representative.
  Annie exemplified the spirit of public service and represents the 
best that our Nation has to offer. I congratulate Annie on the 
successful completion of her internship and thank her on behalf of the 
people of Western North Carolina.
