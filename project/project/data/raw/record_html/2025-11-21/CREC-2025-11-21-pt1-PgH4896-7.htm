           By Mr. BEGICH:
       H.R. 6251.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following provisions of the Constitution of the United 
     States:
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 3 (the Commerce Clause), which 
     grants Congress the authority to regulate commerce with 
     foreign nations. The bill amends the Marine Mammal Protection 
     Act to govern the importation of certain polar bear parts 
     from Canada, an activity squarely within foreign commerce.
       Article I, Section 8, Clause 18 (the Necessary and Proper 
     Clause), which authorizes Congress to make all laws necessary 
     and proper for carrying into execution the foregoing powers, 
     including regulation of wildlife importation and permitting 
     under federal law.
