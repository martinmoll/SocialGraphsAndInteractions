         HONORING FARRAGUT CAREER ACADEMY 16-INCH SOFTBALL TEAM

  (Mr. GARCIA of Illinois asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GARCIA of Illinois. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to proudly honor 
the Chicago city champs, the members of the Farragut Career Academy 16-
inch softball team from my district.
  At a time when ICE raids swept through our neighborhoods while DHS 
carried out Operation Midway Blitz, these remarkable young people 
brought a ray of hope and something truly worth celebrating to Little 
Village.
  They persevered through these challenges, remained focused, and put 
in the hard work required to become champions. They have a great 
underdog story, a testament to determination, discipline, raw talent, 
and what it can achieve.

[[Page H4882]]

  The Farragut Career Academy 16-inch softball team is an inspiring 
example of the pride and resilience that define Little Village.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate them. They have made us beyond proud.
