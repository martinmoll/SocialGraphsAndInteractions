        HONORING KILGORE'S DAUGHTERS OF THE AMERICAN REVOLUTION

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. NATHANIEL MORAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                       Friday, November 21, 2025

  Mr. MORAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Kilgore's Daughters of 
the American Revolution.
  250 years ago, brave warriors fought for a republic founded on 
freedom and liberty. How remarkable is it that each of them came from 
individuals and families who fought for the country we call home today.
  The mission their ancestors started is not over. For 135 years, 
Daughters of the American Revolution have played a vital role in 
preserving history and encouraging patriotism across the country.
  In Kilgore, they have gone above and beyond to continue the legacy of 
our Nation through educational initiatives and supporting our veterans.
  As we approach the 250th anniversary of America, their work is more 
pertinent now than ever before. Leading up to the founding of America, 
their ancestors stood their ground, wrestled with the weight of 
liberty, and lit the spark that would give birth to the greatest Nation 
the world has ever known. Our Nation is grateful for those brave men 
and women, their sacrifices, and the unwavering commitment to the 
founding of our Nation.
  Now, through their impact, East Texans and Americans will be reminded 
of this service and patriotism.
  I look forward to their participation in America 250--a nationwide 
event, with real community impact. I am grateful for their service to 
our community and our country. God Bless Kilgore, and God Bless the 
United States of America.
