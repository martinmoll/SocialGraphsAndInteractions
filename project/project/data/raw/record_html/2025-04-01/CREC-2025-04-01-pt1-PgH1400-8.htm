       HOUSE BILL AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

dates he had approved and signed a bill and a joint resolution of the 
following titles:

           March 14, 2025:
       H.J. Res. 35. An Act providing for congressional 
     disapproval under chapter 8 of title 5,

     Environmental Protection Agency relating to ``Waste Emissions

           March 15, 2025:
       H.R. 1968. An Act making further continuing appropriations 
     and other extensions for the fiscal year ending September 30, 
     2025, and for other purposes.
