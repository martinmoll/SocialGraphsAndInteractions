                           ICE HAS GONE ROGUE

  (Mr. KHANNA asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute.)
  Mr. KHANNA. Mr. Speaker, ICE has gone rogue. I was sickened to see an 
ICE officer shoot a 37-year-old mother, leaving her 6 year old an 
orphan. That officer needs to be arrested and prosecuted.
  Mr. Speaker, I am concerned about the human rights of people in ICE 
detention facilities. I visited the California City Immigration 
Detention Center and spoke with 47 detainees. They talked about rocks 
in their food. They talked about having blood in their urine and not 
being able to see a doctor. They talked about freezing in the night 
because they didn't have clothes.
  These are human rights violations. This is not the way America acts. 
We need accountability to ICE, and we need to act with our values.
