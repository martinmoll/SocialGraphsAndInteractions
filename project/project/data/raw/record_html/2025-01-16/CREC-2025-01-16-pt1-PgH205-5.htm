            CONGRATULATING BAYONNE MODEL UNITED NATIONS TEAM

  (Mr. MENENDEZ asked and was given permission to address the House for 
1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. MENENDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor and congratulate the 
talented students of Bayonne High School Model United Nations team who 
returned from Dubai this week where they represented our community and 
our great State at the Harvard Model United Nations conference.
  Under the leadership of Colleen Rushnak, these young men and women 
are learning the critical skills of leadership, problem-solving, and 
international diplomacy. I had the opportunity to visit with them 
recently and couldn't be prouder that they represented us on the global 
stage.
  Congratulations to the Fighting Bees. You made our entire community 
proud.
