                DIGITAL ASSET MARKET CLARITY ACT OF 2025

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the bill (H.R. 3633) to 
provide for a system of regulation of the offer and sale of digital 
commodities by the Securities and Exchange Commission and the Commodity 
Futures Trading Commission, and for other purposes, on which the yeas 
and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the bill.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 294, 
nays 134, not voting 4, as follows:
