HONORING THE 20TH ANNIVERSARY OF REVEREND TYRONE NELSON AT SIXTH MOUNT 
                          ZION BAPTIST CHURCH

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. JENNIFER L. McCLELLAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 17, 2025

  Ms. McCLELLAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate 
the 20th pastoral anniversary of Reverend Tyrone Nelson, a native of 
Richmond, Virginia, and the esteemed pastor of Sixth Mount Zion Baptist 
Church.
  For two decades, Reverend Nelson has led Sixth Mount Zion with 
vision, compassion, and an unwavering commitment to service. Under his 
leadership, the church has grown not only in membership, but in its 
outreach and impact throughout the Richmond community. He has been a 
steadfast advocate for the needs of his congregation and a champion for 
justice, education, housing, and economic empowerment in the city and 
beyond.
  Reverend Nelson's tenure has been marked by a spirit of innovation 
and inclusivity, fostering a welcoming environment for individuals and 
families from all walks of life. His ability to connect faith with 
action has helped shape the church into a community anchor, offering 
programs and partnerships that address food insecurity, youth 
mentorship, and senior care, while serving as a safe gathering place 
for civic dialogue and community advancement.
  In addition to his responsibilities as pastor of Sixth Mount Zion 
Baptist, Reverend Nelson has remained active in civic life, lending his 
voice to advance equity, opportunity, and public service. His 
leadership extends far beyond the pulpit, touching the lives of 
countless community members through both his words and his work.
  Reverend Nelson's dedication over the past 20 years is a testament to 
what can be achieved when faith, integrity, and community come 
together.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating 
Reverend Tyrone Nelson on 20 years of extraordinary service. May his 
leadership continue to inspire, uplift, and strengthen the Richmond 
community for many years to come.
