                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker, on Thursday, July 17, 2025, announced his signature to 
enrolled bills of the Senate of the following titles:

       S. 1582--An act to provide for the regulation of payment 
     stablecoins, and for other purposes.
       S. 1596--An act to rename the Anahauc National Wildlife 
     Refuge located in the State of Texas as the ``Jocelyn 
     Nungaray National Wildlife Refuge''.
