                                 PRAYER

  Reverend Dr. Lindsay Curtis, Grace Baptist Church, Norwalk, 
Connecticut, offered the following prayer:
  Dear Jehovah God, our divine company keeper, it is with hearts full 
of passion and unwavering faith that we gather today, seeking Your 
divine protection and wisdom. Envelop us in Your safety as we pursue 
our daily tasks, ever mindful of Your promise to send angels to guard 
us.
  Please, dear God, guide this deliberative body in every decision, 
trusting in Your steadfastness and in Your presence as our beacon of 
truth. Walk beside us, gently moving our hearts unto Your intended 
path. Enter our lives, Lord, filling us with Your wisdom to see our 
choices through Your vision. Wrap us in the warmth of Your love, as we 
place all hopes and faith in You. Grateful for Your protection, we 
surrender our worries, aligning our steps and our thoughts to Your will 
and Your way. We pray in the mighty name of Your Son.
  Amen.
