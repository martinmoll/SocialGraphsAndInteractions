       CELEBRATING DJ ROBERTS AND THE LAWSON AND ROBERTS FAMILIES

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. PETE STAUBER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 10, 2025

  Mr. STAUBER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor DJ Roberts, a soon-
to-be deputy sheriff from Itasca County in Minnesota's Eighth 
Congressional District, who will be reclaiming his grandfather Robert 
``Beefy'' Lawson's badge number 703 and carrying forward a legacy of 
heroic service.
  Beefy Lawson was a beloved figure. A Korean War veteran, a talented 
hockey player and referee, and a deputy sheriff whose charisma and 
selflessness touched countless lives. He was a pillar of our community. 
Tragically, he was killed in the line of duty, leaving a void in his 
family and community.
  Inspired by his grandfather's example, DJ Roberts has pursued a path 
of service with remarkable courage. Despite a dyslexia diagnosis, DJ 
transitioned from a 20-year career in construction to become a 
corrections officer at the Itasca County Sheriffs Office in 2018. In 
2023, DJ bravely returned to school in his 40s to pursue his dream of 
becoming a road deputy, overcoming mental, emotional, and physical 
challenges while balancing fatherhood to four children and his duties 
as a husband.
  Today, we celebrate DJ, known to his late grandfather as ``Buddy 
Guy,'' for being honored with Beefy Lawson's badge number as a powerful 
symbol of his commitment to public safety and a full-circle moment for 
the Lawson and Roberts family. With resilience, integrity, quiet 
heroism, and strong faith in God, DJ honors his grandfather's memory, 
inspiring our community by continuing a legacy of service and 
sacrifice.
