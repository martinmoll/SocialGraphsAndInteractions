 RECOGNIZING TIMOTHY TEMPLETON FOR HIS DISTINGUISHED SERVICE WITH THE 
                       UNITED STATES MARINE CORPS

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. EMANUEL CLEAVER

                              of missouri

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 10, 2025

  MR. CLEAVER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Timothy 
Templeton, who dedicated his time to contribute to the safety and 
security of our nation through his service in the United States Marine 
Corps. It is an honor to have an individual that embodies strength, 
courage and commitment in Missouri's Fifth Congressional District.
  Born and raised in Missouri, Corporal Templeton enlisted in the 
United States Marine Corps after graduating from North Platte High 
School in Dearborn, Missouri, in 1982. Upon joining, he became a Small 
Missile Systems Electronics Repair Technician (MOS 2875) and was 
stationed at Camp Lejeune, North Carolina, with the 2nd Force Service 
Support Group, Electronics Maintenance Company, Calibrations Platoon to 
provide maintenance for TOW2 and Dragon anti-tank missile systems.
  During his time at Camp Lejeune, he was temporarily assigned to the 
26th Marine Amphibious Unit Service Support Group which deployed with 
Amphibious Squadron 6 as part of Marine Amphibious Readiness Group 1-86 
to the Mediterranean Sea. During this deployment, he was the TOW 
Subject Matter Expert and was also selected to ship's platoon for the 
USS El Paso (LKA-117) to coordinate embarkation/debarkation of Marines 
and equipment. His unit was awarded the Navy Meritorious Unit 
Commendation for this deployment.
  During his service, Corporal Templeton's unit participated in 
Operation Eldorado Canyon off the coast of Libya in the spring of 1986. 
His unit was awarded the Marine Corps Expeditionary Medal for their 
contribution to the operation. It is evident that Templeton's 
leadership allowed his team to move with direction, motivation, and the 
ability to go far and beyond the standard, receiving recognition as a 
testament.
  Corporal Templeton was released from the Marine Corps active duty in 
1987, serving in the ready reserves and earning an Honorable Discharge 
in 1989. His willingness to put his life on the line and endure the 
hardships that come with military service has not gone unnoticed. As a 
thank you for his sacrifice, bravery and example set, it is my honor to 
present Corporal Templeton with the Marine Corps Expeditionary Medal as 
well as the Navy Meritorious Unit Commendation ribbon he earned while 
serving our country. Corporal Templeton served the Marine Corps with a 
source of immense pride, leading an often-distinguished team and his 
service deserves to be honored. Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to 
join me today in recognizing Timothy Templeton and thank him for his 
service.
