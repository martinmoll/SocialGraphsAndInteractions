SPECIAL RECOGNITION OF ISABELLA ZAHN FOR WINNING THE DIVISION III STATE 
TRACK AND FIELD TITLE FOR THE 200 METER DASH, 400 METER DASH, AND LONG 
                                  JUMP

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. ROBERT E. LATTA

                                of ohio

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 10, 2025

  Mr. LATTA. Mr. Speaker, it is with a great deal of pride that I rise 
to pay a very special tribute to an outstanding high school athlete 
from Ohio's Fifth Congressional District. Isabella Zahn, a senior at 
Coldwater High School, has represented her school ably on her way to 
achieving three Division III State Titles for the 200 Meter Dash, 400 
Meter Dash, and Girls Long Jump at the State Track and Field Meet. Anna 
overcame the challenges posed by intense competition in her effort to 
win the title.
  In pursuing the State Championship, Anna defeated her opponents with 
an impressive time of 23.04 seconds in the 200 Meter Dash, 54.56 
seconds in the 400 Meter Dash, and a jump of 18-08.50 in the Girls Long 
Jump. Her hard work and dedication, both on and off the track, in 
achieving this accomplishment is truly outstanding. She has achieved an 
impressive season, bringing pride to her school and community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in paying special tribute 
to Isabella Zahn of Coldwater High School. On behalf of the people of 
the Fifth District of Ohio, I am proud to recognize this great 
achievement.
