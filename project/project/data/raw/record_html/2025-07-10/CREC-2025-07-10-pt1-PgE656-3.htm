          HONORING THE LIVERMORE STOCKMEN'S RODEO ASSOCIATION

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. ERIC SWALWELL

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, July 10, 2025

  Mr. SWALWELL. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize the Livermore 
Stockmen's Rodeo Association (hereinafter the ``Association'') on the 
occasion of its well-earned induction into the ProRodeo Hall of Fame, 
in Colorado Springs, Colorado, this Saturday, July 12, 2025.
  The Livermore Rodeo has been a valued part of California's Tri-Valley 
community for over a century. Founded in response to a national call 
for help during World War I, the Livermore Rodeo began as a local 
fundraiser and effort to support the American Red Cross and wounded 
soldiers overseas. The first event, held at James Anderson Ranch, 
raised over $1,200.00, an extraordinary sum for the time. Its success 
caught national attention after being filmed by Universal Studios and 
displayed in theatres across the country, turning a community 
fundraiser into a cherished annual tradition.
  The event earned the nickname, ``The World's Fastest Rodeo,'' and 
since moving to Robertson Park in 1967, it has continued to

[[Page E657]]

grow and attract thousands of spectators each year for top-level 
competition. Its years of operation, led by the Association, have 
brought a great deal of pride and belonging to the Tri-Valley. The 
Association stays true to its local roots, supporting not only the 
rodeo itself, but also the broader community. Today, the event includes 
charities such as ``Tough Enough to Wear Pink,'' in support of breast 
cancer awareness, and ``Patriot Day'', in honor of military service. 
Accompanying events like the ``Lil' Pardners Rodeo'' and the Rotary 
Club parade make rodeo weekend an annual regional highlight.
  This year, the Association celebrates its 107th year with its most 
distinguished honor yet, joining a select group of inductees to the 
ProRodeo Hall of Fame, recognizing its long-standing contributions to 
the sport. The ProRodeo Hall of Fame is the only museum in the world 
dedicated exclusively to professional rodeo and its legacy. This honor 
reflects the work of many volunteers, fans, directors, and community 
members who have kept the tradition alive for over 100 years.
  Congratulations to the Association, the rodeo competitors and 
participants, and all the fans that have enjoyed the shows and made the 
Livermore Rodeo the success that it has become.
