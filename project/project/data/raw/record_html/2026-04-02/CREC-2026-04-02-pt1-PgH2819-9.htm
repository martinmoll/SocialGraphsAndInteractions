         REPORTS OF COMMITTEES ON PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XIII, reports of committees were delivered to 
the Clerk for printing and reference to the proper calendar, as 
follows:

       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 6047. A bill 
     to amend title 38, United States Code, to direct the 
     Secretary of Veterans Affairs to increase the dollar amounts 
     for the payment of certain disability compensation and 
     dependency and indemnity compensation under the laws 
     administered by the Secretary; with an amendment (Rept. 119-
     577). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. BOST: Committee on Veterans' Affairs. H.R. 785. A bill 
     to amend title 38, United States Code, to include a 
     representative of the National Association of State Veterans 
     Homes on the Geriatrics and Gerontology Advisory Committee of 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs; with an amendment (Rept. 
     119-578). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 41. A 
     bill to provide for the recognition of certain Alaska Native 
     communities and the settlement of certain claims under the 
     Alaska Native Claims Settlement Act, and for other purposes, 
     with an amendment (Rept. 119-579). Referred to the Committee 
     of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 1352. A 
     bill to designate the General George C. Marshall House, in 
     the Commonwealth of Virginia, as an affiliated area of the 
     National Park System, and for other purposes; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 119-580). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2252. A 
     bill to authorize the relinquishment and in lieu selection of 
     land and minerals in the State of North Dakota, to restore 
     land and minerals to Indian Tribes within the State of North 
     Dakota, and for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-
     581). Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the 
     state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 2768. A 
     bill to amend the National Trails System Act to direct the 
     Secretary of Agriculture to conduct a study on

[[Page H2820]]

     the feasibility of designating the Benton MacKaye Trail as a 
     national scenic trail; with an amendment (Rept. 119-582). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 4463. A 
     bill to amend the Catawba Indian Tribe of South Carolina Land 
     Claims Settlement Act of 1993 (Rept. 119-583). Referred to 
     the Committee of the Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 4684. A 
     bill to direct the Secretary of Agriculture to issue a 
     special use permit with respect to the maintaining of a 
     flagpole bearing the flag of the United States at Kyhv Peak 
     Lookout Point, Utah, and for other purposes; with an 
     amendment (Rept. 119-584). Referred to the Committee of the 
     Whole House on the state of the Union.
       Mr. WESTERMAN: Committee on Natural Resources. H.R. 5682. A 
     bill to take certain land in the State of California into 
     trust for the benefit of the Pechanga Band of Indians, and 
     for other purposes; with an amendment (Rept. 119-585). 
     Referred to the Committee of the Whole House on the state of 
     the Union.
