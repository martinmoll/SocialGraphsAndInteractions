                                 PRAYER

  Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  Almighty God, we give thanks for the Members of this House and for 
their willingness to serve the people of this land.
  Bless them, along with their staffs, families, and all who serve in 
this building with wisdom, patience, and steadfast hearts.
  As many in our country observe Passover and many others prepare to 
celebrate Easter, we are mindful of the enduring hope that these sacred 
days proclaim.
  Grant our Representatives, in their work, spirits of hope and a 
shared commitment to the common good.
  Amen.
