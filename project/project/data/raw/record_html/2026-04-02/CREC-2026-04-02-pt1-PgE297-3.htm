   HONORING WRIGHTSVILLE ELEMENTARY SCHOOL FOR ITS DESIGNATION AS A 
                    PENNSYLVANIA BLUE RIBBON SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. LLOYD SMUCKER

                            of pennsylvania

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 2, 2026

  Mr. SMUCKER. Mr. Speaker, I rise to recognize Wrightsville Elementary 
School for being designated as a Pennsylvania Blue Ribbon School.
  The Commonwealth of Pennsylvania designates exemplary public schools 
Blue Ribbon status to honor their commitment to advancing public 
education. This year, Wrightsville Elementary was proudly named an 
Exemplary Achievement Gap Closing School for their work at reducing 
educational disparities and barriers for their students.
  Wrightsville Elementary is a K-5 public school serving approximately 
350 students in the Eastern York School District in Wrightsville, 
Pennsylvania. The school emphasizes personalized, student-centered 
learning that recognizes each student's unique learning needs. As the 
nature of education and our world evolves, Wrightsville Elementary saw 
the need for students to develop and meet these challenges head on.
  A key feature of this personalized learning model is Wrightsville's 
``Mastery Badging'' system, which tracks progress based on a student's 
skills rather than time spent, promoting engagement and ownership 
during their learning. Systems like ``Mastery Badge'' are an example of 
the innovate approach to ensure that all students are given every 
chance to succeed, not only in the classroom, but in life as well.
  I commend the teachers, administration, and students of Wrightsville 
Elementary for being given this distinction. I am sure they will 
continue to foster a learning-friendly environment that will help 
prepare our young people for the world ahead. Eastern York's commitment 
to the next generation is clear, and I am proud to have such a place 
for students in my district.
  I congratulate Wrightsville Elementary School and Eastern York School 
District on this momentous achievement.
