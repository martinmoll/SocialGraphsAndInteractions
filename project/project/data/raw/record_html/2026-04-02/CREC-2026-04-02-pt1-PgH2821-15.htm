           By Mr. OWENS:
       H.R. 8183.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution of the 
     United States, which grants Congress the authority to provide 
     for the general welfare of the United States, and Article I, 
     Section 8, Clause 3, which grants Congress the authority to 
     regulate commerce among the several states.
