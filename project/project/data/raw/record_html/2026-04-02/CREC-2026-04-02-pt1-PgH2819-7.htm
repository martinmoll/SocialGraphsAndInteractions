 APPOINTMENT OF MEMBER TO ACT AS SPEAKER PRO TEMPORE TO SIGN ENROLLED 
         BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTIONS DURING THE 119TH CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Speaker of the House of Representatives:

                                    Congress of the United States,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, April 2, 2026.
       I hereby appoint the Honorable Matt Van Epps to act as 
     Speaker pro tempore to sign enrolled bills and joint 
     resolutions through the remainder of the One Hundred 
     Nineteenth Congress.
           Sincerely,
                                                     Mike Johnson,
                          Speaker of the House of Representatives.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Without objection, the appointment is 
approved.
  There was no objection.
