               COMMUNICATION FROM THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE

  The SPEAKER laid before the House the following communication from 
the Clerk of the House of Representatives:

                                              Office of the Clerk,


                                     House of Representatives,

                                    Washington, DC, April 2, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     The Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: I have the honor to transmit herewith a 
     copy of a letter received from Ms. Maria Matthews, Esquire, 
     Director of Elections, with the Florida Department of State 
     indicating that, according to the unofficial results for the 
     Special General Election held on April 1, 2025, the Honorable 
     Randy Fine was elected Representative to Congress for the 
     Sixth Congressional District of Florida.
       With best wishes, I am,
           Sincerely,
                                                Kevin F. McCumber,
                                                            Clerk.


                                  Florida Department of State,

                                   Tallahassee, FL, April 2, 2025.
     Hon. Kevin F. McCumber,
     Clerk, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. McCumber: This is to advise you that the election 
     night results of the Special Election held on Tuesday, April 
     1, 2025, for Representative in Congress from the Sixth 
     Congressional District of Florida show that Randy Fine 
     received 110,764 or 56.66% of the total number of votes cast 
     for that office.
       It would appear from these election night results that 
     Randy Fine was elected as Representative in Congress from the 
     Sixth Congressional District of Florida.
       To the best of our knowledge and belief at this time, there 
     is no recount or contest to this election.
       As soon as the official results are certified to this 
     office by Flagler, Putnam, and parts of Lake, Marion, St. 
     Johns, and Volusia counties involved, an official Certificate 
     of

[[Page H1410]]

     Election will be prepared for transmittal as required by law.
           Respectfully,
                                             Maria Matthews, Esq.,
     Director, Division of Elections.
