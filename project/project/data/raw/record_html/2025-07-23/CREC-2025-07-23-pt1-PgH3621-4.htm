              ENHANCING MULTI-CLASS SHARE DISCLOSURES ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 3357) to amend the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 
to require issuers with a multi-class stock structure to make certain 
disclosures in any proxy or consent solicitation material, and for 
other purposes, as amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Arkansas (Mr. Hill) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 381, 
nays 31, not voting 18, as follows:
