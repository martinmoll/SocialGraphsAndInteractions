                      SENATE ENROLLED BILLS SIGNED

  The Speaker announced his signature to enrolled bills of the Senate 
of the following titles:

       S. 201.--An act to provide for a study by the National 
     Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine on the 
     prevalence and mortality of cancer among individuals who 
     served as active duty aircrew in the Armed Forces, and for 
     other purposes.
       S. 423.--An act to protect regular order for budgeting for 
     the Department of Veterans Affairs, and for other purposes.
