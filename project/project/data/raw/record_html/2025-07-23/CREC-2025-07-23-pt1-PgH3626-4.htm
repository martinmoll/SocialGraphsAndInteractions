        RECOGNIZING USAF CHIEF MASTER SERGEANT JASON Q. SHAFFER

  (Mr. FINE asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. FINE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize a warrior of the 
United States Air Force, Chief Master Sergeant Jason Q. Shaffer.
  Chief Shaffer embodies the mindset and diligence that is needed to 
defend our Nation. He entered the Air Force in March 2000 as a security 
forces member and has been serving for 25 years.
  During his time in the Air Force, he developed an impressive 
background, which includes experience at varying levels in the Security 
Forces. He has participated in operations in Africa, Europe, the Middle 
East, South Asia, and the Pacific region. He is now retiring after all 
of that time.
  Being a servicemember is tough. It is demanding, and the time spent 
away from your family is even harder. Yet, we can never forget to thank 
those who make that sacrifice.
  I thank him; his wife, Tawny; and his children, Dallas, Ashlynn, and 
Teagan, for making sacrifices so our Nation can prosper.
  Chief Jason, Aim High and have a happy retirement.
