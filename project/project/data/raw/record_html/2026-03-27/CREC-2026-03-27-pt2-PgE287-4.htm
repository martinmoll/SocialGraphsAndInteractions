                         Friday, March 27, 2026

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, I am grateful that 
President Donald Trump has taken successful actions to reduce 
government regulations, especially in the field of energy, creating 
jobs.
  Last month, the Department of Energy gave approval for the first, 
full-power portable microreactor test to Radiant Industries of 
California.
  Microreactors are smaller than traditional nuclear power plants, 
transportable in a single shipping container--with one-day 
installation.
  The Savannah River Site in Aiken, South Carolina, announced three 
public-private partnerships to develop small modular nuclear reactor 
technologies, ideal by military facilities and islands such as Guam and 
Puerto Rico.
  President Trump, Energy Secretary Chris Wright, and Nuclear 
Regulatory Commission Member David Wright, along with Thomas McAllister 
with Department of Energy Congressional Affairs, are working to make 
America energy independent, reliable, and secure.
