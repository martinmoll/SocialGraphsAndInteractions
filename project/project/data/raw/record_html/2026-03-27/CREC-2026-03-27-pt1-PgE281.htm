                    HONORING MAJOR ALEXANDER FELDMAN

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. ROBERT J. WITTMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 27, 2026

  Mr. WITTMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and honor an 
exceptional Marine and a valued member of our congressional family, 
Alexander Feldman, on the occasion of his well-earned promotion to the 
rank of Major. This milestone is a testament to his devotion, 
professionalism, and unyielding commitment to serving our Nation. 
Advancing to this level of leadership reflects both the trust placed in 
Alex by the Marine Corps and the exceptional standard he has 
consistently demonstrated throughout his career.
  During his time serving as my Department of Defense Legislative 
Fellow, Major Feldman distinguished himself as a professional of the 
highest caliber. His quick wit, sharp judgment, and unwavering 
commitment to excellence made him an invaluable asset to our team. 
Whether navigating complex national security challenges or offering 
thoughtful insight on matters affecting our Armed Forces, Alex 
consistently exhibited a level of competence and character that set him 
apart.
  It is no surprise to those of us who had the privilege of working 
alongside him that he has now been promoted to the rank of Major in the 
United States Marine Corps. This achievement reflects not only his 
remarkable abilities, but also his unbreakable dedication to serving 
our Nation.
  While we deeply miss his presence in our office, we take great pride 
in knowing that he will continue to do great things wherever he goes. 
The Marine Corps, and our country, are stronger because of leaders like 
Major Feldman.
   Mr. Speaker, please join me in recognizing Alexander Feldman for his 
service to Virginia's 1st District and the Nation. I pray for his 
continued success in all that lies ahead.
