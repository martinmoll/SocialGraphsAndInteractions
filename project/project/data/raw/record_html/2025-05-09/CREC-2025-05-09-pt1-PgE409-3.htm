                  HONORING THE LIFE OF DIANE RAULSTON

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. EUGENE SIMON VINDMAN

                              of virginia

                    in the house of representatives

                          Friday, May 9, 2025

  Mr. VINDMAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the extraordinary 
life and service of Diane Raulston, a devoted public servant, community 
leader, and advocate for education in Prince William County, Virginia.
  Diane Raulston served the residents of the Neabsco District with 
distinction on the Prince William County School Board from 2015 to 
2023. Throughout her years of service, she was a passionate and 
determined advocate for public education, promoting efforts to raise 
teacher pay, close opportunity gaps, and create a more equitable, 
student-centered school system.
  Under Diane Raulston's leadership. the school division made 
meaningful strides: graduation rates rose, access to pre-kindergarten 
expanded, and Career and Technical Education programs grew 
significantly. Her steady support helped guide the opening of nine new 
schools to meet the demands of a growing student population, ensuring 
that every child had a safe and welcoming place to learn.
  Beyond her official role, Diane was known for her kindness, 
determination, and unwavering belief in the power of public education 
to uplift communities. She listened, she acted, and she made a 
difference in countless lives. Her legacy is not only visible in the 
buildings and programs she helped establish, but also in the spirit of 
service she inspired in others.
  As we remember Diane Raulston, we celebrate a life lived in service 
to others, a life marked by integrity, compassion, and impact. Her 
passing is a profound loss for our community, but her contributions 
will endure for generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Diane 
Raulston's remarkable life. May her legacy of service and her devotion 
to educational equity continue to inspire and guide our efforts.
