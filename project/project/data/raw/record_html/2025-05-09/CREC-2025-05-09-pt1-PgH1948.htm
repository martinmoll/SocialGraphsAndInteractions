                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-4. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the Legislature 
     of the State of New Mexico, relative to House Memorial 15, 
     raising awareness of the challenges experienced by people 
     exposed to radiation and encouraging the United States 
     Congress to support legislation to expand compensation under 
     the Federal Radiation Exposure Compensation Act; to the 
     Committee on the Judiciary.
       ML-5. Also, a memorial of the General Assembly of the State 
     of Ohio, relative to Senate Resolution No. 21, to urge the 
     U.S. Congress to support the provision of prompt and 
     comprehensive mental health treatment to military personnel 
     and veterans who are suffering from post-traumatic stress 
     disorder and other service-related stress disorders; jointly 
     to the Committees on Armed Services and Veterans' Affairs.
