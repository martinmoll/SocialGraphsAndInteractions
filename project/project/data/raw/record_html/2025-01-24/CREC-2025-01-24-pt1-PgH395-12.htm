           By Mr. HARRIS of North Carolina:
       H.R. 729.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the authority to extend protection to unborn 
     children and their mothers under the Commerce Clause of 
     Article 1, Section 8. This authority also comes from the 
     Equal Protection Clause and Due Process Clauses of Section 1 
     and the Enforcement Clause of Section 5 of the 14th 
     Amendment.
