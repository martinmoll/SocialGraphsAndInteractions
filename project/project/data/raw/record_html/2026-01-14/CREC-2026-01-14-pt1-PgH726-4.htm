                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, let all who are faithful offer their prayers to You. 
Trusting in Your care, teach us the way we should go, that we may yield 
ourselves to Your instruction and have confidence that Your 
righteousness is our strength.
  When we are overwhelmed or in distress, even the inundation of mighty 
waters will not overtake us. For You are our shelter, a hiding place 
where we are protected from discouragement. Your voice delivers us from 
despair.
  Your eye is upon us. You watch over our comings and goings. You 
attend to our motivations and discipline our inclinations. Your counsel 
is omniscient and perceptive, plumbing the depths of our innermost 
souls.
  Your steadfast love surrounds us. There is nowhere we can be, no turn 
or move we can make that we are not hemmed in by Your compassionate 
care and guided by Your generous grace.
  Sovereign God, may Your immeasurable and abiding presence be our 
guardian, guide, and refuge through this day and always.
  In Your infinite mercy, we offer our prayers, and in Your matchless 
name, we pray.
  Amen.
