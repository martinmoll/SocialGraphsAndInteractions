HONORING THE SANDERS TRAVEL CENTRE ON THEIR 30TH ANNIVERSARY AND FORBES 
                        TRAVEL GUIDE ENDORSEMENT

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 14, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Sanders 
Travel Centre in Fort Worth, Texas. For 30 years, Sanders Travel Centre 
has been guiding people in luxury travel and has been recognized with 
an endorsement by Forbes Travel Guide.
  Trust is a major factor in booking travel and for three decades 
Sanders Travel Centre has excelled in building and maintaining that 
much needed trust. Their diverse expertise in advising individual, 
group, and corporate travelers on both domestic and international 
destinations, along with their dedication to a luxury experience is 
what makes them stand out. Forbes Travel Guide, a prestigious luxury 
travel rating system, has also acknowledged and endorsed Sanders Travel 
Centre for their hard work in the hospitality industry. This 
endorsement marks them as one of the top 50 travel agencies in the 
world, quantifying what we, in Fort Worth, have long recognized.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
offer my congratulations to Sanders Travel Centre on their 30th 
Anniversary and endorsement by Forbes Travel Guide.
