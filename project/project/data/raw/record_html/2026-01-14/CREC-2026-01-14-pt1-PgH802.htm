                 HONORING THE LIFE OF FRANK W. ZARDECKI

  (Mr. BRESNAHAN asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. BRESNAHAN. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life of Frank 
Zardecki, a devoted public servant and proud Pennsylvanian who passed 
away on January 11, 2026, at the age of 84.
  Frank dedicated more than six decades of his life to serving our 
Nation. After proudly serving in the United States Air Force, he spent 
over 50 years at the Tobyhanna Army Depot, where he rose to the role of 
deputy commander and helped to lead one of the region's most important 
defense facilities.
  Frank's leadership and commitment to excellence at Tobyhanna and his 
life was exceptional, earning him some of the highest civilian honors 
in Federal service, including the Department of Defense Medal of 
Distinguished Civilian Service, the highest DOD civilian service award.
  Following his retirement in 2022, he was inducted into both the Army 
Materiel Command Hall of Fame and the U.S. Army Communications-
Electronics Command Hall of Fame.
  Above all, Frank was a devoted husband, father, and grandfather who 
cherished time with his family and his community in northeastern 
Pennsylvania.
  I extend my deepest condolences to his loved ones and thank them for 
sharing Frank with our Nation. His legacy of service will not be 
forgotten.
