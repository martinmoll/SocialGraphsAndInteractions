      HONORING THE STANDARD MEAT COMPANY ON THEIR 90TH ANNIVERSARY

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. CRAIG A. GOLDMAN

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 14, 2026

  Mr. GOLDMAN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Standard 
Meat Company in Fort Worth, Texas. For 90 years, the Standard Meat 
Company has been providing quality meat to Texans and the rest of the 
world.
  After its founding in 1935, the Standard Meat Company built a 
reputation of high quality meat products. Quickly they were able to 
expand from local business to a coast to coast clientele and eventually 
to a global market. From innovations in packaging to protect the 
freshness and shelf life of their product to cutting edge production 
and sustainability technology, Standard Meat Company has remained at 
the top of the industry for nearly a century. Now, in its fourth 
generation of family ownership, the Rosenthal family continues to 
prioritize innovation and evolution within the company, honoring the 
Texan spirit of resilience and hard work.
  On behalf of the 12th Congressional District of Texas, I am proud to 
offer my congratulations to Standard Meat Company on their 90th 
Anniversary and I am eager to see their continued growth into the 
future.
