Whole House on the state of the Union for the further consideration of 
the bill (H.R. 7006) making further consolidated appropriations for the 
fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other purposes, with Mr. 
Weber of Texas in the chair.

amendment No. 2, printed in House Report 119-445, offered by the 
gentleman from Arizona (Mr. Crane) had been postponed.

electronic vote after the first vote in this series.
