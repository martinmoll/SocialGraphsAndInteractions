                        RECOGNIZING TODD BOEDING

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens of the 24th Congressional District of Texas.

[[Page E43]]

The 2025 Congressional Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to 
Captain Todd Boeding, United States Marine Corps, of Dallas, Texas, for 
his honorable service in the Armed Forces of the United States of 
America and for his continued service to his community.
  Captain Boeding served with distinction in the United States Marine 
Corps from 1989 to 2001. Beginning his service as an enlisted Marine 
administrative clerk and later commissioning as an infantry officer, he 
embodied the Marine Corps' core values of honor and commitment 
throughout his career. He served with the 1st Marine Regiment and was 
later selected for the elite Marine Barracks in Washington, D.C., where 
he was a member of the famed Silent Drill Team representing the Marine 
Corps in ceremonies honoring presidents, foreign dignitaries, and 
fallen service members. His military decorations include the Navy Unit 
Commendation Medal, two Meritorious Unit Commendations, the National 
Defense Service Medal, Armed Forces Expeditionary Medal, Sea Service 
Deployment Ribbon, Certificate of Commendation, and multiple letters 
and certificates of appreciation, along with Expert Rifle and 
Sharpshooter Pistol Badges.
  After the September 11, 2001, attacks, Captain Boeding extended his 
service to continue supporting the Corps and his country. Since leaving 
active duty, he has remained deeply committed to service and leadership 
within his community. As a Board Member and Ambassador for Carry The 
Load, he has played a vital role in honoring and supporting veterans, 
first responders, and military families helping expand the 
organization's national impact and its mission of restoring the true 
meaning of Memorial Day. His mentorship and friendship have guided 
countless veterans in their transition to civilian life, continuing his 
lifelong legacy of servant leadership.
  Captain Boeding also co-founded Tribe and Trust Leadership Academy, a 
program dedicated to developing the next generation of leaders by 
instilling the principles of teamwork, integrity, and service that 
defined his Marine Corps career. His tireless dedication to veterans, 
young leaders, and the broader North Texas community exemplifies the 
very best of American service and citizenship.
  Captain Boeding's honorable military service and his enduring 
commitment to country and community reflect the highest traditions of 
the United States Marine Corps and bring great credit upon himself, the 
State of Texas, and the United States of America.
