                   RECOGNIZING JAMES LEONARD WILLIAMS

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. BETH VAN DUYNE

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 14, 2026

  Ms. VAN DUYNE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today on behalf of a grateful 
Nation and the citizens

[[Page E42]]

of the 24th Congressional District of Texas. The 2025 Congressional 
Veteran Commendation is hereby presented to Major General James Leonard 
Williams, United States Marine Corps, of Dallas, Texas, for his 
honorable service in the Armed Forces of the United States of America 
and for his continued service to his community.
  Major General Williams served with distinction in the United States 
Marine Corps for more than 38 years, including both active and reserve 
duty during multiple conflicts. Over the course of his career, he held 
numerous leadership and command positions within the 4th Marine 
Division, 1st Marine Expeditionary Force, Combined Joint Task Force 
180, and 2nd Force Reconnaissance Company, Special Operations Capable. 
His operational service included deployments supporting the War on 
Drugs and combat tours in both Afghanistan and Iraq. His military 
decorations include the Defense Meritorious Service Medal, Legion of 
Merit Medal, Bronze Star Medal, Meritorious Service Medal, Navy 
Achievement Medal, Afghanistan Campaign Medal, Iraq Campaign Medal, 
National Defense Service Medal, and the Secretary of Defense Service 
Badge, among numerous other honors.
  Beyond his exemplary military career, Major General Williams has 
demonstrated a lifelong commitment to public service, education, and 
community leadership. He has served on numerous boards and advisory 
groups, including the FBI Citizens Academy Alumni Association, 
InfraGard North Texas Alliance, North Texas Crime Commission, and the 
Veterans Advisory Board for the VA North Texas Health Care System. He 
is President of the Board for International Leadership of Texas, a 
public charter school district, and serves as an advisor to both the 
Tarleton State University School of Criminal Justice and the Tower 
Center for Political Science at Southern Methodist University.
  Major General Williams also dedicates extensive time to helping 
veterans navigate the VA system, offering mentorship, guidance, and 
financial coaching to help fellow servicemembers and their families 
succeed in civilian life. His leadership, compassion, and devotion to 
service continue to strengthen North Texas communities and inspire 
future generations.
  Major General Williams' extraordinary record of military and civic 
service reflects the highest traditions of the United States Marine 
Corps and brings great credit upon himself, the State of Texas, and the 
United States of America.
