   CELEBRATING THE RETIREMENT OF LOS FRESNOS CHIEF OF POLICE, HECTOR 
                                GONZALEZ

                                  _____
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, January 14, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor the City of Los Fresnos Chief of Police, Hector Gonzalez, 
whose retirement marks the conclusion of forty-five years of 
outstanding service in law enforcement.
  Chief Gonzalez's leadership has kept our community safe, enabled our 
continued growth, and made the Rio Grande Valley a desirable place to 
live.
  As a mentor to countless men and women in uniform, he instilled core 
values of accountability, transparency, and respect, which will leave a 
lasting and positive impact on the Los Fresnos Police Department.
  Chief Gonzalez served Los Fresnos with honor, integrity and courage, 
while maintaining a steadfast commitment to the safety and well-being 
of everyone in our community.
  I am honored to represent some of the best of South Texas, including 
those who put their lives on the line each day to keep our communities 
safe. Under Chief Gonzalez's leadership, Los Fresnos Police has gone 
above and beyond, fostering trust between law enforcement and the 
community they serve.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of a grateful South Texas, I thank Chief 
Hector Gonzalez for his service to the people of Los Fresnos and 
congratulate him on his much-deserved retirement.
