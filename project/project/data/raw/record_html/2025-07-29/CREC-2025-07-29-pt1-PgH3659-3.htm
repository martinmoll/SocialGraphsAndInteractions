                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy Lord, how humbling it is to be in Your eternal presence. Even in 
this place of national consequence, to approach Your omnipotence is 
beyond the imaginings of our significance.
  For from You are all things: our lives, our loves, our livelihoods. 
All that we have comes from You.
  Through You do we discover the worth of all things: our purpose, our 
possibilities, our hopes.
  To You then belong all things, ourselves first among all of creation.
  Grant then that we entrust ourselves to Your care and keeping and 
yield ourselves to Your will, in obedience to Your word. On this day 
and onward, may all that we do, say, and are, bring glory to You 
forever.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.
