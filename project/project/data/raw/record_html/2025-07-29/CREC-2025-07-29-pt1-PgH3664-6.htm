           By Mr. CARBAJAL:
       H.R. 4784.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This bill prohibits the official display of a cut flower or 
     green in any public area of a building of the Executive 
     Office of the President, the Department of State, or the 
     Department of Defense unless it is produced in the United 
     States.
       The prohibition does not apply to any cut flower or green 
     used by a federal officer or employee for personal display.
