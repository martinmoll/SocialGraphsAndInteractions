                        MESSAGE FROM THE SENATE

  A message from the Senate by Mr. Ferrari, one of its clerks, 
announced that the Senate has agreed to without amendment a concurrent 
resolution of the House of the following title:

       H. Con. Res. 39. Concurrent Resolution authorizing the use 
     of Emancipation Hall in the Capitol Visitor Center for a 
     ceremony to present the Congressional Gold Medals awarded 
     under the Harlem Hellfighters Congressional Gold Medal Act.

  The message also announced that the Senate has passed a bill of the 
following title in which the concurrence of the House is requested:

       S. 2480. An act to require the Secretary of Homeland 
     Security to publicly release, in full, the unclassified 
     report titled ``U.S. Telecommunications Insecurity 2022'', 
     and for other purposes.
