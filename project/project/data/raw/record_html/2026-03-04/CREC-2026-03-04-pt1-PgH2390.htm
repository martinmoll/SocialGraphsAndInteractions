              TRIBAL TRUST LAND HOMEOWNERSHIP ACT OF 2025

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (S. 723) to require the Bureau of Indian Affairs to 
process and complete all mortgage packages associated with residential 
and business mortgages on Indian land by certain deadlines, and for 
other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Arkansas (Mr. Westerman) that the House suspend the 
rules and pass the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 384, 
nays 40, not voting 8, as follows:
