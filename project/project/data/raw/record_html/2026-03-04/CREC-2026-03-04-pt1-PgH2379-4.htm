                              NO NEW WARS

  (Mr. WALKINSHAW asked and was given permission to address the House 
for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WALKINSHAW. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to oppose President Trump's 
illegal, unconstitutional, and reckless war in Iran.
  We are 5 days in, and we have lost six brave American servicemembers, 
and thousands of Americans are stranded abroad. My prayers are with 
their families and with everyone who is in harm's way.
  After yesterday's briefing, I am more convinced than ever that there 
was no imminent threat to America, and there is no clear endgame 
strategy.
  Americans need affordable healthcare, groceries, and lower gas 
prices, not a war that costs $220 million a day and puts our troops in 
danger without a clearly defined mission.
  That is why I will vote for the bipartisan War Powers Resolution to 
rein in this out-of-control President and end his unauthorized 
hostilities in Iran.
