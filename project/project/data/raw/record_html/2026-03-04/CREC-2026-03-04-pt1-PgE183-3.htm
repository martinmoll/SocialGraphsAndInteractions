                        HONORING DR. PEGGY HICKS

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. BILL FOSTER

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                        Wednesday, March 4, 2026

  Mr. FOSTER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dr. Peggy Hicks, 
founder of the Hicks-Wright Organization, for receiving the Lifetime 
Achievement award from the Aurora African American Heritage Advisory 
Board.
  Since its founding, the Hicks-Wright Organization has focused on 
empowering individuals in the Aurora community through education, 
career development, and mentorship. One of the organization's primary 
goals is to develop urban leaders who make an impact, equipping them to 
serve the community and expand the abilities and knowledge of the next 
generation.
  The Hicks-Wright Organization continues to play a substantial role in 
the development of the Aurora community. Individuals seeking assistance 
with securing employment or managing their everyday finances can turn 
to this organization and expect to receive the support they need. The 
organization is opening conversations around mental health and creating 
long-term changes in addressing mental health. These initiatives are 
vital to helping individuals lead successful and fulfilling lives.
  Mr. Speaker, I am proud to recognize Dr. Peggy Hicks for her 
dedication to the Aurora community and for founding the Hicks-Wright 
Organization. I ask my colleagues to join me in celebrating her receipt 
of the Lifetime Achievement Award from the Aurora African American 
Heritage Advisory Board and in honoring her contributions to the 
community.
