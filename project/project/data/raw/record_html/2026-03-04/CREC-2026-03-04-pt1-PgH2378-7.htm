                      DEFENDING AMERICAN FAMILIES

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, the Tehran regime is the 
head of the snake of terrorism worldwide, chanting death to America. 
They have targeted, kidnapped, wounded, and killed thousands of 
Americans for 47 years.
  President Donald Trump repeatedly sought peace, but the Iranian 
regime boasted of 11 nuclear bombs, as exposed by talented Envoy Steve 
Witkoff.
  As a result, President Trump made the decision with Israel to launch 
Operation Epic Fury, defending American families. The objectives are 
clear: destroy the missile capabilities, annihilate the naval assets, 
and ensure that Iran can never have a nuclear weapon.
  Senior Iranian despots and murderous Khamenei have been destroyed.
  We grieve for the courageous servicemembers wounded and those who 
have given the ultimate sacrifice for freedom.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as the global war on terrorism 
continues. Trump is reiterating peace through strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump as Putin 
declares solidarity with the Iranian mass murderers.
  Our prayers are with devoted Indiana Congressman  Jim Baird upon the 
loss of his wife, Danise.
