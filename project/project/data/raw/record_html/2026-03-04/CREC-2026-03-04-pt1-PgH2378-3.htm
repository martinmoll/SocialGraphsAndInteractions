                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Examine us, O God, and test us. Search our hearts that we would prove 
faithful, and test our minds that we would receive wisdom. For You 
alone are righteous. Let us be mindful of Your truth and attentive to 
Your Word.
  Then judge us when we stray from Your will. Judge between nations and 
between neighbors in the resolution of their discord. Settle the 
disputes that have arisen from disdain and distrust among peoples.
  Take our weapons--our swords of war and conflict--and transform them. 
Take our words of bitterness and redeem them, that we would instead 
wield tools of peace and reconciliation in our time.
  Grant us the hope that is found only in You, a hope that promises a 
day when there will be neither need nor appetite for war anymore.
  In Your mercy, show us the way You would have us go in our pursuit of 
freedom and democracy for our Nation and in our desire for peace and 
security in our world.
  In Your sovereign name, we pray.
  Amen.
