                      PUBLIC BILLS AND RESOLUTIONS

  Under clause 2 of rule XII, public bills and resolutions of the 
following titles were introduced and severally referred, as follows:

           By Mr. BABIN (for himself, Ms. Lofgren, Mr.

       H.R. 7273. A bill to reauthorize the National Aeronautics 
     and Space Administration, and for other purposes; to the 
     Committee on Science, Space, and Technology.

     jurisdiction of the committee concerned.

           By Ms. BUDZINSKI (for herself and Mr. Barrett):
       H.R. 7280. A bill to amend title 38, United States Code, to

     Veterans' Affairs.
           By Mr. CARSON (for himself, Mr. Casar, Ms. Norton, Mr. 
             Johnson of Georgia, Ms. Omar, Mr. Lynch, Mr.

       H.R. 7282. A bill to incentivize States not to enact
