             RECOGNIZING THE PUBLIC SERVICE OF NELDA OLIVO

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, January 30, 2026

  Mr. VICENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Ms. 
Nelda Olivo for over 30 years of public service to the City of Corpus 
Christi and the Port of Corpus Christi.
  Ms. Olivo has dedicated her life to public service and, in doing so, 
has facilitated the growth of our Coastal Bend Region. She started her 
career in public service in the City of Corpus Christi's Housing and 
Community Development Department, where she worked tirelessly to 
improve housing and infrastructure for her community.
  In 1993, she began her work in one of the most important ports in 
Texas, the Port of Corpus Christi. She has served there as a hard-
working liaison between the port and several local, state, and federal 
governments.
  Her work goes far beyond Corpus Christi. She currently serves on the 
American Association of Port Authorities Government Relations 
Committee, the Texas Port Association, the U.S. Chamber of Commerce 
Government Affairs Committee, the Gulf Ports Association of the 
Americas Government Affairs Committee, Port Industries of Corpus 
Christi, and the Coastal Bend Council of Governments.
  In these many roles, she has exemplified leadership, and integrity. I 
am proud to represent constituents like Ms. Olivo every day in 
Congress, who are pillars in our community and cleared the path for 
generations to come.
  Mr. Speaker, on behalf of South Texas, I wish to honor Ms. Nelda 
Olivo on her retirement and for her service to the people of South 
Texas.
