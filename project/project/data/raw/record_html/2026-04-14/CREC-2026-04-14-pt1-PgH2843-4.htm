                   RECOGNIZING PINE CREEK RAIL TRAIL

  (Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. THOMPSON of Pennsylvania. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
the beautiful Pine Creek Rail Trail, which was recently named 
Pennsylvania's 2026 Trail of the Year by the Pennsylvania Department of 
Conservation and Natural Resources.
  This 62-mile trail connects Jersey Shore, Pennsylvania, and 
Wellsboro, Pennsylvania. Visitors hike through the scenic Pine Creek 
Gorge, better known as the Grand Canyon of Pennsylvania.
  The Pine Creek Rail Trail was first used by the Iroquois nation and 
then later developed as a railroad in the 19th century. For more than 
100 years, this railroad helped move tons of iron, coal, and lumber 
through the region. In 1989, the railroad ceased operation.
  Today, the nationally recognized trail attracts more than 44,000 
visitors each year and generates more than $5 million in local revenue.
  Mr. Speaker, the Pine Creek Rail Trail is a shining example of the 
beautiful landscapes in the Commonwealth, and I thank all the 
volunteers and officials who work tirelessly to upkeep the trail and 
ensure that visitors can experience the beauty of the region.
