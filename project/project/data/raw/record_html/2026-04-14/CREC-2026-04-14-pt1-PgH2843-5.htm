                         MAKING OUR SKIES SAFER

  (Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise in strong support of the 
bipartisan ALERT Act.
  Last year, 67 lives were lost in a tragic midair collision of a 
flight from my home State of Kansas to Washington, D.C.
  Those 67 people were parents, children, and friends. Their families' 
lives changed in an instant. In the weeks that followed, I met with 
those families. I listened and made a commitment to turn their grief 
into action.
  This bipartisan bill reflects that work. It takes commonsense steps 
to make our skies safer, modernizing collision avoidance technology, 
strengthening training, and closing dangerous communications gaps.
  It also acts on the National Transportation Safety Board 
recommendations, turning lessons learned into real action.
  We cannot bring those 67 people back, but we can honor them by 
preventing this from happening ever again.
  I urge my colleagues on both sides of the aisle to vote ``yes'' and, 
after the passage of this bill, work together on a final, fully 
thought-out bill that ensures safety measures are efficiently 
implemented. The families deserve nothing less.
