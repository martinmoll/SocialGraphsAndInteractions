       HOUSE BILLS AND JOINT RESOLUTION APPROVED BY THE PRESIDENT

  The President notified the Clerk of the House that on the following 
dates he had approved and signed bills and a joint resolution of the 
following titles:

           January 23, 2026:
       H.R. 6938. An Act making consolidated appropriations for 
     the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
     purposes.
           Febuary 3, 2026:
       H.R. 7148. An Act making further consolidated 
     appropriations for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, 
     and for other purposes.
           Febuary 18, 2026:
       H.J. Res. 142. A joint resolution disapproving the action 
     of the District of Columbia Council in approving the D.C. 
     Income and Franchise Tax Conformity and Revision Temporary 
     Amendment Act of 2025.
           March 26, 2026:
       H.R. 3377. An Act to authorize the President to award the 
     Medal of Honor to James Capers, Jr., for acts of valor as a 
     member of the Marine Corps during the Vietnam War.
       H.R. 7194. An Act to authorize the President to award the 
     Medal of Honor to Nicholas Dockery for acts of valor as a 
     member of the Army while serving in Afghanistan.
       H.R. 7211. An Act to authorize the President to award the 
     Medal of Honor to John W. Ripley for acts of valor during the 
     Vietnam War, and for other purposes.
