        HONORING VATRICE HARMON, FOUNDER AND CEO OF BORN TO POP

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 14, 2026

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor an outstanding entrepreneur, community leader, and woman of 
faith, Ms. Vatrice Harmon, founder and chief executive officer of Born 
to Pop, a thriving gourmet popcorn and nuts brand based in Byram, 
Mississippi.
  Ms. Harmon is a visionary whose journey reflects resilience, faith, 
and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Guided by her deep love for 
God and His Word, she lives by the principle found in Luke 12:48: ``To 
whom much is given, much is required,'' and exemplifies the belief that 
through Christ all things are possible. Her life stands as a testament 
to faith in action and purpose fulfilled.
  Affectionately know as ``The Popologist,'' Ms. Harmon has transformed 
a simple concept into a flourishing enterprise, creating handcrafted 
gourmet products that bring joy and connection to communities across 
Mississippi and beyond. What began as a home-based dream has grown into 
a licensed manufacturing business, proudly recognized as a Genuine 
Mississippi-crafted product--symbolizing quality, creativity, and 
authenticity.
  Beyond her entrepreneurial success, Ms. Harmon is deeply rooted in 
service. She serves as vice president of the board of directors for the 
Byram Chamber of Commerce and contributes actively to the Byram Mayoral 
Health Council, helping to lead impactful community initiatives. Her 
dedication to service is further reflected in her membership in Delta 
Sigma Theta Sorority, Incorporated, and her involvement at Redeemed 
Christian Ministries, where she serves on the Praise and Worship team.
  A proud graduate of Provine High School and Alcorn State University, 
Ms. Harmon is also a devoted wife of 29 years to her husband, Mr. 
Norris Harmon, and loving mother to her two sons, JaNorris and TJ. She 
embodies the values of family, faith, and community leadership.
  For Ms. Harmon, Born to Pop is more than a business--it is a 
ministry. Through her work, she inspires others to trust God, dream 
boldly, and walk confidently in their purpose. Her story is a powerful 
reminder that with faith, perseverance, and vision, extraordinary 
achievements are possible.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Ms. 
Vatrice Harmon for her remarkable contributions to entrepreneurship, 
community development, and faith-based leadership. Her impact continues 
to uplift and inspire, and she is truly deserving of our highest 
recognition.
