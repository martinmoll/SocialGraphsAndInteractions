                                 PRAYER

  Reverend John Kellogg, Christ Church Capitol Hill, Washington, D.C., 
offered the following prayer:
  Gracious God, we give thanks for the goodness of this Nation and for 
the Representatives who offer themselves in service to lead our 
country.
  Bless, guide, and protect them, along with their families, staffs, 
and all who serve in this building.
  Grant them strength, courage, and resilience as they face the 
challenges before them, and help them to make wise decisions for the 
well-being of our people.
  Keep this Nation ever under Your care.
  Amen.
