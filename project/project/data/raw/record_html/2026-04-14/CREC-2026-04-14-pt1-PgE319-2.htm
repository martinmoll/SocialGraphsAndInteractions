  CELEBRATING A HISTORIC FIRST STATE CHAMPIONSHIP FOR THE SAN JOAQUIN 
                 DELTA COLLEGE WOMEN'S BASKETBALL TEAM

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOSH HARDER

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 14, 2026

  Mr. HARDER of California. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
celebrate the San Joaquin Delta College Women's Basketball Team for 
their historic achievement in securing their first-ever State 
Championship title. This remarkable accomplishment marks a defining 
moment for the program and the entire San Joaquin Delta College 
community.
  On March 15, 2026, the Mustangs delivered an unforgettable 
performance, overcoming a significant early deficit to defeat MiraCosta 
College 76-71. After trailing at halftime, the team demonstrated 
extraordinary resilience, determination, and unity--mounting an 
impressive second-half comeback to claim the championship.
  This victory was fueled by a true team effort and outstanding 
leadership on the court. Aaliyah Youngblood led the way with a standout 
performance, earning Most Valuable Player honors for her scoring, 
playmaking, and composure under pressure. She was joined by key 
contributions from Fajr Cuthbertson, Marcelina Chaparro, and La Ne' 
Dunn, whose efforts helped power the Mustangs to victory.
  What makes this championship especially meaningful is the grit and 
perseverance the team showed throughout the game. Facing an early 
deficit, the Mustangs never wavered--steadily chipping away and 
finishing strong to secure a historic win.
  This milestone reflects the hard work, dedication, and commitment of 
the student-athletes, coaches, and supporters who made this moment 
possible. Their achievement will inspire future generations and stands 
as a testament to the strength and spirit of the San Joaquin Delta 
College community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in congratulating the San 
Joaquin Delta College Women's Basketball Team on their historic State 
Championship victory and in celebrating this extraordinary 
accomplishment.
