                       AMERICA LIBERATES NATIONS

  (Mr. WILSON of South Carolina asked and was given permission to 
address the House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, the American military has 
liberated more countries than any other Nation in history. I have 
witnessed personally in Sofia, Bulgaria; Seoul, South Korea; Monrovia, 
Liberia; and Bogota, Colombia, the success of American liberation.
  Mr. Speaker, 2026 has seen spectacular achievements with the 
leadership of President Donald Trump, Secretary of War Pete Hegseth, 
Chairman of Joint Chiefs Dan Caine, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio.
  The dictator of Venezuela has been arrested. Ukraine maintains 
sovereignty, defeating war criminal Putin; and the mass murderers of 
Iran are eliminated.
  President Trump has unified support in Latin America and strengthened 
NATO defense spending, and Arab Gulf states stand with America.
  As the son of a World War II Flying Tiger, I appreciate those who 
fought to liberate China, and I have had four sons who have served in 
the Middle East.
  In conclusion, God bless our troops as Trump is reinstituting peace 
through strength, revealing war criminal Putin lies, insulting and 
mocking Trump, with Putin providing Iran satellite targeting to murder 
Americans.
