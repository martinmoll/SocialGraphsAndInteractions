                         WELCOMING BABY SODARO

  (Mr. AMO asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. AMO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the newest addition 
to Team Amo, Fay Elliot Sodaro. Fay was born on May 13 and is a 
congressional baby through and through.
  She was welcomed by her parents, Dylan and Paige. Dylan serves as my 
Chief of Staff, and Paige serves as Ranking Member  Bobby Scott's 
legislative director.
  Least to say, we anticipate she will soon surpass us all in knowledge 
of how Congress works.
  Fay is named after her great-grandmother and her great-grandfather. 
She is keeping her parents busy, and we can't wait for her to make an 
appearance on the House floor.
  Mr. Speaker, I congratulate Dylan and Paige, and I welcome Fay to 
Team Amo.
