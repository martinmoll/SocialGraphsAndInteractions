         HONORING THE DEL GRECO FAMILY AND TREMONT OFFSET, INC.

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. RITCHIE TORRES

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, April 14, 2026

  Mr. TORRES of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
Tremont Offset, Inc., a family business that has long been part of the 
fabric of the Bronx.
  Founded in 1972 by Robert ``Bob'' Del Greco, a World War II veteran, 
Tremont Offset has served generations of New Yorkers with dedication, 
craftsmanship, and heart. For more than fifty years, and across 
multiple locations before settling on Ericson Place, this small 
business became a cornerstone of community life.
  Three generations of the Del Greco family carried forward its 
mission. From its founder to his son Robert, who led the business after 
1997, Tremont Offset was never just a print shop. It was a place where 
customers were known by name, where every order, whether ten dollars or 
ten thousand, was treated with equal care and respect.
  Their clients tell the story of the Bronx itself: small businesses, 
tradespeople, schools, healthcare providers, nonprofits, restaurants, 
and community organizations. Many have said that even as operations 
move under new ownership beyond the Bronx, their loyalty will follow 
because trust like that is earned over a lifetime.
  Today, we honor Tremont Offset not as it closes, but as it turns the 
page to a new chapter. We thank the Del Greco family for more than half 
a century of service to our borough.
  Their legacy is woven into the history of the Bronx, permanently and 
proudly.
