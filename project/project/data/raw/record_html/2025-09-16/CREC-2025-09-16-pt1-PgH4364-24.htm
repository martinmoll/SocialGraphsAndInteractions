           By Mr. HILL of Arkansas:
       H.R. 5396.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       This legislation is based on the authority of the U.S. 
     Congress to ``regulate Commerce . . . among the several 
     States'', U.S. Const. art. I, Sec.  8, cl 3, ``. . . coin 
     money [and] regulate the value thereof . . .'', U.S. Const. 
     art. I, Sec.  8, cl. 5, and ``make all Laws . . . necessary 
     and proper for carrying into Execution the foregoing 
     Powers[.]'' U.S. Const. art. 1, Sec.  8, cl. 18.
