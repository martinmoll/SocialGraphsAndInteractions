                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of the ages, it is Your almighty hand that guides our lives and 
directs our steps. Often it takes our retrospection to see how Your 
divine love has led us in the past, so caught up are we in the here and 
now.
  This morning, as we reflect back on the life of one of our own former 
colleagues, Representative Charles Rangel, give us eyes to see how You 
led a boy from Harlem, the son of a seamstress, who lived a life filled 
with challenges and triumphs, to place on him the mantle of leadership 
which he bore faithfully and courageously in service to this country.
  It is You, O Lord, who brought him through the bitter cold and brutal 
combat in the Battle of Kunu-ri, revealing to him his own gift of 
leadership and purpose. And it is You, throughout his storied career, 
who equipped him with his genial nature, armed him with his sharp-
tongued reason, and instilled in him his pragmatic desire to get things 
done, even if it meant crossing the aisle.
  Perhaps the court of public opinion has, rightly or wrongly, made 
their judgment on this, Your child. But one day in Your court is better 
than thousands elsewhere. Now, as he rests from his labors and stands 
before You, may he hear Your own well done, good and faithful servant.
  Hear our prayers of gratitude for our brother's life and our hope for 
Your salvation.
  It is in Your eternal name, we pray.
  Amen.
