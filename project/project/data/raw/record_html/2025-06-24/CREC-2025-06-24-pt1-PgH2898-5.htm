          WE ARE AT THE CROSSROADS OF DEMOCRACY AND AUTOCRACY

  (Mr. GREEN of Texas asked and was given permission to address the 
House for 1 minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. GREEN of Texas. Mr. Speaker, and still I rise, and I rise today 
because I believe my country to be at the crossroads of democracy and 
autocracy. The moment of decision is near. We will have to decide 
whether we will have a country that abides by a Constitution that is 
meaningful or ignores a Constitution such that it becomes meaningless.
  I choose to stand on the grounds laid by the Founders and Framers of 
the Constitution. I stand today to announce that at the conclusion of 
this 1-minute message, I will bring Articles of Impeachment to the 
attention of the House of Representatives.
