               WELCOMING ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW

                                  _____
                                 

                            HON. NELLIE POU

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                      Tuesday, September 23, 2025

  Ms. POU. Mr. Speaker, I rise to welcome His All-Holiness Ecumenical 
Patriarch Bartholomew to the United States. On behalf of the many 
Eastern Orthodox Christians who call New Jersey home, including my 
constituents at St. George Greek Orthodox Church in Clifton, I offer my 
heartfelt congratulations and express deep admiration to His All-
Holiness for his ongoing efforts to foster peace, understanding, and 
stewardship of our shared planet.
  Leading the church since 1991, His All-Holiness serves as a spiritual 
guide for more than 300 million faithful Orthodox Christians around the 
world. His commitment to engaging with communities and governments 
around the globe, regardless of faith, has made him one of the most 
recognizable and respected religious figures of our time.
  While in the United States, Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew will be 
awarded the prestigious Templeton Prize for his outstanding 
contributions to the advancement of science and spirituality. He will 
join the company of other spiritual leaders like Mother Teresa and the 
Dalai Lama. I am honored to add my voice to the chorus of others who 
welcome the Ecumenical Patriarch to the United States and honor him for 
his lifetime of incredible service and work.
