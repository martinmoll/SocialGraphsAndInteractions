           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 5542.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 1 of section 8 of article I of the 
     Constitution, Congress has the power to lay and collect taxes 
     and to provide for the common defense and general welfare of 
     the United States. Furthermore, clause 18 of section 8 grants 
     Congress the authority to make all laws necessary and proper 
     for carrying into execution its enumerated powers, including 
     the power of the purse. This legislation is enacted under 
     these constitutional authorities.
