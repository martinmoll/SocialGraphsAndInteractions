       CELEBRATING DESTINI HOUSTON AS THE KINDEST KID IN AMERICA

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, April 21, 2025

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate an 
extraordinary young leader from Western New York--Miss Destini Houston. 
At just 16 years old, Miss Houston is showing us all that leadership, 
kindness, and advocacy can go hand in hand.
  Miss Houston was recently crowned Miss Western New York's Teen, a 
title she has embraced not for the spotlight but to shine a light on 
causes close to her heart. She is now receiving national recognition 
for her inspiring efforts, and she will be honored as New York's 
recipient of the Kindest Kid in America award.
  To celebrate her achievement, the award organization has created a 
children's book inspired by Miss Houston's journey. She will read it to 
students throughout Western New York in hopes of inspiring a new 
generation to lead with compassion and purpose. Miss Houston has said, 
``Being kind isn't just about being nice to people--it's about taking 
action to help others.'' She lives by those words. From volunteering 
and mentoring to working with the Buffalo Urban League to organize an 
informational event for prospective foster and adoptive families, she 
is a true changemaker in her community.
  In addition to her advocacy, Miss Houston is also a competitive 
dancer training in multiple styles and performing at high-level 
competitions. Her dedication to dance has helped build the discipline 
and confidence that now serve her well as she prepares to compete in 
the Miss New York pageant.
  Mr. Speaker, please join me in celebrating Destini Houston--not only 
as Miss Western New York's Teen or the Kindest Kid in America but as a 
role model, a community leader, and a beacon of hope for the future. 
Her impact reaches far beyond titles and trophies. Miss Houston reminds 
us all that even the smallest acts of kindness can lead to something 
truly extraordinary. Destini's leadership has left an indelible mark on 
our city, and we are all the better for it.
