                COMMUNICATION FROM THE DEMOCRATIC LEADER

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from the Honorable Hakeem Jeffries, Democratic Leader:

                                                    June 20, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker of the House,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: Pursuant to clause 5(a)(4)(A), Rule X of 
     the Rules of the House of Representatives, I designate the 
     following Members to be available to serve as Members of the 
     Investigative Subcommittee established by the Committee on 
     Ethics during the 119th Congress:
       The Honorable Nanette Barragan of California
       The Honorable Troy Carter of Louisiana
       The Honorable Madeleine Dean of Pennsylvania
       The Honorable Bill Foster of Illinois
       The Honorable Chrissy Houlahan of Pennsylvania
       The Honorable Ayanna Pressley of Massachusetts
       The Honorable Greg Stanton of Arizona
           Sincerely,
                                                  Hakeem Jeffries,
     Democratic Leader.
