   MOTION TO INSTRUCT CONFEREES ON H.R. 3944, MILITARY CONSTRUCTION, 
    VETERANS AFFAIRS, AND RELATED AGENCIES APPROPRIATIONS ACT, 2026

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The unfinished business is the vote on the 
motion to instruct on the bill (H.R. 3944) making appropriations for 
military construction, the Department of Veterans Affairs, and related 
agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2026, and for other 
purposes, offered by the gentlewoman from Connecticut (Ms. DeLauro) on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk will redesignate the motion.
  The Clerk redesignated the motion.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion to instruct.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 211, 
nays 213, not voting 8, as follows:
