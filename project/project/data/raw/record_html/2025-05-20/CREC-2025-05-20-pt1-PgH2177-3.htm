 PROVIDING FOR CONGRESSIONAL DISAPPROVAL OF THE RULE SUBMITTED BY THE 
  OFFICE OF THE COMPTROLLER OF THE CURRENCY OF THE DEPARTMENT OF THE 
 TREASURY RELATING TO THE REVIEW OF APPLICATIONS UNDER THE BANK MERGER 
                                  ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on passage of the joint resolution 
(S.J. Res. 13) providing for congressional disapproval under chapter 8 
of title 5, United States Code, of the rule submitted by the Office of 
the Comptroller of the Currency of the Department of the Treasury 
relating to the review of applications under the Bank Merger Act, on 
which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the joint resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the passage of the joint 
resolution.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 220, 
nays 207, not voting 6, as follows:
