        REMEMBERING PHILLIP DOUGLAS MORGAN OF FLORA, MISSISSIPPI

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. MICHAEL GUEST

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                        Friday, August 22, 2025

  Mr. GUEST. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to remember Phillip Douglas 
Morgan of Flora, Mississippi. Phillip Morgan was a Mississippian 
through and through who gave back to his community and loved his 
family. Meeting people was a passion of Phillip's, and he never met a 
stranger. Phillip grew up in rural Mississippi and was a graduate of 
Mississippi schools. He studied accounting at the University of 
Southern Mississippi and had a 10-year career as an accountant. Hired 
by Southern Ag Credit to work as their Chief Financial and Operating 
Officer, Phillip worked his way to CEO in 2020. He took his skills and 
passion of people and saw the Southern Ag Credit team as his extended 
family.
  Phillip was a family man who spent time with his wife Christy and his 
two daughters at their hunting club, cheered his daughters on in horse 
shows and cheer camps, and also enjoyed vacationing with extended 
family.
  With a passion for fostering rescue horses, Phillip worked closely 
with the MS Horse Rescue. His support for the Dixie National Rodeo, 
Ducks Unlimited, and Heart of the Horse were passion projects of his. 
Phillip will be greatly missed by his family, friends, and the Southern 
Ag Credit family. I was honored to have had the opportunity to work 
alongside him through the years. His dedication to the Ag industry will 
continue through his legacy.
