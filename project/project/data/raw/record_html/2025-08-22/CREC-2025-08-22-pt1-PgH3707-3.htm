           By Mr. BARR:
       H.R. 5014.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to Article I, Section 8, Clause 1 of the Constitution, which 
     provides the authority to lay and collect taxes and to 
     provide for the general welfare of the United States, and 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 18, which grants Congress the 
     power to make all laws necessary and proper for carrying into 
     execution its enumerated powers, including the regulation of 
     national banking and financial institutions.
