                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Holy God, Your words are pure, like silver that has passed through 
the refiner's fire. Like gold purified in the kiln, Your word is 
flawless, pure on Earth as in Heaven.
  Keep us safe from our own speech, that the words of our mouths may 
ever be acceptable in Your hearing.
  Protect us from the words of the mean-spirted, whose lies spill from 
their lips and with the boastfulness from their tongues flatter 
themselves and deceive those they address.
  Arise, O Lord, and protect the poor who fear being plundered and the 
needy who groan under the weight of anxiety.
  Silence each of us who do not speak Your truth and defend those who 
dare to be true to Your word from slander.
  In Your perfect and all-sufficient name, we pray.
  Amen.
