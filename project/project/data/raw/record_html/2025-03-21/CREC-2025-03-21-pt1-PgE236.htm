                      HONORING ANGELA G. HUTCHINS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 21, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Mrs. 
Angela G. Hutchins--a trailblazer, servant leader, and beacon of hope--
whose extraordinary contributions have forever changed the landscape of 
Adams County and the state of Mississippi.
  Mrs. Hutchins made history as the first Black female to serve as a 
supervisor in Adams County, representing District 3 with distinction 
and purpose. Her groundbreaking election shattered barriers and 
inspired countless individuals to believe in the power of 
representation and progress. Her legacy as a history-maker exemplifies 
the strides our communities continue to make toward greater inclusivity 
and equity.
  Throughout her life and career, Mrs. Hutchins has dedicated herself 
to uplifting others. From her beginnings as an administrative assistant 
to her historic role as Supervisor for District 3, she has led with 
vision, unwavering resolve, and a profound sense of duty. Her 
leadership has addressed critical challenges, fostered meaningful 
community engagement, and earned her widespread respect and admiration 
of those she serves.
  Mrs. Hutchins's influence extends far beyond Adams County. She has 
served on the Board of Directors for the Mississippi Association of 
Supervisors and the Mississippi Minority Caucus. In addition, she has 
been a Commissioner for the Local Workforce Board, a Liaison for 
Mississippi Action in Progress (MAP), and a member of the Board of 
Directors for the Southwest Planning and Development District. Her 
service also includes leadership roles with the National Organization 
of Black County Officials, the National Association of County 
Officials, and her membership in Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc.
  As we commemorate Black History Month, it is essential to recognize 
leaders like Mrs. Angela G. Hutchins. Her historic achievements and 
unwavering dedication serve as a profound source of inspiration. Her 
resilience gives us hope, her advocacy motivates us to act, and her 
leadership assures us of a brighter future for Adams County, 
Mississippi, and beyond.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Mrs. Angela 
G. Hutchins for her outstanding contributions to her community, her 
state, and her country. She has etched her name in history, and her 
legacy will continue to inspire generations to come.
