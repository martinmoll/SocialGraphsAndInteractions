                          HONORING HAROLD HALL

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                         Friday, March 21, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
life and legacy of Mr. Harold Hall, a devoted community servant, 
entrepreneur, and beloved family man.
  Born on August 21, 1937, Mr. Hall was the son of the late Ruby 
Edwards, whose love and guidance instilled in him a deep commitment to 
family and service.
  For over 50 years, he was a pillar of the Leland community, where his 
entrepreneurial spirit and steadfast dedication to others left a 
lasting impact. Through his business ventures and countless acts of 
kindness, he worked tirelessly to uplift and support his neighbors, 
embodying the values of perseverance, generosity, and service.
  Mr. Hall's life was a testament to hard work, love, and an unwavering 
devotion to both family and community. Though he will be deeply missed, 
his legacy lives on in the many lives he touched.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me honoring the life and 
service of Mr. Harold Hall, recognizing his enduring contributions to 
his community and the profound love he had for those around him.
