              HONORING THE LIFE AND LEGACY OF BOB MITCHELL

  (Mr. HOYER asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)

[[Page H5124]]

  

  Mr. HOYER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a great Marylander, a 
fellow University of Maryland Terrapin, and dear friend, Bob Mitchell, 
who passed away on November 12, just days shy of his 89th birthday.
  Bob built our community, literally. An accomplished developer, he 
built more than 5,000 homes in roughly 40 communities throughout the 
capital region.
  Bob was a leader in his industry, serving as president of the 
Maryland National Capital Building Industry Association, the Maryland 
State Builders Association, and the National Association of Home 
Builders.
  His greatest pride, however, was his family.
  My prayers are with his wife, Marlene; their four children; six 
grandchildren; and two great-grandchildren as they mourn this great 
loss. I mourn with them. He was a friend, a friend who I liked, who I 
respected, and who I learned from.
  The House mourns with them. Maryland mourns with them. May God bless 
and keep Bob Mitchell.
