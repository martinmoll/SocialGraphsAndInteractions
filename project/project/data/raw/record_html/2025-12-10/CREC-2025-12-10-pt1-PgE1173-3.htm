                   HONORING VETERAN CHARLES A. MOORE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 10, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize 
and honor Mr. Charles A. Moore, a distinguished veteran of Greenwood, 
Mississippi, whose life of service, leadership, and dedication to 
community stands as an inspiring testament to the values that 
strengthen our Nation.
  Born in September 1943 and raised in the Browning Road community of 
Greenwood, Mr. Moore was shaped by the guidance of his father, a 
laborer and farmer, and his mother, an elementary school teacher. Their 
example instilled in him a deep respect for education, hard work, and 
public service.
  Mr. Moore attended Browning Road Elementary School and graduated from 
Broad Street High School in 1961. He went on to pursue higher education 
at Morehouse College in Atlanta, Georgia before answering the call to 
serve his country. In January 1963, he entered the United States Army, 
serving honorably until January 1966. He continued his military 
commitment in the U.S. Army Reserve from 1966 to 1969, the Air National 
Guard, and later as a Reservist in the United States Air Force. His 
combined service to the Nation spanned more than sixteen years, 
culminating in his honorable discharge in June 1979.
  After completing his active duty, Mr. Moore attended Jackson State 
University, graduated in 1968, and later earned a master's degree in 
counseling from Mississippi State University. In 1969, he began a 
distinguished career at the Mississippi State Employment Service, where 
he served as an Employment Placement Counselor, Area Supervisor for Job 
Corps, and Counselor for Veterans. Through these roles, Mr. Moore 
devoted himself to helping countless individuals secure employment, 
rebuild their lives, and access new opportunities.
  Mr. Moore's service extended far beyond his professional life. He is 
a devoted member of Morning Star Missionary Baptist Church in the 
Browning Road community, where he serves faithfully as a Deacon and 
Sunday School teacher. He is also an active member of American Legion 
Post 29 in Greenwood, Mississippi, honoring and uplifting fellow 
veterans through ongoing service.
  In addition, Mr. Moore contributes to local civic life as a part-time 
radio commentator, providing insight, information, and connections to 
members of the community. In his personal time, he enjoys raising cows, 
a pastime that reflected his roots and his love of the land.
  Mr. Speaker, the life of Charles A. Moore is a powerful example of 
steadfast service--to his Nation, his community, and his fellow 
citizens. I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring his contributions 
and in expressing gratitude for his lifetime of dedication.
