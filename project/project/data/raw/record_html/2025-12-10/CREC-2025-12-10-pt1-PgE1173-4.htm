       CELEBRATING THE STUDENTS OF LENEXA HILLS ELEMENTARY SCHOOL

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. SHARICE DAVIDS

                               of kansas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 10, 2025

  Ms. DAVIDS of Kansas. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to celebrate the 
students of Lenexa Hills Elementary School for designing the ornaments 
now proudly representing Kansas at the site of the National Christmas 
Tree.
  Each year since 1923, President's Park in Washington, D.C. has hosted 
the National Christmas Tree, surrounded by trees representing each U.S. 
state, territory, and the District of Columbia. Those trees feature 
one-of-a-kind, student-crafted ornaments that reflect the unique 
characteristics of the state they call home.
  This year, the students of Lenexa Hills put their creativity and 
Kansas pride on full display--designing colorful ornaments with 
beautiful visuals of sunflowers, bison, western meadowlarks, sprawling 
grasslands, and more of the wonderful things that make Kansas so 
special.
  I thank the students of Lenexa Hills Elementary for this exceptional 
representation of our home state. I'm so proud to showcase the talent 
of our kids to the rest of the country. To quote the Kansas State 
motto: ``To the stars through difficulties--Ad Astra Per Aspera.''
