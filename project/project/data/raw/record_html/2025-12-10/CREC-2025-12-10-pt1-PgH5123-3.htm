                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Abide with us most Holy God. Allow Your holy spirit to descend among 
us and move around us that we would know of Your presence and power in 
the living of these days.
  May Your calm accompany us in every encounter. Your serenity still 
the tumult of our busyness. May Your nearness impress upon us the 
significance of each moment of every day.
  Then throughout this day, train our ears to listen for Your voice and 
heed Your words of guidance.
  Sensitize our souls to the leading of Your spirit as we attend to the 
stewardship of Your grace.
  Discipline our wills to yield with humility to the way You would have 
us serve this Nation and her needs.
  For You are our Father, and we Your children. We are the clay and You 
the potter who fashions our lives and shapes our days. We are all the 
work of Your hand. We pray that our efforts would reveal Your divine 
design.
  In us, O Lord, may You be glorified.
  Amen.
