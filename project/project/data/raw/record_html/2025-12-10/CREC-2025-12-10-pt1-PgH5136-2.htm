       SUBMISSION OF JOINT EXPLANATORY MATERIAL OF THE HOUSE OF 
REPRESENTATIVES TO S. 1071, DISINTERMENT OF REMAINS OF FERNANDO V. COTA 
             FROM FORT SAM HOUSTON NATIONAL CEMETERY, TEXAS

  Pursuant to section 7 of House Resolution 936, the chair of the 
Committee on Armed Services submitted joint explanatory material 
relating to the amendment of the House of Representatives to S. 1071, 
Disinterment of Remains of Fernando v. Cota from Fort Sam Houston 
National Cemetery, Texas. The contents of this submission will be 
published in Book II of the Record.
