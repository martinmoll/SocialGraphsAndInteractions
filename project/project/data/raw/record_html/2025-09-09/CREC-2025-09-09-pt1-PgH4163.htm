    COMMUNICATION FROM THE DISTRICT DIRECTOR TO THE HONORABLE NYDIA 
                     VELAZQUEZ, MEMBER OF CONGRESS

  The SPEAKER pro tempore laid before the House the following 
communication from Evelyn Cruz, District Director to the Honorable 
Nydia Velazquez, Member of Congress:


                                Congress of the United States,

                                Washington, DC, September 8, 2025.
     Hon. Mike Johnson,
     Speaker, House of Representatives,
     Washington, DC.
       Dear Mr. Speaker: This is to notify you formally, pursuant 
     to Rule VIII of the Rules of the House of Representatives, 
     that I, Evelyn Cruz, District Director to the Honorable Nydia 
     Velaquez, U.S. Fermentative for the 7th Congressional 
     District of New York, have been served a third-party 
     deposition subpoena in litigation before the Supreme Court of 
     the State of New York.
       After consultation with the Office of General Counsel, I 
     have determined that compliance with the subpoena is 
     consistent with the privileges and rights of the House.
           Sincerely,
     Evelyn Cruz
