       HONORING HIS ALL-HOLINESS ECUMENICAL PATRIARCH BARTHOLOMEW

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2025

  Mr. VAN DREW. Mr. Speaker, it is with great honor that I rise today 
to welcome to Washington, D.C. His All-Holiness Ecumenical Patriarch 
Bartholomew, the spiritual head of the world's Orthodox Christians and 
one of the most respected peacemakers of our time. Patriarch 
Bartholomew has long been known as the ``Patriarch of Peace'' and the 
``Bridge Builder.'' He joins a long line of true peacemakers in 
history--figures like Martin Luther King, Archbishop Desmond Tutu, 
Nelson Mandela, and others who not only worked to reduce conflict, but 
also showed compassion even toward those who persecuted them. His All-
Holiness has endured great trials, facing hostility and harassment from 
those who would try to silence his faith and his mission. Yet, instead 
of responding with bitterness, he has consistently answered with 
courage, with compassion, and with a steadfast commitment to peace. 
Despite persistent challenges, Patriarch Bartholomew has dedicated his 
life to building bridges between faiths, nations, and peoples. He has 
reached out across the Christian world, across East and West, and even 
across the divide between Christianity and Islam, showing by example 
that dialogue and mutual respect are the only true path to peace. His 
advocacy for religious freedom, for human dignity, and for the 
environment has earned him recognition across the globe. Indeed, he is 
a past recipient of the Congressional Gold Medal and now the Templeton 
Prize, joining the ranks of luminaries such as Mother Teresa and the 
Dalai Lama.
  I also want to express my deep gratitude to my Greek Orthodox 
constituents, especially those of St. Nicholas Greek Orthodox Church in 
Atlantic City and Holy Trinity Greek Orthodox Church in Egg Harbor 
Township, who carry forward the Patriarch's vision of faith, service, 
and community here in South Jersey. They enrich our communities and 
remind us of the enduring strength of their traditions and values. 
Ladies and gentlemen, the visit of His All-Holiness to our Nation's 
capital is an occasion of great significance. At a time when the world 
is too often divided, his voice calls us back to what is essential--
that we are all children of God, and that blessed are the peacemakers, 
for they shall be called the children of God. I ask my colleagues to 
join me in welcoming Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew to the United 
States and in honoring his extraordinary work for peace, justice, and 
reconciliation throughout the world. God bless Patriarch Bartholomew 
and God bless the United States of America. The greatest country on the 
face of the Earth.
