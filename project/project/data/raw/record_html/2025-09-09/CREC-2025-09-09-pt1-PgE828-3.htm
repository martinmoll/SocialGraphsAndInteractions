 COMMEMORATING THE 100-YEAR ANNIVERSARY OF THE LIBBY'S PUMPKIN FACTORY

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. DARIN LaHOOD

                              of illinois

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2025

  Mr. LaHOOD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and celebrate the 
100th anniversary of Libby's Pumpkin Factory in Morton, Illinois. For a 
century, Libby's has been at the heart of our agricultural economy, 
building a legacy that has made Morton the ``Pumpkin Capital of the 
World.''
  Since commencing operations in 1925, the Libby's plant in Morton has 
grown into the largest pumpkin canning operation in the United States. 
Its operations have had an immensely positive impact on local family 
farms, the environment, and cultivating the next generation of 
agricultural professionals.
  The plant's local impact is remarkable, sourcing more than 90 percent 
of its pumpkin crop from within a 45-mile radius of Morton, supporting 
countless family farms and providing an economic boost to the area.
  The Morton facility has a stellar record of environmental 
sustainability, operating as a zero-waste-for-disposal facility, 
recycling water used during pack season, and ensuring that every part 
of the pumpkin--from seed to stem--is repurposed.
  Libby's has also invested in the next generation of agricultural 
professionals by partnering with regional institutions of higher 
education, including the University of Illinois, to fund scholarships 
and create innovative opportunities for learning best practices.
  Mr. Speaker, today we honor Libby's and its employees past and 
present, for their enduring commitment to innovation, excellence, and 
positive community impact. I ask my colleagues to join me in 
congratulating Libby's Pumpkin on 100 years of excellence and in 
recognizing Morton's proud legacy as the Pumpkin Capital of the World.
