                       HONORING DR. JOE LEE SMITH

  (Mr. Haridopolos of Florida was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and 
legacy of Dr. Joe Lee Smith, a beloved leader and friend in Brevard 
County, who passed away recently at the age of 89.
  For 36 years, Dr. Smith faithfully served the people of Rockledge on 
the city council, including chairman and as deputy mayor.
  Beyond city hall, he was a lifelong educator, coach, assistant 
principal, dean, and later provost at Eastern Florida State College. He 
literally shaped a generation. He was a true gentleman who lifted 
everyone around him.
  On a personal note, when I started my career back in 1993, he was a 
mentor and friend and always had a kind word for others. He was truly 
that leader that we need desperately in America today.
  Mr. Speaker, on this day we recognize my good friend, Dr. Joe Lee 
Smith, for his amazing service to our country.


                     Honoring Colonel Casper Berger

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, today I honor Colonel Casper Berger, a 
decorated veteran whose service to our Nation spanned World War II, 
Korea, and Vietnam. A proud Jewish American, Colonel Berger earned the 
Legion of Merit for his extraordinary service to the United States 
Army.
  For years, his daughter, Dr. Susan Berger, worked tirelessly to have 
her parents' headstone at Arlington National Cemetery corrected. When 
she reached out to our office, we were proud to assist her family.
  Working with the Department of the Army, we ensured the replacement 
was made. Today, their headstone stands as it should, a lasting symbol 
of a lifetime of service and devotion to their country.
  Mr. Speaker, may we always remember Colonel Berger's service and his 
legacy.


                          Honoring Ron DiMenna

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, today I honor the life and legacy of 
Ron DiMenna, the founder of Ron Jon Surf Shop. Ron Jon is a landmark 
that has been a cultural center point in Florida's surf culture and has 
welcomed generations of visitors since 1963.
  I have fond memories of bringing my own children there, just as many 
in our district and around the country know those famous signs along I-
95.
  Through his hard work and determination, Ron built something lasting 
and became a symbol of Cocoa Beach itself. On behalf of the people of 
the Space Coast, we extend our deepest condolences to his family and 
his loved ones.
  Mr. Speaker, he made a real legacy in our community. More 
importantly, he created opportunities for others when no one else saw 
the vision. We thank him, and may Ron rest in peace.


         Honoring All-Holiness Ecumenical Patriarch Bartholomew

  Mr. HARIDOPOLOS. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to welcome the visit of a 
man who holds one of the most ancient and revered positions in 
Christendom, His All-Holiness Bartholomew, the ecumenical patriarch of 
Constantinople and the spiritual leader of the Eastern Orthodox Church.
  His visit stands as a powerful symbol of the enduring role faith 
plays in a free society. It affirms the place of spiritual leadership 
in our world. It reflects the ongoing need to uphold religious liberty 
as a cornerstone of our constitutional order.
  Mr. Speaker, I warmly welcome him to the United States and thank him 
for a lifetime of service.
