                 HONORING JUDGE LILLIE BLACKMON SANDERS

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor a 
remarkable servant, Lillie Blackmon Sanders.
  Lillie Blackmon Sanders grew up on a farm in the small community of 
Black Hawk, Mississippi. She attended the Carroll County Public 
Schools, graduating from Vaiden High School. Lillie is one of 11 
children of the late Percy and Leola Blackmon. She is married to 
Attorney Everett Sanders and is the proud mother of Aisha and Keishia 
Sanders.
  Lillie attended Alcorn State University, where she became a 
trailblazer as the first female president of the Student Government 
Association. She was initiated into the Gamma Phi Chapter of Alpha 
Kappa Alpha Sorority, Inc. in the fall of 1973. After graduating from 
Alcorn, Lillie enrolled at the University of Mississippi College of 
Law, where she received her Juris Doctor degree. She began her legal 
career as an attorney with Southwest Mississippi Legal Services, which 
led her to Natchez, Mississippi, where she has made her home for the 
past 44 years.
  Lillie was recognized by Alpha Kappa Alpha as one of the 
``Unforgettable and Unsung Sorors of the Civil Rights Movement.'' She 
became the first female Circuit Court Judge in the state of Mississippi 
and also started a Drug Court, which affords individuals a second 
change. She retired in 2022 but holds Senior Judge status to hear 
special cases around the state.
  Lillie is a member of Zion Chapel AME Church, where she serves on the 
Steward Board. She is also a member of the NAACP, Mississippi Bar 
Association, Magnolia Bar Association, Mississippi Judiciary Advisory 
Study Committee, and the Mississippi Access to Justice Commission, and 
she has been the recipient of many awards.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in recognizing Natchez 
Retired Judge Lillie Blackmon Sanders for her actions and achievements 
in the Natchez, Mississippi community.
