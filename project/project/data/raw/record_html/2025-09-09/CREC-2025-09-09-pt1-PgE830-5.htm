           RECOGNIZING COUNCILWOMAN PATRICIA ``PAT'' ANDALORO

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. JEFFERSON VAN DREW

                             of new jersey

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, September 9, 2025

  Mr. VAN DREW.  Mr. Speaker, it is truly an honor to recognize 
Councilwoman Patricia Andaloro for her many years of devoted service to 
the people of Buena Borough. She was recently celebrated at the Buena 
Boro Republican League dinner, a well-earned recognition of her 
dedication, integrity, and steadfast commitment to her community. Pat 
first served as the Borough Clerk, where she became the backbone of 
local government. In that role, she guided residents and colleagues 
alike with professionalism and care, ensuring that the daily functions 
of the borough ran smoothly. Her attention to detail and her deep 
knowledge of municipal operations earned her the respect and trust of 
everyone who worked alongside her.
  Today, as Councilwoman, Pat continues to bring that same spirit of 
service and leadership. She has shown an unwavering commitment to good 
governance, always keeping the needs of her neighbors at the forefront. 
The recognition she received at the Buena Boro Republican League dinner 
was not just a tribute to her past accomplishments, but a reminder of 
the lasting impact she continues to make. I congratulate Pat on a very 
well-deserved honor and I thank her for the great work she is doing in 
South Jersey. God bless Pat and God bless our United States of America. 
The greatest country on the face of the Earth.
