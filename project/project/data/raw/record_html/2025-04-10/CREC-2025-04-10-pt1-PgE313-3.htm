               RECOGNIZING THE OLYMPUS HIGH SCHOOL TITANS

                                 ______
                                 

                           HON. BURGESS OWENS

                                of utah

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, April 10, 2025

  Mr. OWENS. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate the Olympus High 
School Titans on winning the 5A Boys Basketball State Championship.
  With a commanding 69 to 40 win over Highland High, the Titans capped 
off a season defined by discipline, grit, and teamwork. This 
championship reflects the hours of practice, the leadership of their 
coaches, and the drive of a team that refused to let up.
  I also want to give a special shout-out to three players from the 
Millcreek area--Luke Owen, Jack Evan, and Malakai Philip--whose efforts 
on the court made a lasting impact.
  Their hard work, and the hard work of the entire team, has brought 
pride to their school and to our entire community.
  Congratulations again to the Titans on an outstanding season and a 
well-earned title.
