   HONORING DR. LYUDMILA SARDER AS A DISTINGUISHED LEADER IN CENTRAL 
                   FLORIDA FOR WOMEN'S HISTORY MONTH

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. DARREN SOTO

                               of florida

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 18, 2025

  Mr. SOTO. Mr. Speaker, Dr. Lyudmila Sarder, M.D., is a highly 
esteemed internal medicine resident physician who has made remarkable 
contributions to the healthcare field across nine districts of Florida 
and the greater Central Florida region.
  With years of experience treating patients for a variety of 
conditions in both hospital and clinical settings, she has dedicated 
her career to serving underserved populations with compassion and 
commitment. Through hands-on rotations at prestigious healthcare 
institutions, including HCA Florida Osceola Hospital, AdventHealth 
Cancer Institute, Winter Haven Hospital, and Lakeland Regional Health, 
Dr. Lyudmila Sarder has gained invaluable expertise and national 
recognition for her dedication to advancing medical research. Her 
commendable efforts in addressing the financial barriers associated 
with cancer treatment have provided hope to countless patients and 
their families. She remains steadfast in her mission to pave the way 
for a more equitable healthcare system, ensuring that cost-effective 
treatments are accessible to those who need them most. The AdventHealth 
Cancer Institute has proudly unveiled findings marking a significant 
milestone in the field of cancer research, with Dr. Lyudmila Sarder 
playing a pivotal role in these advancements. As a dedicated health 
advocate, Dr. Lyudmila Sarder leads initiatives to expand healthcare 
access for uninsured and underserved populations. She works tirelessly 
to reduce health disparities by improving social determinants of health 
and enhancing community resources. She volunteers at Kissimmee Women's 
Health Center and Osceola Cancer Center, providing vital medical care 
to underserved populations in need. Additionally, she continues to 
serve as a resident physician for uninsured patients throughout Central 
Florida. Dr. Lyudmila Sarder co-authored the Amazon bestselling book, 
``Intern Mastery: A Roadmap from Med School to PGY-1 Internal Medicine 
Excellence,'' a crucial guide for medical students transitioning into 
residency. She is also a member of the American College of Physicians 
(ACP) and serves as a judge for ACP student presentations.
  Beyond her contributions to medicine and education, Dr. Lyudmila 
Sarder actively promotes sports and public health by participating in 
and volunteering as a physician for marathons such as the Hollis Cancer 
Marathon and Disney marathons. She is a core member of the Internal 
Medicine Academic Curriculum Board and Resident Recruitment and 
Selection Committee, mentoring medical students and shaping innovative 
educational strategies. Despite her demanding career, Dr. Lyudmila 
Sarder is also a devoted mother to two boys, balancing her professional 
and personal responsibilities with grace and determination. Her 
unwavering commitment to medicine, education, and advocacy continues to 
inspire the next generation of healthcare professionals while improving 
the lives of countless patients in her community.
