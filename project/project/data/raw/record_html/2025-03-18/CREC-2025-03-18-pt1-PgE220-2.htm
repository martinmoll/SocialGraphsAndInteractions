              CELEBRATING THE LEGACY OF DR. NELLIE B. KING

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                        Tuesday, March 18, 2025

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
extraordinary life and lasting legacy of Dr. Nellie B. King, a 
trailblazer in education, a steadfast advocate for youth, and a devoted 
public servant to Western New York. Dr King's remarkable contributions, 
both in and out of the classroom, have left an enduring impact on 
generations and continue to serve as a powerful source of inspiration 
for those dedicated to empowering others.
  With more than thirty-five years of service in education, Dr. King 
made history as the first African American Superintendent of the 
Lackawanna City School District. During her nine-year tenure, she 
spearheaded innovative programs such as the Kids in Spotlight Program, 
the Lackawanna Center for Family Achievement, and the District Model 
Aids Program, all of which brought meaningful and lasting change to the 
community.
  Beyond her professional accomplishments, Dr. King was a passionate 
community leader, guided by her strong faith and commitment to service. 
She was an active member of Calvary Christian Methodist Episcopal 
Church, Alpha Kappa Alpha Sorority, the Women's Missionary Council of 
the CME Church, and the World Federation of Methodist Women. Through 
her involvement in these organizations, Dr. King became a staunch 
advocate for social justice, equality, and empowerment, particularly 
for women and underrepresented groups.
  Dr. King's exceptional contributions to education and community 
service were recognized with several prestigious awards. She received 
the National Association of Counties Achievement Award, the Buffalo 
Ambassador Award, and the Martin Luther King, Jr. Award for Education, 
reflecting her impact in both local and national spheres. In 2019, it 
was my privilege to name her a New York State Senate Woman of 
Distinction, a well-deserved honor that celebrated her decades of 
commitment to education, leadership, and community empowerment.
  Today, we celebrate Dr. Nellie B. King--a pioneering educator, 
mentor, and visionary whose tireless efforts transformed not only the 
lives of her students but also the entire community. Her legacy is a 
testament to the profound influence one individual can have in creating 
hope, opportunity, and innovation for future generations.
