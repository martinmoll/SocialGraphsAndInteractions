                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  In You, O Lord, do we take refuge. Though our enemies arm themselves 
to wage war against the righteous, we find our assurance in knowing 
that You, O Lord, remain on Your throne, fully aware of and supreme 
over all that seeks to overwhelm us.
  Lord, You test both the righteous and the wicked. May we withstand 
Your examination and be found in the right. May our behavior stand in 
stark contrast to that of the wicked, whose souls love violence, and 
may the evildoers receive their due. May the devastation they seek be 
the portion of their own cup.
  You, O Lord, are righteous, and You love righteous deeds. May we live 
our lives in anticipation that the upright shall behold Your face.
  In Your eternal name, we pray.
  Amen.
