                    RECOGNIZING MOLLY BRIMMER LOLLI

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. BRETT GUTHRIE

                              of kentucky

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 17, 2025

  Mr. GUTHRIE. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Molly Brimmer Lolli 
for her service to the House Committee on Energy and Commerce.
  A graduate of the University of Maryland for both a bachelor's degree 
in Political Science and a Juris Doctorate degree, Molly began her 
career on Capitol Hill as a Legislative Assistant for Rep. Andy Harris 
(MD-01) in 2019, before becoming Senior Advisor on Health Policy and 
later Legislative Director for Rep. Richard Hudson  (NC-09) in 2021. In 
January 2023, Molly became Counsel for the Subcommittee on Health for 
the House Committee on Energy and Commerce under Chair Cathy McMorris 
Rodgers while I was serving as Subcommittee Chair. When I became 
Chairman in January of this year, I knew I wanted to keep her on my 
team as an expert on health issues.
  After serving two Chairmen and three Subcommittee Chairmen, Molly has 
departed the U.S. House of Representatives to accept another 
opportunity to continue to serve as a health expert. I wish her the 
best, congratulate her on her new role, and thank Molly Brimmer Lolli 
for her service to the House Committee on Energy and Commerce and the 
United States Congress. I also congratulate her on the birth of her and 
her husband Tim's new 6 pound, 2 ounces baby girl Carmela Gray Lolli 
who was born on October 26, 2025, and wish this young family all the 
best. I know Molly, Tim, and Carmela's big sister Breslin are very 
proud to welcome her to the family.
