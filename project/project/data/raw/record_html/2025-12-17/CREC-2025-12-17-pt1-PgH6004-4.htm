  DIRECTING THE PRESIDENT, PURSUANT TO SECTION 5(c) OF THE WAR POWERS 
RESOLUTION, TO REMOVE UNITED STATES ARMED FORCES FROM HOSTILITIES WITH 
   PRESIDENTIALLY DESIGNATED TERRORIST ORGANIZATIONS IN THE WESTERN 
                               HEMISPHERE

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on adoption of the concurrent 
resolution (H. Con. Res. 61) directing the President, pursuant to 
section 5(c) of the War Powers Resolution, to remove United States 
Armed Forces from hostilities with presidentially designated terrorist 
organizations in the Western Hemisphere, on which the yeas and nays 
were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the resolution.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 210, 
nays 216, not voting 7, as follows:
