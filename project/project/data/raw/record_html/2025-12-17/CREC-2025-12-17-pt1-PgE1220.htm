 COMMEMORATING ANNA ADAMIAN'S SERVICE TO MARYLAND'S 1ST CONGRESSIONAL 
                                DISTRICT

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. ANDY HARRIS

                              of maryland

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 17, 2025

  Mr. HARRIS of Maryland. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to congratulate my 
Communications Director, Anna Adamian, on her new position working in 
the Trump Administration. Anna is a dedicated staff member who has 
worked tirelessly to promote my message fighting for the 1st 
Congressional District. The commitment Anna has demonstrated has been 
exceptional and praiseworthy.
  Ms. Adamian has risen through the ranks of my office, beginning as 
Press Secretary to currently serving as my Communications Director. She 
has consistently excelled, working diligently with others to ensure my 
district stays informed of the resources available to them and how I am 
advocating for my constituents.
  It is important that Representatives recognize outstanding staff such 
as Ms. Adamian. Often times, the work of these staffers goes unnoticed 
but is nonetheless critical in maintaining the functions of our 
government. Anna is a true patriot, and Maryland's 1st Congressional 
District undoubtedly owes her a great debt of gratitude. I wish Anna 
well in her future endeavors at the White House.
  I thank Anna. She will be missed.
