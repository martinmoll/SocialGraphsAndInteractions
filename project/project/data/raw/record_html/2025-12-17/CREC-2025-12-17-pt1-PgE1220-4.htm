   RECOGNIZING THE RETIREMENT OF DENNIS EUGENE ``DENNY'' BUTCHER OF 
                          WATERTOWN, MINNESOTA

                                 ______
                                 

                             HON. TOM EMMER

                              of minnesota

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 17, 2025

  Mr. EMMER. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize Dennis Eugene 
``Denny'' Butcher of Watertown, Minnesota, for his retirement after 53 
years as the town's beloved Master Barber. Denny's impact reaches far 
beyond the barber chair through the many other ways he sewed 
Watertown's community.
  Known for his generosity and compassion, he routinely traveled from 
his barbershop to provide haircuts for residents of Elim Home and 
Westwood Place, as well as those confined to their homes due to 
illness. Additionally, Denny served his community as a volunteer 
firefighter and as an Emergency Medical Technician at the Watertown 
Hospital until its closure in 1971.
  He contributed to the cultural life of Watertown in many ways. For 
five years, Denny played with the Highliners Polka Band and later 
played with John Exklund and the Midwesterners, sharing joy and 
tradition with audiences across Minnesota and the region. And, for more 
than 25 years, Denny brought magic and delight to children, and 
families each holiday season playing the role of Santa Claus. Together 
with his wife, Mary, he has shown tremendous love for the community's 
elderly through their shared ministry of offering comfort, 
companionship, and care to those who need it most.
  Denny has become known for his signature style and personality--the 
man who wears shorts all year long, the scooter man, a tender-hearted 
jokester, who is always ready to laugh, and someone who truly embodies 
his well-known motto, ``I'm living the dream.''
  Denny's love for Watertown, his decades of service, and his heart of 
gold have made him a pillar of the community. As he completes his final 
day behind the barber chair on December 19, 2025, we honor his lifetime 
of contributions and celebrate the legacy he leaves behind.
  On behalf of Minnesota's 6th District, I extend my deepest gratitude 
to Dennis Eugene Butcher for his 53 years of dedication to the people 
of Watertown, Minnesota. His kindness, service, and joy have left a 
mark that will be remembered for generations.
