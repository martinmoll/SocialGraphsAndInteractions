HONORING MASTER SERGEANT DAVID FRIETS ON HIS RETIREMENT FROM THE UNITED 
                          STATES MARINE CORPS

                                  _____
                                 

                           HON. JACK BERGMAN

                              of michigan

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 17, 2025

  Mr. BERGMAN. Mr. Speaker, it is my honor to congratulate Master 
Sergeant (MSgt) David Friets on his retirement from the United States 
Marine Corps (USMC) after 20 years of dedicated service. I thank MSgt. 
Friets for his steadfast dedication towards keeping his fellow 
Servicemembers, the communities throughout Northern Michigan, and our 
Nation safe.
  Born and raised in Michigan's Upper Peninsula, the values of 
determination, integrity, and service to his country were instilled in 
him by his parents and community. It was these core values that would 
guide him through a distinguished and proud career with the USMC, 
shaping his dedication to his mission and fellow Marines.
  On December 6, 2005, shortly after graduating from Escanaba High 
School, MSgt. Friets enlisted in the USMC where he would go on to serve 
as a Signals Intelligence/Electronic Warfare (SIGNT/EW) Chief. In this 
role, he directed complex SIGINT collection, analysis, and 
dissemination efforts while also supervising the implementation of 
electronic warfare tactics for operational support. Within a variety of 
high-pressure environments, he ensured the security, integrity, and 
effectiveness of communications and intelligence systems.
  Mr. Speaker, let us all join in congratulating MSgt. David Friets on 
his retirement from the United States Marine Corps after 20 years of 
service. I wish him great happiness in retirement and thank him once 
more for keeping Michigan's 1st Congressional District and our Nation 
safe.
