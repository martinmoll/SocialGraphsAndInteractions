           By Mr. HAMADEH of Arizona:
       H.R. 6794.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Article I, Section 8, Clauses 12, 13, and 14 grant Congress 
     the powers to raise and support armies, to provide and 
     maintain a navy, and to make rules for the government and 
     regulation of the land and naval forces.
