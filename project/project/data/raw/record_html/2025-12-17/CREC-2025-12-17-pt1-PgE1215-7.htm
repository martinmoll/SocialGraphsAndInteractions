                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                         HON. VICENTE GONZALEZ

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 17, 2025

  Mr. VINCENTE GONZALEZ of Texas. Mr. Speaker, due to an important 
constituent engagement, I was not present to vote for Roll Call No. 335 
and Roll Call No. 336.

[[Page E1216]]

  Had I been present, I would have voted YEA on Roll Call No. 335. I 
support the bipartisan Congressional Award Program which encourages our 
youth to develop valuable life skills, foster citizenship, and build 
self-confidence.
  Also, had I been present, I would have voted YEA on Roll Call No. 
336. I support the transfer of a vacant parcel of property to Perry 
County for public use, particularly for youth programs, agricultural 
education, and conservation efforts.
