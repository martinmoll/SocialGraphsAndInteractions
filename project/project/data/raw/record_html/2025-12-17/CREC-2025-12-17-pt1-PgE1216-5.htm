          HONORING RONALD ``RON'' A. SEGE'S LIFETIME OF IMPACT

                                  _____
                                 

                          HON. SAM T. LICCARDO

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                      Wednesday, December 17, 2025

  Mr. LICCARDO. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the life and legacy 
of Mr. Ronald ``Ron'' A. Sege, a beloved member of our Silicon Valley 
community who passed away on November 30, 2025. Ron Sege was a 
respected technology executive, a philanthropist, and a dear friend to 
many. He leaves behind an extraordinary legacy of leadership, and 
service.
  Born in New York and raised in California, Ron earned his B.A. in 
Economics from Pomona College and an M.B.A. from Harvard

[[Page E1217]]

Business School. He embarked on a remarkable 40-year career in the tech 
industry, helping to shape the internet and networking revolution of 
the post-dot-com era. He was perhaps best known for serving as 
President, Chief Executive Officer, and Chairman of Echelon 
Corporation. Prior to Echelon, Ron served as President and Chief 
Operating Officer of the 3Com Corporation, a networking industry 
leader, and he led innovation startups such as Tropos Networks and 
Ellacoya Networks that advanced wireless broadband and internet 
technologies. Throughout these roles, he drove cutting-edge 
advancements across telecom, networking, wireless, and industrial 
internet sectors, earning widespread respect for his vision and 
business acumen.
  Beyond his professional achievements, Ron was deeply committed to his 
community. He generously lent his time and expertise to several 
nonprofit organizations in Silicon Valley, including serving on the 
executive boards of the Silicon Valley Leadership Group, and 
TheatreWorks Silicon Valley, and the Montalvo Arts Center, to improve 
education, public policy, and the arts in our region.
  Ron will be remembered not only for his substantial contributions to 
technology and the community, but also for his personal qualities that 
endeared him to all who knew him. Colleagues and friends alike recall 
him as a wise and thoughtful leader whose steady guidance, sharp 
instincts, and unwavering integrity earned him deep respect across the 
industry. I long admired Ron's personal kindness, always making others 
feel as though they were important enough to have the full attention 
and concern of a respected tech CEO. Ron lived life fully and 
fearlessly, he was an avid motorcyclist, a music and photography 
enthusiast, and an adventurous traveler.
  Above all, Ron was devoted to his family. He is survived by his 
loving wife, Mahasti, and his four children Chris, Jon, Georgia, and 
Alexi. Ron's legacy of leadership, friendship, and humanity will 
continue to guide and inspire us in years to come. He will be greatly 
missed, but his influence endures in the countless lives he touched.
