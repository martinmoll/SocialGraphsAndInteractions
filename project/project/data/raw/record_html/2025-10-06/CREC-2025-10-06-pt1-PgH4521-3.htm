                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Father of lights, shine Your light in our lives this day. You who are 
pure, reveal to us Your righteousness. In You there is no darkness at 
all. Lead us into Your truth.
  Remind us again that even if we find ourselves standing in the shadow 
of deceit, of meanness, and even despair, if we remain in fellowship 
with You, attuning our hearts to Your truth and living our lives as a 
reflection of Your grace and mercy, we will find hope in the light of 
Your abiding presence.
  Cleanse us from all unrighteousness, our own guile, unkindness, and 
pessimism. Remove from us the overwhelming cloud of doubt that 
threatens to overtake our spirits.
  With the light of Your truth before us, illuminate our paths that we 
may live in perfect fellowship with You and with one another.
  Blameless may we come before You, offering our prayers in Your 
eternal and perfect name.
  Amen.
