           By Mr. BEGICH:
       H.R. 5694.
       Congress has the power to enact this legislation pursuant 
     to the following:
       Pursuant to clause 7 of rule XII of the Rules of the House 
     of Representatives, the Congress finds the authority for this 
     legislation in the following provisions of the Constitution: 
     Article I, Section 8, Clause 3--The Congress shall have Power 
     To regulate Commerce with foreign Nations, and among the 
     several States, and with the Indian Tribes. This clause 
     grants Congress the authority to regulate interstate and 
     tribal commerce, including the trade and sale of marine 
     mammal products and Alaska Native handicrafts derived 
     therefrom. The bill amends the Marine Mammal Protection Act 
     of 1972 to clarify and protect lawful Alaska Native commerce 
     in traditional handicrafts and ivory products, and to ensure 
     consistent federal standards governing such trade across 
     state lines and tribal jurisdictions.
