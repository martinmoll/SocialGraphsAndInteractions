                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  Lord, God of hosts, You have gathered us together this week around 
family dining rooms, community soup kitchens, and neighborhood corners, 
that we would bear witness to Your steadfast loving-kindness to all 
people. Now, in the quiet of these Chambers, we come to ask Your 
blessing.
  For it is You who has remained beside us, guided us, and joined us in 
the living of our lives. You have directed our steps and ordered the 
work we have striven to accomplish. Like a parent, from the beginning, 
You have been at our side, quickening our minds to understand and 
correcting our failures to ensure that Your will, and not our own, 
would be done.
  We pray that You will continue to grant us both Your defense and Your 
discipline as we seek to serve You. Protect us from those who wish to 
do evil, but, Lord, chastise us and restrain us when we are the ones 
who hinder Your purposes.
  Nourished by moments of thankfulness we have shared and experienced 
this week, may we live lives, speak words, and do kindnesses that will 
reveal the glory of Your name and the blessing we each have received by 
Your grace.
  May Your name be ever praised, for You forget not Your people.
  Amen.
