           CELEBRATING THE GRAND OPENING OF MOLINA HEALTHCARE

                                 ______
                                 

                        HON. TIMOTHY M. KENNEDY

                              of new york

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 17, 2025

  Mr. KENNEDY of New York. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to recognize and 
celebrate Molina Healthcare, a leader in community-based healthcare 
since its launch nearly five decades ago. It is with great enthusiasm 
that I highlight the opening of their newest location in Buffalo, New 
York. This expansion continues Molina's decades-long tradition of 
providing exceptional, one-on-one services to communities across 
Western New York.
  Structured to address health disparities across the United States, 
Molina Healthcare has provided quality federally supported healthcare 
for more than 5.1 million patients nationwide by using trusted clinical 
expertise to guide individuals in achieving their health and wellness 
goals. Founded with a commitment to patient-centered care, the new 
office will bring Molina Healthcare's physical, social, and emotional 
approach for their patients, ensuring the continuation of Molina's 
commitment to exceptional care.
  Today, we celebrate the ongoing success and growth of Molina 
Healthcare and their steadfast commitment to empowering patients across 
the United States. With the increasing need for quality low-cost and 
reliable healthcare services, Molina remains at the forefront of 
quality member healthcare, continually adapting to meet the needs of 
all communities. Congratulations to Molina Healthcare as they continue 
to provide exceptional care and contribute to the well-being of our 
region.
