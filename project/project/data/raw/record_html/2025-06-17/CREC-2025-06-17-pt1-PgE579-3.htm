  RECOGNIZING THE ARMY & AIR FORCE EXCHANGE SERVICE 130TH ANNIVERSARY

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. MARC A. VEASEY

                                of texas

                    in the house of representatives

                         Tuesday, June 17, 2025

  Mr. VEASEY. Mr. Speaker, today I rise to recognize the Army & Air 
Force Exchange Service, the Department of Defense's largest retailer, 
as it marks its 130th year of serving our military community with 
distinction.
  Since its founding in 1895, the Exchange has brought a piece of home 
to Soldiers, Airmen, and Guardians around the world, staying true to 
its motto: ``We Go Where You Go.'' From providing uniforms and meals to 
managing stores, wellness services, and online retail, the Exchange has 
stood as a pillar of support for those who serve.
  Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, the Exchange continues to reinvest in 
military families--returning $15 billion in value to the community over 
the last decade alone.
  As we reflect on its 130-year legacy, we celebrate not only its 
longevity but the critical role it plays in supporting readiness, 
morale, and quality of life.
  I congratulate the Exchange. Here's to many more years of dedicated 
service.
