 WELCOMING JARED ANDERSON AND FRIENDS OF CLUB 21 CHOIR TO WASHINGTON, 
                                  D.C.

  (Mr. CRANK asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend his remarks.)
  Mr. CRANK. Mr. Speaker, I am honored to welcome Jared Anderson and 
the Friends of Club 21 Choir here to Washington, D.C.
  The choir, composed of talented individuals living with Down syndrome 
and who are based in Colorado's Fifth Congressional District, will be 
performing the national anthem to launch the March for Life rally this 
Friday.
  Club 21 came and visited me just a couple of months ago here in 
Washington, D.C., and it is really one of the highlights of my time as 
being a Member of Congress. They have uplifted the Pikes Peak community 
onto the national platform, amplifying and empowering these brilliant 
performers through the arts and education.
  I thank them for dedicating their time, their energy, and their 
passion in advocating for every human life.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask that you join me in giving a warm welcome to the 
Friends of Club 21 Choir.
