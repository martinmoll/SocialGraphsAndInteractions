  PERMISSION FOR MEMBER TO BE CONSIDERED AS FIRST SPONSOR OF H.R. 4327

be considered to be the first sponsor of H.R. 4327, a bill originally 
introduced by Representative Greene of Georgia, for the purpose of 
adding cosponsors and requesting reprintings pursuant to clause 7 of 
rule XII.

gentleman from Missouri?
