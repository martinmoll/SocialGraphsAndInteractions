               HONORING THE LIFE OF CIRILO TIRADO DELGADO

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. PABLO JOSE HERNANDEZ

                             of puerto rico

                    in the house of representatives

                         Monday, March 31, 2025

  Mr. HERNANDEZ. Mr. Speaker, I rise to honor the life of Cirilo Tirado 
Delgado, who passed away this month at the age of 86. Mr Tirado was a 
respected public servant, educator, and attorney in the great 
Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.
  He served in the Puerto Rico House of Representatives from 1973 to 
1984, worked in the executive branch from 1984 to 1988, and returned to 
the legislature as a Senator from 1988 to 1996. After that, he 
practiced law in the town of Guayama.
  I would like to extend my condolences to his widow, children, and 
grandchildren. May he rest in peace.
