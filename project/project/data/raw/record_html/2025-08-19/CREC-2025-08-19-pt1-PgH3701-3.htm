                                 PRAYER

  Rev. Stephanie Vader, Capitol Hill United Methodist Church, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  God who is known by many names, we pause to give thanks that You are 
present with us.
  God of history, here in this room where history is shaped, we pray 
for all those who work here to see their tasks as sacred and to be 
filled with hope, creativity, and endurance.
  God of empathy and resolve, give us the determination that we, each 
one of us, will do whatever we can to help build God's dream, a world 
where all beings flourish.
  God of humility, You show us the way of the servant. Rather than 
being motivated by pride and self-righteousness, may we, as Your 
people, clothe ourselves with kindness, humility, gentleness, and 
patience. Manifest in us the ability to hold in tension the 
disagreement of others and our own convictions with grace.
  God of impossibilities, You presided over the emancipations and 
transformations of our ancestors. Pour out Your power and emancipate 
and transform the broken parts of our world.
  Amen.
