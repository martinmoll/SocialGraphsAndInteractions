            RESEARCH SECURITY AND ACCOUNTABILITY IN DHS ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore (Mr. Gimenez). Pursuant to clause 8 of rule 
XX, the unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the 
rules and pass the bill (H.R. 901) to require the Under Secretary of 
the Science and Technology Directorate of the Department of Homeland 
Security to develop a Department-wide policy and process to safeguard 
research and development from unauthorized access to or disclosure of 
sensitive information in research and development acquisitions, and for 
other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Tennessee (Mr. Green) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 410, 
nays 1, not voting 21, as follows:
