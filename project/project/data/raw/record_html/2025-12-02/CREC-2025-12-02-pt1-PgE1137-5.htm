                    HONORING PASTOR LARRY G. COZART

                                  _____
                                 

                        HON. BENNIE G. THOMPSON

                             of mississippi

                    in the house of representatives

                       Tuesday, December 2, 2025

  Mr. THOMPSON of Mississippi. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor the 
distinguished life and service of Pastor Larry G. Cozart, a revered 
member of our community whose dedication and leadership have left an 
indelible mark on countless lives. Born and raised in Clarksdale, 
Mississippi, Pastor Cozart has devoted more than 40 years to serving 
his community through ministry, healthcare, and public service.
  Pastor Cozart's journey is a testament to his unwavering commitment 
to service. As the devoted husband of Veleria P. Cozart, father of

[[Page E1138]]

five children, and grandfather of seven grandchildren, he has balanced 
family life with a profound dedication to uplifting those around him. 
His career began in healthcare, where he served as a certified 
Physician Assistant, Phlebotomist, and X-ray Technician, among other 
roles. His leadership further extended into public service, including 
time as my District Coordinator and as a mentor with the Mississippi 
Department of Corrections Outreach Program.
  Spiritually, Pastor Cozart has been a guiding light for many. He 
faithfully served as a youth minister at New Jerusalem M.B. Church and 
St. John M.B. Church before becoming the pastor of New St. Phillips 
Missionary Baptist Church in Sledge, Mississippi, where he has served 
with distinction for 23 years.
  Pastor Cozart is also academically accomplished. He holds a 
Bachelor's and Master's Degree in Counseling, a Doctoral Degree in 
Theology, and numerous certifications in healthcare and public service. 
His dedication to education and lifelong learning reflects his deep 
commitment to personal growth and community advancement.
  Today, as the newly appointed County Coroner, Pastor Cozart continues 
to serve with integrity, compassion, and respect--upholding the dignity 
of every individual and family he encounters. His leadership and 
collaboration with the Board of Supervisors have been instrumental in 
supporting a strong and thriving community.
  Mr. Speaker, I ask my colleagues to join me in honoring Pastor Larry 
G. Cozart for his lifelong dedication to service, his community, and 
his faith. His legacy of leadership, compassion, and commitment will 
continue to inspire us all.
