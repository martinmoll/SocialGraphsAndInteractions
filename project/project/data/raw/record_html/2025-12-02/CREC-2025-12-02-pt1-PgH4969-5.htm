                        HONORING GARY STEINHARDT

  (Mr. Baird of Indiana was recognized to address the House for 5 
minutes.)
  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Gary Steinhardt from 
Lafayette, Indiana.
  Gary Steinhardt served in the U.S. Army for 30 years and is a veteran 
of the Vietnam and Iraq wars.
  Gary has also served as a professor of agronomy at Purdue University, 
focusing on soil conservation and the preservation of agricultural 
land. He has been with Purdue for nearly 50 years.
  Gary has been a great advocate for veterans in Tippecanoe County for 
years. Gary has served in multiple positions within the VFW and the 
American Legion, such as post chaplain and commander of post 38. He has 
also been very active with other Vietnam veterans' organizations in the 
area, furthering legislation affecting veterans' issues.
  Gary was recently diagnosed with a life-limiting illness. My thoughts 
and prayers go out to Gary and his family during this time, and I thank 
him for his many years of selfless service to our country, our 
veterans, and Purdue University.


                           Honoring Rick Lang

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise today to honor Martinsville Police 
Chief Rick Lang, who is retiring from the Martinsville Police 
Department this month after over 50 years in law enforcement.
  He first became a law enforcement officer in 1974 and has spent most 
of his life faithfully serving the people of Morgan County with 
unwavering dedication.
  Rick Lang first served as the Martinsville police chief from 2015 to 
2016 and took over for a second time as chief that year. Prior to 
serving as police chief, Rick Lang served as a Morgan County-based 
detective with the Indiana State Police.
  I thank Rick for his dedicated years of selfless service to our 
Martinsville and Morgan County communities, and I wish him the best in 
his well-deserved retirement as he begins this exciting new chapter in 
his life.


                    Recognizing Brownsburg Bulldogs

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate back-to-back Indiana 
High School Athletic Association's 6A State champion football team of 
Brownsburg Bulldogs.
  The Brownsburg Bulldogs faced Westfield High School once again in the 
State finals at Lucas Oil Stadium. Brownsburg successfully defended its 
title as the reigning State football champion, defeating Westfield with 
a final score of 38-31. The Brownsburg football team finishes their 
remarkable season undefeated with a record of 14-0.
  I congratulate these outstanding student athletes on winning back-to-
back State championships. Every one of these students should be 
incredibly proud of their hard work, dedication, and commitment to 
excellence.
  I also thank the parents, the coaches, and the faculty, who gave 
their time and efforts for this team. They couldn't have won without 
their support.
  Once again, I congratulate the reigning State 6A football champions, 
the Brownsburg Bulldogs.


                    Recognizing South Putnam Eagles

  Mr. BAIRD. Mr. Speaker, I rise to congratulate the South Putnam High 
School football team on winning the IHSAA 1A State championship this 
year. The South Putnam Eagles took on Pioneer. After a back-and-forth 
battle in the first half, the South Putnam Eagles took on a commanding 
lead, achieved a decisive victory, and won with a final score of 55-29 
against Pioneer. This historic victory is South Putnam's first State 
football championship since 1986.
  This State championship win is a testament to the South Putnam 
student athletes' dedication, teamwork, and perseverance. They should 
be incredibly proud of their hard work.
  I thank the parents, teachers, and Coach Chuck Sorrell for their 
support and efforts that helped this team succeed on and off of the 
field.
  Again, I congratulate the South Putnam Eagles on their State 
championship.
