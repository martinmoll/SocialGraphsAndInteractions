      ROTORCRAFT OPERATIONS TRANSPARENCY AND OVERSIGHT REFORM ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (S. 2503), to require all aircraft to be equipped with 
Automatic Dependent Surveillance-Broadcast In, to improve aviation 
safety, and for other purposes, on which the yeas and nays were 
ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Missouri (Mr. Graves) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 264, 
nays 133, not voting 35, as follows:
