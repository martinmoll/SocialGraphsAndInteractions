                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  God of power and might, mercy and peace, we raise our voices on 
behalf of all those who have cried for so long that their voices are 
weary and their words are faint. Especially on this fourth anniversary 
of the enduring conflict in Ukraine, let not their hearts fear. Though 
war has risen up against them without cease or cause, even still may 
they remain confident that You are their light and salvation.
  Lord, be the stronghold that pushes back the enemy's relentless 
campaign against them. Hide them in the shelter of Your sanctuary in 
these endless days of trouble.
  Then lift up their voices as they testify to the world their faith in 
You. Even as they refuse to give up their will to their adversaries, 
may their confidence in You serve as a testimony to all of us. As they 
wait for You, may their faith be strong and their hearts take courage 
that all may know that our salvation is found only in You.
  Sovereign God, may all who believe in You when enemies encamp against 
us see Your goodness, O Lord, in our lifetime.
  Hear the fervent prayers of all who call on Your name these days.
  Amen.
