                          PERSONAL EXPLANATION

                                 ______
                                 

                          HON. JOHN B. LARSON

                             of connecticut

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 9, 2025

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, had I been present for Roll 
Call Vote No. 3, Ordering the Previous Question on H. Res. 5, I would 
have voted Nay.
  Had I been present for Roll Call Vote No. 4, Motion to Commit H. Res. 
5, I would have voted Yea.
  Had I been present for Roll Call Vote No. 5, Agreeing to H. Res. 5, I 
would have voted Nay.
