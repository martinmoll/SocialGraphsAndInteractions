      HIGHLIGHTING GOVERNOR HENRY McMASTER'S 2027 EXECUTIVE BUDGET

                                 ______
                                 

                            HON. JOE WILSON

                           of south carolina

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 22, 2026

  Mr. WILSON of South Carolina. Mr. Speaker, this week Governor Henry 
McMaster announced his 2027 Executive Budget, benefiting the people of 
South Carolina. The Executive Budget highlights include:
  Investing over one billion dollars in South Carolina roads;
  Cutting income tax;
  Raising teacher pay;
  Expanding school choice; and
  Providing scholarships for workforce technical colleges, developing 
training opportunities, creating jobs.
  With President Trump devolving power back to the states, I am 
grateful to continue to work on behalf of the people of South Carolina, 
joining Governor McMaster, the Republican super majorities in the 
Senate, led by President Thomas Alexander, the House, with Speaker 
Murrell Smith, all to Make America Great Again.
  In conclusion, God Bless Our Troops as the Global War on Terrorism 
continues. Trump is reinstituting Peace Through Strength, revealing war 
criminal Putin lies, insulting Trump and mocking Trump with continued 
mass murder of Ukrainian civilians. I support the Arctic security 
initiative President Trump has in Greenland with our allies. The most 
effective and least costly policy is to defeat war criminal Putin in 
Ukraine which deters missile attacks by the Chinese Communist Party.
