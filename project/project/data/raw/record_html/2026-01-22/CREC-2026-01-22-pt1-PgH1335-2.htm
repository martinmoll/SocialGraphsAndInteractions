     DIRECTING THE CLERK OF THE HOUSE OF REPRESENTATIVES TO MAKE A 
               CORRECTION IN THE ENGROSSMENT OF H.R. 7147

  Mr. CISCOMANI. Mr. Speaker, I send to the desk a resolution and ask 
for its immediate consideration.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Is there objection to the request of the 
gentleman from Arizona?
  There was no objection.
  The text of the resolution is as follows:

                              H. Res. 1016

       Resolved, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives 
     shall, in the engrossment of the bill H.R. 7147, make the 
     following correction: In section 230(b), strike ``in this Act 
     or any other act'' and insert ``for the Department of 
     Homeland Security in this or any prior Act''; and such 
     correction shall be incorporated in the engrossment of H.R. 
     7148 pursuant to House Resolution 1014.
  The resolution was agreed to.
  A motion to reconsider was laid on the table.
