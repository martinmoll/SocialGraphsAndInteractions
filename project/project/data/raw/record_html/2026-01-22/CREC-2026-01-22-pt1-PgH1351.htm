                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII,

       ML-49. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the House of 
     Representatives of the State of Ohio, relative to House 
     Resolution No. 243, requesting the Secretary of the Air Force 
     select the 121st Air Refueling Wing at the Rickenbacker Air 
     National Guard Base in Columbus, Ohio as the preferred main 
     operating base for the KC-46 Pegasus refueling aircraft; 
     which was referred to the Committee on Armed Services.
