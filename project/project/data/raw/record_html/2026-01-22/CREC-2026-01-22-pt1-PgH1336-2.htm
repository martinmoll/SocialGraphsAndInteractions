                     RECOGNIZING THE REMEDY CHURCH

  (Mrs. SYKES asked and was given permission to address the House for 1 
minute and to revise and extend her remarks.)
  Mrs. SYKES. Mr. Speaker, today, I rise to recognize The Remedy Church 
and its congregation as Ohio's 13th Congressional District's Champion 
of the Week.
  This past weekend marked a significant milestone as The Remedy Church 
celebrated 5 years of faithful service to God and community. Under the 
leadership of Pastor Deante Lavender and Arnalesa Lavender, 
affectionately known as Pastor Lav and Pastor Lesa, The Remedy Church 
is dedicated to welcoming everyone from our community with open arms.
  They are guided by the motto, Love Christ, Love People, Build 
Community, and the mission to ``teach Christ boldly, love our city 
deeply, and build lasting and strategic partnerships that transform 
lives.''
  Earlier this year, The Remedy Church moved to downtown Akron, further 
establishing themselves in the greater Akron faith community. Their 
reach goes beyond the church doors, as they have fed thousands, housed 
hundreds, and ministered in prisons and throughout the community.
  My husband, Kevin, and I recently had the pleasure of attending a 
service at The Remedy Church, which was also their fifth anniversary. 
We were moved by the enthusiastic spirit of fellowship, joy, and 
genuine camaraderie that filled the sanctuary.
  I sincerely congratulate Pastor Lav, Pastor Lesa, and The Remedy 
Church congregation on their fifth anniversary. May they continue to 
grow, serve, and be a blessing to both our community and the Lord and 
Savior for years to come.
