                                 PRAYER

  The Chaplain, the Reverend Margaret Grun Kibben, offered the 
following prayer:
  In You, O Lord, all things were created. We may be inclined to think 
of Heaven as Your only domain, but everything on Earth is governed by 
You.
  These things we see and the things we cannot see are all part of Your 
divine design. You have allotted thrones and powers, rulers and 
authorities their time. In all things, You remind us that everything 
has been created not just through You, but for You.
  Forgive us when we fail to remember that You never relinquish Your 
authority, leaving us to our own devices. Rather, You grant us 
responsibility for the stewardship of Your creation.
  On this body, You have laid the mantle of governance, not that it 
would take the fate of this country into its own hands, but that each 
of us would look to You for Your direction so that Your purposes would 
be accomplished through our faithfulness to Your will.
  For You, O God, are before all things. Only in You will all things, 
our work, our hopes, our lives, hold together.
  Guide us then, O Sovereign Lord, into this day. It is in Your eternal 
name we pray.
  Amen.
