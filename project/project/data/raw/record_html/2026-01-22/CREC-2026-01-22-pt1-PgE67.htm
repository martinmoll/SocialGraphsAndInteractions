                          HON. JOHN B. LARSON

                    in the house of representatives

                       Thursday, January 22, 2026

  Mr. LARSON of Connecticut. Mr. Speaker, I regrettably missed a vote 
on January 21, 2026. Had I been present, I would have voted: Nay on 
Roll Call No. 38, on passage of H.J. Res. 140, Providing for 
congressional disapproval under Chapter 8 of title 5, United States 
Code, of the rule submitted by the Bureau of Land Management relating 
to Public Land Order No. 7917 for Withdrawal of Federal Lands; Cook, 
Lake, and Saint Louis Counties, MN.
