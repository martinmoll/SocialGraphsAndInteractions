            SINGLE, UNIQUE ZIP CODES FOR CERTAIN COMMUNITIES

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 3095) to direct the United States Postal Service to 
designate single, unique ZIP Codes for certain communities, and for 
other purposes, as amended, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Kentucky (Mr. Comer) that the House suspend the rules 
and pass the bill, as amended.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 278, 
nays 121, not voting 31, as follows:
