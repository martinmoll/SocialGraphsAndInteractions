                               MEMORIALS

  Under clause 3 of rule XII, memorials were presented and referred as 
follows:

       ML-10. The SPEAKER presented a memorial of the General 
     Assembly of the State of Ohio, relative to House Resolution 
     No. 147, to urge Congress to establish a special committee to 
     investigate the East Palestine, Ohio train derailment and the 
     federal Environmental Protection Agency's response to it.; to 
     the Committee on Rules.
       ML-11. Also, a memorial of the Senate of the State of 
     Alaska, relative to Senate Resolution No. 3, urging the 
     United States Congress to grant American Samoans the ability 
     to serve as commissioned officers in the uniformed services 
     of the United States and the right to vote in federal 
     elections.; jointly to the Committees on Armed Services and 
     House Administration.
