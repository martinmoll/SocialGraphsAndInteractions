                      COMMUNICATIONS SECURITY ACT

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on the motion to suspend the rules and 
pass the bill (H.R. 1717) to direct the Federal Communications 
Commission to establish a council to make recommendations on ways to 
increase the security, reliability, and interoperability of 
communications networks, and for other purposes, on which the yeas and 
nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the bill.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on the motion offered by the 
gentleman from Ohio (Mr. Latta) that the House suspend the rules and 
pass the bill.
  This is a 5-minute vote.
  The vote was taken by electronic device, and there were--yeas 380, 
nays 33, not voting 19, as follows:
