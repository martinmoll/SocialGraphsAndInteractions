  PROVIDING FOR CONSIDERATION OF SENATE AMENDMENT TO H. CON. RES. 14, 
       CONCURRENT RESOLUTION ON THE BUDGET FOR FISCAL YEAR 2025.

  The SPEAKER pro tempore. Pursuant to clause 8 of rule XX, the 
unfinished business is the vote on ordering the previous question on 
the resolution (H. Res. 313) providing for consideration of the Senate 
amendment to the concurrent resolution (H. Con. Res. 14) establishing 
the congressional budget for the United States Government for fiscal 
year 2025 and setting forth the appropriate budgetary levels for fiscal 
years 2026 through 2034, on which the yeas and nays were ordered.
  The Clerk read the title of the resolution.
  The SPEAKER pro tempore. The question is on ordering the previous 
question.
