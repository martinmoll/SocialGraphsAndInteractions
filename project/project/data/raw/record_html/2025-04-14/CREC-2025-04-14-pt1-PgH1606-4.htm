              JOINT RESOLUTION PRESENTED TO THE PRESIDENT

  Kevin F. McCumber, Clerk of the House, reported that on April 01, 
2025, the following joint resolution was presented to the President of 
the United States for approval:

       H.J. Res. 25 Providing for congressional disapproval under 
     chapter 8 of title 5, United States Code, of the rule 
     submitted by the Internal Revenue Service relating to ``Gross 
     Proceeds Reporting by Brokers That Regularly Provide Services 
     Effectuating Digital Asset Sales''.
