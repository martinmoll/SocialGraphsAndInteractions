                                 PRAYER

  Monsignor Stephen J. Rossetti, The Catholic University of America, 
Washington, D.C., offered the following prayer:
  Good and gracious God, today, in our world and in our own lives, 
there is much uncertainty and fear. But we also know that You are our 
creator and that You hold the world in the palm of Your hand. You ask 
us to trust in You. Give us a heart full of that divine trust. May we 
look to You with confidence and know that You are the loving Lord of 
our lives. We pray this in Your most holy name.
  Amen.
