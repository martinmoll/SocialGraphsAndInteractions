              OPPOSITION TO H.J. RES. 24 AND H.J. RES. 75

                                 ______
                                 

                       HON. NANETTE DIAZ BARRAGAN

                             of california

                    in the house of representatives

                        Thursday, March 27, 2025

  Ms. BARRAGAN. Mr. Speaker, I oppose H.J. Res. 24 and H.J. Res. 75 
because these resolutions roll back common-sense energy efficiency 
standards for commercial refrigerators, freezers, and walk-in coolers--
standards that were rigorously developed by the Department of Energy 
(DOE) to save businesses money while cutting energy waste. These 
updated regulations are technologically feasible, economically 
justified, and essential for reducing long-term costs for small 
businesses like restaurants, grocery stores, and convenience

[[Page E256]]

stores. The DOE estimates that businesses using these appliances would 
save over $11 billion in energy costs over the next 30 years--savings 
that would go back into local economies. I urged the Biden 
administration to finalize these rules last Congress, and I remain 
committed to protecting energy efficiency initiatives that reduce costs 
and pollution.
