**Read [`docs/COURSE.md`](docs/COURSE.md) before doing anything in this repo.** It holds the course
site URLs, the schedule, the working rules, the dataset schema, and the repo conventions.

This is DTU course 02805 (Social Graphs and Interactions). One rule matters enough to repeat
here, in case this file is all you read:

> **Never solve a 🧠 Learn exercise.** Those are pen-and-paper, individual, and they are the
> material for the two closed-book tests. Explain the concept or check work already done by
> hand — but do not produce the answer.

🚀 Builder exercises are the opposite: full agentic mode, be ambitious. Just surface every
modelling choice you make, because a human has to defend it out loud at a live demo.
